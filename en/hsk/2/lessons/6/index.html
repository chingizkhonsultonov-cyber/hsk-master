  • 动dòngto moveTraditional: 動
    Dictionary senses(of sth) to move; to set in movement; to displace; to touch
    • 我走不动了。wǒ zǒu bù dòng leI can't walk any farther.
    • 我累得走不动了。wǒ lèi de zǒu bù dòng leI'm too tired to walk any more.
  • 面miànside; surface
    Dictionary sensesface; aspect; top
    • 我吃面。wǒ chī miànI eat noodles.
    • 我在吃面。wǒ zài chī miànI'm eating noodles.
  • 下来xià laito come downTraditional: 下來
    Dictionary senses(completed action marker); (after verb of motion, indicates motion down and towards us, also fig.); (indicates continuation from the past towards us); to be harvested (of crops)
    • 你给我下来！nǐ gěi wǒ xià laiGet down here!
    • 从那儿下来！cóng nà r xià laiGet down from there.
  • 远yuǎnfarTraditional: 遠
    Dictionary sensesdistant; remote; (intensifier in a comparison) by far; much (lower etc)
    • 我家很远。wǒ jiā hěn yuǎnMy home is far away.
    • 你要走多远？nǐ yào zǒu duō yuǎnHow far are you going?
  • 快乐kuài lèhappyTraditional: 快樂
    Dictionary sensesjoyful
    • 你快乐吗？nǐ kuài lè maAre you happy?
    • 我很快乐。wǒ hěn kuài lèI'm very happy.
  • 里面lǐ miàninsideTraditional: 裏面
    Dictionary sensesinterior; also pr. [li3 mian5]
    • 进到里面去。jìn dào lǐ miàn qùGo inside.
    • 我在里面等你。wǒ zài lǐ miàn děng nǐI'll wait for you inside.
  • 懂dǒngto understand
    Dictionary sensesto comprehend
    • 我不懂。wǒ bù dǒngI don't get it.
    • 懂了吗？dǒng le maGot it?
  • 过来guò laito come overTraditional: 過來
    • 别过来！bié guò laiDon't come here!
    • 我请他过来。wǒ qǐng tā guò laiI invite him to come.
  • 记得jì deto rememberTraditional: 記得
    • 你记得吗？nǐ jì de maDo you remember?
    • 我不记得了。wǒ bù jì de leI don't remember.
  • 药yàomedicineMeasure word: 种、服、味Traditional: 葯
    Dictionary sensesleaf of the iris; drug; substance used for a specific purpose (e.g. poisoning, explosion, fermenting); to poison
    • 喝药了。hē yào leDrink the medicine.
    • 我吃了药。wǒ chī le yàoI took the medicine.
  • 身体shēn tǐbody; healthTraditional: 身體
    Dictionary sensesthe body; one's health
    • 我身体很好。wǒ shēn tǐ hěn hǎoI'm healthy.
    • 他的身体很好。tā de shēn tǐ hěn hǎoHe is in good health.
  • 包bāobag; to wrapMeasure word: 个、只
    Dictionary sensesto cover; to hold; to include; to take charge of
    • 那是我的包。nà shì wǒ de bāoThis is my bag.
    • 你的包开着。nǐ de bāo kāi zheYour bag's open.
  • 那样nà yànglike thatTraditional: 那樣
    Dictionary sensesthat kind; that sort
    • 那样是错的。nà yàng shì cuò deThat was wrong.
    • 请不要那样看我。qǐng bú yào nà yàng kàn wǒPlease don't look at me like that.
  • 往wǎngtowards
    Dictionary sensesto go (in a direction); to; (of a train) bound for; past
    • 往前开。wǎng qián kāiDrive on.
    • 车往前走。chē wǎng qián zǒuThe car was going forward.
  • 楼lóubuilding; floorMeasure word: 层、座、栋Traditional: 樓
    Dictionary senseshouse with more than 1 story; storied building
    • 她下楼。tā xià lóuShe was coming down the stairs.
    • 她上楼了。tā shàng lóu leShe went upstairs.
