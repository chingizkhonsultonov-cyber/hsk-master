  • 篮球lán qiúbasketballMeasure word: 个、只Traditional: 籃球
    • 他打篮球。tā dǎ lán qiúHe played basketball.
    • 我不打篮球。wǒ bù dǎ lán qiúI don't play basketball.
  • 游泳yóu yǒngto swim; swimming
    • 我会游泳。wǒ huì yóu yǒngI can swim.
    • 他不会游泳。tā bù huì yóu yǒngHe can't swim.
  • 裤子kù zitrousersMeasure word: 条Traditional: 袴子
    Dictionary sensespants
    • 那是我的裤子。nà shì wǒ de kù ziThose are my pants.
    • 我买了两条裤子。wǒ mǎi le liǎng tiáo kù ziI bought two pairs of pants.
  • 跳舞tiào wǔto dance
    • 我想跳舞。wǒ xiǎng tiào wǔI want to dance.
    • 你会跳舞吗？nǐ huì tiào wǔ maDo you know how to dance?
  • 阴yīncloudy; overcastTraditional: 陰
    Dictionary sensesovercast (weather); shady; Yin (the negative principle of Yin and Yang); negative (electric.)
    • 明天不是阴天。míng tiān bù shì yīn tiānTomorrow will not be cloudy.
  • 上网shàng wǎngto go onlineTraditional: 上網
    Dictionary sensesto connect to the Internet; (of a document etc) to be uploaded to the Internet; (tennis, volleyball etc) to move in close to the net
  • 商场shāng chǎngshopping mallMeasure word: 家Traditional: 商場
    Dictionary sensesshopping center; department store; emporium; the business world
    • 我想去商场。wǒ xiǎng qù shāng chǎngI'd like to go to the mall.
  • 外国wài guóforeign countryTraditional: 外國
    • 我想去外国。wǒ xiǎng qù wài guóI want to go abroad.
    • 外国人很有意思。wài guó rén hěn yǒu yì siForeign people intrigue me.
  • 姓名xìng míngfull name
  • 车站chē zhànstation; bus stopMeasure word: 处、个Traditional: 車站
    Dictionary sensesrail station
    • 那是车站吗？nà shì chē zhàn maIs that the station?
    • 那家店正对着车站。nà jiā diàn zhèng duì zhe chē zhànThe shop is just in front of the station.
  • 走路zǒu lùto walk
    Dictionary sensesto go on foot; to leave; to depart; (informal) to clear off
    • 他走路回家。tā zǒu lù huí jiāHe walked home.
    • 他走路很快。tā zǒu lù hěn kuàiHe walks fast.
  • 左边zuǒ bianleft sideTraditional: 左邊
    Dictionary sensesleft; the left side; to the left of
    • 你家的左边是什么？nǐ jiā de zuǒ bian shì shén meWhat is to the left of your house?
  • 从小cóng xiǎosince childhoodTraditional: 從小
    Dictionary sensesfrom childhood; from a young age
  • 爱好ài hàohobby; to be keen onMeasure word: 个Traditional: 愛好
    Dictionary sensesto like; to be fond of; to take pleasure in; interest
    • 你的爱好是什么？nǐ de ài hào shì shén meWhat's your hobby?
  • 教室jiào shìclassroomMeasure word: 间
    • 教室里没有人。jiào shì lǐ méi yǒu rénThere is no one in the classroom.
    • 别在教室里说话。bié zài jiào shì lǐ shuō huàDon't talk in the classroom.
