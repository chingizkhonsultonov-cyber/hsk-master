  • 快要kuài yàoabout to
    Dictionary sensesnearly at the point of (doing sth); about to (do sth)
    • 快要六点了。kuài yào liù diǎn leIt's almost six.
    • 天快要下雨了。tiān kuài yào xià yǔ leIt'll rain soon.
  • 门票mén piàoadmission ticketTraditional: 門票
    Dictionary sensesticket (for theater, cinema etc)
    • 门票多少钱？mén piào duō shao qiánHow much is the admission?
    • 我在哪里可以买到门票？wǒ zài nǎ li kě yǐ mǎi dào mén piàoWhere can I buy an entrance ticket?
  • 机票jī piàoplane ticketMeasure word: 张Traditional: 機票
    Dictionary sensesairline ticket
  • 手表shǒu biǎowristwatchMeasure word: 块、只、个Traditional: 手錶
    • 这不是手表。zhè bù shì shǒu biǎoIt's not a watch.
    • 我的手表坏了。wǒ de shǒu biǎo huài leSomething is wrong with my watch.
  • 出国chū guóto go abroadTraditional: 出國
    Dictionary sensesto leave the country
  • 跑步pǎo bùto run; to jog
    Dictionary senses(military) to march at the double
    • 我喜欢跑步。wǒ xǐ huan pǎo bùI like to run.
    • 她喜欢跑步。tā xǐ huan pǎo bùShe likes to run.
  • 药店yào diànpharmacyTraditional: 藥店
    Dictionary sensesdrugstore
  • 晴qíngsunny; clear
    Dictionary sensesfine (weather)
    • 明天是晴天。míng tiān shì qíng tiānTomorrow will be a fine day.
    • 明天是晴天，很热。míng tiān shì qíng tiān hěn rèTomorrow will be a sunny day, and very hot.
  • 洗手间xǐ shǒu jiāntoilet; restroomTraditional: 洗手間
    Dictionary senseslavatory; washroom
    • 我去下洗手间。wǒ qù xià xǐ shǒu jiānI'm going to the restroom.
    • 洗手间在哪里？xǐ shǒu jiān zài nǎ liWhere is the bathroom?
  • 开学kāi xuéschool startsTraditional: 開學
    Dictionary senses(of a student) to start school; (of a semester) to begin; (old) to found a school; the start of a new term
    • 下周一开学。xià zhōu yī kāi xuéSchool starts next Monday.
    • 明天就开学了。míng tiān jiù kāi xué leSchool begins tomorrow.
  • 个子gè ziheight; buildTraditional: 個子
    Dictionary sensesstature; size (of a person or object); (agriculture) bundle of cereal stalks tied together
    • 我个子高。wǒ gè zi gāoI'm tall.
    • 她个子很高。tā gè zi hěn gāoShe is tall.
  • 没意思méi yì siboringTraditional: 沒意思
    Dictionary sensesof no interest
    • 你们这帮人真没意思。nǐ men zhè bāng rén zhēn méi yì siYou people are no fun at all.
  • 书包shū bāoschool bagMeasure word: 个、只Traditional: 書包
    Dictionary sensesschoolbag; satchel; bookbag
    • 我今天买了一个新书包。wǒ jīn tiān mǎi le yī gè xīn shū bāoI bought a new schoolbag today.
  • 本子běn zinotebookMeasure word: 本
    Dictionary sensesbook; Japanese-style self-published comic (esp. an erotic one), aka "dōjinshi"; edition
  • 过年guò niánto celebrate the New YearTraditional: 過年
    Dictionary sensesto celebrate the Chinese New Year
