  • 帮忙bāng mángto help; to do a favourTraditional: 幫忙
    Dictionary sensesto lend a hand; to do a favor; to do a good turn
    • 我帮忙了。wǒ bāng máng leI helped out.
    • 谢谢你帮忙。xiè xie nǐ bāng mángThank you for your assistance.
  • 进来jìn laito come inTraditional: 進來
    • 进来吧。jìn lai baCome in.
    • 请进来。qǐng jìn laiPlease come in.
  • 颜色yán sècolourTraditional: 顏色
    Dictionary sensescolor; countenance; appearance; facial expression
    • 我也喜欢这颜色。wǒ yě xǐ huan zhè yán sèI like this color too.
    • 你的车什么颜色？nǐ de chē shén me yán sèWhat color is your car?
  • 高中gāo zhōngsenior high school
    • 他是一名高中学生。tā shì yī míng gāo zhōng xué shengHe's a high school student.
  • 考kǎoto take an exam; to testTraditional: 攷
    Dictionary sensesto beat; to hit; to inspect
  • 上去shàng quto go up
    • 你看上去很好。nǐ kàn shàng qu hěn hǎoYou look very good.
    • 你看上去不太好。nǐ kàn shàng qu bù tài hǎoYou don't look so hot.
  • 红色hóng sèredTraditional: 紅色
    Dictionary sensesred (color); revolutionary
    • 我最喜欢红色。wǒ zuì xǐ huan hóng sèMy favorite color is red.
    • 她穿那件红色的衣服很好看。tā chuān nà jiàn hóng sè de yī fu hěn hǎo kànThat red dress looks good on her.
  • 游yóuto swim; to tour
    Dictionary sensesto walk; to roam; to travel
    • 鱼能游。yú néng yóuA fish can swim.
    • 他能游得很快。tā néng yóu de hěn kuàiHe can swim very fast.
  • 一会儿yí huì ra moment; a whileTraditional: 一會兒
    Dictionary sensesin a moment; now...now...; also pr. [yi1 hui3 r5]
    • 一会儿见！yí huì r jiànSee you soon!
    • 一会儿见。yí huì r jiànSee you later!
  • 机场jī chǎngairportMeasure word: 家、处Traditional: 機場
    Dictionary sensesairfield; (slang) service provider for Shadowsocks or similar software for circumventing Internet censorship
    • 我去机场。wǒ qù jī chǎngI'm going to the airport.
    • 机场很近。jī chǎng hěn jìnThe airport is close by.
  • 踢tīto kick; to play (football)
    Dictionary sensesto play (e.g. soccer); (slang) butch (in a lesbian relationship)
    • 他在踢我！tā zài tī wǒHe's kicking me!
    • 我踢足球。wǒ tī zú qiúI play soccer.
  • 舒服shū fucomfortable; well
    Dictionary sensesfeeling well
    • 我有点不舒服。wǒ yǒu diǎn bù shū fuI'm not quite well.
    • 你身体不舒服吗？nǐ shēn tǐ bù shū fu maAre you not feeling well?
  • 黑色hēi sèblack
    • 哪只狗是黑色的？nǎ zhī gǒu shì hēi sè deWhich dog is black?
    • 你喜欢黑色的猫吗？nǐ xǐ huan hēi sè de māo maDo you like black cats?
  • 绿色lǜ sègreenTraditional: 綠色
    • 是绿色。shì lǜ sèIt's green.
    • 她有绿色的眼睛。tā yǒu lǜ sè de yǎn jingShe has green eyes.
  • 网上wǎng shàngonlineTraditional: 網上
    • 我在帮人在网上买东西。wǒ zài bāng rén zài wǎng shàng mǎi dōng xiI'm helping people to buy things online.
    • 这是你在网上找到的吗？zhè shì nǐ zài wǎng shàng zhǎo dào de maDid you find that on the internet?
