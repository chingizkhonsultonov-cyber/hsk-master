  • 就jiùthen; just; right away
    Dictionary senses(after a suppositional clause) in that case; (after a clause of action) as soon as; immediately after; (same as 就是[jiu4 shi4]) merely
    • 现在就做！xiàn zài jiù zuòDo it now!
    • 我就知道！wǒ jiù zhī dàoI knew it!
  • 好hǎovery; easy to; OK
    Dictionary sensesgood; appropriate; proper; all right!
  • 着zhecontinuous aspect particleTraditional: 著
    Dictionary sensesaspect particle indicating action in progress or ongoing state
    • 我睡不着。wǒ shuì bù zheI can't sleep.
    • 他睡着了。tā shuì zhe leHe's asleep.
  • 给gěifor; to (someone)Traditional: 給
    Dictionary sensesto; for the benefit of; to give; to allow
  • 啊asentence-final particle (surprise, softening)
    Dictionary sensesmodal particle ending sentence, showing affirmation, approval, or consent
    • 可以啊。kě yǐ aCould be, could be.
    • 对不起啊！duì bu qǐ aSorry!
  • 那nàthen; in that case
    Dictionary senses(specifier) that; the; those (colloquial pr. [nei4]); (pronoun) that (referring to persons, things or situations)
  • 让ràngto let; to make (sb do)Traditional: 讓
    Dictionary sensesto yield; to permit; to let sb do sth; to have sb do sth
    • 让我看看。ràng wǒ kàn kànShow me.
    • 让我想想。ràng wǒ xiǎng xiǎngLet me think for a minute.
  • 但dànbut
    Dictionary sensesyet; however; still; merely
    • 虽然他是中国人，但他不会说中文。suī rán tā shì Zhōng guó rén dàn tā bù huì shuō Zhōng wénAlthough he is Chinese, he doesn't speak Chinese.
    • 里面很热，但外面很冷。lǐ miàn hěn rè dàn wài miàn hěn lěngInside, it's very hot, but outside its freezing.
  • 自己zì jǐoneself
    Dictionary sensesone's own
    • 自己做！zì jǐ zuòDo it yourself!
    • 我自己做的。wǒ zì jǐ zuò deI did it myself.
  • 得decomplement particle (degree)
    Dictionary sensesstructural particle: used after a verb (or adjective as main verb), linking it to following phrase indicating effect, degree, possibility etc
    • 问得好。wèn de hǎoGood question!
    • 做得好！zuò de hǎoGood job!
  • 点diǎnto order (food); to clickTraditional: 點
    Dictionary sensesto touch briefly; to tap; to mark with a dot; to check off (on a list)
  • 过guòto pass; to spend (time); to crossTraditional: 過
    Dictionary senses(experienced action marker)
    • 我见过。wǒ jiàn guòI've seen it.
    • 我问过他。wǒ wèn guò tāI have asked him.
  • 过guoexperiential particle (have done)Traditional: 過
    Dictionary senses(experienced action marker)
  • 从cóngfrom
    Dictionary sensesthrough; via; (used before a negative) ever; attendant
    • 你从哪里来？nǐ cóng nǎ li láiWhere are you from?
    • 从哪儿来的？cóng nǎ r lái deWhere did it come from?
  • 多duōhow (old, long…); more than
    Dictionary sensesmany; much; more; a lot of
