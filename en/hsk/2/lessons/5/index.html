  • 不错bú cuònot bad; pretty goodTraditional: 不錯
    Dictionary sensescorrect; right
    • 他们不错。tā men bú cuòThey were good.
    • 今天天气不错。jīn tiān tiān qì bú cuòThe weather is nice today.
  • 帮bāngto helpTraditional: 幇
    Dictionary sensesto assist; to support; for sb (i.e. as a help); hired (as worker)
    • 帮我一下。bāng wǒ yí xiàHelp me.
    • 请帮帮她！qǐng bāng bāng tāPlease, help her!
  • 完wánto finish
    Dictionary sensesto be over; whole; complete; entire
    • 卖完了！mài wán leAll sold out!
    • 你做完了吗？nǐ zuò wán le maHave you finished it?
  • 站zhànto stand
    Dictionary sensesstation; to halt; to stop; branch of a company or organization
    • 给我站住！gěi wǒ zhàn zhùStop right there!
    • 别站起来。bié zhàn qǐ laiDon't get up.
  • 路lùroad; way; route (bus number)
    Dictionary sensesroad (CL:條|条[tiao2]); journey; route; line (bus etc)
    • 我问问路。wǒ wèn wèn lùI'll ask for directions.
    • 请您看路。qǐng nín kàn lùPlease watch the road.
  • 门méndoor; gateMeasure word: 扇、个Traditional: 門
    Dictionary sensesgateway; doorway; opening
    • 请开门。qǐng kāi ménPlease open the door.
    • 门在哪里？mén zài nǎ liWhere's the door?
  • 花huāflowerMeasure word: 朵、支、束
    Dictionary sensesblossom; fancy pattern; florid; to spend (money, time)
    • 多漂亮的花啊！duō piào liang de huā aWhat a beautiful flower!
    • 你花太多钱了。nǐ huā tài duō qián leYou spend too much money.
  • 花huāto spend (money, time)Measure word: 朵、支、束
    Dictionary sensesflower; blossom; fancy pattern; florid
  • 虽然suī ránalthoughTraditional: 雖然
    Dictionary senseseven though (often used correlatively with 可是[ke3 shi4] or 但是[dan4 shi4] etc)
    • 虽然他是中国人，但他不会说中文。suī rán tā shì Zhōng guó rén dàn tā bù huì shuō Zhōng wénAlthough he is Chinese, he doesn't speak Chinese.
  • 回来huí laito come backTraditional: 回來
    Dictionary sensesto return
    • 我回来了。wǒ huí lai leI'm back.
    • 我回来了！wǒ huí lai leI'm back.
  • 球qiúballMeasure word: 个、场Traditional: 毬
    Dictionary sensesball used for playing games (variant of 球[qiu2]); sphere; globe; ball game
    • 两好球！liǎng hǎo qiúStrike two!
  • 错cuòwrong; mistakeTraditional: 錯
    Dictionary sensesbad; interlocking; complex
    • 我错了吗？wǒ cuò le maAm I wrong?
    • 是我错了。shì wǒ cuò leIt's my bad.
  • 间jiānmeasure word for roomsTraditional: 間
    Dictionary sensesbetween; among; within a definite time or space; room
    • 这间房间很冷。zhè jiān fáng jiān hěn lěngThe room is cold.
    • 这间房间是谁的？zhè jiān fáng jiān shì shéi deWhose room is this?
  • 跑pǎoto run
    Dictionary sensesto run away; to escape; to run around (on errands etc); (of a gas or liquid) to leak or evaporate
    • 别让他跑。bié ràng tā pǎoDon't let him run.
    • 你跑哪去了？nǐ pǎo nǎ qù leWhere did you run to?
  • 万wànten thousandTraditional: 萬
    Dictionary sensesa great number
