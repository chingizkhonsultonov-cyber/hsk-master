    Morphemes语素1
    • Suffixes 后缀面Place words with the suffix 面

      里面 · 外面 · 前面 · 后面 · 上面 · 下面 · 左面 · 右面

      面 turns a direction into a place noun: 里面 inside, 外面 outside, 前面 in front. It works like 边 (里边 = 里面). The place word goes after the object it relates to: 桌子上面 on the table.

      When to use it

      面 forms the same place words as 边 and is a little more common in writing. They can stand alone (在外面) or after a noun with or without 的 (房子(的)后面).

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 在哪儿？/ 在什么地方？ Negative: 不在里面.

      • 书在桌子上面。Shū zài zhuōzi shàngmiàn.The book is on the table.
      • 外面很冷，我们在里面吧。Wàimiàn hěn lěng, wǒmen zài lǐmiàn ba.It's cold outside, let's stay inside.
      • 我的手机在包里面。Wǒ de shǒujī zài bāo lǐmiàn.My phone is in the bag.
      • 你家后面有什么？Nǐ jiā hòumiàn yǒu shénme?What's behind your home?

      Compare

      里面 = 里边 = 里. Only the short form attaches directly to a noun without sounding heavy: 家里.

      Exam note: Say 桌子上面, not 上面桌子: the object comes first.

    Parts of speech词类32
    • Nouns 名词Location nouns 方位名词左、右左 and 右: left and right

      左边 / 右边 · 在 + N + 的左边 / 右边

      左 left and 右 right usually take 边 or 面 to become place words. Use 在 to say where something is, and 往 to give directions.

      When to use it

      左边/右边 describe positions; 往左/往右 give directions. With a noun: 在 + noun + (的) + 左边.

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 在左边还是右边？

      • 医院在商店的左边。Yīyuàn zài shāngdiàn de zuǒbian.The hospital is to the left of the shop.
      • 往右走，就到了。Wǎng yòu zǒu, jiù dào le.Go right and you're there.
      • 银行在超市右边。Yínháng zài chāoshì yòubian.The bank is to the right of the supermarket.
      • 前面往左走。Qiánmiàn wǎng zuǒ zǒu.Turn left up ahead.
    • Verbs 动词Verb reduplication 动词重叠AA、A一A、A了A、ABABReduplicated verbs

      看看 · 看一看 · 看了看 · 休息休息

      Doubling a verb makes the action short, light or casual: “have a look”, “take a rest”. One-syllable verbs: AA, A一A, or A了A for a finished action. Two-syllable verbs: ABAB.

      When to use it

      Doubling a verb makes the action short, light or a casual try: 看看 have a look, 试试 give it a try. It softens requests.

      Negative and questions

      Not used with the negative or with 在 (in progress): 我在看看 is wrong.

      • 你看看这本书吧。Nǐ kànkan zhè běn shū ba.Have a look at this book.
      • 我们休息休息再走。Wǒmen xiūxi xiūxi zài zǒu.Let's rest a little before we go.
      • 他想了想，说：“好。”Tā xiǎng le xiǎng, shuō: “Hǎo.”He thought for a moment and said, “OK.”
      • 你试试这件衣服。Nǐ shìshi zhè jiàn yīfu.Try this on.
      • 我们一起学习学习吧。Wǒmen yìqǐ xuéxí xuéxí ba.Let's study together for a bit.

      Compare

      One syllable: AA, A一A, A了A (past). Two syllables: ABAB (休息休息), never AABB for verbs.

      Exam note: ABAB, not AABB, for two-syllable verbs: 休息休息, never 休休息息.

    • Verbs 动词Modal verbs 能愿动词可能可能: possibly, may

      可能 + verb · 可能 + 不 + verb

      可能 says something is likely or possible. It goes before the verb, or at the start of the sentence. It also works as a noun: 有可能 “it's possible”.

      When to use it

      可能 says something is possible or likely; it usually comes before the verb, or at the start of the sentence.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 可能不 + verb (probably not); 不可能 (impossible).

      • 明天可能下雨。Míngtiān kěnéng xià yǔ.It may rain tomorrow.
      • 他可能不来了。Tā kěnéng bù lái le.He probably isn't coming.
      • 这不可能！Zhè bù kěnéng!That's impossible!
      • 他可能在路上。Tā kěnéng zài lùshang.He's probably on his way.

      Compare

      可能 = maybe; 会 = will (more certain); 一定 = definitely.

    • Verbs 动词Separable verbs 离合词帮忙、游泳、跳舞、跑步Separable verbs

      帮忙 → 帮我的忙 · 游泳 → 游了一个小时泳

      Verbs like 帮忙, 游泳, 跳舞, 跑步 are verb + object. Other words can go between the two parts: 了, 过, time lengths, or a person (帮他的忙). They don't take another object after them.

      When to use it

      Like other separable verbs, put 了, 过, time and number between the parts: 帮了我很多忙, 跳过舞.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没游泳, 不跑步. Question: 你会游泳吗？

      • 你能帮我一个忙吗？Nǐ néng bāng wǒ yí gè máng ma?Can you do me a favour?
      • 我昨天游了一个小时泳。Wǒ zuótiān yóu le yí gè xiǎoshí yǒng.Yesterday I swam for an hour.
      • 我每天早上跑半个小时步。Wǒ měi tiān zǎoshang pǎo bàn ge xiǎoshí bù.I jog for half an hour every morning.

      Compare

      帮忙 takes no object: 帮忙我 is wrong. Use 帮我 or 帮我的忙.

      Exam note: “帮忙我” is wrong. Say 帮我 or 帮我的忙.

    • Pronouns 代词Question words 疑问代词为什么为什么: why

      Subject + 为什么 + verb phrase？

      为什么 asks for a reason. It usually comes right after the subject. Answer with 因为.

      When to use it

      为什么 goes before the verb or at the start. Answers often use 因为.

      Negative and questions

      为什么不…？ also makes a suggestion: 为什么不试试？ Why not try?

      • 你为什么不吃饭？Nǐ wèishénme bù chī fàn?Why aren't you eating?
      • ——为什么学汉语？——因为我喜欢中国。— Wèishénme xué Hànyǔ? — Yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó.— Why learn Chinese? — Because I like China.
      • 你为什么不去？因为我太忙了。Nǐ wèishénme bú qù? Yīnwèi wǒ tài máng le.Why aren't you going? Because I'm too busy.

      Compare

      为什么 asks for a reason; 怎么 can also ask “how come”, with surprise: 你怎么来了？

    • Pronouns 代词Personal pronouns 人称代词自己自己: oneself

      我自己 · 自己 + verb · 自己的 + N

      自己 means “self”. After a pronoun it stresses who does it (我自己做); before a verb it means “by oneself”; 自己的 means “one's own”.

      When to use it

      自己 after a pronoun stresses “myself/yourself”; before a verb it means “by oneself”.

      • 这件事我自己做。Zhè jiàn shì wǒ zìjǐ zuò.I'll do this myself.
      • 每个人都有自己的房间。Měi gè rén dōu yǒu zìjǐ de fángjiān.Everyone has their own room.
      • 你自己去吧，我不去了。Nǐ zìjǐ qù ba, wǒ bú qù le.Go by yourself, I'm not going.

      Compare

      自己 refers back to the subject: 他喜欢自己的房间 = his own room.

    • Pronouns 代词Demonstratives 指示代词那么、那样、这么、这样、每这么, 那么, 这样, 那样, 每

      这么/那么 + adjective · 这样/那样 + verb · 每 + measure word + N + 都

      这么 and 那么 mean “so, this/that much” before adjectives. 这样 and 那样 mean “like this/that” and describe a way of doing or a kind of thing. 每 means “every” and is usually followed by a measure word and 都 later in the sentence.

      When to use it

      这么/那么 + adjective: “so, this/that much”. 这样/那样 + verb: “like this/that”. 每 + measure word usually pairs with 都.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没有这么 + adj, 不这样 + verb.

      • 今天怎么这么热？Jīntiān zěnme zhème rè?Why is it so hot today?
      • 这个字要这样写。Zhège zì yào zhèyàng xiě.This character is written like this.
      • 我每天都跑步。Wǒ měitiān dōu pǎobù.I run every day.
      • 你怎么能这样说话？Nǐ zěnme néng zhèyàng shuōhuà?How can you talk like that?
      • 每个人都有一本书。Měi ge rén dōu yǒu yì běn shū.Everyone has a book.

      Compare

      这么 = near, now; 那么 = far or not here. 那么 at the start also means “then, in that case”.

      Exam note: After 每 add 都 before the verb: 每天都, not just 每天 + verb, in most sentences.

    • Adjectives 形容词Adjective reduplication 形容词重叠AA、AABBReduplicated adjectives

      AA (的) · AABB (的)

      Doubling an adjective makes it vivid: 高高的 nice and tall, 高高兴兴 very happily. One-syllable: AA; two-syllable: AABB. Doubled adjectives already carry degree, so they don't take 很.

      When to use it

      Doubled adjectives are vivid descriptions, not comparisons; they don't take 很: 大大的, not 很大大的. Before a verb they take 地.

      Negative and questions

      Not used with 不: 不高高 is wrong.

      • 她的眼睛大大的。Tā de yǎnjing dàdà de.Her eyes are big and round.
      • 孩子们高高兴兴地回家了。Háizimen gāogāoxìngxìng de huí jiā le.The children went home happily.
      • 我们要好好学习。Wǒmen yào hǎohǎo xuéxí.We must study hard.
      • 房间里干干净净的。Fángjiān li gāngānjìngjìng de.The room is spotless.

      Exam note: “很高高的” is wrong: a doubled adjective doesn't take 很.

    • Numerals 数词万、好多、多万, 好多, 多

      一万 · 好多 + N · number + 多

      万 is ten thousand: 一万 = 10,000, 十万 = 100,000. 好多 means “a lot of”. 多 after a number means “more than”, for example 二十多 “twenty-odd”.

      When to use it

      万 is 10,000, its own unit: 十万 100,000. 好多 = many (spoken). Number + 多 = “more than”.

      • 这个电脑一万块钱。Zhège diànnǎo yí wàn kuài qián.This computer costs ten thousand yuan.
      • 来了好多人。Lái le hǎoduō rén.A lot of people came.
      • 这辆车十万块。Zhè liàng chē shí wàn kuài.This car is 100,000 yuan.

      Compare

      English counts in thousands, Chinese in ten-thousands: 50,000 = 五万, not 五十千.

      Exam note: 10,000 is 一万, not 十千.

    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（1）专用名量词：条、位、间、名Measure words 条, 位, 间, 名

      条: long things · 位: people (polite) · 间: rooms · 名: people (formal, by role)

      条 is for long, thin things: roads, rivers, fish, trousers. 位 is a polite word for people (老师, 客人). 间 counts rooms. 名 counts people by role, often in formal or written language (学生, 医生).

      When to use it

      条 long, thin things (路, 河, 鱼, 裤子); 位 polite for people (这位先生); 间 rooms; 名 people by role, formal (三名学生).

      • 这条路很长。Zhè tiáo lù hěn cháng.This road is long.
      • 那位老师是我们的汉语老师。Nà wèi lǎoshī shì wǒmen de Hànyǔ lǎoshī.That teacher is our Chinese teacher.
      • 我们学校有一千名学生。Wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu yìqiān míng xuésheng.Our school has a thousand students.
      • 请问，您几位？Qǐngwèn, nín jǐ wèi?How many in your party?

      Compare

      个 works for people in any style; 位 shows respect; 名 sounds official. Don't say 我是一位老师 about yourself.

      Exam note: Don't use 位 for yourself: “我是一位老师” sounds wrong; say 我是老师.

    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（2）借用名量词：包包 as a measure word

      一包 + N

      Some nouns for containers become measure words: 一包 a packet or bag of something.

      When to use it

      包 counts things in packets: 一包茶, 一包烟. The noun “bag” is 包 too: 一个包.

      • 我买了两包茶。Wǒ mǎi le liǎng bāo chá.I bought two packets of tea.
      • 这包东西是谁的？Zhè bāo dōngxi shì shéi de?Whose bag of things is this?
      • 给我一包纸。Gěi wǒ yì bāo zhǐ.Give me a pack of tissues.
    • Measure words 量词Verbal measure words 动量词次次: number of times

      verb + number + 次 · 第一次

      次 counts how many times an action happens. It goes after the verb (and after 了 or 过). 第一次 means “the first time”.

      When to use it

      Object order: noun objects usually after 次 (去过两次北京); pronouns before (见过他两次).

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 几次？ Negative: 一次也没去过 (not even once).

      • 我去过两次中国。Wǒ qù guo liǎng cì Zhōngguó.I've been to China twice.
      • 这是我第一次坐飞机。Zhè shì wǒ dì yī cì zuò fēijī.This is my first time on a plane.
      • 这个电影我一次也没看过。Zhège diànyǐng wǒ yí cì yě méi kàn guo.I've never seen this film, not even once.

      Exam note: The count goes after the verb: 去过两次, not 两次去过.

    • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词多、好、最多, 好, 最: degree adverbs

      多 + adj (+ 啊) · 好 + adj · 最 + adj / verb

      多 and 好 before an adjective mean “how … !” or “so …” in exclamations. 最 means “most”: 最好 the best, 最喜欢 like most.

      When to use it

      多 + adj + 啊 is an exclamation; 好 + adj is spoken “so, very”; 最 is the superlative and also works with verbs of liking.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 最不 + adj (the least): 最不喜欢.

      • 今天的天气多好啊！Jīntiān de tiānqì duō hǎo a!What lovely weather today!
      • 我最喜欢吃面条。Wǒ zuì xǐhuan chī miàntiáo.I like noodles most of all.
      • 你家离学校多远？Nǐ jiā lí xuéxiào duō yuǎn?How far is your home from school?

      Compare

      多 + adj without 啊 can also ask “how …?”: 多大？ 多远？

    • Adverbs 副词Scope adverbs 范围副词一起一起: together

      (跟 + somebody +) 一起 + verb

      一起 means “together” and goes before the verb. Use 跟 or 和 to say who with.

      When to use it

      一起 goes before the verb; the partner comes with 跟/和 before it.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不跟他一起去 / 没一起去.

      • 我们一起去吧。Wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba.Let's go together.
      • 我跟朋友一起吃饭。Wǒ gēn péngyou yìqǐ chī fàn.I eat with my friends.
      • 你想跟我们一起吃饭吗？Nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒmen yìqǐ chī fàn ma?Would you like to eat with us?

      Exam note: 一起 goes before the verb: 一起去, not 去一起.

    • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词还2、已经、有时（候）、快、快要、正Time adverbs 还, 已经, 有时候, 快, 快要, 正

      还 + verb · 已经 + verb + 了 · 快(要) + verb + 了 · 正 + verb (+ 呢)

      还 “still”: the situation continues. 已经 … 了 “already”. 有时候 “sometimes”. 快 / 快要 … 了 “about to”. 正 “right now, in the middle of”, often with 呢.

      When to use it

      还 = still; 已经…了 = already; 快(要)…了 = soon, about to; 有时候 = sometimes; 正 = just now, right in the middle of.

      Negative and questions

      还没 + verb = not yet: 我还没吃饭. 已经 + 不 = no longer: 他已经不在这儿工作了.

      • 他还在睡觉。Tā hái zài shuìjiào.He's still asleep.
      • 我已经吃饭了。Wǒ yǐjīng chī fàn le.I've already eaten.
      • 快要上课了。Kuàiyào shàngkè le.Class is about to start.
      • 你吃饭了吗？还没呢。Nǐ chī fàn le ma? Hái méi ne.Have you eaten? Not yet.
      • 我有时候坐公共汽车，有时候走路。Wǒ yǒu shíhou zuò gōnggòng qìchē, yǒu shíhou zǒulù.Sometimes I take the bus, sometimes I walk.

      Compare

      已经 needs 了 at the end in most sentences.

    • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词常、经常常 and 经常: often

      (经)常 + verb

      常 and 经常 mean “often” and go before the verb. 不常 means “not often”.

      When to use it

      经常 is a little more frequent and formal than 常. Both go before the verb.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不常 (not often); 不经常 also works.

      • 我经常在家看书。Wǒ jīngcháng zài jiā kàn shū.I often read at home.
      • 他不常运动。Tā bù cháng yùndòng.He doesn't exercise often.
      • 你经常来这儿吗？不常来。Nǐ jīngcháng lái zhèr ma? Bù cháng lái.Do you come here often? Not often.
    • Adverbs 副词Negation adverbs 否定副词别别: don't

      别 + verb (+ 了)

      别 tells someone not to do something. 别 … 了 means “stop doing it”.

      When to use it

      别 = 不要: “don't”. 别…了 means “stop doing”.

      • 别说话！Bié shuōhuà!Don't talk!
      • 别看手机了。Bié kàn shǒujī le.Stop looking at your phone.
      • 别担心，没问题。Bié dānxīn, méi wèntí.Don't worry, it's no problem.

      Compare

      别 is more direct and spoken than 不要; add 请 to be polite.

    • Adverbs 副词Linking adverbs 关联副词再2、就1再 and 就 linking actions

      A + 再 + B (B after A) · A + 就 + B (B right after A)

      再 joins two actions: first A, then B, usually about the future. 就 says B follows A immediately or easily.

      When to use it

      再 links a future action after another: do A, then B. 就 shows B happens immediately or easily after A.

      • 吃了饭再走吧。Chī le fàn zài zǒu ba.Eat first, then go.
      • 他下了课就回家了。Tā xià le kè jiù huí jiā le.He went home as soon as class ended.
      • 你做完作业再看电视。Nǐ zuò wán zuòyè zài kàn diànshì.Finish your homework, then watch TV.

      Compare

      吃了饭再走 = eat first, then go (future). 吃了饭就走了 = left right after eating.

      Exam note: For past events use 就 or 又, not 再: 他昨天就来了.

    • Adverbs 副词Modal (attitude) adverbs 语气副词都2、就2、还是1Mood adverbs 都, 就, 还是

      都 … 了 (already) · 就 (exactly, only) · 还是 (had better)

      都 can mean “already” with 了: 都十点了. 就 can stress “exactly this, just this”: 就是他. 还是 can suggest the better choice: 还是你去吧.

      When to use it

      都…了 stresses “already, so late/much”. 就 stresses “right, exactly”. 还是 + suggestion = “you'd better”.

      • 都十点了，快睡觉吧。Dōu shí diǎn le, kuài shuìjiào ba.It's already ten, go to bed.
      • 我家就在前面。Wǒ jiā jiù zài qiánmiàn.My home is just ahead.
      • 下雨了，还是坐车去吧。Xià yǔ le, háishi zuò chē qù ba.It's raining, we'd better take a car.
      • 我就要这个。Wǒ jiù yào zhège.This is the one I want.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing time or place 引出时间、处所从1从: from (a time or place)

      从 + time/place + (到 + time/place)

      从 marks a starting point in time or space, often with 到 for the end point.

      When to use it

      从 marks a starting point in time or space, often paired with 到 for the end: 从北京到上海.

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 你从哪儿来？ 从几点开始？

      • 我从八点到十二点上课。Wǒ cóng bā diǎn dào shí'èr diǎn shàngkè.I have class from eight to twelve.
      • 他是从中国来的。Tā shì cóng Zhōngguó lái de.He came from China.
      • 从我家到学校要二十分钟。Cóng wǒ jiā dào xuéxiào yào èrshí fēnzhōng.It takes twenty minutes from my home to school.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing direction or route 引出方向、路径往往: towards

      往 + direction/place + verb

      往 shows the direction of movement and goes before the verb.

      When to use it

      往 + direction + verb gives the direction of movement: 往前走, 往东开.

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 往哪儿走？

      • 往前走，就是火车站。Wǎng qián zǒu, jiù shì huǒchēzhàn.Go straight and you'll reach the station.
      • 这个飞机往哪儿飞？Zhège fēijī wǎng nǎr fēi?Where is this plane flying to?
      • 一直往前走，然后往右拐。Yìzhí wǎng qián zǒu, ránhòu wǎng yòu guǎi.Go straight ahead, then turn right.

      Compare

      往 = towards (direction); 到 = arrive at (destination).

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing direction or route 引出方向、路径从2从: through, via

      从 + place + 过 / 走

      从 can also mark the route: from where or through where something passes.

      When to use it

      从 + place + 走/过/进 tells the route: through or by way of a place.

      • 你从这儿走，近一点。Nǐ cóng zhèr zǒu, jìn yìdiǎn.Go this way, it's a bit shorter.
      • 我们从门口进去吧。Wǒmen cóng ménkǒu jìnqu ba.Let's go in through the doorway.
      • 我们从公园里走过去吧。Wǒmen cóng gōngyuán li zǒu guòqù ba.Let's walk through the park.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象给给: for, to (a person)

      给 + person + verb

      给 introduces the person who receives or benefits from the action. It comes before the verb.

      When to use it

      给 + person before the verb: the action is for or directed at that person: 给你打电话, 给他写信.

      Negative and questions

      Negative before 给: 我没给他打电话.

      • 我给妈妈打电话。Wǒ gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.I'm phoning my mum.
      • 他给我们介绍了一个饭店。Tā gěi wǒmen jièshào le yí gè fàndiàn.He recommended a restaurant to us.
      • 你到了给我发个短信。Nǐ dào le gěi wǒ fā ge duǎnxìn.Text me when you arrive.

      Compare

      给 + person + verb (for someone) vs verb + 给 + person (to someone): 我给他写信 / 我写给他一封信.

      Exam note: “打电话给妈妈” is possible, but the most common order is 给妈妈打电话.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象比比: compared with

      A + 比 + B + adjective

      比 compares two things: A is more … than B. The adjective comes last, without 很.

      When to use it

      比 compares two things; the adjective comes at the end. Don't use 很/非常 before it: 他比我很高 is wrong.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: A 没有 B + adjective: 他没有我高. (A 不比 B means “A is not more … than B”.)

      • 今天比昨天冷。Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng.Today is colder than yesterday.
      • 我哥哥比我高。Wǒ gēge bǐ wǒ gāo.My brother is taller than me.
      • 坐地铁比坐出租车便宜。Zuò dìtiě bǐ zuò chūzūchē piányi.The subway is cheaper than a taxi.

      Compare

      To add how much: 比 … + 多了/得多/一点儿/number: 他比我高一点儿.

      Exam note: “今天比昨天很冷” is wrong: no 很 after 比.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象跟1跟: with (a person)

      跟 + person + verb / 一起

      跟 introduces the person you do something with or speak to.

      When to use it

      跟 + person + verb = with someone; 跟 + person + 说 = say to someone.

      Negative and questions

      Negative before 跟: 我不跟他说话.

      • 我跟你说一件事。Wǒ gēn nǐ shuō yí jiàn shì.Let me tell you something.
      • 她跟朋友去看电影了。Tā gēn péngyou qù kàn diànyǐng le.She went to a film with a friend.
      • 你跟谁一起去？Nǐ gēn shéi yìqǐ qù?Who are you going with?

      Compare

      跟 and 和 are the same here; 跟 is more spoken.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing cause 引出原因因为1因为 as a preposition: because of

      因为 + noun phrase, …

      因为 can go before a noun phrase to give a cause: “because of …”.

      When to use it

      因为 + noun phrase gives a cause at the start of the sentence.

      • 因为下雨，我们没去。Yīnwèi xià yǔ, wǒmen méi qù.Because of the rain, we didn't go.
      • 因为这件事，他很不高兴。Yīnwèi zhè jiàn shì, tā hěn bù gāoxìng.He's upset because of this.
      • 因为工作，他很少回家。Yīnwèi gōngzuò, tā hěn shǎo huí jiā.Because of work, he rarely goes home.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining words or phrases 连接词或短语跟2跟 as a conjunction: and

      N + 跟 + N

      跟 can join two nouns, like 和: “A and B”. It is more spoken than 和.

      When to use it

      跟 joins nouns like 和, but only nouns and pronouns, not verbs or clauses.

      • 我跟他都是学生。Wǒ gēn tā dōu shì xuésheng.He and I are both students.
      • 桌子上有书跟杯子。Zhuōzi shang yǒu shū gēn bēizi.There are books and cups on the table.
      • 我跟我妹妹都喜欢唱歌。Wǒ gēn wǒ mèimei dōu xǐhuan chàng gē.My younger sister and I both like singing.

      Exam note: Don't use 跟 or 和 to join two sentences; use a comma or 也.

    • Conjunctions 连词Joining clauses or sentences 连接分句或者句子但、但是、那、虽然、还是2、因为2、所以Joining clauses: 但是, 那, 虽然, 还是, 因为, 所以

      …，但(是)… · 那… · 虽然…，但是… · 因为…，所以…

      但 / 但是 “but”. 那 “in that case, then”, starting a reply. 虽然 “although” pairs with 但是. 还是 “or” in questions. 因为 “because” pairs with 所以 “so”.

      When to use it

      但是 contrasts; 那(么) “then, in that case” follows up on what was said; 虽然…但是 concedes; 因为…所以 gives cause and result; 还是 in questions offers a choice.

      • 这件衣服很好看，但是太贵了。Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn hǎokàn, dànshì tài guì le.This dress is pretty, but too expensive.
      • ——我不想去。——那我自己去吧。— Wǒ bù xiǎng qù. — Nà wǒ zìjǐ qù ba.— I don't want to go. — Then I'll go alone.
      • 我很想去，但是没有时间。Wǒ hěn xiǎng qù, dànshì méiyǒu shíjiān.I'd love to go, but I don't have time.

      Compare

      但 is shorter and more written than 但是; 可是 is more spoken.

    • Particles 助词Structural particles 结构助词得、地得 and 地

      verb + 得 + description · description + 地 + verb

      得 comes after a verb and introduces how well or how something is done: 说得很好. 地 comes before a verb and turns a word into an adverb: 高兴地说.

      When to use it

      得 follows a verb and introduces how well/how it is done; 地 follows a description and comes before a verb.

      Negative and questions

      Negative goes after 得: 说得不好. Question: 说得好不好？/ 说得怎么样？

      • 他汉语说得很好。Tā Hànyǔ shuō de hěn hǎo.He speaks Chinese very well.
      • 她高兴地笑了。Tā gāoxìng de xiào le.She smiled happily.
      • 你唱得怎么样？唱得不太好。Nǐ chàng de zěnmeyàng? Chàng de bú tài hǎo.How well do you sing? Not very well.

      Compare

      的 + noun; 地 + verb; verb + 得. All three are pronounced de.

      Exam note: Three “de” sound the same: 的 before nouns, 地 before verbs, 得 after verbs.

    • Particles 助词Aspect particles 动态助词过过: experience

      verb + 过 · 没 + verb + 过

      过 after a verb means you have done something at least once in the past. The negative is 没(有) + verb + 过.

      When to use it

      过 says you have had the experience at some time; the exact time doesn't matter.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + 过. Question: verb + 过 + 吗/没有？

      • 我吃过中国菜。Wǒ chī guo Zhōngguó cài.I have eaten Chinese food.
      • 他没去过中国。Tā méi qù guo Zhōngguó.He has never been to China.
      • 你去过长城吗？去过一次。Nǐ qù guo Chángchéng ma? Qù guo yí cì.Have you been to the Great Wall? Once.

      Compare

      了 = it happened (this time); 过 = you've done it before (experience).

      Exam note: Negate with 没, not 不: “不去过” is wrong.

    • Particles 助词Aspect particles 动态助词着着: ongoing state

      verb + 着

      着 after a verb shows a continuing state or an action in progress: 门开着 the door is open, 他笑着说 he said with a smile.

      When to use it

      Verb + 着 describes a state that continues (门开着) or a manner for another action (笑着说).

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + 着: 门没开着.

      • 门开着呢。Mén kāi zhe ne.The door is open.
      • 她笑着说：“谢谢！”Tā xiào zhe shuō: “Xièxie!”She said with a smile, “Thank you!”
      • 他喜欢听着音乐做作业。Tā xǐhuan tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè.He likes doing homework while listening to music.
    • Particles 助词Modal particles 语气助词呢4、吧2、的2、啊1Sentence particles 呢, 吧, 的, 啊

      …呢 (ongoing) · …吧 (guess) · …的 (certainty) · …啊 (feeling)

      呢 at the end can show an action is going on: 他在睡觉呢. 吧 can mark a guess: 你是学生吧? 的 adds certainty: 他会来的. 啊 adds feeling or softens: 好啊!

      When to use it

      的 at the end makes the speaker sound sure: 他会来的. 啊 adds warmth or surprise and changes sound after some vowels (呀, 哪).

      • 你是老师吧？Nǐ shì lǎoshī ba?You're a teacher, aren't you?
      • 你等等，他会来的。Nǐ děngdeng, tā huì lái de.Wait a bit, he will come.
      • 你说得对啊！Nǐ shuō de duì a!You're so right!

    • Structure types 结构类型Basic structures 基本结构类型动补短语1Verb + complement phrases

      verb + result/direction (看完, 走进)

      A verb is often followed by a second word that gives its result or direction: 看完 finish reading, 听懂 understand by listening, 走进 walk in.

      When to use it

      A result or direction word follows the verb directly: 看完, 听懂, 走进.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + result: 没听懂.

      • 我听懂了。Wǒ tīng dǒng le.I understood (what I heard).
      • 他走进了教室。Tā zǒu jìn le jiàoshì.He walked into the classroom.

    • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（1）“的”字短语的 phrases: the one that …

      adjective/verb/noun + 的 (= the one …)

      A phrase ending in 的 can stand for a noun already known: 红的 the red one, 我的 mine, 卖东西的 the person who sells things.

      When to use it

      的 + nothing = “the one that…”: 吃的 food, 新的 a new one, 我的 mine.

      Negative and questions

      Question: 你要哪个？红的还是白的？

    • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（2）连谓短语Verbs in a series

      subject + verb 1 (+ object) + verb 2 (+ object)

      Two verb phrases in a row share one subject. The first often shows how or where, the second the purpose: 坐车去 go by car, 去商店买东西 go to the shop to buy things.

      When to use it

      The second verb is the purpose or the result of the first: 去图书馆借书.

      Negative and questions

      Negative before the first verb: 我不去图书馆借书.

      • 我坐飞机去中国。Wǒ zuò fēijī qù Zhōngguó.I'm flying to China.
      • 他去商店买水果了。Tā qù shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐguǒ le.He went to the shop to buy fruit.
      • 他回家吃饭了。Tā huí jiā chī fàn le.He went home to eat.

      Exam note: The order follows time: 去商店买东西, not 买东西去商店 in this meaning.

    • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（3）兼语短语Pivot phrases: ask someone to do something

      请/叫/让 + person + verb

      The person after 请, 叫 or 让 is both the object of that verb and the subject of the next one.

      When to use it

      The person is both the object of 请/叫/让 and the doer of the next verb.

      Negative and questions

      Negative before 让/叫: 妈妈不让我去.

      • 老师让我们读课文。Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen dú kèwén.The teacher had us read the text.
      • 我请你吃饭。Wǒ qǐng nǐ chī fàn.Let me treat you to a meal.
      • 妈妈不让我看电视。Māma bú ràng wǒ kàn diànshì.Mum doesn't let me watch TV.

      Compare

      请 = invite, polite; 叫 = tell (spoken); 让 = let, make.

    • Functions 功能类型Noun phrases 名词性短语—Noun phrases

      description + (的) + noun

      A noun phrase works like a noun. Its describing words come before the noun, often joined with 的: 我的朋友, 很大的房间, 一个新手机.

      When to use it

      The head noun comes last; everything describing it comes first.

      • 他是我最好的朋友。Tā shì wǒ zuì hǎo de péngyou.He's my best friend.
      • 我想买一个新手机。Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí gè xīn shǒujī.I want to buy a new phone.
      • 我买了一件很漂亮的衣服。Wǒ mǎi le yí jiàn hěn piàoliang de yīfu.I bought a beautiful piece of clothing.
    • Functions 功能类型Verb phrases 动词性短语—Verb phrases

      adverb + verb + object/complement

      A verb phrase is a verb with the words that go with it: adverbs before it, objects and complements after it. It can be a whole predicate.

      When to use it

      Adverbs and time words go before the verb; objects and complements after.

      • 我们明天一起去看电影。Wǒmen míngtiān yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng.We're going to see a film together tomorrow.
      • 他每天早上跑步。Tā měitiān zǎoshang pǎobù.He runs every morning.
      • 他每天都很早起床。Tā měi tiān dōu hěn zǎo qǐchuáng.He gets up early every day.
    • Functions 功能类型Adjective phrases 形容词性短语—Adjective phrases

      degree adverb + adjective (+ complement)

      An adjective with words like 很, 太, 非常, 最 or a complement after it works as a predicate on its own, without 是.

      When to use it

      Adjective phrases can be predicates (这个菜太好吃了) or describe nouns with 的 (很好吃的菜).

      • 这个菜太好吃了！Zhège cài tài hǎochī le!This dish is so tasty!
      • 今天非常冷。Jīntiān fēicháng lěng.It's extremely cold today.
      • 这是一个非常有意思的电影。Zhè shì yí ge fēicháng yǒu yìsi de diànyǐng.This is a really interesting film.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他什么的什么的: and so on

      A、B + 什么的

      什么的 at the end of a list means “and so on, and things like that”. It is spoken.

      When to use it

      什么的 ends an incomplete list, like “and so on, and stuff”. It is spoken.

      • 我买了苹果、牛奶什么的。Wǒ mǎi le píngguǒ, niúnǎi shénme de.I bought apples, milk and so on.
      • 星期天我看书、看电影什么的。Xīngqītiān wǒ kàn shū, kàn diànyǐng shénme de.On Sundays I read, watch films and so on.
      • 周末我常常打扫房间、洗衣服什么的。Zhōumò wǒ chángcháng dǎsǎo fángjiān, xǐ yīfu shénme de.At weekends I usually clean my room, do the washing and so on.

      还是 + suggestion + 吧

      还是 … 吧 gives a suggestion after thinking it over: “we'd better …”.

      When to use it

      还是…吧 gives a suggestion after thinking about it: “we'd better…”.

      • 太晚了，你还是明天去吧。Tài wǎn le, nǐ háishi míngtiān qù ba.It's too late, you'd better go tomorrow.

    • Subject 主语受事主语The object as the topic

      thing + (subject +) verb + complement/了

      The thing acted on can start the sentence as the topic, without a passive word: 饭做好了 the meal is ready.

      When to use it

      When the thing is already known, put it first as the topic; the doer can be left out: 作业做完了.

      • 饭做好了。Fàn zuò hǎo le.The meal is ready.
      • 那本书我看过。Nà běn shū wǒ kàn guo.That book, I've read it.
      • 房间打扫干净了。Fángjiān dǎsǎo gānjìng le.The room has been cleaned.
    • Predicate 谓语名词性短语作谓语Noun phrases as predicates

      subject + number/time/age/price phrase

      Dates, ages, prices and times can be the predicate without 是: 今天星期一, 他二十岁. The negative needs 不是.

      When to use it

      Only for numbers, dates, ages, prices, weather and origin; negate with 不是.

      • 今天十月一号。Jīntiān shí yuè yī hào.Today is the first of October.
      • 这个苹果五块钱。Zhège píngguǒ wǔ kuài qián.This apple is five yuan.
      • 明天晴天。Míngtiān qíngtiān.Tomorrow will be sunny.

      Exam note: Negative: 今天不是星期一, not 今天不星期一.

    • Attributive 定语动词或动词性短语、主谓短语作定语Verb phrases and clauses as descriptions

      verb phrase / clause + 的 + noun

      A verb phrase or a whole small sentence can describe a noun. It comes before the noun with 的: 我买的书 the book I bought.

      When to use it

      A whole verb phrase or small sentence + 的 can describe a noun, like an English relative clause, but it goes before the noun.

      • 这是我昨天买的衣服。Zhè shì wǒ zuótiān mǎi de yīfu.These are the clothes I bought yesterday.
      • 来上课的学生不多。Lái shàngkè de xuésheng bù duō.Not many students came to class.
      • 坐在我旁边的人是我同学。Zuò zài wǒ pángbiān de rén shì wǒ tóngxué.The person sitting next to me is my classmate.

      Compare

      English: the clothes (that) I bought. Chinese: 我买的衣服 (I bought DE clothes).

      Exam note: The description comes first: 我买的书, never 书我买的 for “the book I bought”.

    • Complement 补语Result complements 1 结果补语1动词+错/懂/好/会/完Result complements 错, 懂, 好, 会, 完

      verb + 错/懂/好/会/完 (+ 了) · 没 + verb + result

      The second verb shows the result: 写错 write wrongly, 听懂 understand, 做好 finish properly, 学会 master, 吃完 finish eating. Negate with 没.

      When to use it

      The result word shows the outcome: 完 finished, 懂 understood, 错 wrong, 好 done well, 会 learned.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + result. Question: … 了吗？/ … 了没有？

      • 这个字你写错了。Zhège zì nǐ xiě cuò le.You wrote this character wrong.
      • 我还没看完这本书。Wǒ hái méi kàn wán zhè běn shū.I haven't finished reading this book yet.
      • 饭做好了，快来吃吧！Fàn zuò hǎo le, kuài lái chī ba!Dinner's ready, come and eat!

      Compare

      看 = look; 看完 = finish reading; 看懂 = read and understand; 看错 = misread.

      Exam note: Negate results with 没, not 不: 没听懂.

    • Complement 补语Direction complements 1 趋向补语1简单趋向补语的趋向意义用法：（1）动词+来/去Direction complements 来 and 去

      verb + 来 (towards the speaker) / 去 (away)

      来 after a verb shows movement towards the speaker, 去 away from the speaker. A place object goes between the verb and 来/去: 回家去.

      When to use it

      来 = towards the speaker, 去 = away. A place object goes before 来/去: 回家来, 进教室去.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + 来/去: 他没回来.

      • 你快进来吧。Nǐ kuài jìnlai ba.Come in, quick.
      • 他回家去了。Tā huí jiā qù le.He went back home.
      • 你什么时候回来？Nǐ shénme shíhou huílai?When are you coming back?

      Exam note: Place words go before 来/去: 回家去, not 回去家.

    • Complement 补语Direction complements 1 趋向补语1（2）动词+上/下/进/出/过/回Direction complements 上, 下, 进, 出, 过, 回

      verb + 上/下/进/出/过/回 (+ place)

      These words after a movement verb show its direction: 走进 walk in, 跑出 run out, 坐下 sit down, 拿回 take back, 走过 walk past.

      When to use it

      上 up, 下 down, 进 in, 出 out, 过 across/past, 回 back. A place usually follows: 走进教室, 跑回家.

      • 请坐下。Qǐng zuò xia.Please sit down.
      • 他跑进了房间。Tā pǎo jìn le fángjiān.He ran into the room.
      • 他拿出一本书。Tā ná chū yì běn shū.He took out a book.
    • Complement 补语Direction complements 1 趋向补语1复合趋向补语的趋向意义用法：动词+出来/出去/过来/过去/回来/回去/进来/进去/起来/上来/上去/下来/下去Compound direction complements

      verb + 进来/出去/回来/起来/下去 …

      Two direction words can follow a verb: the first gives the direction (进, 出, 回, 上, 下, 过, 起), the second the viewpoint (来 towards me, 去 away).

      When to use it

      Compound complements add 来/去 to the direction: 进来 come in (to me), 出去 go out (away from me).

      Negative and questions

      A place goes between the parts: 走进教室来; a thing can go before or after 来/去: 拿出一本书来 / 拿出来一本书.

      • 他从房间里走出来了。Tā cóng fángjiān li zǒu chūlai le.He walked out of the room.
      • 孩子们跑回去了。Háizimen pǎo huíqu le.The children ran back.
      • 外面太冷了，快进来吧。Wàimiàn tài lěng le, kuài jìnlai ba.It's too cold outside, come in.
    • Complement 补语Complements of state 1 状态补语1动词+得+形容词性短语Verb + 得 + description

      (verb + object +) verb + 得 + (很) adjective

      得 after a verb introduces a comment on how the action is done. With an object, repeat the verb or put the object first: 他说汉语说得很好 / 他汉语说得很好.

      When to use it

      With an object, repeat the verb or put the object first: 他说汉语说得很好 / 他汉语说得很好.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: verb + 得 + 不 + adj: 写得不好.

      • 她跑得很快。Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.She runs fast.
      • 你汉字写得真好！Nǐ Hànzì xiě de zhēn hǎo!You write characters really well!
      • 他每天起得很早。Tā měi tiān qǐ de hěn zǎo.He gets up early every day.

      Compare

      他说汉语得很好 is wrong; the object can't sit between the verb and 得.

      Exam note: “他说汉语得很好” is wrong: 得 must follow the verb directly.

    • Complement 补语Quantity complements 2 数量补语2（1）表示动作持续的时间How long an action lasts

      verb + (了) + time length (+ 的) + object

      A time length after the verb tells how long the action lasts: 学了两年 studied for two years. With an object, repeat the verb or put the time before the object: 学汉语学了两年 / 学了两年(的)汉语.

      When to use it

      The length of time comes after the verb. With an object, repeat the verb or put the time before the object: 学汉语学了两年 / 学了两年(的)汉语.

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 多长时间？/ 多久？ Negative of duration: 两天没吃饭 (time before 没).

      • 我等了你一个小时。Wǒ děng le nǐ yí gè xiǎoshí.I waited for you for an hour.
      • 他学汉语学了两年。Tā xué Hànyǔ xué le liǎng nián.He studied Chinese for two years.
      • 你等了多长时间？等了二十分钟。Nǐ děng le duō cháng shíjiān? Děng le èrshí fēnzhōng.How long did you wait? Twenty minutes.

      Compare

      我两年学汉语 is wrong: duration goes after the verb.

      Exam note: Duration goes after the verb: 睡了八个小时, not 八个小时睡了.

    • Complement 补语Quantity complements 2 数量补语2（2）形容词+数量补语Adjective + amount

      adjective + 一点儿 / number + measure word

      After an adjective, an amount tells by how much: 大一点儿 a bit bigger, 高两公分 two centimetres taller, 贵十块 ten yuan dearer. Usually in comparisons.

      When to use it

      After an adjective, 一点儿 or a number says how much more: 便宜一点儿, 大两岁.

      • 这件衣服大一点儿。Zhè jiàn yīfu dà yìdiǎnr.This piece of clothing is a bit big.
      • 这个比那个贵十块。Zhège bǐ nàge guì shí kuài.This one is ten yuan dearer than that one.
      • 能便宜一点儿吗？Néng piányi yìdiǎnr ma?Can it be a bit cheaper?

      Compare

      一点儿 after the adjective = a bit more (comparison); 有点儿 before = a bit too (complaint).

      Exam note: Say 大一点儿, not 一点儿大, for “a bit bigger”.

    • Sentence patterns 句型Simple sentences 单句主谓句4：主谓谓语句Topic + small sentence

      topic + subject + predicate

      The predicate can itself be a small subject–predicate sentence about the topic: 他身体很好 “he, his health is good”.

      When to use it

      The first noun is the topic, then a small sentence says something about it: 我 + 身体很好.

      • 我妈妈身体很好。Wǒ māma shēntǐ hěn hǎo.My mum is in good health.
      • 这个饭店菜很好吃。Zhège fàndiàn cài hěn hǎochī.The food in this restaurant is delicious.
      • 北京冬天很冷。Běijīng dōngtiān hěn lěng.Beijing is cold in winter.
    • Sentence functions 句类Questions 疑问句用“还是”连接的选择问句Choice questions with 还是

      A 还是 B？

      还是 asks the listener to choose between options: “A or B?”. No 吗 at the end.

      When to use it

      还是 offers two choices in a question; don't add 吗.

      • 你喝茶还是喝咖啡？Nǐ hē chá háishi hē kāfēi?Will you have tea or coffee?
      • 我们今天去还是明天去？Wǒmen jīntiān qù háishi míngtiān qù?Shall we go today or tomorrow?
      • 你要大的还是小的？Nǐ yào dà de háishi xiǎo de?Do you want the big one or the small one?

      Compare

      还是 is for questions; 或者 (HSK 3) is “or” in statements: 今天或者明天都可以.

      Exam note: In questions use 还是, not 或者; and no 吗: “你喝茶还是咖啡吗？” is wrong.

    • Sentence functions 句类Commands and requests 祈使句用“别”的祈使句Commands with 别

      别 + verb (+ 了)

      别 makes a negative command. 别……了 asks someone to stop. Add 请 to be polite: 请别….

      When to use it

      别 + verb is a direct “don't”; 别…了 asks someone to stop.

      • 别忘了给妈妈打电话。Bié wàng le gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.Don't forget to call Mum.
      • 请别在这儿吃东西。Qǐng bié zài zhèr chī dōngxi.Please don't eat here.
      • 别哭了，没事儿。Bié kū le, méi shìr.Stop crying, it's fine.
    • Special constructions 特殊句式是 sentences 2 “是”字句2表示说明或特征是 to explain or describe

      A + 是 + description

      是 can explain or describe a feature, not only link two nouns: 他是个好人, 这儿是北边.

      When to use it

      是 here explains or describes rather than naming: 他是很好的人, 问题是….

      • 他是一个很好的人。Tā shì yí gè hěn hǎo de rén.He is a very good person.
      • 我的问题是没有时间。Wǒ de wèntí shì méiyǒu shíjiān.My problem is that I have no time.
      • 我的意思是明天再说。Wǒ de yìsi shì míngtiān zài shuō.What I mean is, let's talk tomorrow.
    • Special constructions 特殊句式有 sentences 2 “有”字句2表示达到有 meaning “reach, as much as”

      subject + 有 + amount (+ adjective)

      有 before an amount says something reaches that amount: 他有一米八 he's 1.8 m tall, 有十个人 as many as ten people.

      When to use it

      有 + amount says something reaches a level: 有两米高, 有一百个人.

      Negative and questions

      Ask: 有多高？ 有多远？

      • 他儿子有一米八了。Tā érzi yǒu yì mǐ bā le.His son is already 1.8 metres tall.
      • 你孩子有多高了？Nǐ háizi yǒu duō gāo le?How tall is your child now?
      • 从这儿到机场有多远？Cóng zhèr dào jīchǎng yǒu duō yuǎn?How far is it from here to the airport?
    • Special constructions 特殊句式Existential sentences 2 存现句2处所+动词+着(+数量短语)+名词Something is somewhere: place + verb + 着

      place + verb + 着 (+ number + measure word) + noun

      This pattern describes what is in a place and how it is placed: 桌子上放着一本书 a book is lying on the table. The place comes first, without 在.

      When to use it

      Place first, then verb + 着, then what is there: 墙上挂着一张画.

      • 桌子上写着他的名字。Zhuōzi shang xiě zhe tā de míngzi.His name is written on the table.
      • 门口站着一个人。Ménkǒu zhàn zhe yí gè rén.Someone is standing at the door.
      • 墙上挂着一张地图。Qiáng shang guà zhe yì zhāng dìtú.There's a map hanging on the wall.

      Compare

      Compared with 有, verb + 着 also shows how it is there (hanging, standing, written).

      Exam note: Don't start with 在: “在桌子上放着书” is wrong; say 桌子上放着书.

    • Special constructions 特殊句式Comparisons 1 比较句1（1）A比B+形容词A 比 B + adjective

      A + 比 + B + adjective

      The basic comparison: A is more … than B. The negative is A 没有 B + adjective.

      When to use it

      A 比 B + adjective. The adjective has no 很; you can add 更 or 还 for “even more”: 今天比昨天更冷.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: A 没有 B + adj. Question: A 比 B + adj + 吗？

      • 今天比昨天冷。Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng.Today is colder than yesterday.
      • 我的手机比你的新。Wǒ de shǒujī bǐ nǐ de xīn.My phone is newer than yours.
      • 这个比那个还贵。Zhège bǐ nàge hái guì.This one is even more expensive than that one.

      Compare

      他比我很高 ✗ → 他比我高 / 他比我高很多 ✓.

      Exam note: Negative: 我没有他高, not 我不比他高 for “I'm not as tall as him”.

    • Special constructions 特殊句式Comparisons 1 比较句1（2）A比B+形容词+数量补语/程度补语比 + adjective + how much

      A 比 B + adjective + 一点儿/得多/多了/number

      After the adjective you can add how big the difference is: 一点儿 a little, 得多 / 多了 much, or an exact number.

      When to use it

      The amount goes after the adjective: 一点儿 a bit, 多了/得多 much, or an exact number: 大三岁.

      • 今天比昨天冷多了。Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng duō le.Today is much colder than yesterday.
      • 他比我大三岁。Tā bǐ wǒ dà sān suì.He is three years older than me.
      • 哥哥比我高十厘米。Gēge bǐ wǒ gāo shí límǐ.My older brother is ten centimetres taller than me.

      Compare

      Don't put the amount before the adjective: 他比我三岁大 is wrong.

      Exam note: Don't use 很 or 非常 in 比 sentences; use 得多 or 多了.

    • Special constructions 特殊句式Comparisons 1 比较句1（3）A有/没有B（+这么/那么）+形容词A 有/没有 B (这么/那么) + adjective

      A + 有/没有 + B + (这么/那么) + adjective

      A 有 B … asks or says that A reaches B's level; A 没有 B … says A is less … than B.

      When to use it

      A 有 B (这么/那么) + adj: A is as … as B. Mostly used in questions and the negative.

      • 我没有他那么高。Wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme gāo.I'm not as tall as him.
      • 你家有我家这么大吗？Nǐ jiā yǒu wǒ jiā zhème dà ma?Is your home as big as mine?
      • 今天没有昨天那么热。Jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān nàme rè.Today isn't as hot as yesterday.

      Compare

      他没有我高 = he isn't as tall as me (the usual negative of 比).

    • Special constructions 特殊句式Comparisons 1 比较句1（4）A比B+动词+得+形容词/A+动词+得+比+B+形容词Comparing how actions are done

      A 比 B + verb + 得 + adjective · A + verb + 得 + 比 B + adjective

      To compare how well two people do something, combine 比 with 得. 比 B can go before the verb or after 得.

      When to use it

      比 can go before the verb or after 得; the meaning is the same.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: A 没有 B + verb + 得 + adj: 我没有他跑得快.

      • 他比我跑得快。Tā bǐ wǒ pǎo de kuài.He runs faster than me.
      • 她唱得比我好。Tā chàng de bǐ wǒ hǎo.She sings better than me.
      • 我没有他说得好。Wǒ méiyǒu tā shuō de hǎo.I don't speak as well as he does.
    • Special constructions 特殊句式是……的 sentences 1 “是……的”句1强调时间、地点、方式、动作者是……的: stressing when, where, how, who

      (是) + time/place/manner/person + verb + 的

      For a finished action, 是……的 puts the focus on when, where, how or by whom it happened. 是 can be dropped in positive sentences.

      When to use it

      Use 是…的 about a past action everyone knows happened, to focus on when, where, how or who. 是 can be dropped in positive sentences.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不是…的: 我不是坐飞机来的.

      • 你是什么时候来的？Nǐ shì shénme shíhou lái de?When did you come?
      • 我是坐飞机来的。Wǒ shì zuò fēijī lái de.I came by plane.
      • 你是在哪儿学的汉语？Nǐ shì zài nǎr xué de Hànyǔ?Where did you learn Chinese?

      Compare

      我来了 = I've come (news). 我是昨天来的 = It was yesterday that I came (detail).

      Exam note: Don't use 了 in this pattern: “我是昨天来了的” is wrong.

    • Special constructions 特殊句式Pivotal sentences 1 兼语句1表使令：主语+请/叫/让……+宾语1+动词+宾语2请, 叫, 让: asking or making someone do something

      subject + 请/叫/让 + person + verb + object

      请 politely invites, 叫 and 让 tell or let someone do something. The person in the middle is the doer of the second verb.

      When to use it

      The person after 请/叫/让 does the second action. 让 is the most common; 请 is polite; 叫 is spoken.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不让 / 没让 + person + verb.

      • 妈妈叫我早点儿回家。Māma jiào wǒ zǎo diǎnr huí jiā.Mum told me to come home early.
      • 我想请你喝咖啡。Wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ hē kāfēi.I'd like to buy you a coffee.
      • 老师让我们明天早点儿来。Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen míngtiān zǎo diǎnr lái.The teacher asked us to come a bit early tomorrow.
    • Special constructions 特殊句式Double-object sentences 2 双宾语句2主语+动词+给+宾语1+宾语2Verb + 给 + person + thing

      subject + verb + 给 + person + thing

      With verbs like 送, 借, 还, 卖, add 给 to show who receives the thing: 送给他一本书.

      When to use it

      Verbs like 送, 还, 借, 交, 介绍 take 给 before the person: 送给她一本书.

      • 我送给她一件衣服。Wǒ sòng gěi tā yí jiàn yīfu.I gave her a piece of clothing.
      • 他还给我一百块钱。Tā huán gěi wǒ yìbǎi kuài qián.He paid me back a hundred yuan.
      • 我想介绍给你一个朋友。Wǒ xiǎng jièshào gěi nǐ yí ge péngyou.I'd like to introduce a friend to you.
    • Complex sentences 复句Alternative clauses 选择复句（是）……，还是……(是)……，还是……: either … or …?

      (是) A，还是 B？

      Two full clauses can be offered as alternatives with 还是. 是 at the start is optional.

      When to use it

      Two full options in one question; 是 at the start is optional.

      • 你是今天走，还是明天走？Nǐ shì jīntiān zǒu, háishi míngtiān zǒu?Are you leaving today or tomorrow?
      • 是你去，还是我去？Shì nǐ qù, háishi wǒ qù?Are you going or am I?
      • 你是坐车去，还是走路去？Nǐ shì zuò chē qù, háishi zǒulù qù?Are you going by car or on foot?
    • Complex sentences 复句Contrast clauses 转折复句虽然……，但是……虽然……，但是……: although

      虽然 A，但是/可是 B

      虽然 gives a fact and 但是 or 可是 gives the contrast. Unlike English, Chinese uses both words together.

      When to use it

      虽然 can go before or after the subject; the second part usually has 但是/可是, often with 还/也.

      • 虽然很累，但是我很高兴。Suīrán hěn lèi, dànshì wǒ hěn gāoxìng.Although I'm tired, I'm happy.
      • 他虽然不高，但是跑得很快。Tā suīrán bù gāo, dànshì pǎo de hěn kuài.He isn't tall, but he runs fast.
      • 虽然下雨了，但是我们还是去了。Suīrán xià yǔ le, dànshì wǒmen háishi qù le.Although it rained, we still went.

      Compare

      English uses only one: “although” or “but”. Chinese normally uses both: 虽然…，但是….

    • Complex sentences 复句Cause and effect clauses 因果复句因为……，所以……因为……，所以……: because … so …

      因为 A，所以 B

      因为 gives the cause and 所以 the result. Either word can be left out, but using both is normal in Chinese.

      When to use it

      Either half can be dropped: 因为… alone or …，所以… alone are both common.

      Negative and questions

      Ask the reason: 为什么？ Answer: 因为….

      • 因为下雨了，所以我们没出去。Yīnwèi xià yǔ le, suǒyǐ wǒmen méi chūqu.Because it rained, we didn't go out.
      • 他生病了，所以没来上课。Tā shēngbìng le, suǒyǐ méi lái shàngkè.He's ill, so he didn't come to class.
      • 我昨天睡得太晚了，所以今天很累。Wǒ zuótiān shuì de tài wǎn le, suǒyǐ jīntiān hěn lèi.I went to bed too late yesterday, so I'm tired today.
    • Complex sentences 复句Contracted complex sentences 紧缩复句一……就……一……就……: as soon as

      subject + 一 + A，(subject +) 就 + B

      B happens right after A, or whenever A happens.

      When to use it

      一 and 就 both go before verbs. B happens immediately after A, or every time A happens.

      • 我一到家就给你打电话。Wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.I'll call you as soon as I get home.
      • 他一看书就想睡觉。Tā yí kàn shū jiù xiǎng shuìjiào.Whenever he reads, he gets sleepy.
      • 我一说，他就明白了。Wǒ yì shuō, tā jiù míngbai le.As soon as I said it, he understood.

      Compare

      Two subjects: each goes before its own part: 我一说，他就懂了.

    • Continuous aspect 持续态动词+着：（1）表示状态的持续着: a state that continues

      verb + 着 · 没 + verb + 着

      着 after verbs like 开, 关, 穿, 坐, 站 describes the state that results and continues: 窗户开着 the window is open.

      When to use it

      Verb + 着 for a lasting state: wearing, open, hanging, sitting.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + 着. Question: … 着吗？/ … 着没有？

      • 他穿着一件红衣服。Tā chuān zhe yí jiàn hóng yīfu.He's wearing red clothes.
      • 电视开着，可是没有人看。Diànshì kāi zhe, kěshì méiyǒu rén kàn.The TV is on, but no one's watching.
      • 窗户开着吗？没开着。Chuānghu kāi zhe ma? Méi kāi zhe.Is the window open? No.
    • Continuous aspect 持续态（2）表示动作的持续着: an action in progress

      (正/在) + verb + 着 (+ 呢)

      着 can also show an action going on, often with 正, 在 or 呢. Verb 1 + 着 + verb 2 describes the manner of the second action.

      When to use it

      着 with an action shows it continuing; often with 正/在 and 呢. Verb1 + 着 + verb2: doing the second while the first continues.

      • 外面下着雨呢。Wàimiàn xià zhe yǔ ne.It's raining outside.
      • 他们走着去学校。Tāmen zǒu zhe qù xuéxiào.They walk to school.
      • 她听着音乐跑步。Tā tīng zhe yīnyuè pǎobù.She runs while listening to music.

      Compare

      在 + verb focuses on the action in progress; verb + 着 more on the ongoing state or manner.

    • Experiential aspect 经历态用动态助词“过”表示过: have done before

      verb + 过 · 没 + verb + 过 · verb + 过 + 没有？

      过 shows something has happened at some time in the past. Ask with 吗 or with 没有 at the end.

      When to use it

      Use 过 for experiences, often with 以前, 从来没 or a number of times.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 没 + verb + 过; never: 从来没 + verb + 过.

      • 你看过这个电影没有？Nǐ kàn guo zhège diànyǐng méiyǒu?Have you seen this film?
      • 我以前学过一点儿汉语。Wǒ yǐqián xué guo yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.I studied a little Chinese before.
      • 我从来没吃过这么好吃的菜。Wǒ cónglái méi chī guo zhème hǎochī de cài.I've never eaten such delicious food.

      • 我家在五楼。Wǒ jiā zài wǔ lóu.My home is on the fifth floor.
      • 今天是十月五号。Jīntiān shì shí yuè wǔ hào.Today is the fifth of October.
      • 我坐三路公共汽车上班。Wǒ zuò sān lù gōnggòng qìchē shàngbān.I take the number 3 bus to work.
    • Expressing numbers 数的表达法概数表达法1：（1）用“几”表示概数几 for “a few”

      几 + measure word + noun

      In statements 几 means “a few, several”: 几个人 a few people, 十几 a dozen or so.

      When to use it

      几 in a statement means “a few”: 几本书 a few books, 十几 a dozen or so, 几十 dozens.

      • 我买了几本书。Wǒ mǎi le jǐ běn shū.I bought a few books.
      • 教室里有十几个学生。Jiàoshì li yǒu shí jǐ gè xuésheng.There are a dozen or so students in the classroom.
      • 我等了几分钟他就来了。Wǒ děng le jǐ fēnzhōng tā jiù lái le.I waited a few minutes and he came.

      Compare

      In a question 几 asks “how many”; in a statement it means “a few”.

    • Expressing numbers 数的表达法（2）数词+多+量词Number + 多 + measure word: more than

      10, 20, 100 … + 多 + measure word

      After a round number (10, 20, 100…), 多 before the measure word means “and more”: 二十多个 twenty-something.

      When to use it

      With round numbers (10, 20, 100…), 多 goes before the measure word: 二十多个, 一百多块.

      • 我们班有二十多个学生。Wǒmen bān yǒu èrshí duō gè xuésheng.There are twenty-odd students in our class.
      • 他一百多块钱买了这个。Tā yìbǎi duō kuài qián mǎi le zhège.He bought this for a hundred-odd yuan.
      • 这件衣服三百多块钱。Zhè jiàn yīfu sānbǎi duō kuài qián.This clothing costs over 300 yuan.

      Compare

      十多个 = 11–19 items; 十个多 is wrong.

    • Expressing numbers 数的表达法（3）数词+量词+多Number + measure word + 多: a bit more than

      1–9 + measure word + 多

      With numbers 1–9, 多 goes after the measure word: 一个多月 a month and a bit, 三块多 three yuan something.

      When to use it

      With 1–9, 多 goes after the measure word: 一个多月, 三块多, 两年多.

      • 我来中国一个多月了。Wǒ lái Zhōngguó yí gè duō yuè le.I've been in China for a month and a bit.
      • 这个苹果三块多。Zhège píngguǒ sān kuài duō.This apple is three-something yuan.
      • 我们走了一个多小时。Wǒmen zǒu le yí ge duō xiǎoshí.We walked for over an hour.

      Compare

      一个多小时 = a bit over an hour; 一多个小时 is wrong.

      Exam note: Position matters: 二十多个 (round number) but 一个多月 (1–9).
