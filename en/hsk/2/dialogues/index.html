Sports and hobbies

1. What's your hobby?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你有什么爱好？Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?What are your hobbies?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà我喜欢游泳和跑步。你呢？Wǒ xǐhuan yóuyǒng hé pǎobù. Nǐ ne?I like swimming and running. You?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我从小就喜欢踢足球。Wǒ cóng xiǎo jiù xǐhuan tī zúqiú.I've loved playing football since I was little.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà你经常踢吗？Nǐ jīngcháng tī ma?Do you play often?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng每个周末都踢，下次你跟我们一起来吧。Měi ge zhōumò dōu tī, xià cì nǐ gēn wǒmen yìqǐ lái ba.Every weekend. Come with us next time.
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà好啊，不过我踢得不好。Hǎo a, búguò wǒ tī de bù hǎo.Sure, but I don't play well.

2. Watching a basketball game

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng今天晚上有篮球比赛，你看吗？Jīntiān wǎnshang yǒu lánqiú bǐsài, nǐ kàn ma?There's a basketball game tonight. Are you watching?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng当然看！几点开始？Dāngrán kàn! Jǐ diǎn kāishǐ?Of course! What time does it start?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng七点半。来我家一起看吧。Qī diǎn bàn. Lái wǒ jiā yìqǐ kàn ba.Half past seven. Come and watch at my place.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng好，我带点儿吃的过去。Hǎo, wǒ dài diǎnr chī de guòqu.OK, I'll bring something to eat.
  5. 李明 · Lǐ Míng太好了，一会儿见！Tài hǎo le, yíhuìr jiàn!Great, see you soon!

Travel plans

1. Where are you going on holiday?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà放假你准备去哪儿旅游？Fàngjià nǐ zhǔnbèi qù nǎr lǚyóu?Where are you planning to travel in the holidays?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我想去上海，你去过吗？Wǒ xiǎng qù Shànghǎi, nǐ qù guo ma?I want to go to Shanghai. Have you been?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà去过，那儿非常漂亮。Qù guo, nàr fēicháng piàoliang.Yes, it's beautiful there.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng你觉得坐飞机好还是坐火车好？Nǐ juéde zuò fēijī hǎo háishi zuò huǒchē hǎo?Do you think it's better to fly or take the train?
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà坐火车吧，又快又便宜。Zuò huǒchē ba, yòu kuài yòu piányi.Take the train; it's fast and cheap.

2. Buying a train ticket

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng您好，我要买一张去上海的票。Nín hǎo, wǒ yào mǎi yì zhāng qù Shànghǎi de piào.Hello, I'd like a ticket to Shanghai.
  2. 售票员 · shòupiàoyuán哪天的？Nǎ tiān de?For which day?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng这个星期五，上午的。Zhège xīngqīwǔ, shàngwǔ de.This Friday, in the morning.
  4. 售票员 · shòupiàoyuán九点的可以吗？五百五十块。Jiǔ diǎn de kěyǐ ma? Wǔbǎi wǔshí kuài.Is nine o'clock OK? 550 yuan.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng可以。我可以在网上付钱吗？Kěyǐ. Wǒ kěyǐ zài wǎng shang fù qián ma?Yes. Can I pay online?
  6. 售票员 · shòupiàoyuán可以，用手机就行。Kěyǐ, yòng shǒujī jiù xíng.Yes, just use your phone.

At the airport

1. Picking up a friend

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng喂，你的飞机到了吗？Wéi, nǐ de fēijī dào le ma?Hello, has your plane landed?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng到了，我正在拿东西。Dào le, wǒ zhèngzài ná dōngxi.Yes, I'm getting my bags.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我在三号门口等你。Wǒ zài sān hào ménkǒu děng nǐ.I'm waiting for you at gate 3.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好，十分钟以后出来。Hǎo, shí fēnzhōng yǐhòu chūlai.OK, I'll be out in ten minutes.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng不着急，我开车来的。Bù zháojí, wǒ kāichē lái de.No rush, I came by car.

2. My flight is late

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà请问，去北京的飞机为什么还没走？Qǐngwèn, qù Běijīng de fēijī wèishénme hái méi zǒu?Excuse me, why hasn't the Beijing flight left yet?
  2. 工作人员 · gōngzuò rényuán因为天气不好，要晚一个小时。Yīnwèi tiānqì bù hǎo, yào wǎn yí ge xiǎoshí.Because of bad weather it'll be an hour late.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà那我可以在哪儿休息？Nà wǒ kěyǐ zài nǎr xiūxi?Where can I rest then?
  4. 工作人员 · gōngzuò rényuán前面右边有咖啡店。Qiánmiàn yòubian yǒu kāfēidiàn.There's a café ahead on the right.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà好的，谢谢。Hǎo de, xièxie.OK, thanks.

Bus and metro

1. Which line?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà请问，去火车站坐几号线？Qǐngwèn, qù huǒchēzhàn zuò jǐ hào xiàn?Excuse me, which line goes to the railway station?
  2. 路人 · lùrén坐二号线，三站就到。Zuò èr hào xiàn, sān zhàn jiù dào.Take Line 2; it's three stops.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà地铁站离这儿远吗？Dìtiězhàn lí zhèr yuǎn ma?Is the metro station far from here?
  4. 路人 · lùrén不远，往前走五分钟。Bù yuǎn, wǎng qián zǒu wǔ fēnzhōng.Not far; walk straight on for five minutes.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà太谢谢您了！Tài xièxie nín le!Thank you so much!

2. Bus or taxi?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我们坐公交车去吧。Wǒmen zuò gōngjiāochē qù ba.Let's take the bus.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà公交车太慢了，打车吧。Gōngjiāochē tài màn le, dǎchē ba.The bus is too slow; let's get a taxi.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng打车太贵了。Dǎchē tài guì le.A taxi is too expensive.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà可是我们快要迟到了！Kěshì wǒmen kuàiyào chídào le!But we're about to be late!
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng好吧，那就打车。Hǎo ba, nà jiù dǎchē.All right, a taxi then.

At a hotel

1. Checking in

  1. 服务员 · fúwùyuán您好，欢迎来我们酒店！Nín hǎo, huānyíng lái wǒmen jiǔdiàn!Hello, welcome to our hotel!
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà你好，我在网上订了一个房间。Nǐ hǎo, wǒ zài wǎng shang dìng le yí ge fángjiān.Hi, I booked a room online.
  3. 服务员 · fúwùyuán请告诉我您的姓名。Qǐng gàosu wǒ nín de xìngmíng.Please tell me your full name.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà我姓安，叫安娜。Wǒ xìng Ān, jiào Ānnà.My surname is An; I'm Anna.
  5. 服务员 · fúwùyuán好的，您的房间是八〇六，在八楼。Hǎo de, nín de fángjiān shì bā líng liù, zài bā lóu.Right, your room is 806, on the eighth floor.
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà谢谢！早饭几点开始？Xièxie! Zǎofàn jǐ diǎn kāishǐ?Thanks! What time is breakfast?

2. Something's wrong with the room

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你好，我房间的电视坏了。Nǐ hǎo, wǒ fángjiān de diànshì huài le.Hello, the TV in my room is broken.
  2. 服务员 · fúwùyuán不好意思，您是哪个房间？Bù hǎoyìsi, nín shì nǎge fángjiān?Sorry about that. Which room are you in?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng五〇二。Wǔ líng èr.502.
  4. 服务员 · fúwùyuán我们马上让人过去看一下。Wǒmen mǎshàng ràng rén guòqu kàn yíxià.We'll send someone to take a look right away.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng好，我在房间等。Hǎo, wǒ zài fángjiān děng.OK, I'll wait in my room.

At a café

1. Coffee or tea?

  1. 服务员 · fúwùyuán您好，想喝点儿什么？Nín hǎo, xiǎng hē diǎnr shénme?Hello, what would you like to drink?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng一杯咖啡，不要太热的。Yì bēi kāfēi, búyào tài rè de.A coffee, not too hot.
  3. 服务员 · fúwùyuán好的。还要别的吗？Hǎo de. Hái yào bié de ma?Sure. Anything else?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng再要一杯绿茶。Zài yào yì bēi lǜchá.And a green tea.
  5. 服务员 · fúwùyuán一共四十二块。Yígòng sìshí'èr kuài.That's 42 yuan altogether.

2. Bubble tea

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你喝过奶茶吗？Nǐ hē guo nǎichá ma?Have you ever had milk tea?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng没喝过，好喝吗？Méi hē guo, hǎohē ma?No, never. Is it good?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà很好喝！中国年轻人都喜欢。Hěn hǎohē! Zhōngguó niánqīngrén dōu xǐhuan.Delicious! Young people in China all love it.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng那我试试。哪种最好？Nà wǒ shìshi. Nǎ zhǒng zuì hǎo?Then I'll try it. Which kind is best?
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà红茶的最好喝。Hóngchá de zuì hǎohē.The black tea one is the best.

Birthdays

1. A birthday present

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng明天是李明的生日，我们送他什么？Míngtiān shì Lǐ Míng de shēngrì, wǒmen sòng tā shénme?Tomorrow is Li Ming's birthday. What shall we give him?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà他喜欢运动，送他一个篮球吧。Tā xǐhuan yùndòng, sòng tā yí ge lánqiú ba.He likes sport; let's give him a basketball.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng他已经有篮球了。Tā yǐjīng yǒu lánqiú le.He already has a basketball.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà那送一块手表怎么样？Nà sòng yí kuài shǒubiǎo zěnmeyàng?How about a watch, then?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng好主意！我们下午去商场买。Hǎo zhǔyi! Wǒmen xiàwǔ qù shāngchǎng mǎi.Good idea! Let's buy one at the mall this afternoon.

2. Happy birthday!

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà李明，祝你生日快乐！Lǐ Míng, zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!Li Ming, happy birthday!
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng谢谢！快进来吧。Xièxie! Kuài jìnlai ba.Thanks! Come on in.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà这是我们送你的手表。Zhè shì wǒmen sòng nǐ de shǒubiǎo.Here's a watch from us.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng太漂亮了！我非常喜欢。Tài piàoliang le! Wǒ fēicháng xǐhuan.It's beautiful! I love it.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà你今年多大了？Nǐ jīnnián duō dà le?How old are you this year?
  6. 李明 · Lǐ Míng二十二岁。Èrshí'èr suì.Twenty-two.

Exams

1. Getting ready for an exam

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng下个星期就要考试了，你准备得怎么样？Xià ge xīngqī jiù yào kǎoshì le, nǐ zhǔnbèi de zěnmeyàng?The exam is next week. How's your preparation going?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà不太好，很多词我都记不住。Bú tài hǎo, hěn duō cí wǒ dōu jì bu zhù.Not great; I can't remember lots of words.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng你可以每天写一写、读一读。Nǐ kěyǐ měi tiān xiě yi xiě, dú yi dú.You could write and read them a bit every day.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà好主意。我们一起复习吧？Hǎo zhǔyi. Wǒmen yìqǐ fùxí ba?Good idea. Shall we revise together?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng没问题，明天下午在教室见。Méi wèntí, míngtiān xiàwǔ zài jiàoshì jiàn.No problem, see you in the classroom tomorrow afternoon.

2. How did the exam go?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà今天考得怎么样？Jīntiān kǎo de zěnmeyàng?How did today's exam go?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng不错，题不太难。Búcuò, tí bú tài nán.Not bad; the questions weren't too hard.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我有两个题没做完。Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge tí méi zuò wán.I didn't finish two questions.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng别想了，考完了就去玩儿吧！Bié xiǎng le, kǎo wán le jiù qù wánr ba!Stop thinking about it — the exam's over, let's go and have fun!

At the pharmacy

1. I have a stomach ache

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你好，我肚子很疼。Nǐ hǎo, wǒ dùzi hěn téng.Hello, I have a bad stomach ache.
  2. 药店的人 · yàodiàn de rén从什么时候开始的？Cóng shénme shíhou kāishǐ de?Since when?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng从昨天晚上开始。Cóng zuótiān wǎnshang kāishǐ.Since last night.
  4. 药店的人 · yàodiàn de rén这个药一天吃三次，吃饭以后吃。Zhège yào yì tiān chī sān cì, chī fàn yǐhòu chī.Take this medicine three times a day, after meals.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng好的，多少钱？Hǎo de, duōshao qián?OK, how much is it?
  6. 药店的人 · yàodiàn de rén二十八块。Èrshíbā kuài.28 yuan.

2. Feeling better?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你身体好点儿了吗？Nǐ shēntǐ hǎo diǎnr le ma?Are you feeling better?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng好多了，吃了药就不疼了。Hǎo duō le, chī le yào jiù bù téng le.Much better; it stopped hurting after the medicine.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà以后别吃太多冷的东西。Yǐhòu bié chī tài duō lěng de dōngxi.Don't eat so much cold food from now on.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng知道了，谢谢你！Zhīdào le, xièxie nǐ!Got it, thank you!

Weather and seasons

1. Tomorrow's weather

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng明天天气怎么样？Míngtiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?What's the weather tomorrow?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà上午晴天，下午阴天。Shàngwǔ qíngtiān, xiàwǔ yīntiān.Sunny in the morning, cloudy in the afternoon.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng会下雨吗？Huì xià yǔ ma?Will it rain?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà可能会，你最好带上伞。Kěnéng huì, nǐ zuìhǎo dài shàng sǎn.Maybe; you'd better take an umbrella.

2. Favourite season

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你最喜欢哪个季节？Nǐ zuì xǐhuan nǎge jìjié?Which season do you like best?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng夏天，因为可以游泳。Xiàtiān, yīnwèi kěyǐ yóuyǒng.Summer, because I can swim.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我喜欢冬天，我们那儿冬天有很多雪。Wǒ xǐhuan dōngtiān, wǒmen nàr dōngtiān yǒu hěn duō xuě.I like winter; where I'm from there's lots of snow.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng不冷吗？Bù lěng ma?Isn't it cold?
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà很冷，但是非常漂亮。Hěn lěng, dànshì fēicháng piàoliang.Very, but it's beautiful.

Clothes and colours

1. Trying on trousers

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng这条裤子有大一点儿的吗？Zhè tiáo kùzi yǒu dà yìdiǎnr de ma?Do you have these trousers a size bigger?
  2. 服务员 · fúwùyuán有，您穿多大的？Yǒu, nín chuān duō dà de?Yes. What size do you wear?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我个子高，要最大的。Wǒ gèzi gāo, yào zuì dà de.I'm tall; I need the biggest.
  4. 服务员 · fúwùyuán您试试这条，洗手间旁边可以试。Nín shìshi zhè tiáo, xǐshǒujiān pángbiān kěyǐ shì.Try these on; the fitting room is next to the toilets.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng正好！就要这条。Zhènghǎo! Jiù yào zhè tiáo.Just right! I'll take these.

2. Which colour?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你看，红色的好看还是白色的好看？Nǐ kàn, hóngsè de hǎokàn háishi báisè de hǎokàn?Look — does the red one or the white one look better?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我觉得红色的更好看。Wǒ juéde hóngsè de gèng hǎokàn.I think the red one looks better.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà可是我已经有很多红衣服了。Kěshì wǒ yǐjīng yǒu hěn duō hóng yīfu le.But I already have lots of red clothes.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng那买黑色的吧，黑色的什么时候都能穿。Nà mǎi hēisè de ba, hēisè de shénme shíhou dōu néng chuān.Then buy the black one; you can wear black any time.

Describing people

1. Who's your teacher?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你们的新老师长什么样？Nǐmen de xīn lǎoshī zhǎng shénme yàng?What does your new teacher look like?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà个子很高，眼睛很大，戴眼镜。Gèzi hěn gāo, yǎnjing hěn dà, dài yǎnjìng.Very tall, big eyes, wears glasses.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng他是哪国人？Tā shì nǎ guó rén?Where's he from?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà中国人，他说话说得很慢，我们都能听懂。Zhōngguó rén, tā shuōhuà shuō de hěn màn, wǒmen dōu néng tīng dǒng.He's Chinese. He speaks slowly so we can all understand.

2. Looking for someone

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你看见我妹妹了吗？Nǐ kànjiàn wǒ mèimei le ma?Have you seen my little sister?
  2. 同学 · tóngxué她长什么样？Tā zhǎng shénme yàng?What does she look like?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng个子不高，穿红衣服，背着一个书包。Gèzi bù gāo, chuān hóng yīfu, bēi zhe yí ge shūbāo.Not tall, wearing red, carrying a school bag.
  4. 同学 · tóngxué我刚才看见她在门口跟朋友说话。Wǒ gāngcái kànjiàn tā zài ménkǒu gēn péngyou shuōhuà.I just saw her at the door talking with friends.
  5. 李明 · Lǐ Míng太好了，谢谢！Tài hǎo le, xièxie!Great, thanks!

Left, right and how far

1. Looking for the post office

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà请问，邮局离这儿远不远？Qǐngwèn, yóujú lí zhèr yuǎn bu yuǎn?Excuse me, is the post office far from here?
  2. 路人 · lùrén不太远。你往前走，到路口往左走。Bú tài yuǎn. Nǐ wǎng qián zǒu, dào lùkǒu wǎng zuǒ zǒu.Not too far. Go straight, then turn left at the crossing.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà然后呢？Ránhòu ne?And then?
  4. 路人 · lùrén邮局就在银行右边。Yóujú jiù zài yínháng yòubian.The post office is right next to the bank, on its right.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà明白了，谢谢您！Míngbai le, xièxie nín!Got it, thank you!

2. Where's the toilet?
