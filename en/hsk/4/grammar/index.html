    Parts of speech词类23
    • Verbs 动词Modal verbs 能愿动词敢敢: dare

      subject + (不) 敢 + verb

      敢 means have the courage to. 不敢 is very common: not dare to, be too scared to. The question form is 敢不敢.

      When to use it

      敢 = have the courage to. Most often used in the negative or in questions.

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不敢 / 没敢. Question: 你敢不敢…？

      • 我不敢一个人晚上出去。Wǒ bù gǎn yí gè rén wǎnshang chūqu.I don't dare go out alone at night.
      • 你敢不敢跟他说？Nǐ gǎn bu gǎn gēn tā shuō?Do you dare tell him?
      • 我不敢一个人走夜路。Wǒ bù gǎn yí ge rén zǒu yèlù.I'm afraid to walk alone at night.

      Compare

      不敢 = afraid to; 不会 = can't (skill); 不能 = not able / not allowed.

    • Pronouns 代词Demonstratives 指示代词各、各位、各种、任何、此各, 任何, 此: each, any, this

      各 + noun / measure word · 任何 + noun · 此 (written)

      各 each, every one of several: 各国 every country, 各位 everyone (polite address), 各种 all kinds. 任何 any at all, often with 都 or 也: 任何人都可以. 此 is written “this”: 因此, 从此, 此外.

      When to use it

      各 + noun/MW = each, every (各国, 各位); 任何 + noun = any, often with 都/也; 此 = this (written).

      Negative and questions

      任何 + 都/也 + 不/没 = no … at all: 任何人都不知道.

      • 各位，请安静一下。Gè wèi, qǐng ānjìng yíxià.Everyone, quiet please.
      • 商店里有各种水果。Shāngdiàn li yǒu gè zhǒng shuǐguǒ.The shop has all kinds of fruit.
      • 任何人都可以参加。Rènhé rén dōu kěyǐ cānjiā.Anyone can take part.
      • 如果有任何问题，请给我打电话。Rúguǒ yǒu rènhé wèntí, qǐng gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.If you have any questions, please call me.

      Compare

      各位 is a polite way to address a group: 各位朋友.

    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（1）专用名量词：打、袋、棵、台、幅Measure words 打, 袋, 棵, 台, 幅

      一打 · 一袋 · 一棵 · 一台 · 一幅

      打 a dozen. 袋 a bag of. 棵 plants and trees. 台 machines: computers, TVs. 幅 paintings and pictures.

      When to use it

      打 dozen, 袋 bag, 棵 trees and plants, 台 machines, 幅 pictures and paintings.

      • 我家门口有两棵树。Wǒ jiā ménkǒu yǒu liǎng kē shù.There are two trees outside my door.
      • 他买了一台新电脑。Tā mǎile yì tái xīn diànnǎo.He bought a new computer.
      • 客厅里有一幅画。Kètīng li yǒu yì fú huà.There is a painting in the living room.
      • 我们家买了一台新电脑。Wǒmen jiā mǎi le yì tái xīn diànnǎo.We bought a new computer.
    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（2）借用名量词：脸、手、盒、屋子、桌子Borrowed measure words: 脸, 手, 盒, 屋子

      一 + 脸 / 手 / 身 / 屋子 + noun

      With 一, nouns like 脸, 手, 屋子 mean all over, a whole … of: 一脸汗 a face covered in sweat, 一屋子人 a roomful of people. 盒 a box of.

      When to use it

      一 + body part or place + noun: “all over”: 一脸汗, 一身水, 一屋子人.

      • 他跑得一脸汗。Tā pǎo de yì liǎn hàn.He ran until his face was covered in sweat.
      • 一屋子人都在等你。Yì wūzi rén dōu zài děng nǐ.A whole roomful of people is waiting for you.
      • 我买了一盒饼干。Wǒ mǎile yì hé bǐnggān.I bought a box of biscuits.
      • 他跑回来，出了一身汗。Tā pǎo huílai, chū le yì shēn hàn.He ran back drenched in sweat.
    • Measure words 量词Verbal measure words 动量词场、顿、趟Action measure words 场, 顿, 趟

      verb + number + 场 / 顿 / 趟

      场 for events and weather: 一场比赛 a match, 下了一场雨 it rained once. 顿 for meals or scoldings: 吃一顿饭, 骂了一顿. 趟 for trips there and back: 去一趟.

      When to use it

      场 events and games (一场比赛, 下了一场雨); 顿 meals and scoldings (吃一顿饭); 趟 round trips (去一趟).

      • 我得去一趟银行。Wǒ děi qù yí tàng yínháng.I need to make a trip to the bank.
      • 我们一起吃顿饭吧。Wǒmen yìqǐ chī dùn fàn ba.Let's have a meal together.
      • 昨天下了一场大雨。Zuótiān xiàle yì cháng dà yǔ.There was a heavy rain yesterday.
      • 我得去一趟银行。Wǒ děi qù yí tàng yínháng.I need to make a trip to the bank.

      Compare

      次 = any occurrence; 趟 = a trip there and back.

    • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词十分、更加、稍、稍微、尤其、多么Degree adverbs: 十分, 更加, 稍微, 尤其, 多么

      adverb + adjective / verb

      十分 very (a bit formal). 更加 even more. 稍 or 稍微 slightly, often with 一点儿 or 一下. 尤其 especially. 多么 how (exclamations): 多么美啊!

      When to use it

      十分 = very (more formal than 很); 稍微 = slightly, often with 一点儿/一下; 尤其 = especially; 多么 = how (exclamations).

      • 请稍微等一下。Qǐng shāowēi děng yíxià.Please wait a moment.
      • 我喜欢运动，尤其是游泳。Wǒ xǐhuan yùndòng, yóuqí shì yóuyǒng.I like sport, especially swimming.
      • 这里的风景多么美啊！Zhèlǐ de fēngjǐng duōme měi a!How beautiful the scenery is here!
      • 请稍微等一下。Qǐng shāowēi děng yíxià.Please wait a moment.

      Compare

      稍微 is followed by a small amount: 稍微等一下, 稍微大一点儿.

    • Adverbs 副词Scope adverbs 范围副词共、全、光、仅、仅仅、至少Range adverbs: 全, 光, 仅, 至少, 共

      全 (all) · 光 / 仅 / 仅仅 (only) · 至少 (at least) · 共 (in total)

      全 all, entirely: 全家 the whole family, 全都. 光 only, spoken: 光吃不练. 仅 and 仅仅 only, written. 至少 at least. 共 in total, written for 一共.

      When to use it

      全 = all, whole; 光/仅/仅仅 = only; 至少 = at least; 共 = in total (written).

      • 我们全家都去了。Wǒmen quán jiā dōu qù le.Our whole family went.
      • 你至少要睡七个小时。Nǐ zhìshǎo yào shuì qī gè xiǎoshí.You need at least seven hours of sleep.
      • 光说不做没有用。Guāng shuō bú zuò méiyǒu yòng.Just talking without doing is useless.
      • 这本书至少要看两遍。Zhè běn shū zhìshǎo yào kàn liǎng biàn.You need to read this book at least twice.
    • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词按时、从来、赶紧、赶快、已、忽然、将、将要、从、本来Time adverbs: 从来, 赶快, 忽然, 将, 本来 …

      从来 + 不 / 没 · 赶紧 / 赶快 · 按时 · 忽然 · 将 / 将要 · 本来 · 已

      从来 always, mostly with a negative: 从来不 never. 赶紧 / 赶快 hurry up and. 按时 on time. 忽然 suddenly. 将 / 将要 will, written future. 本来 originally, at first. 已 written for 已经. 从 with a negative: 从不.

      When to use it

      从来 + 不/没 = never; 赶快/赶紧 = hurry; 忽然 = suddenly; 将 = will (written); 本来 = originally.

      • 他从来不喝咖啡。Tā cónglái bù hē kāfēi.He never drinks coffee.
      • 要下雨了，赶快回家吧。Yào xià yǔ le, gǎnkuài huí jiā ba.It's about to rain, hurry home.
      • 我本来想去，后来没时间了。Wǒ běnlái xiǎng qù, hòulái méi shíjiān le.I meant to go, but then I had no time.
      • 我从来没去过那儿。Wǒ cónglái méi qù guo nàr.I've never been there.

      Compare

      从来不 = never (habit); 从来没 = never ever (experience) + 过.

    • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词重新、往往、偶尔、再次、老Frequency adverbs: 往往, 偶尔, 重新, 再次, 老

      往往 · 偶尔 · 重新 · 再次 · 老 + verb

      往往 tends to, usually, about patterns under certain conditions. 偶尔 occasionally. 重新 again from the start, anew. 再次 once more (formal). 老 always, often with complaint: 你老迟到.

      When to use it

      往往 = usually (a pattern); 偶尔 = occasionally; 重新 = again from scratch; 老 + verb = keep doing (complaining).

      • 周末的时候，公园里往往人很多。Zhōumò de shíhou, gōngyuán li wǎngwǎng rén hěn duō.At weekends the park tends to be crowded.
      • 写错了，你重新写一遍吧。Xiěcuò le, nǐ chóngxīn xiě yí biàn ba.That's wrong, write it again from the start.
      • 你怎么老迟到？Nǐ zěnme lǎo chídào?Why are you always late?
      • 这篇文章写得不好，你重新写吧。Zhè piān wénzhāng xiě de bù hǎo, nǐ chóngxīn xiě ba.This essay isn't good; write it again.

      Compare

      往往 describes a pattern seen before; 常常 can also be for the future.

      Exam note: 往往 is about what generally happens, not the future; for a future habit use 常常.

    • Adverbs 副词Manner adverbs 方式副词故意、互相、相互故意, 互相: on purpose, each other

      故意 + verb · 互相 / 相互 + verb

      故意 on purpose, deliberately. 互相 or 相互 each other, mutually: 互相帮助 help each other.

      When to use it

      故意 = on purpose; 互相 = each other (before the verb).

      Negative and questions

      Negative: 不是故意的 (I didn't mean it).

      • 对不起，我不是故意的。Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bú shì gùyì de.Sorry, I didn't do it on purpose.
      • 同学们应该互相帮助。Tóngxuémen yīnggāi hùxiāng bāngzhù.Classmates should help each other.
      • 对不起，我不是故意的。Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bú shì gùyì de.Sorry, I didn't mean to.
    • Adverbs 副词Linking adverbs 关联副词却却: but, yet

      subject + 却 + verb

      却 marks a contrast or something unexpected. Unlike 但是, it goes after the subject, never at the start of the clause.

      When to use it

      却 shows an unexpected contrast. It goes after the subject, before the verb.

      • 我等了他很久，他却没来。Wǒ děngle tā hěn jiǔ, tā què méi lái.I waited ages for him, yet he didn't come.
      • 东西不多，价格却很贵。Dōngxi bù duō, jiàgé què hěn guì.There isn't much, yet the price is high.
      • 天气很冷，他却只穿了一件衬衫。Tiānqì hěn lěng, tā què zhǐ chuān le yí jiàn chènshān.It was cold, yet he wore only a shirt.

      Compare

      但是 starts the clause; 却 comes after the subject. They can go together: 但是他却….

      Exam note: Not 却他没来: 却 goes after the subject.

    • Adverbs 副词Manner and mood adverbs 情态副词也许、恐怕也许 and 恐怕: perhaps, I'm afraid

      也许 + statement · 恐怕 + statement

      也许 maybe, perhaps. 恐怕 I'm afraid that, probably, used when the guess is unwelcome.

      When to use it

      也许 = maybe, neutral; 恐怕 = I'm afraid that, when the result is unwelcome.

      • 也许他今天不来了。Yěxǔ tā jīntiān bù lái le.Maybe he isn't coming today.
      • 恐怕来不及了。Kǒngpà lái bu jí le.I'm afraid there isn't time.
      • 恐怕今天完不成了。Kǒngpà jīntiān wán bu chéng le.I'm afraid we won't finish today.
    • Adverbs 副词Modal (attitude) adverbs 语气副词才4、就5、并1、还4、竟然、究竟、正好、到底、难道、千万、确实、只好、差（一）点儿Tone adverbs: 到底, 竟然, 难道, 千万, 只好 …

      adverb + verb / sentence

      到底 / 究竟 in questions: what on earth. 竟然 unexpectedly, to my surprise. 难道 surely not (rhetorical). 千万 by all means, often 千万别. 只好 have no choice but to. 正好 just right, as it happened. 确实 truly. 差点儿 nearly. 并 + negative: not at all, actually not. 还 can show surprise that something is so.

      When to use it

      到底/究竟 press for an answer; 竟然 = surprisingly; 难道 = surely not; 千万 = by all means (in warnings); 正好 = just right.

      • 你到底想去哪儿？Nǐ dàodǐ xiǎng qù nǎr?Where on earth do you want to go?
      • 没有车了，我们只好走回去。Méiyǒu chē le, wǒmen zhǐhǎo zǒu huiqu.There were no buses left, so we had to walk back.
      • 你千万别忘了。Nǐ qiānwàn bié wàng le.Whatever you do, don't forget.
      • 我差点儿迟到。Wǒ chàdiǎnr chídào.I was nearly late.
      • 你到底去不去？Nǐ dàodǐ qù bu qù?So are you going or not?
      • 千万别忘了带护照。Qiānwàn bié wàng le dài hùzhào.Whatever you do, don't forget your passport.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing time or place 引出时间、处所自、当、由1、随着、等1Prepositions of time and place: 自, 当, 由, 随着, 等

      自 / 由 + place · 当……的时候 · 随着 + change · 等 + event

      自 from (written). 由 from, starting at. 当……的时候 when, at the time that. 随着 along with, as something changes. 等 wait until: 等他来了再说 wait till he comes.

      When to use it

      自/由 + place = from (written); 当…的时候 = when; 随着 + change = along with; 等 + event = when, after.

      • 当我回到家的时候，已经十点了。Dāng wǒ huídào jiā de shíhou, yǐjīng shí diǎn le.When I got home, it was already ten.
      • 随着天气变冷，人们穿得越来越多。Suízhe tiānqì biàn lěng, rénmen chuān de yuè lái yuè duō.As it gets colder, people wear more and more.
      • 等你到了，给我打个电话。Děng nǐ dào le, gěi wǒ dǎ ge diànhuà.When you arrive, give me a call.
      • 随着经济的发展，人们的生活越来越好。Suízhe jīngjì de fāzhǎn, rénmen de shēnghuó yuè lái yuè hǎo.As the economy develops, life gets better and better.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing agent or patient 引出施事、受事将、由2、叫、让将, 由, 叫, 让: marking who acts

      将 = 把 (written) · 由 + doer + verb · 叫 / 让 = 被 (spoken)

      将 can replace 把 in writing: 将门关上. 由 says whose job something is: 由我负责 I'm in charge. 叫 and 让 can mark the doer in a spoken passive, like 被: 钱包叫人偷了.

      When to use it

      将 = 把 in writing; 由 + doer = done by (responsibility); 叫/让 = 被 in speech, doer required.

      • 这件事由我来负责。Zhè jiàn shì yóu wǒ lái fùzé.I'll be responsible for this.
      • 我的自行车让人骑走了。Wǒ de zìxíngchē ràng rén qízǒu le.Someone made off with my bike.
      • 这件事由我负责。Zhè jiàn shì yóu wǒ fùzé.I'm in charge of this.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing cause 引出原因由于1由于: because of

      由于 + reason，result

      由于 gives a reason, a bit more formal than 因为. It pairs with 所以 or 因此.

      When to use it

      由于 = because of, more formal than 因为; often with 因此 or 所以.

      • 由于下大雨，比赛推迟了。Yóuyú xià dà yǔ, bǐsài tuīchí le.Because of heavy rain, the match was put off.
      • 由于时间不够，我们只好先回去了。Yóuyú shíjiān bú gòu, wǒmen zhǐhǎo xiān huíqu le.Because there wasn't enough time, we had to go back first.
      • 由于天气原因，飞机晚点了。Yóuyú tiānqì yuányīn, fēijī wǎndiǎn le.The flight was delayed because of the weather.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象对于、与1对于 and 与: regarding, with

      对于 + topic，… · 与 + person + verb

      对于 regarding, as for, introduces what an opinion or attitude is about. 与 is written for 跟 or 和: 与他见面.

      When to use it

      对于 + topic = regarding; 与 + person = with (written 跟).

      • 对于这个问题，大家有不同的看法。Duìyú zhège wèntí, dàjiā yǒu bù tóng de kànfǎ.People have different views on this question.
      • 我想与你谈一谈。Wǒ xiǎng yǔ nǐ tán yi tán.I'd like to have a talk with you.
      • 对于这个问题，大家的看法不同。Duìyú zhège wèntí, dàjiā de kànfǎ bù tóng.People have different views on this issue.

      Compare

      对于 only marks the topic; 对 can also mean “towards someone”.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing means or basis 引出凭借、依据作为、按、按照作为, 按, 按照: as, according to

      作为 + role，… · 按 / 按照 + rule + verb

      作为 as, in the role of: 作为学生 as a student. 按 and 按照 according to, following a rule or plan: 按时 on time, 按照计划.

      When to use it

      作为 + role = as; 按/按照 + rule = according to.

      • 作为学生，你应该按时上课。Zuòwéi xuésheng, nǐ yīnggāi ànshí shàngkè.As a student, you should come to class on time.
      • 我们按照老师说的做吧。Wǒmen ànzhào lǎoshī shuō de zuò ba.Let's do as the teacher said.
      • 请按照说明书使用。Qǐng ànzhào shuōmíngshū shǐyòng.Please use it according to the instructions.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining words or phrases 连接词或词组并2、而1、与2并, 而, 与 joining words

      A 并 B (verbs) · A 而 B (adjectives) · A 与 B (nouns)

      These written words join parts of a sentence. 并 and also, between verbs: 讨论并通过. 而 and yet, between adjectives: 少而精 few but good. 与 and, between nouns: 老师与学生.

      When to use it

      并 joins verbs (讨论并通过), 而 joins adjectives (短而有力), 与 joins nouns (老师与学生). All are written style.

      • 老师与学生都参加了。Lǎoshī yǔ xuésheng dōu cānjiā le.Teachers and students both took part.
      • 这个房间小而干净。Zhège fángjiān xiǎo ér gānjìng.This room is small but clean.
      • 这篇文章短而有力。Zhè piān wénzhāng duǎn ér yǒulì.This essay is short but powerful.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining clauses or sentences 连接分句或句子此外、而2、既然、甚至、不过、并且Linking clauses: 此外, 既然, 甚至, 不过, 并且, 而

      此外 (besides) · 既然 (since) · 甚至 (even) · 不过 (but) · 并且 (and also) · 而 (whereas)

      此外 in addition. 既然 since, given that, with 就. 甚至 even, going further. 不过 but, softer than 但是. 并且 and also. 而 contrasts two clauses: 他喜欢唱歌，而我喜欢跳舞.

      When to use it

      此外 = besides; 既然 = since (given that); 甚至 = even; 不过 = but (softer); 并且 = and also.

      • 这件衣服很好看，不过有点儿贵。Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn hǎokàn, búguò yǒudiǎnr guì.This is nice, but a bit expensive.
      • 他喜欢安静，而他妹妹喜欢热闹。Tā xǐhuan ānjìng, ér tā mèimei xǐhuan rènao.He likes quiet, while his sister likes lively places.
      • 既然你不舒服，就别去了。Jìrán nǐ bù shūfu, jiù bié qù le.Since you're not well, don't go.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining clauses or sentences 连接分句或句子不光、不仅、另外、要是、因此、由于2、加Linking clauses: 不仅, 另外, 要是, 因此, 由于

      不光 / 不仅 (not only) · 另外 (also) · 要是 (if) · 因此 (therefore) · 由于 (because) · 加 (plus)

      不光 and 不仅 not only, with 还 or 而且. 另外 in addition, also. 要是 if, spoken. 因此 therefore. 由于 because. 加 plus, adding numbers: 二加三.

      When to use it

      不仅/不光 = not only; 另外 = in addition; 要是 = if (spoken); 因此 = therefore (written).

      • 这个房子不错，不过离公司有点儿远。Zhège fángzi búcuò, búguò lí gōngsī yǒudiǎnr yuǎn.The flat's nice, though a bit far from work.
    • Complex sentences 复句Contrast clauses 转折复句……X是X，就是/不过……X是X，就是 / 不过: X, sure, but

      A 是 A，就是 / 不过 B

      Grant the first point, then add the problem.

      When to use it

      Grant a point, then add the problem: X 是 X，就是/不过 ….

      • 这个房子大是大，就是离学校太远。Zhège fángzi dà shì dà, jiùshì lí xuéxiào tài yuǎn.This flat is big all right, it's just too far from school.
      • 这个手机好是好，不过有点儿贵。Zhège shǒujī hǎo shì hǎo, búguò yǒudiǎnr guì.The phone is good, but a bit expensive.
    • Complex sentences 复句Hypothetical clauses 假设复句……，否则……否则: otherwise

      A，否则 B

      否则 says what will happen if A isn't done: otherwise, or else.

      When to use it

      否则 = otherwise (if not).

      • 快点儿走，否则就迟到了。Kuài diǎnr zǒu, fǒuzé jiù chídào le.Hurry up, otherwise we'll be late.
      • 快点儿走，否则要迟到了。Kuài diǎnr zǒu, fǒuzé yào chídào le.Hurry, or we'll be late.
    • Complex sentences 复句Hypothetical clauses 假设复句要是……，就……要是……就: if … then

      要是 A，(subject) 就 B

      要是 is a spoken 如果.

      When to use it

      要是 = if (spoken 如果).

      • 要是你有时间，就来我家吃饭吧。Yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān, jiù lái wǒ jiā chīfàn ba.If you have time, come to my place for dinner.
      • 要是明天有空，我就去看你。Yàoshi míngtiān yǒu kòng, wǒ jiù qù kàn nǐ.If I'm free tomorrow, I'll visit you.
    • Complex sentences 复句Hypothetical clauses 假设复句要是……，（就）……，否则……要是……就……，否则……

      要是 A，就 B，否则 C

      This lays out both outcomes: if A then B, otherwise C.

      When to use it

      要是 A，就 B，否则 C: if A then B, otherwise C.

      • 要是明天天气好，我们就去爬山，否则就在家看电影。Yàoshi míngtiān tiānqì hǎo, wǒmen jiù qù pá shān, fǒuzé jiù zài jiā kàn diànyǐng.If the weather's good tomorrow we'll go hiking, otherwise we'll watch a film at home.
      • 要是你同意，我们就开始，否则就再等等。Yàoshi nǐ tóngyì, wǒmen jiù kāishǐ, fǒuzé jiù zài děngdeng.If you agree, we'll start; otherwise we'll wait a bit.
    • Complex sentences 复句Conditional clauses 条件复句不管……，都/也……不管……都 / 也: no matter

      不管 + question / options，都 / 也 + result

      不管 is followed by something open, like a question word or A还是B, and the result stays the same.

      When to use it

      不管 + question word or options, 都/也 + result: no matter what.

      • 不管天气怎么样，他都去跑步。Bùguǎn tiānqì zěnmeyàng, tā dōu qù pǎobù.Whatever the weather, he goes running.
      • 不管你去不去，我都去。Bùguǎn nǐ qù bu qù, wǒ dōu qù.Whether you go or not, I'm going.
      • 不管多忙，他都每天锻炼。Bùguǎn duō máng, tā dōu měi tiān duànliàn.No matter how busy he is, he exercises every day.

      Compare

      不管 is spoken; 无论 is more formal. Both need a question word or options after them.

      Exam note: Not 不管下雨: it needs an open choice, such as 不管下不下雨.

    • Complex sentences 复句Conditional clauses 条件复句无论……，都/也……无论……都 / 也: no matter

      无论 + question / options，都 / 也 + result

      无论 means the same as 不管 but is more formal.

      When to use it

      无论 = no matter (formal 不管).

      • 无论多难，我都要学好汉语。Wúlùn duō nán, wǒ dōu yào xuéhǎo Hànyǔ.No matter how hard, I'll learn Chinese well.
      • 无论遇到什么困难，我们都不放弃。Wúlùn yù dào shénme kùnnan, wǒmen dōu bú fàngqì.Whatever difficulties we meet, we won't give up.
    • Complex sentences 复句Cause and effect clauses 因果复句既然……，就……既然……就: since

      既然 A，(subject) 就 B

      既然 takes a known fact as the reason for a suggestion or conclusion.

      When to use it

      既然 A，就 B: since A is the case, then B.

      • 既然你不舒服，就回家休息吧。Jìrán nǐ bù shūfu, jiù huí jiā xiūxi ba.Since you don't feel well, go home and rest.
      • 既然来了，就多住几天吧。Jìrán lái le, jiù duō zhù jǐ tiān ba.Since you've come, stay a few more days.
    • Complex sentences 复句Cause and effect clauses 因果复句（由于）……，因此……由于……因此: because … therefore

      (由于) A，因此 B

      A formal cause and result pair.

      When to use it

      由于 A，因此 B: formal cause and result.

      • 由于天气不好，因此比赛推迟了。Yóuyú tiānqì bù hǎo, yīncǐ bǐsài tuīchí le.Because of bad weather, the match was postponed.
      • 由于准备充分，因此比赛很顺利。Yóuyú zhǔnbèi chōngfèn, yīncǐ bǐsài hěn shùnlì.Because we prepared well, the match went smoothly.
    • Complex sentences 复句Purpose clauses 目的复句……，好……好: so that

      A，好 + verb

      好 at the start of the second clause means so that, in order to be able to.

      When to use it

      …，好 + verb = so that it's easy to.

      • 你把电话号码给我，到了我好给你打电话。Nǐ bǎ diànhuà hàomǎ gěi wǒ, dàole wǒ hǎo gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.Give me your number so I can call you when I arrive.
      • 你把电话号码给我，好联系你。Nǐ bǎ diànhuà hàomǎ gěi wǒ, hǎo liánxì nǐ.Give me your number so I can contact you.
    • Complex sentences 复句Concessive clauses 让步复句即使……，也……即使……也: even if

      即使 A，(subject) 也 B

      即使 introduces a supposed situation; the result does not change even so.

      When to use it

      即使 A，也 B: even if A, still B (hypothetical).

      • 即使下雨，我们也要去。Jíshǐ xià yǔ, wǒmen yě yào qù.Even if it rains, we're going.
      • 即使下雨，比赛也照常进行。Jíshǐ xià yǔ, bǐsài yě zhàocháng jìnxíng.Even if it rains, the match will go ahead.

      Compare

      即使 = even if (hypothetical); 尽管 = even though (fact).

    • Complex sentences 复句Concessive clauses 让步复句就是……，也……就是……也: even if

      就是 A，(subject) 也 B

      A spoken form of 即使……也.

      When to use it

      就是 A，也 B = 即使…也…, spoken.

      • 就是你不说，我也知道。Jiùshì nǐ bù shuō, wǒ yě zhīdào.Even if you don't tell me, I know.
      • 就是再忙，也要吃饭。Jiùshì zài máng, yě yào chī fàn.However busy you are, you still need to eat.
    • Complex sentences 复句Contracted complex sentences 紧缩复句无标记Clauses joined without linking words

      A，B (no linking word)

      Chinese often joins two short clauses with no word at all; the meaning (if, so, when) comes from context: 不去不行 it won't do not to go, 有问题问我 if there's a problem, ask me.

      When to use it

      Chinese often links clauses by meaning alone; work out the link from context.

      • 有问题问我。Yǒu wèntí wèn wǒ.If you have questions, ask me.
      • 你不去我去。Nǐ bú qù wǒ qù.If you don't go, I will.
      • 你不去，我去。Nǐ bú qù, wǒ qù.If you won't go, I will.

      不 + A + 也 + B

      A short pattern: even if not A, still B: 不说也知道 I know without being told.

      When to use it

      不 A 也 B: even without A, B happens.

      • 你不说我也知道。Nǐ bù shuō wǒ yě zhīdào.I know without you saying.
      • 这么近，不坐车也能到。Zhème jìn, bú zuò chē yě néng dào.It's so close, you can get there without a car.
      • 你不说我也知道。Nǐ bù shuō wǒ yě zhīdào.I know even if you don't tell me.

      来 after a round number means about, a little under or over: 十来个人 ten or so people.

      When to use it

      Round number + 来 + MW = about, a bit over or under.

      大约 + number · number + 左右 · time + 前后

      大约 before a number. 左右 after a number or amount. 前后 after a point in time, especially festivals: 春节前后.

      When to use it

      大约 before the number; 左右 after it; 前后 after a time point.

    • Expressing numbers 数的表示法小数、分数、百分数、倍数的表示法Decimals, fractions, percentages, multiples

      三点五 · 三分之一 · 百分之二十 · 两倍

      点 is the decimal point: 三点五 3.5. Fractions say the bottom first: 三分之一 one third. Percentages: 百分之二十 20%. 倍 times: 两倍 twice as much.

      When to use it

      Decimals 点 (三点五), fractions 分之 (三分之一 = 1/3), percent 百分之 (百分之二十), multiples 倍 (两倍).

      • 班里百分之八十的学生通过了考试。Bān li bǎi fēn zhī bāshí de xuésheng tōngguòle kǎoshì.Eighty percent of the class passed the exam.
      • 今年的人数是去年的两倍。Jīnnián de rénshù shì qùnián de liǎng bèi.This year's number is twice last year's.
      • 全班百分之八十的学生通过了考试。Quán bān bǎi fēn zhī bāshí de xuésheng tōngguò le kǎoshì.Eighty percent of the class passed the exam.

      Compare

      Fractions read bottom first: 三分之一 = one third.
