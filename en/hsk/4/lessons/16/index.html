  • 态度tài duattitudeMeasure word: 个Traditional: 態度
    Dictionary sensesmanner; bearing; approach
    • 我对他的态度变了。wǒ duì tā de tài du biàn leMy attitude towards him changed.
    • 试着用积极的态度去面对任何事情。shì zhe yòng jī jí de tài du qù miàn duì rèn hé shì qingTry to have a positive attitude about everything.
  • 周围zhōu wéiaround; surroundingsTraditional: 周圍
    Dictionary sensesenvirons; periphery
    • 我看了看周围。wǒ kàn le kàn zhōu wéiI looked around.
  • 小组xiǎo zǔgroupTraditional: 小組
  • 复杂fù zácomplicatedTraditional: 複雜
    Dictionary sensescomplex
    • 它有多复杂？tā yǒu duō fù záHow complex is it?
    • 事情总比我们想的要复杂得多。shì qing zǒng bǐ wǒ men xiǎng de yào fù zá de duōThings are always more complex than we think.
  • 幅fúmeasure word for paintings
    Dictionary senseswidth; roll; classifier for textiles or pictures
    • 哪幅画小？nǎ fú huà xiǎoWhich painting is small?
    • 这幅画是谁画的？zhè fú huà shì shéi huà deWho was this picture painted by?
  • 数字shù zìnumber; digitMeasure word: 个Traditional: 數字
    Dictionary sensesnumeral; figure; amount
  • 压yāto pressTraditional: 壓
    Dictionary sensesto push down; to keep under (control); pressure
  • 邀请yāo qǐngto inviteMeasure word: 个Traditional: 邀請
    Dictionary sensesinvitation
    • 感谢您的邀请。gǎn xiè nín de yāo qǐngThank you for your invitation.
    • 他邀请我去他家。tā yāo qǐng wǒ qù tā jiāHe invited me to his house.
  • 加上jiā shàngplus; in addition
    Dictionary sensesto put in; to add; to add on; to add into
    • 请在名单上加上我的名字。qǐng zài míng dān shàng jiā shàng wǒ de míng ziPlease add my name to the list.
  • 队员duì yuánteam memberTraditional: 隊員
  • 交通jiāo tōngtraffic; transport
    Dictionary sensesto be connected; transportation; communications; liaison
    • 这地方交通不方便。zhè dì fang jiāo tōng bù fāng biànThis place isn't convenient to public transportation.
    • 这个城市的交通方便得很。zhè ge chéng shì de jiāo tōng fāng biàn de hěnThe transportation in this city is very convenient.
  • 优秀yōu xiùexcellentTraditional: 優秀
    Dictionary sensesoutstanding
    • 他是一个优秀的作家。tā shì yī gè yōu xiù de zuò jiāHe's an excellent author.
  • 亮liàngbright; to shine
    Dictionary senseslight; to flash; loud and clear
    • 灯亮着。dēng liàng zheThe light is on.
    • 她照亮你的世界。tā zhào liàng nǐ de shì jièShe sets your world alight.
  • 篇piānmeasure word for articles
    Dictionary sensessheet; piece of writing; bound set of bamboo slips used for record keeping (old); classifier for written items: chapter, article
    • 这篇文章很好。zhè piān wén zhāng hěn hǎoThis article is very good.
    • 你有没有看过这篇文章？nǐ yǒu méi yǒu kàn guò zhè piān wén zhāngHave you read this article?
  • 儿童ér tóngchildTraditional: 兒童
  • 出口chū kǒuexit; to exportMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesan exit; to speak; (of a ship) to leave port
  • 既jìboth … and; since
    Dictionary sensesalready; both... (and...)
    • 我既紧张又害怕。wǒ jì jǐn zhāng yòu hài pàI'm nervous and scared.
    • 既要马好，又要马不吃草。jì yào mǎ hǎo yòu yào mǎ bù chī cǎoYou can't have it both ways.
  • 味道wèi daotaste; flavour
    Dictionary sensesflavor; (fig.) feeling (of ...); sense (of ...); hint (of ...)
    • 尝尝成功的味道。cháng cháng chéng gōng de wèi daoTaste the flavour of success.
    • 我喜欢西瓜的味道。wǒ xǐ huan xī guā de wèi daoI love the taste of watermelon.
  • 千万qiān wànbe sure to; ten millionTraditional: 千萬
    Dictionary sensescountless; many; one must by all means
    • 千万不要！qiān wàn bú yàoPlease don't!
    • 千万别误会我的意思。qiān wàn bié wù huì wǒ de yì siPlease don't get me wrong.
  • 距离jù lídistanceMeasure word: 个Traditional: 距離
    Dictionary sensesto be apart from
    • 两人之间的距离已拉近。liǎng rén zhī jiān de jù lí yǐ lā jìnThe gap between them has narrowed.
  • 轻松qīng sōngrelaxed; easyTraditional: 輕鬆
    Dictionary senseslight; gentle; effortless; uncomplicated
    • 让我们放轻松吧。ràng wǒ men fàng qīng sōng baLet's take it easy.
    • 你工作得太辛苦了。放轻松一下。nǐ gōng zuò de tài xīn kǔ le fàng qīng sōng yí xiàYou are working too hard. Take it easy for a while.
  • 毕业bì yèto graduateTraditional: 畢業
    Dictionary sensesgraduation; to finish school
    • 我毕业快两年了。wǒ bì yè kuài liǎng nián leI graduated almost two years ago.
    • 我去年中学毕业。wǒ qù nián zhōng xué bì yèI finished high school last year.
  • 怀疑huái yíto doubt; to suspectTraditional: 懷疑
    Dictionary sensesto doubt (sth); to be skeptical of; to have one's doubts; to harbor suspicions
    • 别怀疑他的话。bié huái yí tā de huàDon't doubt his words.
    • 我怀疑他是否会来。wǒ huái yí tā shì fǒu huì láiI am doubtful whether he will come.
  • 食物shí wùfood
    • 它是食物。tā shì shí wùIt's food.
    • 请自带食物。qǐng zì dài shí wùPlease bring your own food.
  • 垃圾lā jīrubbish
    Dictionary sensestrash; refuse; garbage; (coll.) of poor quality
    • 别忘了把垃圾拿出去。bié wàng le bǎ lā jī ná chū quDon't forget to take out the garbage.
    • 在街上乱丢垃圾不是好习惯，记得吗？zài jiē shàng luàn diū lā jī bù shì hǎo xí guàn jì de maThrowing garbage on the street is not a good habit, remember?
