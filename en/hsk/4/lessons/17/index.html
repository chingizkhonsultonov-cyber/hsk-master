  • 修xiūto repair; to build
    Dictionary sensesto decorate; to embellish; to write
    • 我在修房子。wǒ zài xiū fáng ziI'm repairing the house.
    • 我在修洗衣机。wǒ zài xiū xǐ yī jīI'm fixing the washing machine.
  • 价格jià gépriceMeasure word: 个Traditional: 價格
  • 戴dàito wear (glasses, hat)
    Dictionary sensesto put on or wear (glasses, hat, gloves etc); to respect; to bear; to support
    • 他戴眼镜。tā dài yǎn jìngHe wears glasses.
    • 把眼镜戴上！bǎ yǎn jìng dài shàngPut your glasses on.
  • 大量dà liànga large amount
    Dictionary sensesgreat amount; large quantity; bulk; numerous
  • 究竟jiū jìngafter all; on earth
    Dictionary sensesto go to the bottom of a matter; when all is said and done; (in an interrogative sentence) finally; outcome
  • 轻qīnglight; gentleTraditional: 輕
    Dictionary senseseasy; soft; reckless
    • 狗轻轻地叫了。gǒu qīng qīng de jiào leThe dog barked softly.
    • 轻一点还是重一点？qīng yī diǎn hái shi zhòng yī diǎnIs it a bit lighter or a bit heavier?
  • 信号xìn hàosignalTraditional: 信號
    • 收到一个信号。shōu dào yī gè xìn hàoCatch a signal.
    • 他给了我们信号让我们开始。tā gěi le wǒ men xìn hào ràng wǒ men kāi shǐHe gave us the signal to begin.
  • 然而rán érhowever
    Dictionary sensesbut; yet
    • 我很累，然而，我必须完成我的回家作业。wǒ hěn lèi rán ér wǒ bì xū wán chéng wǒ de huí jiā zuò yèI am tired; nevertheless, I must finish my homework.
  • 赢得yíng déto winTraditional: 贏得
    Dictionary sensesto gain
    • 她赢得一切了。tā yíng dé yī qiè leShe won everything.
    • 我还要赢得更多。wǒ hái yào yíng dé gèng duōI still need to win more.
  • 交流jiāo liúto communicate; exchange
    Dictionary sensesto exchange; communication; interaction; to have social contact (with sb)
  • 教练jiào liàncoachTraditional: 教練
    Dictionary sensesto coach; to train; instructor; sports coach; trainer (CL:個|个[ge4],位[wei4],名[ming2])
    • 这位女士是我的游泳教练吗？zhè wèi nǚ shì shì wǒ de yóu yǒng jiào liàn maIs this woman my swim teacher?
    • 球迷们都希望球队能换个教练。qiú mí men dōu xī wàng qiú duì néng huàn gè jiào liànThe fans all hope the club changes the manager.
  • 兴奋xīng fènexcitedTraditional: 興奮
    Dictionary sensesexcitement; (physiology) excitation
    • 他们非常兴奋。tā men fēi cháng xīng fènThey were very excited.
    • 他有点兴奋了。tā yǒu diǎn xīng fèn leHe was somewhat excited.
  • 付fùto pay
    Dictionary sensesto hand over to; classifier for pairs or sets of things
    • 我们怎么付钱？wǒ men zěn me fù qiánHow are we going to pay for that?
    • 她坚持让我付钱。tā jiān chí ràng wǒ fù qiánShe insisted on my paying the bill.
  • 全球quán qiúglobal; worldwide
    Dictionary sensesthe whole world
  • 困kùnsleepy
    Dictionary sensesto trap; to surround; hard-pressed; stranded
    • 你困吗？nǐ kùn maAre you tired?
    • 她不困。tā bù kùnShe's not sleepy.
  • 符合fú héto conform to
    Dictionary sensesin accordance with; to agree with; to meet (a requirement); (physics) coincidence
    • 我不认识符合条件的人。wǒ bù rèn shi fú hé tiáo jiàn de rénI don't know anyone who meets that description.
  • 吹chuīto blow
    Dictionary sensesto play a wind instrument; to blast; to puff; to boast
    • 树被风吹倒了。shù bèi fēng chuī dào leThe tree was blown down.
  • 取消qǔ xiāoto cancel
    Dictionary sensesto call off; to revoke; to rescind
    • 这场比赛将可能被取消。zhè chǎng bǐ sài jiāng kě néng bèi qǔ xiāoThe game will probably be called off.
    • 我们的航班被取消了。wǒ men de háng bān bèi qǔ xiāo leOur flight was canceled.
  • 躺tǎngto lie down
    Dictionary sensesto recline
    • 躺在你右边。tǎng zài nǐ yòu bianLie on your right side.
    • 他躺在床上。tā tǎng zài chuáng shàngHe stayed in bed.
  • 答案dá ànanswerMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensessolution
    • 那就是答案。nà jiù shì dá ànThat's the answer.
    • 你知道答案吗？nǐ zhī dào dá àn maDo you know the answer?
  • 皮肤pí fūskinMeasure word: 层、块Traditional: 皮膚
    • 你的皮肤真好。nǐ de pí fū zhēn hǎoYou have nice skin.
  • 浪费làng fèito wasteTraditional: 浪費
    Dictionary sensesto squander
    • 一刻也不浪费。yī kè yě bù làng fèiThere's not a moment to waste.
    • 你在浪费我们时间。nǐ zài làng fèi wǒ men shí jiānYou're wasting our time.
  • 厉害lì haiamazing; severeTraditional: 厲害
    Dictionary senses(used to describe sb or sth that makes a very strong impression, whether favorable or unfavorable) terrible; intense; devastating
    • 我头疼得厉害。wǒ tóu téng de lì haiI have a bad headache.
    • 她牙疼得厉害。tā yá téng de lì haiHer teeth hurt a lot.
  • 尊重zūn zhòngto respect
    Dictionary sensesto esteem; to honor; to value; eminent
    • 他总是很尊重妻子的意见。tā zǒng shì hěn zūn zhòng qī zi de yì jiànHe is always very respectful of his wife's opinions.
    • 我的父母告诉我，必须尊重老年人。wǒ de fù mǔ gào su wǒ bì xū zūn zhòng lǎo nián rénMy parents told me that I need to respect old people.
  • 激动jī dòngexcited; movedTraditional: 激動
    Dictionary sensesto move emotionally; to stir up (emotions); to excite
    • 他越激动就越安静。tā yuè jī dòng jiù yuè ān jìngThe more excited he gets, the quieter he becomes.
