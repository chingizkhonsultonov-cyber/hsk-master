  • 由于yóu yúdue toTraditional: 由於
    Dictionary sensesas a result of; thanks to; owing to; since
    • 由于错过了火车，我不得不等下一班等一小时。yóu yú cuò guò le huǒ chē wǒ bù dé bù děng xià yī bān děng yī xiǎo shíHaving missed the train, I had to wait about an hour for the next one.
  • 母亲mǔ qīnmotherMeasure word: 个Traditional: 母親
    Dictionary sensesalso pr. [mu3 qin5]
    • 母亲节快乐！mǔ qīn jié kuài lèHappy Mother's Day!
    • 她比母亲高。tā bǐ mǔ qīn gāoShe's taller than her mother.
  • 再次zài cìonce again
    Dictionary sensesonce more
    • 他再次被选为市长。tā zài cì bèi xuǎn wèi shì chángHe was re-elected mayor.
    • 我希望再次见到你。wǒ xī wàng zài cì jiàn dào nǐI want to see you again.
  • 部门bù méndepartmentMeasure word: 个Traditional: 部門
    Dictionary sensesbranch; section; division
    • 谁负责这个部门？shéi fù zé zhè ge bù ménWho's in charge of this section?
  • 放弃fàng qìto give upTraditional: 放棄
    Dictionary sensesto renounce; to abandon
    • 放弃吧。fàng qì baForget it.
    • 我放弃。wǒ fàng qìI resign.
  • 意见yì jiànopinion; objectionMeasure word: 点、条Traditional: 意見
    Dictionary sensesidea; suggestion; complaint
    • 我无法同意你的意见。wǒ wú fǎ tóng yì nǐ de yì jiànI can't agree with you.
    • 我的意见才是最重要的！wǒ de yì jiàn cái shì zuì zhòng yào deMy opinion is the one that counts!
  • 过程guò chéngprocessMeasure word: 个Traditional: 過程
    Dictionary sensescourse of events
    • 在这过程中，我回答了好几个问题。zài zhè guò chéng zhōng wǒ huí dá le hǎo jǐ gè wèn tíDuring this process, I answered quite a few questions.
    • 在我们讨论问题的过程中，我想到了一个好主意。zài wǒ men tǎo lùn wèn tí de guò chéng zhōng wǒ xiǎng dào le yī gè hǎo zhǔ yiWhile we were discussing the problem, I hit upon a good idea.
  • 即使jí shǐeven if
    Dictionary senseseven though
    • 即使我穿得很多仍然是很冷。jí shǐ wǒ chuān de hěn duō réng rán shì hěn lěngEven though I'm wearing a lot of clothing, it is still cold.
    • 今天即使再忙，也要做完这些事情才走。jīn tiān jí shǐ zài máng yě yào zuò wán zhè xiē shì qing cái zǒuEven though it's busy today, this bit of work must be finished before leaving.
  • 尽管jǐn guǎnalthoughTraditional: 儘管
    Dictionary sensesdespite; even though; in spite of; unhesitatingly
    • 尽管他有那么多钱，他并不快乐。jǐn guǎn tā yǒu nà me duō qián tā bìng bù kuài lèEven with all his money, he's unhappy.
  • 深shēndeepTraditional: 㴱
    Dictionary sensesdeep (lit. and fig.)
    • 他被那个故事深深地感动了。tā bèi nà ge gù shi shēn shēn de gǎn dòng leHe was deeply moved by the story.
    • 他的故事给我留下了很深的印象。tā de gù shi gěi wǒ liú xià le hěn shēn de yìn xiàngI was much impressed by his story.
  • 各gèeach; every
  • 仍然réng ránstill
    Dictionary sensesas before; yet
    • 他仍然还在教室里学习。tā réng rán hái zài jiào shì lǐ xué xíHe is still studying in the classroom.
    • 我们仍然不知道为什么。wǒ men réng rán bù zhī dào wèi shén meWe still don't know why.
  • 秒miǎosecond (of time)
    Dictionary sensessecond (unit of time); arc second (angular measurement unit); (coll.) instantly
    • 一分钟有六十秒。yī fēn zhōng yǒu liù shí miǎoA minute has sixty seconds.
  • 值得zhí deto be worth
    Dictionary sensesworth the money; to merit; to deserve; to be worth (doing sth)
    • 它值得一试。tā zhí de yī shìIt's worth a go.
    • 不值得去记。bù zhí de qù jìIt's not worth remembering.
  • 经历jīng lìto experience; experienceTraditional: 經歷
    Dictionary sensesto go through; experience (CL:段[duan4],次[ci4])
  • 讨论tǎo lùnto discussTraditional: 討論
    Dictionary sensesto talk over
    • 讨论讨论吧。tǎo lùn tǎo lùn baLet's discuss it.
    • 我们结束了讨论。wǒ men jié shù le tǎo lùnWe closed the discussion.
  • 超过chāo guòto exceed; to overtakeTraditional: 超過
    Dictionary sensesto surpass; to outstrip
    • 我的书不超过十本。wǒ de shū bù chāo guò shí běnI have not more than ten books.
    • 她的成绩超过我了。tā de chéng jì chāo guò wǒ leShe surpassed my grades.
  • 仍réngstill
    Dictionary sensesyet; to remain; (literary) frequently; often
  • 假jiǎfalse; fake
    Dictionary sensesartificial; to borrow; if
    • 原来都是假的。yuán lái dōu shì jiǎ deSo it was all a lie.
    • 这是我奶奶的假牙。zhè shì wǒ nǎi nai de jiǎ yáIt's my grandmother's set of false teeth.
  • 加入jiā rùto join
    Dictionary sensesto become a member; to mix into; to participate in; to add in
    • 来加入我们吧。lái jiā rù wǒ men baJoin us.
    • 谁都可以加入。shéi dōu kě yǐ jiā rùAnyone can join.
  • 兄弟xiōng dìbrothersMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesyounger brother; I, me (humble term used by men in public speech); brotherly; fraternal
    • 他是我兄弟。tā shì wǒ xiōng dìHe is my brother.
    • 你的兄弟多高？nǐ de xiōng dì duō gāoHow tall is your brother?
  • 地址dì zhǐaddressMeasure word: 个
    • 你有地址吗？nǐ yǒu dì zhǐ maDo you have an address?
    • 我给你写下了地址。wǒ gěi nǐ xiě xià le dì zhǐI wrote down the address for you.
  • 无论wú lùnno matterTraditional: 無論
    Dictionary sensesno matter what or how; regardless of whether...
  • 改gǎito change; to correct
    Dictionary sensesto alter; to transform
    • 习惯难改。xí guàn nán gǎiHabits are hard to break.
    • 如果他们知道会发生那样的事，一定会改用别的计划的。rú guǒ tā men zhī dào huì fā shēng nà yàng de shì yī dìng huì gǎi yòng bié de jì huà deHad they known what was about to happen, they would've changed their plans.
  • 紧张jǐn zhāngnervous; tenseMeasure word: 阵Traditional: 緊張
    Dictionary senseskeyed up; intense; strained
    • 别紧张。bié jǐn zhāngTake it easy.
    • 我也紧张。wǒ yě jǐn zhāngI was nervous, too.
