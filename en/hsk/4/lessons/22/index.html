  • 公里gōng lǐkilometre
    Dictionary senseskilometer
    • 我们跑了三公里。wǒ men pǎo le sān gōng lǐWe ran three kilometers.
  • 工资gōng zīwages; salaryMeasure word: 份Traditional: 工資
    Dictionary sensespay
    • 您想要多少工资？nín xiǎng yào duō shao gōng zīHow much do you want in terms of salary?
    • 教师的工资比律师低。jiào shī de gōng zī bǐ lǜ shī dīA teacher's salary is lower than a lawyer's.
  • 好处hǎo chubenefit; advantageTraditional: 好處
    Dictionary sensesmerit; gain; profit
    • 这对谁有好处？zhè duì shéi yǒu hǎo chuWho benefits?
    • 会用电脑是一个好处。huì yòng diàn nǎo shì yī gè hǎo chuBeing able to use a computer is advantageous.
  • 庆祝qìng zhùto celebrateTraditional: 慶祝
  • 伤心shāng xīnsad; heartbrokenTraditional: 傷心
    Dictionary sensesto grieve; to be broken-hearted; to feel deeply hurt
    • 别伤心。bié shāng xīnDon't be sad.
    • 她看上去很伤心。tā kàn shàng qu hěn shāng xīnShe looked sad.
  • 眼前yǎn qiánbefore one's eyes; at present
    Dictionary sensesnow
  • 区别qū biédifferenceMeasure word: 个Traditional: 區別
    Dictionary sensesto distinguish; to discriminate; to make a distinction
    • 这没有任何区别。zhè méi yǒu rèn hé qū biéThat makes no difference.
    • 你们很容易就能看出区别。nǐ men hěn róng yì jiù néng kàn chū qū biéYou'll be able to see the difference very easily.
  • 家长jiā zhǎngparentTraditional: 家長
    Dictionary senseshead of a household; family head; patriarch; parent or guardian of a child
    • 老师请了我的家长。lǎo shī qǐng le wǒ de jiā zhǎngThe teacher requested to see my parents.
  • 将来jiāng láithe futureMeasure word: 个Traditional: 將來
    Dictionary sensesin the future; future
    • 请你将来注意点。qǐng nǐ jiāng lái zhù yì diǎnPlease take more care in the future.
    • 将来我要去国外旅游。jiāng lái wǒ yào qù guó wài lǚ yóuI want to go abroad one day.
  • 自信zì xìnconfident
    Dictionary sensesto have confidence in oneself; self-confidence
    • 他对自己很有自信。tā duì zì jǐ hěn yǒu zì xìnHe has great confidence in himself.
    • 哪来那么大的自信啊？nǎ lái nà me dà de zì xìn aWhere does all that confidence come from?
  • 小说xiǎo shuōnovelMeasure word: 本、部Traditional: 小說
    Dictionary sensesfiction
    • 我爱看小说。wǒ ài kàn xiǎo shuōI love reading novels.
    • 这部小说很难读懂。zhè bù xiǎo shuō hěn nán dú dǒngThis novel is difficult to understand.
  • 稍shāoslightly
    Dictionary sensessomewhat; a little
    • 请稍等。qǐng shāo děngPlease wait.
    • 请稍等一下。qǐng shāo děng yí xiàPlease wait a bit.
  • 教师jiào shīteacherMeasure word: 个Traditional: 教師
    • 他是教师。tā shì jiào shīHe's a teacher.
    • 他是个教师。tā shì gè jiào shīHe's a teacher.
  • 鼓励gǔ lìto encourageTraditional: 鼓勵
    • 没有人鼓励他。méi yǒu rén gǔ lì tāNobody encouraged him.
    • 没有人鼓励她。méi yǒu rén gǔ lì tāNobody encouraged her.
  • 工厂gōng chǎngfactoryMeasure word: 家、座Traditional: 工廠
    • 这座村里没有工厂。zhè zuò cūn lǐ méi yǒu gōng chǎngThere is no factory in this village.
    • 下周工厂可能会关。xià zhōu gōng chǎng kě néng huì guānPossibly the factory will be closed down next week.
  • 理想lǐ xiǎngideal; dream
    Dictionary sensesan ideal; a dream; perfect
  • 指出zhǐ chūto point out
    Dictionary sensesto indicate
  • 后悔hòu huǐto regretTraditional: 後悔
    Dictionary sensesto feel remorse
    • 我后悔当老师。wǒ hòu huǐ dāng lǎo shīI regret becoming a teacher.
    • 我后悔告诉了你。wǒ hòu huǐ gào su le nǐI regret telling you.
  • 举办jǔ bànto hold (an event)Traditional: 舉辦
    Dictionary sensesto conduct
  • 感动gǎn dòngto move (emotionally)Traditional: 感動
    Dictionary sensesto move (sb); to touch (sb emotionally); moving
    • 我不想被他感动。wǒ bù xiǎng bèi tā gǎn dòngI don't want him to touch me.
    • 我们感动到哭了。wǒ men gǎn dòng dào kū leWe were moved to tears.
  • 提到tí dàoto mention
    Dictionary sensesto raise (a subject); to refer to
  • 不如bù rúnot as good as; it would be better to
    Dictionary sensesnot equal to; inferior to
    • 不如一起去看电影吧。bù rú yì qǐ qù kàn diàn yǐng baWould you like to go see a movie with me?
    • 对我来说，质量不如数量。duì wǒ lái shuō zhì liàng bù rú shù liàngI put quantity after quality.
  • 顾客gù kècustomerMeasure word: 位Traditional: 顧客
    Dictionary sensesclient
    • 顾客永远是正确的吗？gù kè yǒng yuǎn shì zhèng què de maIs the customer always right?
  • 流行liú xíngpopular; to be in fashion
    Dictionary senses(of a contagious disease etc) to spread; to propagate; (of a style of clothing, song etc) popular; fashionable
    • 这不是很流行。zhè bù shì hěn liú xíngThis is not very stylish.
    • 网球在学生中非常流行。wǎng qiú zài xué sheng zhōng fēi cháng liú xíngTennis is very popular among students.
  • 支付zhī fùto pay
    Dictionary sensesto pay (money)
    • 我想用信用卡支付。wǒ xiǎng yòng xìn yòng kǎ zhī fùI'd like to pay by credit card.
