  • 当时dāng shíat that timeTraditional: 當時
    Dictionary sensesthen; while
    • 我当时是学生。wǒ dāng shí shì xué shengI was a student at that time.
    • 我们当时很累。wǒ men dāng shí hěn lèiWe were very tired.
  • 转zhuǎnto turn; to transferTraditional: 轉
    Dictionary sensesto change direction; to forward (mail); (Internet) to share (sb else's content)
    • 向左转。xiàng zuǒ zhuǎnTurn left.
    • 向右转。xiàng yòu zhuǎnTurn right.
  • 调查diào cháto investigate; surveyMeasure word: 项、个Traditional: 調查
    Dictionary sensesinvestigation; inquiry; to survey
    • 我们一定会调查它。wǒ men yī dìng huì diào chá tāWe'll be sure to look into it.
    • 他们正在调查这件事。tā men zhèng zài diào chá zhè jiàn shìThey are inquiring into the matter.
  • 部分bù fenpart
    Dictionary sensesportion; piece; Taiwan pr. [bu4fen4]
    • 大部分是年轻人。dà bù fen shì nián qīng rénThey were for the most part young people.
    • 那是我最喜欢的部分。nà shì wǒ zuì xǐ huan de bù fenThat's my favorite part.
  • 方式fāng shìway; mannerMeasure word: 种
    Dictionary sensesmethod; mode; pattern (of behavior etc)
    • 我喜欢你说话的方式。wǒ xǐ huan nǐ shuō huà de fāng shìI like the way you talk.
    • 她不喜欢我说话的方式。tā bù xǐ huan wǒ shuō huà de fāng shìShe doesn't like the way I speak.
  • 强qiángstrongTraditional: 強
    Dictionary sensespowerful; better; slightly more than; vigorous
    • 他们挺进了八强。tā men tǐng jìn le bā qiángThey advanced to the quarter-finals.
  • 能力néng lìabilityMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensescapability
    • 他没有能力买新车。tā méi yǒu néng lì mǎi xīn chēHe can't afford to buy a new car.
    • 你有能力去做这个吗？nǐ yǒu néng lì qù zuò zhè ge maWere you able to do it?
  • 保护bǎo hùto protectMeasure word: 种Traditional: 保護
    Dictionary sensesto defend; to safeguard; protection
    • 保护我！bǎo hù wǒProtect me!
    • 只有你能保护他们。zhǐ yǒu nǐ néng bǎo hù tā menYou're the only one who can protect them.
  • 另lìngother; another
    Dictionary sensesseparate; separately
    • 那是另一回事。nà shì lìng yī huí shìThat's another matter.
    • 他到另一边去了。tā dào lìng yī biān qù leHe went over to the other side.
  • 真正zhēn zhèngreal; true
    Dictionary sensesgenuine; really; indeed
    • 你是个真正的朋友。nǐ shì gè zhēn zhèng de péng youYou're a real friend.
    • 她是一位真正的美人。tā shì yī wèi zhēn zhèng de měi rénShe's a real beauty.
  • 管guǎnto manage; to care aboutTraditional: 筦
    Dictionary sensesto take care (of); to control; to be in charge of; to look after
    • 管他呢！guǎn tā neWhatever!
    • 不要管我。bú yào guǎn wǒDon't mind me.
  • 间jiānbetween; room; measure word for roomsTraditional: 間
    Dictionary sensesamong; within a definite time or space; section of a room or lateral space between two pairs of pillars
  • 支持zhī chíto supportMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesto be in favor of; to back; support; backing
    • 我支持你。wǒ zhī chí nǐI'm on your side.
    • 他总是支持她。tā zǒng shì zhī chí tāHe always takes sides with her.
  • 掉diàoto fall; to drop
    Dictionary sensesto lag behind; to lose; to go missing
    • 把它吃掉。bǎ tā chī diàoEat it.
    • 把她干掉了。bǎ tā gàn diào leGet rid of her.
  • 改变gǎi biànto changeTraditional: 改變
    Dictionary sensesto alter; to transform
    • 什么都改变了。shén me dōu gǎi biàn leEverything changed.
    • 进球改变比赛。jìn qiú gǎi biàn bǐ sàiGoals change games.
  • 是否shì fǒuwhether
    Dictionary senseswhether (or not); if; is or isn't
    • 他问我是否很忙？tā wèn wǒ shì fǒu hěn mángHe asked me if I was busy.
    • 谁也不知道他是否爱她。shéi yě bù zhī dào tā shì fǒu ài tāNo one knows if he loves her or not.
  • 活huóto live; alive
    Dictionary sensesliving; work; workmanship
    • 干活慢点。gàn huó màn diǎnWork slowly.
    • 她活了很久。tā huó le hěn jiǔShe lived a long life.
  • 生shēngto give birth; to grow
    Dictionary sensesto be born; life; raw
    • 我是个国中生。wǒ shì gè guó zhōng shēngI'm a junior high school student.
    • 你是高中生吗？nǐ shì gāo zhōng shēng maAre you a high school student?
  • 生shēngraw; unfamiliar
    Dictionary sensesto be born; to give birth; life; to grow
  • 片piànslice; measure word for flat pieces
    Dictionary sensesthin piece; flake; a slice; film
    • 她喜欢拍照片。tā xǐ huan pāi zhào piànShe likes taking pictures.
    • 你不喜欢生鱼片，是吗？nǐ bù xǐ huan shēng yú piàn shì maYou don't like sashimi, do you?
  • 同时tóng shíat the same timeTraditional: 同時
    Dictionary sensessimultaneously
    • 大家别同时说。dà jiā bié tóng shí shuōDon't all speak at once.
  • 自zìfrom; self
    Dictionary sensesoneself; since; naturally; as a matter of course
    • 好自大啊！hǎo zì dà aHow arrogant!
    • 请自带食物。qǐng zì dài shí wùPlease bring your own food.
  • 拍pāito clap; to take (a photo)
    Dictionary sensesto pat; to slap; to swat
    • 我拍手。wǒ pāi shǒuI clapped.
    • 我不拍手他们。wǒ bù pāi shǒu tā menI am not applauding them.
  • 整个zhěng gèwholeTraditional: 整個
    Dictionary sensesentire; total
    • 你整个周末会做什么？nǐ zhěng gè zhōu mò huì zuò shén meWhat'll you be doing over the weekend?
    • 他整个上午都在看书。tā zhěng gè shàng wǔ dōu zài kàn shūHe read a book all morning.
  • 组zǔgroup; to formTraditional: 組
    Dictionary sensesto organize; team; classifier for sets, series, groups of people, batteries
    • 你想加入哪一组？nǐ xiǎng jiā rù nǎ yī zǔWhich group do you want to join?
