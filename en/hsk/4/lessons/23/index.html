    • 赶快回家。gǎn kuài huí jiāHurry home.
    • 您要赶快些。nín yào gǎn kuài xiēYou have to hurry.
  • 整理zhěng lǐto tidy up
    Dictionary sensesto arrange; to sort out; to straighten out; to list systematically
  • 丰富fēng fùrich; abundantTraditional: 豐富
    Dictionary sensesto enrich; plentiful
    • 他经验丰富。tā jīng yàn fēng fùHe has ample experience.
  • 答dáto answer
    Dictionary sensesto reply; to respond
    • 我不知道该怎么答他。wǒ bù zhī dào gāi zěn me dá tāI didn't know how to answer his question.
    • 你本来能答上那个问题。nǐ běn lái néng dá shàng nà ge wèn tíYou could've answered that question.
  • 熊xióngbear
    Dictionary senses(coll.) to scold; to rebuke; (coll.) weak; incapable
    • 熊会爬树。xióng huì pá shùBears can climb trees.
    • 你是熊猫。nǐ shì xióng māoYou're a panda.
  • 原谅yuán liàngto forgiveTraditional: 原諒
    Dictionary sensesto excuse; to pardon
    • 原谅她。yuán liàng tāForgive her.
    • 请您原谅。qǐng nín yuán liàngPlease forgive me.
  • 肚子dù zibelly; stomachMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesabdomen
    • 我肚子疼。wǒ dù zi téngMy tummy hurts.
    • 你肚子饿吗？nǐ dù zi è maAre you hungry?
  • 大赛dà sàibig competitionTraditional: 大賽
    Dictionary sensesgrand contest
  • 辣làspicyTraditional: 辢
    Dictionary senseshot (spicy); pungent; (of chili pepper, raw onions etc) to sting; to burn
    • 不是很辣。bù shì hěn làIt's not very spicy.
    • 我不吃辣。wǒ bù chī làI don't eat spicy food.
  • 作者zuò zhěauthor
    Dictionary senseswriter
    • 这本小说的作者是谁？zhè běn xiǎo shuō de zuò zhě shì shéiWho's the author of this story?
    • 那本小说的作者是谁呢？nà běn xiǎo shuō de zuò zhě shì shéi neWho's the author of the novel?
  • 质量zhì liàngqualityTraditional: 質量
    Dictionary senses(physics) mass; quality (i.e. degree of excellence); quality and quantity
    • 对我来说，质量不如数量。duì wǒ lái shuō zhì liàng bù rú shù liàngI put quantity after quality.
  • 到来dào láiarrival; to arriveTraditional: 到來
    Dictionary sensesadvent
    • 谢谢你的到来。xiè xie nǐ de dào láiThanks for arriving.
    • 他感谢我的到来。tā gǎn xiè wǒ de dào láiHe thanked me for coming.
  • 愉快yú kuàihappy; pleasant
    Dictionary sensescheerful; delighted
    • 周末愉快。zhōu mò yú kuàiHave a good weekend.
    • 我和她聊得很愉快。wǒ hé tā liáo de hěn yú kuàiI had a nice chat with her.
  • 寄jìto send (by post)
    Dictionary sensesto entrust; to place in sb's care; to attach oneself to; to reside temporarily
    • 别忘了寄信。bié wàng le jì xìnDon't forget to post the letter.
    • 我忘了寄信。wǒ wàng le jì xìnI forgot to send the letter.
  • 准确zhǔn quèaccurateTraditional: 準確

  • 负责人fù zé rénperson in chargeTraditional: 負責人

    • 他为我做了详细说明。tā wèi wǒ zuò le xiáng xì shuō míngHe explained it to me in detail.
  • 降jiàngto fall; to lower
    Dictionary sensesto drop; to come down; to descend
  • 禁止jìn zhǐto forbid
    Dictionary sensesto prohibit; to ban
    • 禁止拍照。jìn zhǐ pāi zhàoNo taking photos.
    • 这里禁止吸烟。zhè li jìn zhǐ xī yānSmoking is prohibited here.
  • 冷静lěng jìngcalmTraditional: 冷靜

    • 冷静点。lěng jìng diǎnBe calm.
    • 冷静下来！lěng jìng xià laiGet a grip on yourself!
  • 购物gòu wùshoppingTraditional: 購物
    • 我喜欢购物。wǒ xǐ huan gòu wùI like shopping.
    • 她正要去购物。tā zhèng yào qù gòu wùShe is just going shopping.

    • 您能估计一下数量吗？nín néng gū jì yí xià shù liàng maCould you give me a ball-park figure?

    • 你几点吃早餐？nǐ jǐ diǎn chī zǎo cānWhat time do you have breakfast?
  • 图片tú piànpictureMeasure word: 张Traditional: 圖片

    • 这本书有很多图片。zhè běn shū yǒu hěn duō tú piànThis book has many pictures.
