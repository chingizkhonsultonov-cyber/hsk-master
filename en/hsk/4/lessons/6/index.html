  • 台táimeasure word for machines; stage
    Dictionary senses(classical) you (in letters); desk; table; counter
    • 我来自台北。wǒ lái zì tái běiI am from Taipei.
    • 我有一台电脑。wǒ yǒu yī tái diàn nǎoI have a computer.
  • 算suànto count; to be considered
    Dictionary sensesto regard as; to figure; to calculate; to compute
    • 她的女朋友真算飞机场。tā de nǚ péng you zhēn suàn fēi jī chǎngHer girlfriend is completely flat-chested.
    • 好，算你赢。hǎo suàn nǐ yíngOK, you win.
  • 获得huò déto obtain; to winTraditional: 獲得
    Dictionary sensesto receive; to get
  • 留liúto stay; to leave (behind)Traditional: 㽞
    Dictionary sensesto leave (a message etc); to retain; to remain; to keep
    • 留着吧。liú zhe baKeep it.
    • 我得留在家。wǒ de liú zài jiāI have to stay home.
  • 生命shēng mìnglife
    Dictionary senseslife (as the characteristic of living beings); living being; creature (CL:個|个[ge4],條|条[tiao2])
    • 这就是生命。zhè jiù shì shēng mìngThis is life.
    • 没有太阳就没有生命。méi yǒu tài yang jiù méi yǒu shēng mìngWithout the sun, life is impossible.
  • 法fǎlaw; methodTraditional: 㳒
    Dictionary sensesway; to emulate; (Buddhism) dharma
    • 法国万岁。fǎ guó wàn suìLong live France.
    • 他是法国人。tā shì fǎ guó rénHe is French.
  • 家庭jiā tíngfamilyMeasure word: 户、个
    Dictionary senseshousehold
    • 你必须保护你的家庭。nǐ bì xū bǎo hù nǐ de jiā tíngYou have to protect your family.
    • 大学里也有家庭作业吗？dà xué lǐ yě yǒu jiā tíng zuò yè maDo colleges give homework too?
  • 交jiāoto hand in; to pay; to make (friends)
    Dictionary sensesto hand over; to deliver; to pay (money); to turn over
    • 我想跟你交朋友。wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ jiāo péng youI do want to be your friend.
    • 你把东西交给谁了？nǐ bǎ dōng xi jiāo gěi shéi leWho did you give that to?
  • 全部quán bùall
    Dictionary senseswhole
    • 这就是全部了吗？zhè jiù shì quán bù le maIs this all?
    • 你打算自己一人把它全部吃了吗？nǐ dǎ suàn zì jǐ yī rén bǎ tā quán bù chī le maDo you plan to eat all of that yourself?
  • 正常zhèng chángnormal
    Dictionary sensesregular; ordinary
    • 一切正常。yī qiè zhèng chángEverything's normal.
    • 有点怕很正常。yǒu diǎn pà hěn zhèng chángIt's normal to be a little afraid.
  • 另外lìng wàibesides; another
    Dictionary sensesadditional; in addition; separate; other
    • 我认为大家都应该学另外一种语言。wǒ rèn wéi dà jiā dōu yīng gāi xué lìng wài yī zhǒng yǔ yánI think everybody should learn another language.
  • 血xuèbloodMeasure word: 滴、片
    Dictionary sensescolloquial pr. [xie3]
  • 半bànhalf; semi-
    Dictionary sensesincomplete; (after a number) and a half
  • 克kègram
    Dictionary sensesto be able to; to subdue; to restrain; to overcome
    • 我们去马克家吧。wǒ men qù mǎ kè jiā baLet's go to Mark's place.
    • 一碗饭大约重一百八十克。yī wǎn fàn dà yuē zhòng yī bǎi bā shí kèA bowl of rice is about 180 grams.
  • 到底dào dǐafter all; on earth (in questions)
    Dictionary sensesfinally; in the end; when all is said and done; to the end
    • 坚持到底！jiān chí dào dǐStiff upper lip!
    • 你到底怎么了啊？nǐ dào dǐ zěn me le aWhat happened to you?
  • 装zhuāngto load; to install; to pretendTraditional: 裝
    Dictionary sensesadornment; to adorn; dress; clothing
    • 请把这个瓶子装满水。qǐng bǎ zhè ge píng zi zhuāng mǎn shuǐPlease fill this bottle with water.
    • 这个箱子里装满了苹果。zhè ge xiāng zi lǐ zhuāng mǎn le píng guǒThis box is filled with apples.
  • 感谢gǎn xièto thankTraditional: 感謝
    Dictionary senses(express) thanks; gratitude; grateful; thankful
    • 非常感谢！fēi cháng gǎn xièMany thanks.
    • 我感谢你。wǒ gǎn xiè nǐI thank you.
  • 座zuòmeasure word for buildings, mountains
    Dictionary sensesseat; base; stand; (archaic) suffix used in a respectful form of address (e.g. 師座|师座[shi1 zuo4])
    • 那座山很容易爬。nà zuò shān hěn róng yì páThat mountain is easy to climb.
    • 我的房子有两座楼梯。wǒ de fáng zi yǒu liǎng zuò lóu tīMy house has two flights of stairs.
  • 好好hǎo hǎowell; properly
    Dictionary sensescarefully; nicely
    • 他们的讲词好好笑。tā men de jiǎng cí hǎo hǎo xiàoTheir speech is funny.
    • 好好准备就不会出问题。hǎo hǎo zhǔn bèi jiù bù huì chū wèn tíWell prepared means no worries.
  • 入rùto enter
    Dictionary sensesto go into; to join; to become a member of; (on product packaging, after {number n} + {classifier}) containing (n pieces) (from Japanese 入 "iri")
    • 我无法入睡。wǒ wú fǎ rù shuìI couldn't sleep.
    • 我花了比平常多一点的时间入睡。wǒ huā le bǐ píng cháng duō yī diǎn de shí jiān rù shuìIt took me a little more time than usually to fall asleep.
  • 技术jì shùtechnology; techniqueMeasure word: 门、种、项Traditional: 技術
    Dictionary sensesskill
  • 停tíngto stop
    Dictionary sensesto halt; to park (a car)
    • 雪停了。xuě tíng leIt's stopped snowing.
    • 雨停了。yǔ tíng leThe rain stopped.
  • 气qìair; gas; angryTraditional: 氣
    Dictionary sensessmell; weather; to make angry
    • 我气坏了。wǒ qì huài leI couldn't control my anger.
    • 我还是很气。wǒ hái shi hěn qìI'm still mad.
  • 性xìngnature; gender; -ity (suffix)Measure word: 个
    Dictionary sensescharacter; property; quality; attribute
    • 我要和她发生性关系。wǒ yào hé tā fā shēng xìng guān xiI want to have sex with her.
    • 今天没有任何下雨的可能性。jīn tiān méi yǒu rèn hé xià yǔ de kě néng xìngThere's no chance of rain today.
  • 倒dàoto pour; to fall over
    Dictionary sensesto invert; to place upside down or frontside back; to pour out; to tip out
    • 他倒在地上。tā dào zài de shàngHe fell to the floor.
    • 我倒觉得挺好的。wǒ dào jué de tǐng hǎo deI think it's pretty good.
