  • 使shǐto make; to cause
    Dictionary sensesto enable; to use; to employ
    • 她的故事使我很感动。tā de gù shi shǐ wǒ hěn gǎn dòngI was very moved by her story.
  • 计划jì huàplan; to planMeasure word: 个、项Traditional: 計劃
    Dictionary sensesproject; program; to map out
    • 你周末有什么计划？nǐ zhōu mò yǒu shén me jì huàWhat are your weekend plans?
    • 该计划是一个大成功。gāi jì huà shì yī gè dà chéng gōngThe project was a great success.
  • 全quánwhole; all
    Dictionary sensesentire; every; complete
    • 这全是真的。zhè quán shì zhēn deThat's all true.
    • 那不全是错的。nà bù quán shì cuò deThat's not entirely wrong.
  • 作为zuò wéias; in the role ofTraditional: 作為
    Dictionary sensesone's conduct; deed; activity; accomplishment
    • 我爸爸送了一块电子表给我作为生日礼物。wǒ bà ba sòng le yī kuài diàn zi biǎo gěi wǒ zuò wéi shēng rì lǐ wùMy father bought me a digital watch for birthday present.
  • 连liáneven; to linkTraditional: 連
    Dictionary sensesto join; to connect; continuously; in succession
    • 这里连夏天也冷。zhè li lián xià tiān yě lěngIt is cold there even in summer.
    • 那里连夏天也冷。nà li lián xià tiān yě lěngIt is cold there, even in summer.
  • 队duìteam; queueMeasure word: 个Traditional: 隊
    Dictionary sensessquadron; group
    • 我们三个一队。wǒ men sān gè yī duìWe are a team of three.
    • 我们的足球队打败了市里所有其他队。wǒ men de zú qiú duì dǎ bài le shì lǐ suǒ yǒu qí tā duìOur soccer team defeated all the other teams in the city.
  • 部bùdepartment; part; measure word for films
    Dictionary sensesministry; section; division
    • 我想看这部电影。wǒ xiǎng kàn zhè bù diàn yǐngI want to see the movie.
    • 我想要这部电话。wǒ xiǎng yào zhè bù diàn huàI want this phone.
  • 原因yuán yīnreasonMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensescause; origin; root cause
    • 不可做的原因多了。bù kě zuò de yuán yīn duō leThere are many good reasons not to do it.
    • 这就是我为什么回来的原因。zhè jiù shì wǒ wèi shén me huí lai de yuán yīnThis is why I came back.
  • 消息xiāo xinews; messageMeasure word: 条
    Dictionary senses(piece of) news; information
    • 我有消息。wǒ yǒu xiāo xiI have news.
    • 我知道了那个消息。wǒ zhī dào le nà ge xiāo xiI knew about that.
  • 表现biǎo xiànto show; performanceTraditional: 表現
    Dictionary sensesto show off; to display; to manifest; expression
    • 孩子想和大人一样表现。hái zi xiǎng hé dà ren yī yàng biǎo xiànChildren want to act like grown-ups.
  • 进入jìn rùto enterTraditional: 進入
    Dictionary sensesto join; to go into
    • 你进入我的房间之前都要敲门。nǐ jìn rù wǒ de fáng jiān zhī qián dōu yào qiāo ménYou are always to knock before entering my room.
    • 当我进入那个房间的时候，她正在弹钢琴。dāng wǒ jìn rù nà ge fáng jiān de shí hou tā zhèng zài dàn gāng qínEntering the room, I found her playing the piano.
  • 干gànto do; to workTraditional: 幹
    Dictionary sensestree trunk; main part of sth; to manage
  • 接受jiē shòuto accept
    Dictionary sensesto accept (a suggestion, punishment, bribe etc); to acquiesce
    • 我不接受。wǒ bù jiē shòuI don't accept it.
    • 他们接受了这些礼物。tā men jiē shòu le zhè xiē lǐ wùThey accepted those gifts.
  • 其中qí zhōngamong them
    Dictionary sensesamong; in; included among these
    • 这些人其中哪一个是你的朋友？zhè xiē rén qí zhōng nǎ yī gè shì nǐ de péng youWhich one of these people is your friend?
  • 成功chéng gōngto succeed; success
    Dictionary sensessuccessful; fruitful
    • 他一定会成功。tā yī dìng huì chéng gōngHe is sure to succeed.
    • 我肯定你会成功。wǒ kěn dìng nǐ huì chéng gōngI'm sure you'll succeed.
  • 之间zhī jiānbetweenTraditional: 之間
    Dictionary senses(after a noun) between; among; amid; (used after certain bisyllabic words to form expressions indicating a short period of time, e.g. 彈指之間|弹指之间[tan2 zhi3 zhi1 jian1])
    • 一下子之间我忘了她的名字了。yí xià zi zhī jiān wǒ wàng le tā de míng zi leFor a moment I forgot what her name was.
    • 两人之间的距离已拉近。liǎng rén zhī jiān de jù lí yǐ lā jìnThe gap between them has narrowed.
  • 道dàoroad; measure word for questions
    Dictionary sensesroad; path (CL:條|条[tiao2],股[gu3]); reason; principle; an art
    • 我去过北海道。wǒ qù guò běi hǎi dàoI've been to Hokkaido before.
    • 你很能说会道。nǐ hěn néng shuō huì dàoYou have a way with words.
  • 甚至shèn zhìeven
    Dictionary sensesso much so that
    • 我甚至在周日工作。wǒ shèn zhì zài zhōu rì gōng zuòI work even on Sunday.
    • 甚至在夏天，这里都很冷。shèn zhì zài xià tiān zhè li dōu hěn lěngIt's cold here even in summer.
  • 之前zhī qiánbefore
    Dictionary sensesprior to; ago; previously; beforehand
    • 六点之前要回家。liù diǎn zhī qián yào huí jiāCome home before six.
    • 你之前知道那个吗？nǐ zhī qián zhī dào nà ge maDid you know that before?
  • 能够néng gòuto be able toTraditional: 能夠
    Dictionary sensesto be capable of; can
    • 我很高兴能够认识您。wǒ hěn gāo xìng néng gòu rèn shi nínI'm glad to make your acquaintance.
    • 没有人能够解决这个问题。méi yǒu rén néng gòu jiě jué zhè ge wèn tíNobody can solve this problem.
  • 无法wú fǎunable toTraditional: 無法
    Dictionary sensesincapable of
    • 他无法完成它。tā wú fǎ wán chéng tāHe is unable to complete it.
    • 我无法再做下去了。wǒ wú fǎ zài zuò xià qu leI can't do any more than this.
  • 拉lāto pull
    Dictionary sensesto play (a bowed instrument); to drag; to draw; to chat
  • 对于duì yúregardingTraditional: 對於
    Dictionary sensesas far as (sth) is concerned; with regard to
    • 对于我，我不反对。duì yú wǒ wǒ bù fǎn duìAs for me, I have no objection.
  • 肯定kěn dìngcertainly; to affirm
    Dictionary sensesto be certain; to be positive; assuredly; definitely
    • 你肯定吗？nǐ kěn dìng maSeriously?
    • 我很肯定！wǒ hěn kěn dìngI'm absolutely sure!
  • 者zhěperson who (suffix)
    Dictionary senses(after a verb or adjective) one who (is) ...; (after a noun) person involved in ...; -er; -ist
    • 我们为死者放弃了他。wǒ men wèi sǐ zhě fàng qì le tāWe gave him up for dead.
    • 骄傲是失败的先行者。jiāo ào shì shī bài de xiān xíng zhěPride goes before a fall.
