  • 输shūto loseTraditional: 輸
    Dictionary sensesto be beaten; (literary) to donate; to contribute; (coll.) to enter (a password)
    • 我是个输家。wǒ shì gè shū jiāI'm a loser.
    • 问她出去约会你有什么好输的？也许一点点的骄傲？wèn tā chū qu yuē huì nǐ yǒu shén me hǎo shū de yě xǔ yī diǎn diǎn de jiāo àoWhat do you have to lose by asking her out on a date? A little pride maybe?
  • 要是yào shiif
    Dictionary senses(coll.) if
    • 要是雨停了该多好！yào shi yǔ tíng le gāi duō hǎoIf only the rain would stop!
    • 要是有东西坏了呢？yào shi yǒu dōng xi huài le neWhat if something breaks?
  • 否则fǒu zéotherwiseTraditional: 否則
    Dictionary sensesif not; or (else)
    • 快一点，否则你会迟到。kuài yī diǎn fǒu zé nǐ huì chí dàoHurry up or you'll be late.
    • 快一点，否则就要迟到了。kuài yī diǎn fǒu zé jiù yào chí dào leHurry up or we'll be late.
  • 免费miǎn fèifree (of charge)Traditional: 免費
    • 没有更多免费座位了。méi yǒu gèng duō miǎn fèi zuò wèi leThere were no more free seats.
  • 牌páisign; brand; playing card
    Dictionary sensessignboard; plaque; plate; tablet (CL:塊|块[kuai4]); brand; trademark (CL:個|个[ge4]); mahjong tile; domino; playing card (CL:張|张[zhang1])
    • 我们常常在周日打牌。wǒ men cháng cháng zài zhōu rì dǎ páiWe often play cards on Sunday.
    • 请仔细洗牌。qǐng zǐ xì xǐ páiPlease shuffle the cards carefully.
  • 动作dòng zuòmovement; actionTraditional: 動作
    Dictionary sensesmovement; motion; action (CL:個|个[ge4]); to act; to move
    • 你动作快点就能赶上他。nǐ dòng zuò kuài diǎn jiù néng gǎn shàng tāIf you hurry, you'll catch up with him.
  • 不够bú gòunot enoughTraditional: 不夠
    Dictionary sensesinsufficient; inadequate
    • 你还不够经验。nǐ hái bù gòu jīng yànYou're still green.
    • 我不够钱去旅行。wǒ bù gòu qián qù lǚ xíngI am short of money for my trip.
  • 俩liǎtwo; bothTraditional: 倆
    Dictionary sensestwo (colloquial equivalent of 兩個|两个); some
    • 咱们俩是朋友。zán men liǎ shì péng youYou and I are friends.
    • 你们俩都错了。nǐ men liǎ dōu cuò leBoth of you are wrong.
  • 增加zēng jiāto increase
    Dictionary sensesto raise
    • 你的体重增加了，不是吗？nǐ de tǐ zhòng zēng jiā le bù shì maYou've put on weight, haven't you?
  • 举行jǔ xíngto hold (a meeting)Traditional: 舉行
    Dictionary sensesto hold (a meeting, ceremony etc)
  • 想法xiǎng fǎidea; opinion
    Dictionary sensesway of thinking; notion; to think of a way (to do sth)
    • 想法很重要。xiǎng fǎ hěn zhòng yàoIdeas matter.
    • 他改变想法了。tā gǎi biàn xiǎng fǎ leHe changed his idea.
  • 落luòto fall
    Dictionary sensesto fall or drop; (of the sun) to set; (of a tide) to go out; to lower
    • 我落选了。wǒ luò xuǎn leI did not pass.
    • 我落下了。wǒ luò xià leI fell.
  • 著名zhù míngfamous
    Dictionary sensesnoted; well-known; celebrated
    • 他是个著名的画家。tā shì gè zhù míng de huà jiāHe is a famous painter.
    • 她是一个著名的歌手。tā shì yī gè zhù míng de gē shǒuShe is a noted singer.
  • 拒绝jù juéto refuseTraditional: 拒絕
    Dictionary sensesto decline; to reject
    • 我必须拒绝。wǒ bì xū jù juéI must decline.
    • 他拒绝相信我们。tā jù jué xiāng xìn wǒ menHe refuses to believe us.
  • 梦想mèng xiǎngdream; to dream ofTraditional: 夢想
    Dictionary senses(fig.) to dream of
    • 我不能放弃我的梦想。wǒ bù néng fàng qì wǒ de mèng xiǎngI can't give up my dream.
    • 我没有钱，但是我有梦想。wǒ méi yǒu qián dàn shì wǒ yǒu mèng xiǎngI have no money, but I have dreams.
  • 帅shuàihandsomeTraditional: 帥
    Dictionary sensesto command; graceful; dashing; elegant
    • 他很帅。tā hěn shuàiHe's handsome.
    • 你真的很帅！nǐ zhēn de hěn shuàiYou are really handsome!
  • 受伤shòu shāngto be injuredTraditional: 受傷
    Dictionary sensesto sustain injuries; wounded (in an accident etc); harmed
    • 我受伤了。wǒ shòu shāng leI am hurt.
    • 你受伤了吗？nǐ shòu shāng le maAre you hurt?
  • 不仅bù jǐnnot onlyTraditional: 不僅
    Dictionary sensesnot just; not limited to; (as a correlative conjunction) not only (..., but also ...)
    • 她不仅友好还诚实。tā bù jǐn yǒu hǎo hái chéng shíShe is not only kind, but also honest.
  • 期qīperiod; issueTraditional: 朞
    Dictionary sensescycle; a period of time; phase; stage
    • 这张票的有效期是三天。zhè zhāng piào de yǒu xiào qī shì sān tiānThe ticket is good for three days.
  • 姑娘gū nianggirl; young womanMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesyoung lady; daughter; paternal aunt (old)
  • 顺利shùn lìsmoothlyTraditional: 順利
    Dictionary senseswithout a hitch
    • 工作一切顺利吗？gōng zuò yī qiè shùn lì maIs everything going OK at work?
  • 艺术yì shùartTraditional: 藝術
    • 为艺术而艺术。wèi yì shù ér yì shùArt for art's sake.
    • 你了解艺术吗？nǐ liǎo jiě yì shù maDo you know anything about art?
  • 麻烦má fantrouble; to botherTraditional: 麻煩
    Dictionary sensesinconvenience; inconvenient; troublesome; annoying
    • 他有麻烦。tā yǒu má fanHe is in trouble.
    • 麻烦您了！má fan nín leSorry to bother you!
  • 美好měi hǎowonderful
    Dictionary sensesbeautiful; fine
    • 人生是美好的。rén shēng shì měi hǎo deLife is sweet.
    • 多美好的家庭啊！duō měi hǎo de jiā tíng aWhat a wonderful family.
  • 申请shēn qǐngto applyTraditional: 申請
    Dictionary sensesto apply for sth; application form (CL:份[fen4])
    • 我申请了签证。wǒ shēn qǐng le qiān zhèngI applied for a visa.
