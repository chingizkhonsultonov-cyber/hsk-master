  • 正确zhèng quècorrectTraditional: 正確
    Dictionary sensessound; right; proper
    • 你完全正确。nǐ wán quán zhèng quèYou're absolutely right.
    • 她是正确的吗？tā shì zhèng què de maIs she right?
  • 爱情ài qínglove (romantic)Measure word: 份Traditional: 愛情
    Dictionary sensesromance
    • 你不喜欢爱情故事。nǐ bù xǐ huan ài qíng gù shiYou don't like love stories.
    • 您能为爱情而死吗？nín néng wèi ài qíng ér sǐ maCan you die for love?
  • 够gòuenoughTraditional: 夠
    Dictionary sensesenough (sufficient); enough (too much); (coll.) (before adj.) really; (coll.) to reach by stretching out
    • 钱够吗？qián gòu maWas there enough money?
    • 我受够了。wǒ shòu gòu leI have had it.
  • 火huǒfire; popular
    Dictionary sensesurgent; ammunition; fiery or flaming; internal heat (Chinese medicine)
    • 她在火旁边让自己暖和起来。tā zài huǒ páng biān ràng zì jǐ nuǎn huo qǐ laiShe warmed herself by the fire.
  • 更加gèng jiāeven more
    Dictionary sensesmore (than sth else)
    • 下次我会更加努力。xià cì wǒ huì gèng jiā nǔ lìI'll try harder next time.
    • 我希望你更加努力地工作。wǒ xī wàng nǐ gèng jiā nǔ lì de gōng zuòI want you to work harder.
  • 排páirow; to line up
    Dictionary sensesa row; a line; to set in order; to arrange
    • 在第二排房子的第三家。zài dì èr pái fáng zi de dì sān jiāIt's at the third home in the second row of houses.
  • 市场shì chǎngmarketTraditional: 市場
    Dictionary sensesmarketplace; bazaar; (economics) market
  • 节jiésection; festival; to saveTraditional: 節
    Dictionary sensesjoint; node; segment; solar term (one of the 24 divisions of the year in the traditional Chinese calendar)
  • 留下liú xiàto leave behind; to stay
    Dictionary sensesto stay behind; to remain; to keep; not to let (sb) go
    • 留下来！liú xià láiStay!
    • 欢迎留下来。huān yíng liú xià láiFeel free to stay.
  • 教育jiào yùeducation; to educate
    Dictionary sensesto teach
    • 教育从家庭开始。jiào yù cóng jiā tíng kāi shǐEducation starts at home.
    • 他总是把孩子的教育问题留给他的妻子。tā zǒng shì bǎ hái zi de jiào yù wèn tí liú gěi tā de qī ziHe always left the problem of his children's education to his wife.
  • 日子rì ziday; life
    Dictionary sensesa (calendar) date; days of one's life
    • 你最坏的日子怎么样？nǐ zuì huài de rì zi zěn me yàngWhat was the worst day of your life?
  • 祝zhùto wish
    Dictionary sensesto pray for; to wish (sb bon voyage, happy birthday etc); person who invokes the spirits during sacrificial ceremonies
    • 祝你健康！zhù nǐ jiàn kāngTo your health!
    • 祝你成功。zhù nǐ chéng gōngI wish you success.
  • 取qǔto take; to get
    Dictionary sensesto choose; to fetch
    • 我去银行取钱。wǒ qù yín háng qǔ qiánI'm going to the bank to get money.
  • 危险wēi xiǎndangerous; dangerTraditional: 危險
    • 这很危险。zhè hěn wēi xiǎnIt's very dangerous.
    • 危险已过去。wēi xiǎn yǐ guò quThe danger is over.
  • 管理guǎn lǐto manage; managementMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesto supervise; to administer; administration
  • 乱luànmessyTraditional: 亂
    Dictionary sensesin confusion or disorder; in a confused state of mind; disorder; upheaval
    • 这不是能乱动的。zhè bù shì néng luàn dòng deIt's not a toy.
    • 如果你弄乱了，你来打扫。rú guǒ nǐ nòng luàn le nǐ lái dǎ sǎoIf you make a mess, clean it up.
  • 待dàito treat; to wait
    Dictionary sensesto deal with; to need; going to (do sth)
    • 待在那儿别动。dài zài nà r bié dòngRemain there and don't move.
    • 你准备待多久？nǐ zhǔn bèi dài duō jiǔHow long do you plan to stay?
  • 适合shì héto suitTraditional: 適合
    Dictionary sensesto fit
    • 绿色适合你。lǜ sè shì hé nǐGreen suits you.
    • 绿色很适合你。lǜ sè hěn shì hé nǐGreen suits you very well.
  • 正式zhèng shìformal; official
  • 引起yǐn qǐto cause
    Dictionary sensesto give rise to; to lead to; to arouse
    • 我们的到来引起了注意。wǒ men de dào lái yǐn qǐ le zhù yìOur arrival drew attention.
  • 从来cóng láialways; never (with negative)Traditional: 從來
    Dictionary sensesat all times; never (if used in negative sentence)
    • 我从来没有坐过飞机。wǒ cóng lái méi yǒu zuò guò fēi jīI've never flown in a plane.
    • 他从来没有上学迟到。tā cóng lái méi yǒu shàng xué chí dàoHe has never been late for school.
  • 共gòngin total; together
    Dictionary sensescommon; general; to share; total
    • 我们肯定有很多共通点。wǒ men kěn dìng yǒu hěn duō gòng tōng diǎnI am sure we have a lot in common.
  • 棒bànggreat; stick
    Dictionary sensesclub; cudgel; smart; capable
    • 我感觉非常棒。wǒ gǎn jué fēi cháng bàngI do feel pretty good.
    • 这游戏太棒了！zhè yóu xì tài bàng leWhat a game!
  • 图túpictureMeasure word: 张Traditional: 圖
    Dictionary sensesdiagram; drawing; chart; map
    • 请你帮我在这张纸上画个草图。qǐng nǐ bāng wǒ zài zhè zhāng zhǐ shàng huà gè cǎo túCan you sketch it for me on this piece of paper please?
  • 实际上shí jì shàngin factTraditional: 實際上
    Dictionary sensesin reality; as a matter of fact; in practice
    • 她看起来是年轻的，但实际上她四十多岁了。tā kàn qǐ lai shì nián qīng de dàn shí jì shàng tā sì shí duō suì leShe looks young, but in reality she's over 40.
