Changing jobs

1. Thinking of quitting

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我最近一直在考虑换工作。Wǒ zuìjìn yìzhí zài kǎolǜ huàn gōngzuò.I've been thinking about changing jobs lately.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì为什么？你现在的工资不是挺高的吗？Wèishénme? Nǐ xiànzài de gōngzī bú shì tǐng gāo de ma?Why? Isn't your salary pretty good now?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng工资是不错，可是压力太大，每天都加班到很晚。Gōngzī shì búcuò, kěshì yālì tài dà, měi tiān dōu jiābān dào hěn wǎn.The pay is good, but the pressure is huge and I work late every day.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那你得先找好下一个工作，再辞职。Nà nǐ děi xiān zhǎo hǎo xià yí ge gōngzuò, zài cízhí.Then find your next job first, and only then quit.
  5. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你说得对，不能太着急。Nǐ shuō de duì, bù néng tài zháojí.You're right; I shouldn't rush.

2. A new direction

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà听说你不当老师了？Tīngshuō nǐ bù dāng lǎoshī le?I hear you've stopped teaching?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng对，我开了一家小咖啡店。Duì, wǒ kāi le yì jiā xiǎo kāfēidiàn.Yes, I've opened a small café.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà真勇敢！刚开始一定很辛苦吧？Zhēn yǒnggǎn! Gāng kāishǐ yídìng hěn xīnkǔ ba?How brave! It must be hard at the start?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng是挺累的，不过做自己喜欢的事，再累也值得。Shì tǐng lèi de, búguò zuò zìjǐ xǐhuan de shì, zài lèi yě zhídé.It's tiring, but doing what you love is worth it, however tiring.

Protecting the environment

1. Sorting rubbish

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà这个瓶子应该扔到哪个垃圾桶里？Zhège píngzi yīnggāi rēng dào nǎge lājītǒng li?Which bin should this bottle go in?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng扔到蓝色的那个，那是可回收的。Rēng dào lánsè de nàge, nà shì kě huíshōu de.The blue one; that's for recycling.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà剩下的饭菜呢？Shèng xià de fàncài ne?And leftover food?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng扔到绿色的。垃圾分类虽然麻烦，但是对环境很有好处。Rēng dào lǜsè de. Lājī fēnlèi suīrán máfan, dànshì duì huánjìng hěn yǒu hǎochu.In the green one. Sorting rubbish is a hassle, but it's good for the environment.

2. Less plastic

  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì有道理。我以后也带自己的杯子去买咖啡。Yǒu dàolǐ. Wǒ yǐhòu yě dài zìjǐ de bēizi qù mǎi kāfēi.Makes sense. From now on I'll bring my own cup for coffee.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng每个人做一点儿，变化就会很大。Měi ge rén zuò yìdiǎnr, biànhuà jiù huì hěn dà.If everyone does a little, it makes a big difference.

Volunteering

1. Helping at an old people's home

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà这个周末你有空吗？我们去养老院当志愿者。Zhège zhōumò nǐ yǒu kòng ma? Wǒmen qù yǎnglǎoyuàn dāng zhìyuànzhě.Are you free this weekend? Let's volunteer at the old people's home.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng好啊，要做些什么？Hǎo a, yào zuò xiē shénme?Sure. What will we do?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà陪老人聊聊天，给他们唱唱歌。Péi lǎorén liáoliao tiān, gěi tāmen chàngchang gē.Chat with the old folks and sing them some songs.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng这个有意义，我一定去。Zhège yǒu yìyì, wǒ yídìng qù.That's worthwhile; I'll definitely come.

2. After volunteering

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng今天那位老奶奶拉着我的手说了很久。Jīntiān nà wèi lǎo nǎinai lā zhe wǒ de shǒu shuō le hěn jiǔ.Today that old lady held my hand and talked for ages.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà她的孩子都在外地工作，很少来看她。Tā de háizi dōu zài wàidì gōngzuò, hěn shǎo lái kàn tā.Her children all work in other cities and rarely visit.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng怪不得她那么高兴。Guàibude tā nàme gāoxìng.No wonder she was so happy.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà以后我们每个月都来一次吧。Yǐhòu wǒmen měi ge yuè dōu lái yí cì ba.Let's come once a month from now on.

Parents' expectations

1. Mum wants me to be a doctor

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我妈希望我当医生，可是我对医学没兴趣。Wǒ mā xīwàng wǒ dāng yīshēng, kěshì wǒ duì yīxué méi xìngqù.My mum wants me to be a doctor, but I'm not interested in medicine.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那你喜欢什么？Nà nǐ xǐhuan shénme?What do you like, then?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我想学设计。Wǒ xiǎng xué shèjì.I want to study design.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì你应该跟她好好谈谈，让她了解你的想法。Nǐ yīnggāi gēn tā hǎohǎo tántan, ràng tā liǎojiě nǐ de xiǎngfǎ.You should have a proper talk with her so she understands you.

2. When are you getting married?

  1. 妈妈 · māma你都三十了，什么时候结婚啊？Nǐ dōu sānshí le, shénme shíhou jiéhūn a?You're already thirty — when are you getting married?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng妈，我现在工作太忙了，以后再说吧。Mā, wǒ xiànzài gōngzuò tài máng le, yǐhòu zài shuō ba.Mum, I'm too busy with work right now. Let's talk about it later.
  3. 妈妈 · māma你每次都这么说。Nǐ měi cì dōu zhème shuō.You say that every time.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng放心吧，遇到合适的人我一定告诉你。Fàngxīn ba, yù dào héshì de rén wǒ yídìng gàosu nǐ.Don't worry; when I meet the right person, I'll tell you.

Choosing a major

1. What should I study?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà老师，我不知道应该选什么专业。Lǎoshī, wǒ bù zhīdào yīnggāi xuǎn shénme zhuānyè.Teacher, I don't know which major to choose.
  2. 老师 · lǎoshī你对什么最感兴趣？Nǐ duì shénme zuì gǎn xìngqù?What are you most interested in?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我喜欢语言，也喜欢经济。Wǒ xǐhuan yǔyán, yě xǐhuan jīngjì.I like languages, and economics too.
  4. 老师 · lǎoshī那可以考虑国际贸易，两方面都能用上。Nà kěyǐ kǎolǜ guójì màoyì, liǎng fāngmiàn dōu néng yòng shang.Then consider international trade; you'd use both.

2. Changing majors

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我想从数学专业换到计算机专业。Wǒ xiǎng cóng shùxué zhuānyè huàn dào jìsuànjī zhuānyè.I want to switch from maths to computer science.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng可以换吗？Kěyǐ huàn ma?Is that allowed?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng可以，不过第一年的成绩要好，还要参加一个考试。Kěyǐ, búguò dì-yī nián de chéngjì yào hǎo, hái yào cānjiā yí ge kǎoshì.Yes, but you need good first-year grades and to pass an exam.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你数学那么好，肯定没问题。Nǐ shùxué nàme hǎo, kěndìng méi wèntí.You're so good at maths — no problem for you.

Buying a home

1. Rent or buy?

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì你觉得是租房好还是买房好？Nǐ juéde shì zū fáng hǎo háishi mǎi fáng hǎo?Do you think renting or buying is better?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng看情况。如果工作不稳定，租房更灵活。Kàn qíngkuàng. Rúguǒ gōngzuò bù wěndìng, zū fáng gèng línghuó.It depends. If your job isn't stable, renting is more flexible.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì可是房租每年都在涨。Kěshì fángzū měi nián dōu zài zhǎng.But rent goes up every year.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng这也是个问题。买房的话压力也很大。Zhè yě shì ge wèntí. Mǎi fáng de huà yālì yě hěn dà.That's a problem too. But buying is a lot of pressure as well.

2. Looking at a new flat

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这套房子多大？Zhè tào fángzi duō dà?How big is this flat?
  2. 销售 · xiāoshòu九十平方米，两室一厅，南北通透。Jiǔshí píngfāngmǐ, liǎng shì yì tīng, nánběi tōngtòu.Ninety square metres, two bedrooms and a living room, light on both sides.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng周围有学校吗？Zhōuwéi yǒu xuéxiào ma?Are there schools nearby?
  4. 销售 · xiāoshòu有，小学和中学走路都只要十分钟。Yǒu, xiǎoxué hé zhōngxué zǒulù dōu zhǐ yào shí fēnzhōng.Yes, both primary and secondary are ten minutes' walk.

Talking about a film

1. What did you think?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你觉得这部电影怎么样？Nǐ juéde zhè bù diànyǐng zěnmeyàng?What did you think of the film?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng故事很感人，最后我都哭了。Gùshi hěn gǎnrén, zuìhòu wǒ dōu kū le.The story was moving; I even cried at the end.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我也是。男主角演得特别好。Wǒ yě shì. Nán zhǔjué yǎn de tèbié hǎo.Me too. The lead actor was brilliant.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng听说他为了这个角色减了二十斤。Tīngshuō tā wèile zhège juésè jiǎn le èrshí jīn.I heard he lost ten kilos for the role.

2. Not worth watching

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这部电影太无聊了，我差点儿睡着。Zhè bù diànyǐng tài wúliáo le, wǒ chàdiǎnr shuì zháo.That film was so boring I nearly fell asleep.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì网上评分那么高，没想到这么一般。Wǎng shang píngfēn nàme gāo, méi xiǎngdào zhème yìbān.It had such high ratings online — I didn't expect it to be so average.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng下次我们先看看朋友的推荐。Xià cì wǒmen xiān kànkan péngyou de tuījiàn.Next time, let's check our friends' recommendations first.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì同意。Tóngyì.Agreed.

A business meeting

1. Scheduling a meeting

  1. 秘书 · mìshū王经理，张总想下周跟您见个面。Wáng jīnglǐ, Zhāng zǒng xiǎng xià zhōu gēn nín jiàn ge miàn.Manager Wang, Director Zhang would like to meet you next week.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我周二和周四下午都有空。Wǒ zhōu'èr hé zhōusì xiàwǔ dōu yǒu kòng.I'm free Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.
  3. 秘书 · mìshū那就定在周四下午两点，在我们公司会议室。Nà jiù dìng zài zhōusì xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn, zài wǒmen gōngsī huìyìshì.Then Thursday at 2 p.m., in our meeting room.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng好的，我会提前准备好材料。Hǎo de, wǒ huì tíqián zhǔnbèi hǎo cáiliào.Fine; I'll prepare the materials in advance.

2. At the meeting

  1. 张总 · Zhāng zǒng我们对贵公司的产品很感兴趣。Wǒmen duì guì gōngsī de chǎnpǐn hěn gǎn xìngqù.We're very interested in your company's products.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng谢谢。这是我们最新的介绍，请您看一下。Xièxie. Zhè shì wǒmen zuìxīn de jièshào, qǐng nín kàn yíxià.Thank you. Here's our latest brochure.
  3. 张总 · Zhāng zǒng价格方面还能再商量吗？Jiàgé fāngmiàn hái néng zài shāngliang ma?Is the price negotiable?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng如果数量大，我们可以给百分之十的折扣。Rúguǒ shùliàng dà, wǒmen kěyǐ gěi bǎi fēn zhī shí de zhékòu.For a large order, we can offer a 10% discount.

Chinese customs

1. Gifts to avoid

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我想送中国朋友一个钟，可以吗？Wǒ xiǎng sòng Zhōngguó péngyou yí ge zhōng, kěyǐ ma?I want to give a Chinese friend a clock. Is that OK?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì最好别送。“送钟”听起来像“送终”，不吉利。Zuìhǎo bié sòng. “Sòng zhōng” tīng qǐlai xiàng “sòngzhōng”, bù jílì.Better not. “Giving a clock” sounds like “seeing someone off to death” — unlucky.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà原来是这样！那送什么好呢？Yuánlái shì zhèyàng! Nà sòng shénme hǎo ne?I see! What's a good gift then?
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì送茶叶或者你们国家的特产都不错。Sòng cháyè huòzhě nǐmen guójiā de tèchǎn dōu búcuò.Tea, or a speciality from your country, would be good.

2. At the dinner table

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng在中国吃饭，筷子不能插在米饭上。Zài Zhōngguó chī fàn, kuàizi bù néng chā zài mǐfàn shang.In China, you shouldn't stick chopsticks upright in rice.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà为什么？Wèishénme?Why?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng因为那样像给去世的人上香。Yīnwèi nàyàng xiàng gěi qùshì de rén shàng xiāng.Because it looks like incense offered to the dead.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà谢谢你告诉我，我以后一定注意。Xièxie nǐ gàosu wǒ, wǒ yǐhòu yídìng zhùyì.Thanks for telling me; I'll be careful from now on.

Adopting a pet

1. Adopt, don't buy

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我想养一只狗，你觉得去哪儿买好？Wǒ xiǎng yǎng yì zhī gǒu, nǐ juéde qù nǎr mǎi hǎo?I want a dog. Where do you think I should buy one?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng为什么不去动物救助站领养一只呢？Wèishénme bú qù dòngwù jiùzhùzhàn lǐngyǎng yì zhī ne?Why not adopt one from an animal shelter?
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì领养要花钱吗？Lǐngyǎng yào huā qián ma?Does adopting cost money?
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng基本不要钱，但是要保证好好照顾它。Jīběn bú yào qián, dànshì yào bǎozhèng hǎohǎo zhàogù tā.Hardly anything, but you must promise to take good care of it.

2. The first week

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你的小狗适应新家了吗？Nǐ de xiǎo gǒu shìyìng xīn jiā le ma?Has your puppy settled into its new home?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì刚开始有点儿怕人，现在每天跟着我。Gāng kāishǐ yǒudiǎnr pà rén, xiànzài měi tiān gēn zhe wǒ.It was a bit scared at first; now it follows me everywhere.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你给它起名字了吗？Nǐ gěi tā qǐ míngzi le ma?Have you named it?
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì叫“豆豆”，因为它的眼睛像两颗黑豆。Jiào “Dòudou”, yīnwèi tā de yǎnjing xiàng liǎng kē hēi dòu.“Doudou”, because its eyes look like two black beans.
