  • 当然dāng ránof courseTraditional: 當然
    Dictionary sensesonly natural; as it should be; certainly; without doubt
    • 当然了。dāng rán leOf course.
    • 当然哪！dāng rán nǎOf course!
  • 接jiēto meet; to pick up; to answer (a call)
    Dictionary sensesto receive; to answer (the phone); to meet or welcome sb; to connect
    • 接电话。jiē diàn huàAnswer the phone.
    • 我会去接他的。wǒ huì qù jiē tā deI'll pick him up.
  • 相信xiāng xìnto believe
    Dictionary sensesto be convinced; to accept as true
    • 我相信你。wǒ xiāng xìn nǐI believe you.
    • 你相信他吗？nǐ xiāng xìn tā maDo you trust him?
  • 刚gāngjust; only justTraditional: 剛
    Dictionary senseshard; firm; strong; barely
    • 我刚到家。wǒ gāng dào jiāI only just got home.
    • 我们刚认识。wǒ men gāng rèn shiWe just met.
  • 只要zhǐ yàoas long as
    Dictionary sensesso long as; provided; if
    • 只要十元！zhǐ yào shí yuánIt only costs $10.00!
    • 我只要你跟我来。wǒ zhǐ yào nǐ gēn wǒ láiI just need you to come with me.
  • 不同bù tóngdifferent
    Dictionary sensesdistinct; not the same; not alike
    • 她二十五岁时已经在五个不同的国家住过。tā èr shí wǔ suì shí yǐ jīng zài wǔ gè bù tóng de guó jiā zhù guòShe lived in five different countries by age 25.
    • 这蛋糕吃起来不同一般。zhè dàn gāo chī qǐ lai bù tóng yī bānThe cake tastes divine!
  • 解决jiě juéto solveTraditional: 解決
    Dictionary sensesto resolve; to settle (a problem); to eliminate; to wipe out (an enemy, bandits etc)
    • 问题还没有解决。wèn tí hái méi yǒu jiě juéThe matter has not been settled yet.
    • 问题已经解决了。wèn tí yǐ jīng jiě jué leThe problem is solved.
  • 干gànto do; to workTraditional: 幹
    Dictionary sensestree trunk; main part of sth; to manage
    • 干洗吧。gàn xǐ baDry clean it.
    • 干的好！gàn de hǎoGood job!
  • 关于guān yúabout; concerningTraditional: 關於
    Dictionary sensespertaining to; with regard to; a matter of
    • 他写了一本关于中国的书。tā xiě le yī běn guān yú Zhōng guó de shūHe wrote a book on China.
    • 他有几十本关于日本的书。tā yǒu jǐ shí běn guān yú rì běn de shūHe has dozens of books about Japan.
  • 完成wán chéngto complete
    Dictionary sensesto accomplish
    • 我们完成了！wǒ men wán chéng leWe're done!
    • 我们完成了吗？wǒ men wán chéng le maAre we done?
  • 结束jié shùto end; to finishTraditional: 結束
    Dictionary sensestermination; to conclude; to close
    • 休息结束了！xiū xi jié shù leRest time's over!
    • 已经结束了。yǐ jīng jié shù leIt's already over.
  • 帮助bāng zhùto help; helpTraditional: 幫助
    Dictionary sensesassistance; aid; to assist
    • 请帮助我。qǐng bāng zhù wǒPlease help me.
    • 她需要帮助。tā xū yào bāng zhùShe needs help.
  • 变biànto change; to becomeTraditional: 變
    Dictionary sensesto become different; to transform; to vary; rebellion
    • 他们不想变老。tā men bù xiǎng biàn lǎoThey don't want to grow old.
    • 天气每天都在变。tiān qì měi tiān dōu zài biànThe weather varies from day to day.
  • 加jiāto add; plus
    Dictionary senses(math.) plus; to increase; to augment; (used before a disyllabic verb, often after an adverb like 不[bu4], 大[da4], 稍[shao1] etc, to indicate that the action applies to sth previously mentioned, as in 稍加改良[shao1 jia1 gai3 liang2] "make some minor improvements to (it)")
    • 我那时在加拿大。wǒ nà shí zài jiā ná dàI was in Canada then.
    • 那么再加我两只！nà me zài jiā wǒ liǎng zhīThen give me two more!
  • 努力nǔ lìto try hard; hard-working
    Dictionary sensesto make an effort; to strive; conscientious
    • 你工作努力。nǐ gōng zuò nǔ lìYou work hard.
    • 她努力地工作。tā nǔ lì de gōng zuòShe worked hard.
  • 有关yǒu guānrelated; to be aboutTraditional: 有關
    Dictionary sensesto have sth to do with; to relate to; related to; to concern
    • 你有关门吗？nǐ yǒu guān mén maDid you lock the door?
  • 以前yǐ qiánbefore; in the past
    Dictionary sensesformerly; previous; ago
    • 他以前不是那样。tā yǐ qián bù shì nà yàngHe didn't use to be like that.
    • 我们以前见过吗？wǒ men yǐ qián jiàn guò maHave we met before?
  • 办法bàn fǎway; method; solutionMeasure word: 条、个Traditional: 辦法
    Dictionary sensesway of handling sth; means; measure; (practical) solution to a problem
    • 有个办法。yǒu gè bàn fǎThere is a way.
    • 再想别的办法吧。zài xiǎng bié de bàn fǎ baWe'll find some other way to do that.
  • 特别tè biéespecially; specialTraditional: 特別
    Dictionary sensesunusual; very; particularly
    • 它很特别。tā hěn tè biéIt's very special.
    • 她特别想去。tā tè bié xiǎng qùShe so wants to go.
  • 别的bié deother; elseTraditional: 別的
    • 还有别的吗？hái yǒu bié de maAnything else?
    • 我没有别的要告诉你。wǒ méi yǒu bié de yào gào su nǐI have nothing else to tell you.
