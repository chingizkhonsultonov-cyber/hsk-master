  • 练liànto practiseTraditional: 練
    Dictionary sensesto practice; to train; to drill; to perfect (one's skill)
  • 聊liáoto chat
    Dictionary senses(coll.) to chat; (literary) temporarily; for a while; (literary) somewhat
    • 我们聊聊吧！wǒ men liáo liáo baLet's have a chat.
    • 你有几分钟时间吗？我想跟你聊一下。nǐ yǒu jǐ fēn zhōng shí jiān ma wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ liáo yí xiàCan you spare me a few minutes? I'd like to have a word with you.
  • 同事tóng shìcolleague
    Dictionary sensesco-worker
  • 体育tǐ yùsport; PETraditional: 體育
    Dictionary sensessports; physical education
    • 我知道你喜欢体育运动。wǒ zhī dào nǐ xǐ huan tǐ yù yùn dòngI know you like sports.
    • 我对体育不感兴趣。wǒ duì tǐ yù bù gǎn xìng qùI'm not interested in sports.
  • 大人dà renadult
    Dictionary sensesgrownup; title of respect toward superiors
    • 他是加拿大人。tā shì jiā ná dà renHe's Canadian.
    • 你们是加拿大人吗？nǐ men shì jiā ná dà ren maAre you Canadian?
  • 角jiǎocorner; angle; jiao (0.1 yuan)Measure word: 个
    Dictionary senseshorn; horn-shaped; unit of money equal to 0.1 yuan, or 10 cents (a dime)
  • 老人lǎo rénold person
    Dictionary sensesold man or woman; the elderly; one's aged parents or grandparents
    • 这个老人是谁？zhè ge lǎo rén shì shéiWho is this old person?
    • 她帮助了一位老人过马路。tā bāng zhù le yī wèi lǎo rén guò mǎ lùShe helped an old man cross the street.
  • 胖pàngfat
    Dictionary sensesplump
    • 你有点胖。nǐ yǒu diǎn pàngYou are a bit fat.
    • 她变胖了。tā biàn pàng leShe became fat.
  • 页yèpageTraditional: 頁
    Dictionary sensesleaf
    • 请读第四十九页。qǐng dú dì sì shí jiǔ yèPlease read out page 49.
    • 你每天必须学习五页。nǐ měi tiān bì xū xué xí wǔ yèYou must study five pages per day.
  • 不见bú jiànto disappear; to be missingTraditional: 不見
    Dictionary sensesnot to see; not to meet; to have disappeared
    • 我听不见你。wǒ tīng bù jiàn nǐI can't hear you.
    • 很久不见了！hěn jiǔ bù jiàn leLong time no see!
  • 干净gān jìngcleanTraditional: 乾淨
    Dictionary sensesneat
    • 你的房间真干净。nǐ de fáng jiān zhēn gān jìngYour room is really clean.
    • 这张桌子是干净的。zhè zhāng zhuō zi shì gān jìng deThis table is clean.
  • 放心fàng xīnto feel at ease; not to worry
    Dictionary sensesto feel relieved; to feel reassured; to be at ease
  • 草cǎograssMeasure word: 棵、撮、株Traditional: 艸
    Dictionary sensesstraw; manuscript; draft (of a document); careless
    • 马吃草。mǎ chī cǎoHorses eat grass.
    • 这几种动物是吃草的。zhè jǐ zhǒng dòng wù shì chī cǎo deThese animals feed on grass.
  • 甜tiánsweet
    • 你要什么甜点？nǐ yào shén me tián diǎnWhat dessert do you want?
    • 我要一些甜的东西。wǒ yào yì xiē tián de dōng xiI want something sweet.
  • 安静ān jìngquiet; peacefulTraditional: 安靜
    Dictionary sensescalm
    • 请安静。qǐng ān jìngPlease be quiet.
    • 他告诉了学生要安静。tā gào su le xué sheng yào ān jìngHe told the students to be quiet.
  • 牙yátoothMeasure word: 颗
    Dictionary sensesivory
    • 我牙疼。wǒ yá téngMy teeth hurt.
    • 他们都在西班牙吗？tā men dōu zài xī bān yá maAre they all in Spain?
  • 羊yángsheep; goatMeasure word: 头、只
  • 作业zuò yèhomeworkMeasure word: 个Traditional: 作業
    Dictionary sensesschool assignment; work; task; operation
    • 我正在做我的作业。wǒ zhèng zài zuò wǒ de zuò yèI'm doing my homework.
    • 你做完你的作业了吗？nǐ zuò wán nǐ de zuò yè le maAre you done with your homework yet?
  • 瘦shòuthin; tight (clothes)
    Dictionary sensesto lose weight; (of clothing) tight; (of meat) lean; (of land) unproductive
    • 她很瘦。tā hěn shòuShe's skinny.
    • 她太瘦了。tā tài shòu leShe was so thin.
  • 啤酒pí jiǔbeerMeasure word: 杯、瓶、罐
    Dictionary sensesbeer (loanword)
    • 谁在喝啤酒？shéi zài hē pí jiǔWho's drinking beer?
    • 这里没有啤酒。zhè li méi yǒu pí jiǔThere's no beer here.
