  • 蛋糕dàn gāocakeMeasure word: 块、个
    • 那有两块蛋糕。nà yǒu liǎng kuài dàn gāoThere were two cakes.
    • 你们自己吃蛋糕。nǐ men zì jǐ chī dàn gāoPlease help yourself to the cake.
  • 新鲜xīn xiānfreshTraditional: 新鮮
    Dictionary sensesfresh (experience, food etc); freshness; novel; uncommon
    • 面包不新鲜。miàn bāo bù xīn xiānThe bread isn't fresh.
    • 有什么新鲜事吗？yǒu shén me xīn xiān shì maWhat's new?
  • 难过nán guòsadTraditional: 難過
    Dictionary sensesto feel sad; to feel unwell; (of life) to be difficult
    • 我很难过。wǒ hěn nán guòI'm sad.
    • 她看来有点难过。tā kàn lai yǒu diǎn nán guòShe looked a little sad.
  • 不久bù jiǔsoon; not long after
    Dictionary sensesnot long (after); before too long; soon after
    • 他们刚结婚不久。tā men gāng jié hūn bù jiǔThey married recently.
  • 客人kè rénguestMeasure word: 位
    Dictionary sensesvisitor; customer; client
    • 你有客人。nǐ yǒu kè rénYou have a visitor.
    • 做我的客人吧。zuò wǒ de kè rén baBe my guest.
  • 常常cháng chángoften
    Dictionary sensesfrequently
    • 她常常开我的玩笑。tā cháng cháng kāi wǒ de wán xiàoShe would often play tricks on me.
    • 你常常去电影院吗？nǐ cháng cháng qù diàn yǐng yuàn maDo you go to the movies often?
  • 平时píng shíusually; normallyTraditional: 平時
    Dictionary sensesordinarily; in normal times; in peacetime
    • 今天他比平时起得早。jīn tiān tā bǐ píng shí qǐ de zǎoHe woke up earlier than usual today.
  • 男生nán shēngboy; male student
    Dictionary sensesschoolboy; guy (young adult male)
    • 这个班有多少男生？zhè ge bān yǒu duō shao nán shēngHow many boys are in this class?
  • 碗wǎnbowlMeasure word: 只、个Traditional: 㼝
    Dictionary sensescup
    • 我们有几个碗？wǒ men yǒu jǐ gè wǎnHow many bowls do we have?
    • 我的工作是洗碗。wǒ de gōng zuò shì xǐ wǎnMy job is to wash dishes.
  • 懂得dǒng deto understand; to know
    Dictionary sensesto comprehend
    • 我不懂得游泳。wǒ bù dǒng de yóu yǒngI don't know how to swim.
    • 他们懂得读这几个字吗？tā men dǒng de dú zhè jǐ gè zì maAre they able to read these words?
  • 好久hǎo jiǔa long time
    Dictionary sensesquite a while
    • 好久不见。hǎo jiǔ bù jiànLong time no see.
    • 好久不见！hǎo jiǔ bù jiànLong time no see!
  • 夏天xià tiānsummerMeasure word: 个
    • 夏天过去了。xià tiān guò qu leSummer is over.
    • 今年夏天很冷。jīn nián xià tiān hěn lěngSummer is cold this year.
  • 以外yǐ wàiapart from; beyond
    Dictionary sensesother than; except for; external; outside of
  • 数学shù xuémathsTraditional: 數學
    Dictionary sensesmathematics
    • 你数学好吗？nǐ shù xué hǎo maAre you good at math?
    • 我想学数学。wǒ xiǎng xué shù xuéI want to study math.
  • 刷shuāto brush; brush
    Dictionary sensesa brush; to paint; to daub; to scrub
    • 你刷过牙没有？nǐ shuā guò yá méi yǒuDid you brush your teeth?
    • 他知道怎么刷牙。tā zhī dào zěn me shuā yáHe knows how to brush his teeth.
  • 练习liàn xíto practise; exerciseMeasure word: 个Traditional: 練習
    Dictionary sensesto practice; drill; practice
    • 是的，你需要每天练习。shì de nǐ xū yào měi tiān liàn xíYes, you need to practice every day.
    • 你只要每天练习就可以了。nǐ zhǐ yào měi tiān liàn xí jiù kě yǐ leYou have only to practice every day.
  • 新年xīn niánNew YearMeasure word: 个
    • 新年快乐！xīn nián kuài lèHappy New Year!
    • 你怎么过新年？nǐ zěn me guò xīn niánHow do you spend the New Year?
  • 运动员yùn dòng yuánathleteMeasure word: 名、个Traditional: 運動員
  • 姐妹jiě mèisisters
    Dictionary sensessiblings; sister (school, city etc)
    • 他两个姐妹都很漂亮。tā liǎng gè jiě mèi dōu hěn piào liangBoth his sisters are beautiful.
    • 几乎没有人认为我们是姐妹。jī hū méi yǒu rén rèn wéi wǒ men shì jiě mèiAlmost nobody thinks we're sisters.
  • 南方nán fāngthe south
    Dictionary sensessouth; southern direction; (in China) southern regions, often referring to areas south of the Yangtze River
