  • 长zhǎngto grow; head; chiefTraditional: 長
    Dictionary senseselder; to develop
  • 地方dì fangplaceMeasure word: 处、个、块
    Dictionary sensesarea; space; room; territory
    • 我喜欢那个地方。wǒ xǐ huan nà ge dì fangI like that place.
    • 那是个好地方吗？nà shì gè hǎo dì fang maIs it a nice place?
  • 最后zuì hòulast; finallyTraditional: 最後
    Dictionary sensesfinal; ultimate; in the end
    • 周日是一周的最后一天。zhōu rì shì yī zhōu de zuì hòu yī tiānSunday is the last day of the week.
    • 快点准备！明天是最后一天了。kuài diǎn zhǔn bèi míng tiān shì zuì hòu yī tiān leBetter get preparing! Tomorrow will be the last day.
  • 老lǎoold; (before surnames, friendly)
    Dictionary sensesprefix used before the surname of a person or a numeral indicating the order of birth of the children in a family or to indicate affection or familiarity; old (of people); venerable (person); experienced
    • 老问题。lǎo wèn tíThe same old problem.
    • 猫老了。māo lǎo leThe cat is old.
  • 其他qí tāother
    Dictionary senses(sth or sb) else; the rest
    • 其他学生都笑了。qí tā xué sheng dōu xiào leThe other pupils laughed.
    • 其他人没有兴趣。qí tā rén méi yǒu xìng qùThe others can't be bothered.
  • 行xíngOK; to be all right; to go
    Dictionary sensesto travel; (literary) trip; journey; visit
    • 你行吗？nǐ xíng maCan you?
    • 六点您行吗？liù diǎn nín xíng maIs 6 o'clock OK?
  • 比赛bǐ sàimatch; competition; to competeMeasure word: 场、次Traditional: 比賽
    Dictionary sensescompetition (sports etc)
    • 我喜欢在电视上看足球比赛。wǒ xǐ huan zài diàn shì shàng kàn zú qiú bǐ sàiI enjoy watching soccer on TV.
  • 然后rán hòuthen; after thatTraditional: 然後
    Dictionary sensesafterwards
    • 对，然后呢？duì rán hòu neYes, and?
    • 是的，然后呢？shì de rán hòu neYes, and?
  • 担心dān xīnto worryTraditional: 擔心
    Dictionary sensesanxious; worried; uneasy; to be anxious
    • 别担心。bié dān xīnDon't fret.
    • 不要担心。bú yào dān xīnDon't fret.
  • 放fàngto put; to let go
    Dictionary sensesto place; to release; to free
    • 放开他。fàng kāi tāRelease him.
    • 您往里放了什么？nín wǎng lǐ fàng le shén meWhat did you put in it?
  • 先xiānfirst; earlier
    Dictionary sensesbeforehand; at first; originally
    • 谁先来？shéi xiān láiWhose go is it first?
    • 先谢谢了。xiān xiè xie leThanks in advance.
  • 或huòor
    Dictionary sensesmaybe; perhaps; might; possibly
    • 下周我会给你写信或打电话的。xià zhōu wǒ huì gěi nǐ xiě xìn huò dǎ diàn huà deI'll write or phone you next week.
  • 得到dé dàoto get; to receive
    Dictionary sensesto obtain
    • 我听得到。wǒ tīng dé dàoI can hear it.
    • 我们什么也没有得到。wǒ men shén me yě méi yǒu dé dàoWe got nothing at all.
  • 张zhāngmeasure word for flat things; to openTraditional: 張
    Dictionary sensesto open up; to spread; sheet of paper; classifier for flat objects, sheet
    • 我有三张票。wǒ yǒu sān zhāng piàoI have three tickets.
    • 这张桌子不好。zhè zhāng zhuō zi bù hǎoThis table is not good.
  • 关系guān xirelationship; connectionMeasure word: 个Traditional: 關係
    Dictionary sensesrelation; to concern; to affect; to have to do with
    • 我和朋友关系很好。wǒ hé péng you guān xi hěn hǎoI have a good relationship with my friends.
  • 头tounoun suffix (木头, 前头)Traditional: 頭
    Dictionary sensessuffix for nouns
  • 总zǒngalways; totalTraditional: 總
    Dictionary sensesoverall; to sum up; in every case; invariably
    • 半个面包总比没有好。bàn gè miàn bāo zǒng bǐ méi yǒu hǎoHalf a loaf is better than none.
    • 一月总会在这里下雪。yī yuè zǒng huì zài zhè li xià xuěWe have snow in January.
  • 了解liǎo jiěto understand; to find out
    Dictionary sensesto realize
    • 我不了解他。wǒ bù liǎo jiě tāI can't figure him out.
    • 她非常了解汉字。tā fēi cháng liǎo jiě Hàn zìShe knows the Chinese characters very well.
  • 过去guò qu(after a verb: over, past, away)Traditional: 過去
    Dictionary senses(verb suffix)
  • 参加cān jiāto take part in; to joinTraditional: 參加
    Dictionary sensesto participate; to take part
    • 参加吧！cān jiā baEnlist!
    • 我想参加。wǒ xiǎng cān jiāI want to participate.
