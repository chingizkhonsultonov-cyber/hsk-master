  • 愿意yuàn yìto be willingTraditional: 願意
    Dictionary sensesto wish; to want; ready; willing (to do sth)
    • 你愿意等吗？nǐ yuàn yì děng maDo you want to wait?
    • 你愿意来吗？nǐ yuàn yì lái maWould you like to come?
  • 音乐yīn yuèmusicMeasure word: 张、曲、段Traditional: 音樂
    • 你爱音乐吗？nǐ ài yīn yuè maDo you love music?
    • 我不懂音乐。wǒ bù dǒng yīn yuèI don't understand music.
  • 坚持jiān chíto keep on; to insistTraditional: 堅持
    Dictionary sensesto persevere with; to persist in; to insist on
    • 她坚持认为那是我的错。tā jiān chí rèn wéi nà shì wǒ de cuòShe insisted that it was my fault.
  • 感到gǎn dàoto feel
    Dictionary sensesto sense; to perceive
    • 我对我的工作感到满意。wǒ duì wǒ de gōng zuò gǎn dào mǎn yìI'm content with my job.
  • 清楚qīng chuclear
    Dictionary sensesdistinct; to understand thoroughly; to be clear about
    • 我不太清楚。wǒ bù tài qīng chuI'm not quite sure.
    • 你很清楚我想要什么。nǐ hěn qīng chu wǒ xiǎng yào shén meYou know exactly what I want.
  • 照片zhào piànphotoMeasure word: 张、套、幅
    Dictionary sensesphotograph; picture
    • 这张照片真漂亮！zhè zhāng zhào piàn zhēn piào liangWhat a beautiful picture!
    • 这些照片是她的。zhè xiē zhào piàn shì tā deThose photos are hers.
  • 银行yín hángbankMeasure word: 家、个Traditional: 銀行
    • 我在银行工作。wǒ zài yín háng gōng zuòI work in a bank.
    • 他在银行换钱。tā zài yín háng huàn qiánHe exchanges money at the bank.
  • 故事gù shistory
    Dictionary sensesnarrative; tale
    • 这个故事是真的。zhè ge gù shi shì zhēn deThis story is true.
    • 谁写了这些故事？shéi xiě le zhè xiē gù shiWho wrote these stories?
  • 看来kàn laiit seemsTraditional: 看來
    Dictionary sensesapparently; it seems that
    • 她看来有钱。tā kàn lai yǒu qiánShe seems rich.
    • 看来，他很快乐。kàn lai tā hěn kuài lèIt seems that he's happy.
  • 酒jiǔalcohol; wineMeasure word: 杯、瓶、罐
    Dictionary senseswine (esp. rice wine); liquor; spirits; alcoholic beverage
    • 我有酒。wǒ yǒu jiǔI have wine.
    • 不要喝酒。bú yào hē jiǔDon't drink alcohol.
  • 欢迎huān yíngto welcome; welcomeTraditional: 歡迎
    • 欢迎回来。huān yíng huí laiWelcome back.
    • 中国欢迎你！Zhōng guó huān yíng nǐWelcome to China!
  • 一般yì bānusually; ordinary
    Dictionary sensessame; so-so; common; general
    • 你一般几点下班？nǐ yī bān jǐ diǎn xià bānWhen do you usually get off work?
    • 我一般六点起床。wǒ yī bān liù diǎn qǐ chuángI usually get up at six.
  • 可爱kě àicute; lovelyTraditional: 可愛
    Dictionary sensesadorable
    • 好可爱！hǎo kě àiHow cute!
    • 真可爱！zhēn kě àiCute!
  • 新闻xīn wénnewsMeasure word: 条、个Traditional: 新聞
    • 你看新闻吗？nǐ kàn xīn wén maDo you watch the news?
  • 怎样zěn yànghowTraditional: 怎樣
    Dictionary senseswhat kind
    • 那又怎样？nà yòu zěn yàngAnd?
    • 去看电影怎样？qù kàn diàn yǐng zěn yàngHow about going to a movie?
  • 收shōuto receive; to collect
    Dictionary sensesto accept; to put away; to restrain
    • 我不能收下这个礼物。wǒ bù néng shōu xià zhè ge lǐ wùI can't accept this gift.
    • 对不起。我收回我的话。duì bu qǐ wǒ shōu huí wǒ de huàI'm sorry. I take back my words.
  • 声音shēng yīnsound; voiceMeasure word: 个Traditional: 聲音
    • 这是什么声音？zhè shì shén me shēng yīnWhat's this noise?
    • 他的声音听着很舒服。tā de shēng yīn tīng zhe hěn shū fuHis voice is pleasant to listen to.
  • 马上mǎ shàngat once; right awayTraditional: 馬上
    Dictionary sensesimmediately; on horseback (i.e. by military force)
    • 马上回来。mǎ shàng huí laiReturn at once.
    • 我马上就来。wǒ mǎ shàng jiù láiI'll be right with you.
  • 变成biàn chéngto turn into; to becomeTraditional: 變成
    Dictionary sensesto change into
    • 她的女儿已经变成一个漂亮的女人。tā de nǚ ér yǐ jīng biàn chéng yī gè piào liang de nǚ renHer daughter became a beautiful woman.
    • 红绿灯变成绿色了。hóng lǜ dēng biàn chéng lǜ sè leThe light turned green.
  • 受到shòu dàoto receive; to be (praised, affected …)
    Dictionary sensesto receive (praise, an education, punishment etc); to be ...ed (praised, educated, punished etc)
    • 这本书受到学生的欢迎。zhè běn shū shòu dào xué sheng de huān yíngThis book is popular with students.
