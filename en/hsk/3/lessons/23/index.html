  • 上衣shàng yījacket; topMeasure word: 件
    Dictionary sensesupper outer garment
    • 可以看一下那件上衣吗？kě yǐ kàn yí xià nà jiàn shàng yī maCan I take a look at that jacket?
  • 菜单cài dānmenuMeasure word: 份、张Traditional: 菜單
    • 请给我菜单。qǐng gěi wǒ cài dānPlease show me the menu.
  • 年级nián jígrade; year (at school)Measure word: 个Traditional: 年級
    Dictionary sensesyear (in school, college etc)
    • 她是个二年级学生。tā shì gè èr nián jí xué shengShe is a second year student.
    • 我弟弟是个一年级的学生。wǒ dì di shì gè yī nián jí de xué shengMy brother is a first-year student.
  • 搬家bān jiāto move house
    Dictionary sensesto relocate; to remove (sth)
    • 我们去年搬家了。wǒ men qù nián bān jiā leWe moved house last year.
    • 我下个月要搬家了。wǒ xià gè yuè yào bān jiā leI'm moving next month.
  • 笔记bǐ jìnotes; to take notesMeasure word: 本Traditional: 筆記
    Dictionary sensesto take down (in writing); a type of literature consisting mainly of short sketches
  • 外地wài dìanother part of the country
    Dictionary sensesparts of the country other than where one is
    • 我是这里的外地人。wǒ shì zhè li de wài dì rénI'm a stranger here.
  • 休假xiū jiàto be on holiday
    Dictionary sensesto go on vacation; to have a holiday; to take leave
    • 我下个月要休假。wǒ xià gè yuè yào xiū jiàI'll be taking a vacation next month.
  • 前天qián tiānthe day before yesterday
  • 斤jīnjin (half a kilo)
    Dictionary sensescatty; (PRC) weight equal to 500 g; (Tw) weight equal to 600 g; (HK, Malaysia, Singapore) slightly over 604 g
  • 过节guò jiéto celebrate a festivalTraditional: 過節
    Dictionary sensesafter the celebrations (i.e. once the festival is over)
  • 邮箱yóu xiāngmailbox; email addressTraditional: 郵箱
    Dictionary sensespost office box; email; email inbox
    • 邮箱在哪里？yóu xiāng zài nǎ liWhere is the mailbox?
    • 这是我的电子邮箱。zhè shì wǒ de diàn zi yóu xiāngHere is my email address.
  • 晚点wǎn diǎnlate; delayedTraditional: 晚點
    Dictionary senses(of trains etc) late; behind schedule; light dinner
    • 我晚点打给你。wǒ wǎn diǎn dǎ gěi nǐI'll call you later.
  • 照相zhào xiàngto take a photo
    Dictionary sensesto take a photograph
  • 动物园dòng wù yuánzooMeasure word: 个Traditional: 動物園
    • 今天下午你想去动物园吗？jīn tiān xià wǔ nǐ xiǎng qù dòng wù yuán maWould you like to go to the zoo this afternoon?
    • 让我们带孩子们去动物园。ràng wǒ men dài hái zi men qù dòng wù yuánLet's take the children to the zoo.
  • 盘子pán ziplateMeasure word: 个Traditional: 盤子
    Dictionary sensestray; dish
    • 盘子很脏。pán zi hěn zàngThe plate is dirty.
    • 我的盘子在哪？wǒ de pán zi zài nǎWhere's my plate?
  • 草地cǎo dìlawn; meadowMeasure word: 片
    Dictionary sensessod; turf
    • 我们坐在草地上吧。wǒ men zuò zài cǎo dì shàng baLet's sit on the grass.
  • 西瓜xī guāwatermelonMeasure word: 颗、个
    • 这是一种西瓜。zhè shì yī zhǒng xī guāThis is a kind of watermelon.
    • 我很喜欢吃西瓜。wǒ hěn xǐ huan chī xī guāI like to eat watermelon.
  • 铅笔qiān bǐpencilMeasure word: 支、枝、杆Traditional: 鉛筆
    Dictionary senses(lead) pencil
    • 铅笔长吗？qiān bǐ cháng maIs the pencil long?
    • 你有铅笔吗？nǐ yǒu qiān bǐ maDo you have pencils?
  • 认得rèn deto recogniseTraditional: 認得
    Dictionary sensesto recognize; to remember sth (or sb) on seeing it; to know
    • 你认得它吗？nǐ rèn de tā maDo you recognise it?
  • 关机guān jīto switch off (a device)Traditional: 關機
    Dictionary sensesto turn off (a machine or device); to finish shooting a film
    • 他的手机关机了。tā de shǒu jī guān jī leHis cell phone is off.
