  • 习惯xí guànhabit; to be used toMeasure word: 个Traditional: 習慣
    Dictionary sensescustom; usual practice
    • 你会习惯的。nǐ huì xí guàn deYou'll get used to it.
    • 我习惯早起。wǒ xí guàn zǎo qǐI'm used to getting up early.
  • 变化biàn huàchange; to changeTraditional: 變化
    Dictionary senses(intransitive) to change; to vary; change; variation (CL:個|个[ge4])
  • 周末zhōu mòweekendTraditional: 週末
    • 周末见！zhōu mò jiànSee you this weekend!
    • 你周末过得怎么样？nǐ zhōu mò guò de zěn me yàngHow was your weekend?
  • 听说tīng shuōto hear that; it is saidTraditional: 聽說
    Dictionary sensesto hear (sth said); one hears (that); hearsay; listening and speaking
    • 听说你病了。tīng shuō nǐ bìng leI hear you've been sick.
    • 你可能听说过。nǐ kě néng tīng shuō guòYou might've heard of it.
  • 直到zhí dàountil
    • 直到下周。zhí dào xià zhōuUntil next week.
    • 我会在这里等直到他来。wǒ huì zài zhè li děng zhí dào tā láiI will wait here till he comes.
  • 毛máomao (0.1 yuan); hair; fur
    Dictionary sensesfeather; down; wool; mildew
  • 忘记wàng jìto forgetTraditional: 忘記
    • 别忘记买鸡肉。bié wàng jì mǎi jī ròuDon't forget to buy chicken.
    • 我忘记了他的名字。wǒ wàng jì le tā de míng ziI forget his name.
  • 船chuánboat; shipMeasure word: 条、艘、只Traditional: 舩
    Dictionary sensesvessel
    • 你的船不大。nǐ de chuán bù dàYour boat is not big.
    • 他买我的船。tā mǎi wǒ de chuánHe's buying my boat.
  • 养yǎngto raise; to keep (pets)Traditional: 養
    Dictionary sensesto raise (animals); to bring up (children); to support; to give birth
    • 我养过一只猫。wǒ yǎng guò yī zhī māoI had a cat.
    • 他养了几条狗。tā yǎng le jǐ tiáo gǒuHe has dogs.
  • 鸡jīchickenMeasure word: 只Traditional: 雞
    Dictionary sensesfowl; (slang) prostitute
  • 遍biàntime (from start to finish); all overTraditional: 徧
    Dictionary senseseverywhere; classifier for actions: one time
    • 我就觉得我该再检查一遍。wǒ jiù jué de wǒ gāi zài jiǎn chá yī biànI just thought I should double check.
    • 请问你能再告诉我一遍你的名字吗？qǐng wèn nǐ néng zài gào su wǒ yī biàn nǐ de míng zi maCan you please tell me your name once more?
  • 打算dǎ suànto plan; planMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesto intend; to calculate; intention
    • 你打算出国吗？nǐ dǎ suàn chū guó maDo you plan to go abroad?
    • 你打算做什么？nǐ dǎ suàn zuò shén meWhat're you up to?
  • 室shìroom
    Dictionary senseswork unit; grave; scabbard; family or clan
  • 极jíextremelyTraditional: 極
    Dictionary sensespole (geography, physics); utmost; top
  • 咱们zán menwe (including you)Traditional: 咱們
    Dictionary senseswe or us (including both the speaker and the person(s) spoken to); (dialect) I or me; (dialect) (in a coaxing or familiar way) you; also pr. [za2 men5]
    • 咱们都是学生。zán men dōu shì xué shengBoth you and I are students.
    • 这都是咱们听说的。zhè dōu shì zán men tīng shuō deThat’s what we heard.
  • 小心xiǎo xīnto be careful
    Dictionary sensesto take care
    • 小心脚下。xiǎo xīn jiǎo xiàWatch your step.
    • 小心打开。xiǎo xīn dǎ kāiOpen it carefully.
  • 怎么办zěn me bànwhat to doTraditional: 怎麼辦
    Dictionary senseswhat's to be done
    • 怎么办？zěn me bànWhat to do?
    • 怎么办！zěn me bànAnd how!
  • 动物dòng wùanimalMeasure word: 只、群、个Traditional: 動物
    • 我们在画动物。wǒ men zài huà dòng wùWe are drawing animals.
    • 人是一种动物。rén shì yī zhǒng dòng wùHumans are animals.
  • 不行bù xíngwon't do; no good
    Dictionary sensesbe out of the question; be no good; not work; not be capable
    • 那天我不行。nà tiān wǒ bù xíngThat's a bad day for me.
    • 他要不行了。tā yào bù xíng leHe's on his last legs.
  • 饿èhungryTraditional: 餓
    Dictionary sensesto starve (sb)
    • 我好饿！wǒ hǎo èAm I hungry!
    • 我饿了！wǒ è leI'm hungry!
