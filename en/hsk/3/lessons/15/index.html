  • 爬páto climb; to crawl
    Dictionary sensesto get up or sit up
    • 猫会爬树，狗不会。māo huì pá shù gǒu bù huìCats can climb trees, but dogs can't.
    • 上周末我爬山去了。shàng zhōu mò wǒ pá shān qù leOn the weekend, I climbed the mountain.
  • 差不多chà bu duōalmost; about the same
    Dictionary sensesnearly; more or less; good enough
    • 差不多六点了。chà bu duō liù diǎn leIt's almost six.
    • 我差不多完成了。wǒ chà bu duō wán chéng leI'm almost done.
  • 冰bīngice; icyMeasure word: 块
    Dictionary sensesto chill sth; (of an object or substance) to feel cold; (of a person) cold; unfriendly
    • 这是冰块。zhè shì bīng kuàiThis is ice.
    • 真的很冰冷。zhēn de hěn bīng lěngIt's frozen hard.
  • 糖tángsugar; sweetsMeasure word: 颗、块
    Dictionary sensescandy
    • 没有糖。méi yǒu tángThere's no sugar.
    • 我们没有糖。wǒ men méi yǒu tángWe have no sugar.
  • 以下yǐ xiàbelow; or less
    Dictionary sensesthat level or lower; that amount or less; the following
  • 合适hé shìsuitable; fittingTraditional: 合適
    Dictionary sensesappropriate
  • 号码hào mǎnumberMeasure word: 堆、个Traditional: 號碼
    • 给我你的电话号码。gěi wǒ nǐ de diàn huà hào mǎGive me your phone number.
    • 你电话号码是多少？nǐ diàn huà hào mǎ shì duō shaoWhat's your phone number?
  • 出生chū shēngto be born
    • 她几点出生的？tā jǐ diǎn chū shēng deWhat time was she born?
    • 我出生在上海。wǒ chū shēng zài shàng hǎiI was born in Shanghai.
  • 生气shēng qìto get angryTraditional: 生氣
    Dictionary sensesto be furious; vitality; liveliness
    • 我很生气！wǒ hěn shēng qìI'm angry!
    • 他非常生气。tā fēi cháng shēng qìHe's very angry.
  • 以上yǐ shàngabove; or more
    Dictionary sensesthat level or higher; that amount or more; the above-mentioned; (used to indicate that one has completed one's remarks) That is all.
  • 名单míng dānlist of namesTraditional: 名單
  • 公园gōng yuánparkMeasure word: 座Traditional: 公園
    Dictionary sensespark (for public recreation)
    • 公园里有很多人。gōng yuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō rénThere are many people in the park.
    • 那里也有个公园。nà li yě yǒu gè gōng yuánThere's also a park there.
  • 热情rè qíngwarm; enthusiasticTraditional: 熱情
    Dictionary sensescordial; passion; passionate; passionately
  • 到处dào chùeverywhereTraditional: 到處
  • 一会儿yí huì ra moment; a while; now … now …Traditional: 一會兒
    Dictionary sensesin a moment; now...now...; also pr. [yi1 hui3 r5]
  • 的话de huàifTraditional: 的話
    Dictionary sensesif (coming after a conditional clause)
    • 请相信我的话。qǐng xiāng xìn wǒ de huàJust take my word for it.
    • 他不相信本的话。tā bù xiāng xìn běn de huàHe didn't believe Ben's words.
  • 骑qíto rideTraditional: 騎
    Dictionary sensesto sit astride; to ride (a horse, bike etc); classifier for saddle horses
    • 你会骑马吗？nǐ huì qí mǎ maCan you ride a horse?
    • 我不会骑车。wǒ bù huì qí chēI can't ride a bike.
  • 报纸bào zhǐnewspaperMeasure word: 份、期、张Traditional: 報紙
    Dictionary sensesnewsprint
    • 我不看报纸。wǒ bù kàn bào zhǐI don't read the newspapers.
    • 我不太看报纸。wǒ bù tài kàn bào zhǐI don't read newspapers much.
  • 河hériver
    Dictionary sensesriver (CL:條|条[tiao2],道[dao4])
    • 他坐在河边。tā zuò zài hé biānHe sat next to the river.
    • 河里没有水了。hé lǐ méi yǒu shuǐ leThere was no water in the river.
  • 搬bānto move (something heavy); to move house
    Dictionary sensesto move (i.e. relocate oneself); to move (sth relatively heavy or bulky); to shift; to copy indiscriminately
