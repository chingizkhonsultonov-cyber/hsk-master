Renting a flat

1. Viewing a flat

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà这套房子一个月多少钱？Zhè tào fángzi yí ge yuè duōshao qián?How much is this flat a month?
  2. 房东 · fángdōng三千五，包括水电费。Sānqiān wǔ, bāokuò shuǐdiànfèi.3,500, including water and electricity.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà离地铁站近吗？Lí dìtiězhàn jìn ma?Is it close to the metro?
  4. 房东 · fángdōng很近，走路只要五分钟，附近还有超市。Hěn jìn, zǒulù zhǐ yào wǔ fēnzhōng, fùjìn hái yǒu chāoshì.Very close, five minutes on foot, and there's a supermarket nearby.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà房间有点儿小，不过很干净。我能考虑一下吗？Fángjiān yǒudiǎnr xiǎo, búguò hěn gānjìng. Wǒ néng kǎolǜ yíxià ma?The room is a bit small but very clean. Can I think it over?
  6. 房东 · fángdōng当然可以，明天给我回电话就行。Dāngrán kěyǐ, míngtiān gěi wǒ huí diànhuà jiù xíng.Of course; just call me back tomorrow.

2. Signing the contract

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我决定租这套房子了。Wǒ juédìng zū zhè tào fángzi le.I've decided to rent this flat.
  2. 房东 · fángdōng太好了。你打算住多长时间？Tài hǎo le. Nǐ dǎsuàn zhù duō cháng shíjiān?Great. How long do you plan to stay?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà先住一年。Xiān zhù yì nián.A year to start with.
  4. 房东 · fángdōng好，要先付一个月的房租，再加一个月的押金。Hǎo, yào xiān fù yí ge yuè de fángzū, zài jiā yí ge yuè de yājīn.OK. You pay one month's rent up front, plus a month's deposit.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà没问题。我什么时候能搬进来？Méi wèntí. Wǒ shénme shíhou néng bān jìnlai?No problem. When can I move in?
  6. 房东 · fángdōng这个周末就可以。Zhège zhōumò jiù kěyǐ.This weekend.

A job interview

1. Tell us about yourself

  1. 经理 · jīnglǐ请你先简单介绍一下自己。Qǐng nǐ xiān jiǎndān jièshào yíxià zìjǐ.Please briefly introduce yourself first.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我叫王明，去年大学毕业，学的是电脑。Wǒ jiào Wáng Míng, qùnián dàxué bìyè, xué de shì diànnǎo.I'm Wang Ming. I graduated last year in computing.
  3. 经理 · jīnglǐ你为什么想来我们公司？Nǐ wèishénme xiǎng lái wǒmen gōngsī?Why do you want to join our company?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng因为贵公司很有名，而且我对这个工作很感兴趣。Yīnwèi guì gōngsī hěn yǒumíng, érqiě wǒ duì zhège gōngzuò hěn gǎn xìngqù.Your company is well known, and I'm very interested in this job.
  5. 经理 · jīnglǐ你会说英语吗？Nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma?Do you speak English?
  6. 王明 · Wáng Míng会，我的英语水平还可以。Huì, wǒ de Yīngyǔ shuǐpíng hái kěyǐ.Yes, my English is decent.

2. After the interview

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì面试怎么样？Miànshì zěnmeyàng?How did the interview go?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我有点儿紧张，不过回答得还不错。Wǒ yǒudiǎnr jǐnzhāng, búguò huídá de hái búcuò.I was a bit nervous, but I answered quite well.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì他们什么时候通知你？Tāmen shénme shíhou tōngzhī nǐ?When will they let you know?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng说是一个星期以内。Shuō shì yí ge xīngqī yǐnèi.They said within a week.
  5. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì别担心，我相信你一定没问题！Bié dānxīn, wǒ xiāngxìn nǐ yídìng méi wèntí!Don't worry, I'm sure you'll be fine!

At the bank

1. Opening an account

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你好，我想办一张银行卡。Nǐ hǎo, wǒ xiǎng bàn yì zhāng yínhángkǎ.Hello, I'd like to get a bank card.
  2. 银行职员 · yínháng zhíyuán请给我看一下您的护照。Qǐng gěi wǒ kàn yíxià nín de hùzhào.Please show me your passport.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà给您。需要多长时间？Gěi nín. Xūyào duō cháng shíjiān?Here you are. How long will it take?
  4. 银行职员 · yínháng zhíyuán大概十分钟。请在这儿写上您的电话号码。Dàgài shí fēnzhōng. Qǐng zài zhèr xiě shang nín de diànhuà hàomǎ.About ten minutes. Please write your phone number here.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà好的。Hǎo de.OK.

2. Changing money

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我想把美元换成人民币。Wǒ xiǎng bǎ Měiyuán huàn chéng Rénmínbì.I'd like to change US dollars into renminbi.
  2. 银行职员 · yínháng zhíyuán您要换多少？Nín yào huàn duōshao?How much would you like to change?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng五百美元。今天一美元换多少人民币？Wǔbǎi Měiyuán. Jīntiān yì Měiyuán huàn duōshao Rénmínbì?Five hundred dollars. What's today's rate?
  4. 银行职员 · yínháng zhíyuán七块一。一共三千五百五十块，请您数一下。Qī kuài yī. Yígòng sānqiān wǔbǎi wǔshí kuài, qǐng nín shǔ yíxià.7.1. That's 3,550 yuan in total; please count it.

At the library

1. Borrowing books

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà请问，这本书可以借多长时间？Qǐngwèn, zhè běn shū kěyǐ jiè duō cháng shíjiān?Excuse me, how long can I borrow this book?
  2. 图书馆员 · túshūguǎnyuán一个月。到时候没看完，还可以再借一次。Yí ge yuè. Dào shíhou méi kàn wán, hái kěyǐ zài jiè yí cì.A month. If you haven't finished, you can renew it once.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà一次最多能借几本？Yí cì zuì duō néng jiè jǐ běn?How many can I borrow at once?
  4. 图书馆员 · túshūguǎnyuán五本。别忘了按时还书。Wǔ běn. Bié wàng le ànshí huán shū.Five. Don't forget to return them on time.

2. Please be quiet

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng小声点儿，这里是图书馆。Xiǎo shēng diǎnr, zhèlǐ shì túshūguǎn.Keep it down, this is a library.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng对不起。我找不到我要的那本书。Duìbuqǐ. Wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ yào de nà běn shū.Sorry. I can't find the book I need.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng你可以用电脑查一下在哪儿。Nǐ kěyǐ yòng diànnǎo chá yíxià zài nǎr.You can look it up on the computer.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好主意，我去试试。Hǎo zhǔyi, wǒ qù shìshi.Good idea; I'll try.

Keeping fit

1. Joining a gym

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我最近胖了，想去健身房锻炼。Wǒ zuìjìn pàng le, xiǎng qù jiànshēnfáng duànliàn.I've put on weight lately; I want to work out at a gym.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà我家附近有一个，环境很好。Wǒ jiā fùjìn yǒu yí ge, huánjìng hěn hǎo.There's one near my home; it's very nice.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì贵不贵？Guì bu guì?Is it expensive?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà一年两千块，比别的地方便宜。Yì nián liǎngqiān kuài, bǐ bié de dìfang piányi.Two thousand a year, cheaper than other places.
  5. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那我们一起去吧，有人一起就不容易放弃。Nà wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba, yǒu rén yìqǐ jiù bù róngyì fàngqì.Let's go together — it's harder to give up with a partner.

2. Morning run

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你每天早上都跑步吗？Nǐ měi tiān zǎoshang dōu pǎobù ma?Do you run every morning?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng对，一般跑半个小时。Duì, yìbān pǎo bàn ge xiǎoshí.Yes, usually for half an hour.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我也想跑，可是起不来。Wǒ yě xiǎng pǎo, kěshì qǐ bu lái.I want to run too, but I can't get up.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你晚上早点儿睡，习惯了就好了。Nǐ wǎnshang zǎo diǎnr shuì, xíguàn le jiù hǎo le.Go to bed earlier; once you get used to it, it's easy.

Cooking together

1. Tomato and eggs

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà西红柿炒鸡蛋怎么做？Xīhóngshì chǎo jīdàn zěnme zuò?How do you make tomato and egg stir-fry?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng很简单。先把鸡蛋炒好，放在一边。Hěn jiǎndān. Xiān bǎ jīdàn chǎo hǎo, fàng zài yì biān.It's simple. First fry the eggs and set them aside.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà然后呢？Ránhòu ne?Then what?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng然后炒西红柿，最后把鸡蛋放进去，加一点儿盐和糖。Ránhòu chǎo xīhóngshì, zuìhòu bǎ jīdàn fàng jìnqu, jiā yìdiǎnr yán hé táng.Then fry the tomatoes, finally add the eggs back, plus a little salt and sugar.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà要放糖？我第一次听说。Yào fàng táng? Wǒ dì-yī cì tīngshuō.Sugar? That's the first I've heard of it.

2. Too salty

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你尝尝，我做的汤怎么样？Nǐ chángchang, wǒ zuò de tāng zěnmeyàng?Have a taste. How's my soup?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì嗯……有点儿咸。Ǹg…… yǒudiǎnr xián.Hmm… a bit salty.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng是吗？我可能放了两次盐。Shì ma? Wǒ kěnéng fàng le liǎng cì yán.Really? I might have added salt twice.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì没关系，再加点儿水就好了。Méi guānxi, zài jiā diǎnr shuǐ jiù hǎo le.Never mind; add some water and it'll be fine.

Making an appointment

1. Booking a doctor

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我想挂一个明天的号。Wǒ xiǎng guà yí ge míngtiān de hào.I'd like to book an appointment for tomorrow.
  2. 护士 · hùshi您哪儿不舒服？Nín nǎr bù shūfu?What's the problem?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我的牙疼了好几天了。Wǒ de yá téng le hǎo jǐ tiān le.My tooth has hurt for several days.
  4. 护士 · hùshi明天上午十点可以吗？Míngtiān shàngwǔ shí diǎn kěyǐ ma?Is ten tomorrow morning OK?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng可以，谢谢。Kěyǐ, xièxie.Yes, thanks.

2. Changing the time

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我们约的是明天下午三点吧？Wǒmen yuē de shì míngtiān xiàwǔ sān diǎn ba?We agreed on three tomorrow afternoon, right?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng对。怎么了？Duì. Zěnme le?Yes. What's up?
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我突然要开会，能不能改到四点半？Wǒ tūrán yào kāihuì, néng bu néng gǎi dào sì diǎn bàn?I suddenly have a meeting. Could we change it to half past four?
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng没问题，四点半见。Méi wèntí, sì diǎn bàn jiàn.No problem, see you at half past four.

Moving house

1. Packing

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà东西太多了，我一个人搬不完。Dōngxi tài duō le, wǒ yí ge rén bān bu wán.There's too much stuff; I can't move it all by myself.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我周六有空，可以来帮你。Wǒ zhōuliù yǒu kòng, kěyǐ lái bāng nǐ.I'm free on Saturday; I can help you.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà太好了！我先把书放进箱子里。Tài hǎo le! Wǒ xiān bǎ shū fàng jìn xiāngzi li.Great! I'll put the books in boxes first.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng我再叫一辆车，一次就能搬完。Wǒ zài jiào yí liàng chē, yí cì jiù néng bān wán.I'll book a van too; we can do it in one trip.

2. The new flat

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你的新家真亮！Nǐ de xīn jiā zhēn liàng!Your new home is so bright!
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà是啊，每个房间都有大窗户。Shì a, měi ge fángjiān dōu yǒu dà chuānghu.Yes, every room has big windows.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng冰箱和洗衣机都是新的吗？Bīngxiāng hé xǐyījī dōu shì xīn de ma?Are the fridge and washing machine new?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà冰箱是新的，洗衣机是我自己带来的。Bīngxiāng shì xīn de, xǐyījī shì wǒ zìjǐ dài lái de.The fridge is new; the washing machine I brought myself.

Neighbours

1. Too noisy

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng不好意思，已经十二点了，你们的音乐能小一点儿吗？Bù hǎoyìsi, yǐjīng shí'èr diǎn le, nǐmen de yīnyuè néng xiǎo yìdiǎnr ma?Sorry, it's already midnight. Could you turn the music down?
  2. 邻居 · línjū真对不起，我们没注意时间。Zhēn duìbuqǐ, wǒmen méi zhùyì shíjiān.So sorry, we didn't notice the time.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng没关系，明天我得早起上班。Méi guānxi, míngtiān wǒ děi zǎo qǐ shàngbān.That's OK; I have to get up early for work.
  4. 邻居 · línjū明白，我们马上关了。Míngbai, wǒmen mǎshàng guān le.Understood; we'll turn it off now.

2. A friendly neighbour

  1. 邻居 · línjū你是新搬来的吧？我住在你对面。Nǐ shì xīn bān lái de ba? Wǒ zhù zài nǐ duìmiàn.You've just moved in, right? I live opposite.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà您好！我叫安娜，以后请多关照。Nín hǎo! Wǒ jiào Ānnà, yǐhòu qǐng duō guānzhào.Hello! I'm Anna. Nice to have you as a neighbour.
  3. 邻居 · línjū有什么需要帮忙的，就来找我。Yǒu shénme xūyào bāngmáng de, jiù lái zhǎo wǒ.If you need any help, just come to me.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà谢谢您，您太客气了。Xièxie nín, nín tài kèqi le.Thank you, that's very kind.

A trip to the mountains

1. Planning a hike

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这个周末我们去爬山，怎么样？Zhège zhōumò wǒmen qù pá shān, zěnmeyàng?How about climbing a mountain this weekend?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng好啊！天气预报说周六是晴天。Hǎo a! Tiānqì yùbào shuō zhōuliù shì qíngtiān.Sure! The forecast says Saturday will be sunny.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng那我们早上七点出发，带上水和面包。Nà wǒmen zǎoshang qī diǎn chūfā, dài shang shuǐ hé miànbāo.Then let's set off at seven, with water and bread.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng还要穿舒服的鞋。Hái yào chuān shūfu de xié.And wear comfortable shoes.

2. At the top

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng终于爬上来了！累死我了。Zhōngyú pá shànglai le! Lèi sǐ wǒ le.We finally made it up! I'm exhausted.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你看，下面的风景多美啊！Nǐ kàn, xiàmiàn de fēngjǐng duō měi a!Look how beautiful the view is down there!
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng真的很值得。我们照张相吧。Zhēn de hěn zhídé. Wǒmen zhào zhāng xiàng ba.It was really worth it. Let's take a photo.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好，一、二、三！Hǎo, yī, èr, sān!OK, one, two, three!

Returning something

1. The wrong size

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你好，我在网上买的鞋小了，可以换吗？Nǐ hǎo, wǒ zài wǎng shang mǎi de xié xiǎo le, kěyǐ huàn ma?Hello, the shoes I bought online are too small. Can I exchange them?
  2. 客服 · kèfú可以，七天以内都能换。您要换多大的？Kěyǐ, qī tiān yǐnèi dōu néng huàn. Nín yào huàn duō dà de?Yes, exchanges are possible within seven days. What size do you need?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà大一号的，三十八号。Dà yí hào de, sānshíbā hào.One size up, 38.
  4. 客服 · kèfú好的，请您把鞋寄回来，我们收到以后马上给您寄新的。Hǎo de, qǐng nín bǎ xié jì huílai, wǒmen shōu dào yǐhòu mǎshàng gěi nín jì xīn de.OK, please send the shoes back; we'll send new ones as soon as we get them.

2. It doesn't work

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我昨天在这儿买的耳机，今天就坏了。Wǒ zuótiān zài zhèr mǎi de ěrjī, jīntiān jiù huài le.I bought these headphones here yesterday, and they broke today.
  2. 售货员 · shòuhuòyuán您带发票了吗？Nín dài fāpiào le ma?Do you have the receipt?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng带了，在这儿。Dài le, zài zhèr.Yes, here it is.
  4. 售货员 · shòuhuòyuán我们给您换一副新的，或者退钱都可以。Wǒmen gěi nín huàn yí fù xīn de, huòzhě tuì qián dōu kěyǐ.We can give you a new pair or a refund, whichever you prefer.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng换新的吧。Huàn xīn de ba.A new pair, please.

Learning Chinese

1. How do you remember characters?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà汉字太难记了，你有什么好办法吗？Hànzì tài nán jì le, nǐ yǒu shénme hǎo bànfǎ ma?Characters are so hard to remember. Any good tips?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì你可以先记偏旁，很多字的意思跟偏旁有关系。Nǐ kěyǐ xiān jì piānpáng, hěn duō zì de yìsi gēn piānpáng yǒu guānxi.Learn the radicals first; many characters' meanings relate to them.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà比如说呢？Bǐrú shuō ne?For example?
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì比如“河”“海”“洗”都有三点水，都跟水有关。Bǐrú “hé” “hǎi” “xǐ” dōu yǒu sān diǎn shuǐ, dōu gēn shuǐ yǒuguān.河, 海 and 洗 all have the water radical and all relate to water.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà原来是这样！Yuánlái shì zhèyàng!So that's how it works!

2. Practising speaking

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你的口语进步得真快！Nǐ de kǒuyǔ jìnbù de zhēn kuài!Your spoken Chinese has improved so fast!
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà因为我每天都跟中国朋友聊天。Yīnwèi wǒ měi tiān dōu gēn Zhōngguó péngyou liáotiān.Because I chat with Chinese friends every day.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我怕说错，所以不敢说。Wǒ pà shuō cuò, suǒyǐ bù gǎn shuō.I'm afraid of making mistakes, so I don't dare speak.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà别怕，说错了也没关系，多说才能进步。Bié pà, shuō cuò le yě méi guānxi, duō shuō cái néng jìnbù.Don't be — mistakes are fine. You only improve by speaking a lot.

Invitations
