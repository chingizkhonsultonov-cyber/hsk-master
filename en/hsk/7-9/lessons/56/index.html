  • 开拓kāi tuòto open upTraditional: 開拓
    Dictionary sensesto break new ground (for agriculture); to open up (a new seam); to develop (border regions); fig. to open up (new horizons)
    • 年轻的时候你应该开拓你的思想。nián qīng de shí hou nǐ yīng gāi kāi tuò nǐ de sī xiǎngYou should enrich your mind when young.
  • 办事处bàn shì chùofficeTraditional: 辦事處
    Dictionary sensesagency; bureau
    • 他在福利办事处上班。tā zài fú lì bàn shì chù shàng bānHe works at the welfare office.
    • 我想知道最近的美国运通办事处的电话号码。wǒ xiǎng zhī dào zuì jìn de měi guó yùn tōng bàn shì chù de diàn huà hào mǎI'd like to know the phone number of the nearest American Express office.
  • 风筝fēng zhengkiteTraditional: 風箏
    Dictionary senseskite (Etymology: Kites with bamboo whistles attached produced musical sounds when flown.)
    • 让我们放风筝。ràng wǒ men fàng fēng zhengLet's fly a kite.
    • 我买了一个风筝给你。wǒ mǎi le yī gè fēng zheng gěi nǐI bought a kite to give to you.
  • 要好yào hǎoclose

    • 你要好好把握这个时机。nǐ yào hǎo hǎo bǎ wò zhè ge shí jīYou should make good use of this opportunity.
