  • 亲眼qīn yǎnwith one's own eyesTraditional: 親眼

    • 我亲眼见到了它。wǒ qīn yǎn jiàn dào le tāI saw it with my own eyes.
  • 骚扰sāo rǎoto harassTraditional: 騷擾
    Dictionary sensesto disturb; to cause a commotion
    • 市长一家人一整天不断遭到了恐吓电话的骚扰。shì cháng yī jiā rén yī zhěng tiān bù duàn zāo dào le kǒng hè diàn huà de sāo rǎoThe mayor's family was harassed with threatening phone calls all day.
  • 花样huā yàngpattern; trickTraditional: 花樣

  • 亲手qīn shǒuwith one's own handsTraditional: 親手
    Dictionary sensespersonally
  • 激活jī huóto activate
  • 上诉shàng sùto appealTraditional: 上訴
    Dictionary sensesto appeal (a judicial case); appeal

  • 憋biēto hold back
    Dictionary sensesto choke; to stifle; to restrain; to hold in (urine)
  • 摊tānstall; to spreadTraditional: 攤
    Dictionary sensesto spread out; vendor's stand

  • 环球huán qiúaround the worldTraditional: 環球
    Dictionary sensesworldwide
  • 任命rèn mìngto appointMeasure word: 纸
    Dictionary senses(job) appointment
    • 她被任命为主席。tā bèi rèn mìng wèi zhǔ xíShe was appointed chairperson.
  • 轰hōngto boomTraditional: 轟
    Dictionary sensesexplosion; bang; boom; rumble
  • 撤离chè líto evacuateTraditional: 撤離

  • 无情wú qíngruthlessTraditional: 無情
    Dictionary sensespitiless; merciless; heartless
  • 为人wéi rénto conduct oneselfTraditional: 為人
    Dictionary sensesbehavior; conduct; personal character
    • 我为人生感到很开心。wǒ wéi rén shēng gǎn dào hěn kāi xīnI get a kick out of life.
    • 我很喜欢你为人设想的性格。wǒ hěn xǐ huan nǐ wéi rén shè xiǎng de xìng géI love how you think of other people's needs before your own.
  • 开场kāi chǎngto beginTraditional: 開場
    Dictionary sensesto open; to start; beginning of an event
  • 容忍róng rěnto tolerate
    Dictionary sensesto put up with
    • 我难以容忍他的行为。wǒ nán yǐ róng rěn tā de xíng wéiI can barely stand his behavior.
    • 那女士容忍了那男人。nà nǚ shì róng rěn le nà nán rénThe lady tolerated the man.
  • 权益quán yìrights and interestsTraditional: 權益

    • 我朋友邀我共赴晚餐。wǒ péng you yāo wǒ gòng fù wǎn cānMy friends invited me to supper.
  • 防护fáng hùto protectTraditional: 防護
    Dictionary sensesto defend
  • 丸wánpill
    Dictionary sensesball; pellet
    • 我想要五个肉丸。wǒ xiǎng yào wǔ gè ròu wánI want five meatballs.
    • 炸肉丸不是一种健康的零食。zhà ròu wán bù shì yī zhǒng jiàn kāng de líng shíA frikandel is not a healthy snack.
  • 坚信jiān xìnto firmly believeTraditional: 堅信
    Dictionary sensesto believe firmly; without any doubt
    • 我坚信他在商业界会有所作为。wǒ jiān xìn tā zài shāng yè jiè huì yǒu suǒ zuò wéiI'm sure he will go far in the business world.

    Dictionary sensesusual; common

    • 那场事故让我们陷入了不寻常的局势。nà chǎng shì gù ràng wǒ men xiàn rù le bù xún cháng de jú shìWe were faced with an unusual situation because of the accident.
  • 侮辱wǔ rǔto insult
    Dictionary sensesto humiliate; dishonor
    • 她侮辱了他。tā wǔ rǔ le tāShe insulted him.

    • 我们同意了第二天一早开始。wǒ men tóng yì le dì èr tiān yī zǎo kāi shǐWe agreed to start early the next morning.

  • 致力于zhì lì yúto devote oneself to

  • 扮bànto dress up as
    Dictionary sensesto disguise oneself as; to dress up; to play (a role); to put on (an expression)
    • 她为了掩饰自己公主的身份，女扮男装，逃出了皇宫。tā wèi le yǎn shì zì jǐ gōng zhǔ de shēn fèn nǚ bàn nán zhuāng táo chū le huáng gōngTo hide the fact that she's a princess, she disguised herself as a boy and escaped the palace.

    • 一个差劲的厨师和下毒者唯一的不同在于他们的意图。yī gè chà jìn de chú shī hé xià dú zhě wéi yī de bù tóng zài yú tā men de yì túThe only difference between a bad cook and a poisoner is the intent.
