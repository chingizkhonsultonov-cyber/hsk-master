  • 进程jìn chéngprocessTraditional: 進程
    Dictionary sensescourse of events
    • 这个进程必须立即停止，否则服务器会故障。zhè ge jìn chéng bì xū lì jí tíng zhǐ fǒu zé fú wù qì huì gù zhàngThis process must be stopped immediately, otherwise the server will break down.

    Dictionary sensesto rob; to plunder; thief; bandit
    • 盗亦有道。dào yì yǒu dàoThere is honor even among thieves.
  • 异性yì xìngopposite sexTraditional: 異性
    Dictionary sensesthe opposite sex; of the opposite sex; heterosexual; different in nature
    • 跟异性交往最重要的不是他有多好，而是他对你有多好。gēn yì xìng jiāo wǎng zuì zhòng yào de bù shì tā yǒu duō hǎo ér shì tā duì nǐ yǒu duō hǎoThe most important thing about relations with the opposite sex is not how good the other person is, but that they treat you well.
    • 有人形容，跟异性交往就好像在海边捡石头，大家都能捡喜欢的那一个。yǒu rén xíng róng gēn yì xìng jiāo wǎng jiù hǎo xiàng zài hǎi biān jiǎn shí tou dà jiā dōu néng jiǎn xǐ huan de nà yī gèThe way some people see it, associating with the opposite sex is just like going to the seaside and gathering stones; everyone can pick up the ones they like.
  • 颇pōratherTraditional: 頗
    Dictionary sensesquite; considerably; oblique; inclined
    • 这一切在我看来都颇为荒唐。zhè yī qiè zài wǒ kàn lai dōu pō wèi huāng tángI think that this is all quite absurd.
  • 溜liūto slip away
    Dictionary sensesto escape in stealth; to skate
    • 他每天早晨溜狗。tā měi tiān zǎo chén liū gǒuHe walks his dog every morning.
    • 不要让机会溜走。bú yào ràng jī huì liū zǒuDon't miss this chance.

    • 他全家都在农场工作。tā quán jiā dōu zài nóng chǎng gōng zuòAll his family works on a farm.
  • 魔鬼mó guǐdevil

    • 地狱空空荡荡，魔鬼都在人间。dì yù kōng kōng dàng dàng mó guǐ dōu zài rén jiānHell is empty, and all the devils are here!
  • 疯子fēng zimadmanTraditional: 瘋子

    • 这座城市在战争中被摧毁。zhè zuò chéng shì zài zhàn zhēng zhōng bèi cuī huǐThe city was destroyed in the war.

    • 但愿我有一辆车。dàn yuàn wǒ yǒu yī liàng chēI wish I had a car.
  • 温馨wēn xīnwarmTraditional: 溫馨
    Dictionary sensescomfort; soft and fragrant
  • 挨āinext to; one by one
    Dictionary sensesin order; in sequence; close to; adjacent to

    • 对于这些国家的人民来说，挨饿是很正常的事。duì yú zhè xiē guó jiā de rén mín lái shuō āi è shì hěn zhèng cháng de shìIn these countries hunger is the rule.

    • 那个时候，我们只能听到关于盗窃、杀人、以及饿死了人的故事。nà ge shí hou wǒ men zhǐ néng tīng dào guān yú dào qiè shā rén yǐ jí è sǐ le rén de gù shiDuring that time, we only heard about thieves, about murders, about men who had died of hunger.
  • 设定shè dìngto setTraditional: 設定

    • 在你上床睡觉之前，你必须把闹钟设定好。zài nǐ shàng chuáng shuì jiào zhī qián nǐ bì xū bǎ nào zhōng shè dìng hǎoYou've got to set the alarm clock before you go to bed.
  • 游行yóu xíngparadeTraditional: 遊行

    • 我要参加一个示威游行。wǒ yào cān jiā yī gè shì wēi yóu xíngI'm going to join a demonstration.
    • 几十个年轻人参加了示威游行。jǐ shí gè nián qīng rén cān jiā le shì wēi yóu xíngDozens of young people attended the demonstration.

    Dictionary sensesselfishness

    • 她是一个自私的人。tā shì yī gè zì sī de rénShe is a selfish person.
  • 凑còuto gatherTraditional: 湊
    Dictionary sensesto gather together, pool or collect; to happen by chance; to move close to; to exploit an opportunity
    • 我们所有人凑份子给老师买生日礼物。wǒ men suǒ yǒu rén còu fèn zi gěi lǎo shī mǎi shēng rì lǐ wùWe all chipped in to buy our teacher a birthday present.
  • 警惕jǐng tìvigilant
    Dictionary sensesto be on the alert; alert; on guard; to warn
    • 不要放松警惕。bú yào fàng sōng jǐng tìDon't let your guard down.
  • 降临jiàng línto descendTraditional: 降臨
    Dictionary sensesto arrive; to come
    • 当贫穷降临在门口，爱从窗外飞走。dāng pín qióng jiàng lín zài mén kǒu ài cóng chuāng wài fēi zǒuWhen poverty comes in at the door, love flies out the window.
  • 截jiéto cut off; section
    Dictionary sensesto cut off (a length); to stop; to intercept; chunk
    • 她从不要求他直截了当地给出答复。tā cóng bù yāo qiú tā zhí jié le dāng dì gěi chū dá fùShe never expects a straight answer from him.
    • 能够直截了当地表达自己想法的人很直爽。néng gòu zhí jié le dāng dì biǎo dá zì jǐ xiǎng fǎ de rén hěn zhí shuǎngPeople who can express their thoughts in a clear-cut way are very straightforward.
