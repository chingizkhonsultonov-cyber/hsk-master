    Dictionary sensesto make a bet; a wager
  • 代言dài yánto endorse
    Dictionary sensesto be a spokesperson; to be an ambassador (for a brand)
  • 当机立断dāng jī lì duànto decide promptlyTraditional: 當機立斷
    Dictionary sensesto make prompt decisions (idiom)
  • 单价dān jiàunit priceTraditional: 單價
  • 胆量dǎn liàngcourageTraditional: 膽量
    Dictionary sensesboldness; guts
  • 道别dào biéto say goodbyeTraditional: 道別
    Dictionary sensesto bid farewell; to pay a farewell call
    • 火车开出的时候，他们都挥手向父母道别。huǒ chē kāi chū de shí hou tā men dōu huī shǒu xiàng fù mǔ dào biéThey waved good-bye to their parents as the train pulled out.
  • 倒退dào tuìto go backwards
    Dictionary sensesto fall back; to go in reverse
  • 大为dà wéigreatlyTraditional: 大為
    Dictionary sensesvery
  • 打消dǎ xiāoto dispel
    Dictionary sensesto dispel (doubts, misgivings etc); to give up on
  • 德才兼备dé cái jiān bèivirtuous and ableTraditional: 德才兼備
    Dictionary senses(idiom) possessing both integrity and talent
  • 登场dēng chǎngto appear on stageTraditional: 登場
    Dictionary sensesto go on stage; fig. to appear on the scene
    • 这部漫画登场的人物全是少女。zhè bù màn huà dēng chǎng de rén wù quán shì shào nǚAll the characters of this comic are girls.
  • 登顶dēng dǐngto reach the summitTraditional: 登頂
    Dictionary senses(lit. and fig.) to get to the top
  • 得心应手dé xīn yìng shǒuwith easeTraditional: 得心應手
    Dictionary senseslit. what the heart wishes, the hand accomplishes (idiom) skilled at the job; entirely in one's element; going smoothly and easily
  • 得益dé yìto benefit
    Dictionary sensesto derive benefit
  • 抵dǐto resist; to offset
    Dictionary sensesto press against; to support; to prop up; to withstand
  • 颠倒黑白diān dǎo hēi báito confuse right and wrongTraditional: 顛倒黑白
    Dictionary senseslit. to invert black and white (idiom); to distort the truth deliberately; to misrepresent the facts; to invert right and wrong
  • 雕像diāo xiàngstatueMeasure word: 尊
    Dictionary sensessculpture; (carved) statue
    • 我们用木材做雕像。wǒ men yòng mù cái zuò diāo xiàngWe made statues out of wood.
  • 凋谢diāo xièto witherTraditional: 凋謝
    Dictionary sensesto wilt; wizened
    • 花凋谢了。huā diāo xiè leThe flowers withered up.
  • 碟diésaucer
    Dictionary sensesdish; plate
    • 晚饭后我洗了碗碟。wǎn fàn hòu wǒ xǐ le wǎn diéI washed the dishes after supper.
  • 跌幅diē fúdrop
    Dictionary sensesdecline (in value); extent of a drop
  • 嘀咕dí guto mutter
    Dictionary sensesto feel apprehensive
  • 低落dī luòlow
    Dictionary sensesdowncast; gloomy; to decline; to get worse
    • 她情绪低落。tā qíng xù dī luòShe felt blue.
    • 雨天让我情绪低落。yǔ tiān ràng wǒ qíng xù dī luòRainy days make me depressed.
  • 定律dìng lǜlaw (scientific)
    Dictionary sensesscientific law (e.g. law of conservation of energy); (in human affairs) a generalization based on observation (e.g. "power corrupts")
  • 订阅dìng yuèto subscribeTraditional: 訂閱
    Dictionary sensessubscription; to subscribe to
    • 你订阅了什么报纸？nǐ dìng yuè le shén me bào zhǐWhat newspaper do you subscribe to?
    • 你有订阅任何杂志吗？nǐ yǒu dìng yuè rèn hé zá zhì maDo you subscribe to any magazines?
  • 逗留dòu liúto stay
    Dictionary sensesto stay for a while; to sojourn
    • 他们会坚持要她逗留久一点。tā men huì jiān chí yào tā dòu liú jiǔ yī diǎnThey will insist on her staying there longer.
  • 断然duàn ránresolutelyTraditional: 斷然
    Dictionary sensesresolute; definitive; categorically; absolutely
    • 他断然地拒绝让我进去。tā duàn rán de jù jué ràng wǒ jìn quHe flatly refused to let me in.
  • 断章取义duàn zhāng qǔ yìto quote out of contextTraditional: 斷章取義
    Dictionary senses(idiom) to quote out of context
    • 媒体对他所说的话断章取义并发展到无法控制的地步了。méi tǐ duì tā suǒ shuō de huà duàn zhāng qǔ yì bìng fā zhǎn dào wú fǎ kòng zhì de dì bù leJust one part of what he said got picked up by the media and took on a life of its own.
  • 兑duìto exchangeTraditional: 兌
    Dictionary sensesto cash; to add (liquid); to blend; one of the Eight Trigrams 八卦[ba1 gua4], symbolizing swamp
    • 超市出兑。chāo shì chū duìThe supermarket is out of business.
  • 对岸duì ànopposite bankTraditional: 對岸
    Dictionary sensesopposite bank (of a body of water)
    • 她的家在河对岸。tā de jiā zài hé duì ànHer house is across the river.
  • 对流duì liúconvectionTraditional: 對流
  • 对牛弹琴duì niú tán qínto cast pearls before swineTraditional: 對牛彈琴
    Dictionary senseslit. to play the lute to a cow (idiom); fig. offering a treat to an unappreciative audience; caviar to the general; to preach to deaf ears
    • 跟小人说理，就如同对牛弹琴。gēn xiǎo rén shuō lǐ jiù rú tóng duì niú tán qínReasoning with a villain is pointless.
  • 对症下药duì zhèng xià yàoto suit the remedy to the caseTraditional: 對症下藥
    Dictionary senseslit. to prescribe the right medicine for an illness (idiom); fig. to study a problem to find the right way to solve it; to take appropriate steps
  • 读物dú wùreading materialTraditional: 讀物
  • 额度é dùlimitTraditional: 額度
    Dictionary sensesquota; (credit) limit
  • 噩耗è hàosad news
    Dictionary sensesnews of sb's death
  • 恩爱ēn àilovingTraditional: 恩愛
