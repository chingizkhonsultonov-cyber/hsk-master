    Dictionary sensesto disdain; to look down upon
  • 元首yuán shǒuhead of state
  • 苍白cāng báipaleTraditional: 蒼白
    Dictionary senseswan
    • 他有点苍白。tā yǒu diǎn cāng báiHe's a bit pasty.
    • 你脸色苍白。nǐ liǎn sè cāng báiYou are pale.
  • 纱shāgauzeTraditional: 紗
    Dictionary sensescotton yarn; muslin
  • 阶级jiē jíclassMeasure word: 个Traditional: 階級
    Dictionary senses(social) class
    • 这个国家里不同年龄阶级的人都减少了对棒球的兴趣。zhè ge guó jiā lǐ bù tóng nián líng jiē jí de rén dōu jiǎn shǎo le duì bàng qiú de xìng qùInterest in baseball cuts across classes and ages in this country.
  • 中途zhōng túmidway
  • 独一无二dú yī wú èruniqueTraditional: 獨一無二
    Dictionary senses(idiom) unique; unmatched; unrivalled
    • 这里的天气有独一无二的特点。zhè li de tiān qì yǒu dú yī wú èr de tè diǎnThe weather here has its own unique characteristics.
  • 经受jīng shòuto undergoTraditional: 經受
    Dictionary sensesto undergo (hardship); to endure; to withstand
    • 她经受得够多了。tā jīng shòu de gòu duō leShe's been through enough.
  • 亮相liàng xiàngto appear
    Dictionary sensesto strike a pose (Chinese opera); (fig.) to make a public appearance; to come out in public (revealing one's true personality, opinions etc); (of a product) to appear on the market or at a trade show etc
  • 懒得lǎn denot in the mood toTraditional: 懶得
    Dictionary sensesto not feel like (doing sth); to be disinclined to
  • 捐献juān xiànto donateTraditional: 捐獻
    Dictionary sensesto contribute
  • 接手jiē shǒuto take over
    Dictionary sensesto take over (duties etc); catcher (baseball etc)
    • 他退休的时候，他儿子接手了他的生意。tā tuì xiū de shí hou tā ér zi jiē shǒu le tā de shēng yiWhen he retired, his son took over the business.
  • 即可jí kěthen it's fine
    Dictionary sensesequivalent to 就可以; can then (do sth); can immediately (do sth); (do sth) and that will suffice
  • 小品xiǎo pǐnskit
    Dictionary sensesshort, simple literary or artistic creation; essay
  • 线条xiàn tiáolineTraditional: 線條
    Dictionary sensesline (in drawing, calligraphy etc); the lines or contours of a three-dimensional object (hairstyle, clothing, car etc)
    • 他有明显的肌肉线条。tā yǒu míng xiǎn de jī ròu xiàn tiáoHe's ripped.
  • 栽zāito plant; to fall
    Dictionary sensesto grow; to insert; to erect (e.g. a bus stop sign); to impose sth on sb
  • 取胜qǔ shèngto winTraditional: 取勝
    Dictionary sensesto score a victory; to prevail over one's opponents
    • 他现在这个年纪，就已经在国际比赛中取胜，前途实在无可限量。tā xiàn zài zhè ge nián jì jiù yǐ jīng zài guó jì bǐ sài zhōng qǔ shèng qián tú shí zài wú kě xiàn liàngHis victory at this age in an international competition is a good indication of a bright future.
  • 联手lián shǒuto join forcesTraditional: 聯手
    Dictionary senseslit. to join hands; to act together
    • 当地企业正在联手举办一项慈善活动。dāng dì qǐ yè zhèng zài lián shǒu jǔ bàn yī xiàng cí shàn huó dòngLocal businesses are teaming up for a charity initiative.
  • 遗体yí tǐremainsTraditional: 遺體
    Dictionary sensesremains (of a dead person)
  • 残留cán liúto remainTraditional: 殘留
    Dictionary sensesto be left over; residual; remnant; residue
  • 震动zhèn dòngto shakeTraditional: 震動
    Dictionary sensesto vibrate; to strongly affect; shock; vibration
  • 角逐jué zhúto contend
    Dictionary sensesto tussle; to contest
  • 特权tè quánprivilegeTraditional: 特權
    Dictionary sensesprerogative; privileged
    • 我们得到了在这个海湾内捕鱼的特权。wǒ men dé dào le zài zhè ge hǎi wān nèi bǔ yú de tè quánWe were granted the privilege of fishing in this bay.
  • 可不是kě bu shìexactly
    Dictionary senses(coll.) exactly!; indeed; that's for sure
    • 这可不是我做的。zhè kě bu shì wǒ zuò deI didn't do this.
    • 他这次测验不合格可不是开玩笑的。tā zhè cì cè yàn bù hé gé kě bu shì kāi wán xiào deHis failing the test is no laughing matter.
  • 巨额jù éhuge sumTraditional: 巨額
    Dictionary sensesa huge sum; an enormous amount (of money)
  • 口水kǒu shuǐsaliva
  • 热带rè dàitropicsTraditional: 熱帶
    Dictionary sensesthe tropics; tropical
    • 这类植物只生长在热带地区。zhè lèi zhí wù zhī shēng zhǎng zài rè dài dì qūThis kind of plant grows only in the tropical regions.
  • 遮zhēto cover
    Dictionary sensesto cover up (a shortcoming); to screen off; to hide; to conceal
    • 他用手遮住眼睛。tā yòng shǒu zhē zhù yǎn jingHe put his hands over his eyes.
    • 她的新发型遮住了耳朵。tā de xīn fà xíng zhē zhù le ěr duoHer new hairstyle covers her ears.
  • 战友zhàn yǒucomrade-in-armsTraditional: 戰友
    Dictionary sensesbattle companion
  • 扒bāto cling to; to strip off
    Dictionary sensesto peel; to skin; to tear; to pull down
    • 小心扒手。xiǎo xīn bā shǒuBeware of pickpockets.
    • 这里要小心扒手。zhè li yào xiǎo xīn bā shǒuBeware of pickpockets here.
  • 蘑菇mó gumushroom
    Dictionary sensesto pester; to dawdle
    • 我做了蘑菇汤。wǒ zuò le mó gu tāngI made mushroom soup.
    • 我特别喜欢蘑菇的味道。wǒ tè bié xǐ huan mó gu de wèi daoI especially like the flavour of the mushroom.
  • 时髦shí máofashionableTraditional: 時髦
    Dictionary sensesin vogue
    • 虽然时髦一点，但是价钱太大！suī rán shí máo yī diǎn dàn shì jià qian tài dàIt's kind of in vogue, but the price is too much!
    • 你可能认为那些鞋子很时髦，但是他们不是。nǐ kě néng rèn wéi nà xiē xié zi hěn shí máo dàn shì tā men bù shìYou may think those shoes are in fashion, but they aren't.
  • 音响yīn xiǎngaudio equipmentTraditional: 音響
    Dictionary sensessound; acoustics; audio; hi-fi system
  • 急性jí xìngacute
    • 他一生为急性疼痛所苦。tā yī shēng wèi jí xìng téng tòng suǒ kǔHe's suffering from acute pain in his life.
  • 钞票chāo piàobanknoteMeasure word: 张、扎Traditional: 鈔票
    Dictionary sensespaper money; a bill (e.g. 100 yuan)
    • 这张钞票是假造的。zhè zhāng chāo piào shì jiǎ zào deThis is a forged banknote.
  • 虽说suī shuōalthoughTraditional: 雖說
    Dictionary sensesthough
    • 虽说是高学历，也未必就有教养。suī shuō shì gāo xué lì yě wèi bì jiù yǒu jiào yǎngA good education doesn't imply a good upbringing.

  • 徘徊pái huáito pace back and forth
    Dictionary sensesto dither; to hesitate; (of sales figures etc) to fluctuate
  • 显现xiǎn xiànto appearTraditional: 顯現
    Dictionary sensesappearance
  • 雇员gù yuánemployeeTraditional: 雇員
