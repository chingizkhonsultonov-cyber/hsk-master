    • 这手表有防震功能。zhè shǒu biǎo yǒu fáng zhèn gōng néngThis watch is shock-proof.
    • 印度昨天发生了一场大地震。yìn dù zuó tiān fā shēng le yī chǎng dà dì zhènA big earthquake occurred in India yesterday.

    • 拜托你开门让我进来吧。bài tuō nǐ kāi mén ràng wǒ jìn lai baOpen the door and let me in, please.

In fact, the bigger the lies, the better lies, and the more the common folks and critics will praise you.

    • 俗话说：人不犯我，我不犯人；可俗话又说：先下手为强，后下手遭殃！sú huà shuō rén bù fàn wǒ wǒ bù fàn rén kě sú huà yòu shuō xiān xià shǒu wèi qiáng hòu xià shǒu zāo yāngAs the saying goes: As long as you don't offend me, I won't offend you.
