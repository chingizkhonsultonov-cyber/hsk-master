  • 倒塌dǎo tāto collapse
    Dictionary sensesto collapse (of building); to topple over
    • 桥在重压之下倒塌了。qiáo zài zhòng yā zhī xià dǎo tā leThe bridge crashed under the pressure.
  • 附属fù shǔaffiliatedTraditional: 附屬
    Dictionary sensessubsidiary; auxiliary; attached; subordinate
  • 屈服qū fúto yield
    Dictionary sensesto surrender; to succumb; (as a transitive verb) to defeat; to prevail over
    • 他向权力屈服。tā xiàng quán lì qū fúHe submits to authority.
    • 不要屈服于这些要求。bú yào qū fú yú zhè xiē yāo qiúDo not give in to those demands.
  • 捍卫hàn wèito defendTraditional: 捍衛
    Dictionary sensesto uphold; to safeguard
  • 点火diǎn huǒto igniteTraditional: 點火
    Dictionary sensesto light a fire; to agitate; to start an engine; ignition
    • 一点火花让火药爆炸。yī diǎn huǒ huā ràng huǒ yào bào zhàA spark led the gunpowder to explode.
  • 订购dìng gòuto orderTraditional: 訂購
    Dictionary sensesto place an order; to subscribe
    • 所有订购必须在今年年底前支付款项。suǒ yǒu dìng gòu bì xū zài jīn nián nián dǐ qián zhī fù kuǎn xiàngAll subscriptions must be paid before the end of this year.
  • 急于jí yúeager toTraditional: 急於
    Dictionary sensesin a hurry to
    • 他急于要走。tā jí yú yào zǒuHe is anxious to go.
    • 她急于见她的父亲。tā jí yú jiàn tā de fù qīnShe was in a hurry to see her father.
  • 不算bú suànnot count as
    Dictionary sensesto not calculate; to not count; to not be considered (as); to have no weight
    • 我们的问题和她的比起来不算什么。wǒ men de wèn tí hé tā de bǐ qǐ lai bù suàn shén meOur problems are nothing compared to hers.
  • 雪山xuě shānsnow mountain
    Dictionary sensessnow-capped mountain
  • 沸腾fèi téngto boilTraditional: 沸騰
    Dictionary senses(of a liquid) to boil; (of sentiments etc) to boil over; to flare up; to be impassioned
  • 满怀mǎn huáifull ofTraditional: 滿懷
    Dictionary sensesto have one's heart filled with; (to collide) full on; (of farm animals) heavy with young
  • 君子jūn zǐgentleman
    Dictionary sensesnobleman; person of noble character
    • 美国的罪犯大多是吸毒成瘾的瘾君子。měi guó de zuì fàn dà duō shì xī dú chéng yǐn de yǐn jūn zǐMany criminals in America are addicted to drugs.
  • 势头shì tóumomentumTraditional: 勢頭
    Dictionary sensespower; tendency; impetus; situation
  • 拎līnto carry
    Dictionary sensesto lift up; to carry in one's hand; Taiwan pr. [ling1]
  • 小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yìvery cautiously
    Dictionary senses(idiom) cautious; careful; prudent
    • 警察小心翼翼地抬起箱子。jǐng chá xiǎo xīn yì yì de tái qǐ xiāng ziThe policeman lifted the box carefully.
    • 我妈妈小心翼翼地打开门。wǒ mā ma xiǎo xīn yì yì de dǎ kāi ménMy mother opened the door very carefully.
  • 风采fēng cǎieleganceTraditional: 風采
    Dictionary sensessvelte; elegant manner; graceful bearing
  • 插手chā shǒuto interfere
    Dictionary sensesto get involved in; to meddle; interference
    • 别插手。bié chā shǒuStay out of this.
    • 别再插手。bié zài chā shǒuStop meddling.
  • 合伙hé huǒto form a partnership
    Dictionary sensesto act jointly
    • 他只是个事业合伙人。tā zhǐ shì gè shì yè hé huǒ rénHe's just a business associate.
  • 前台qián táifront deskTraditional: 前臺
    Dictionary sensesstage; proscenium; foreground in politics etc (sometimes derog.); reception desk
    • 她请了前台的人为她打那个号码。tā qǐng le qián tái de rén wéi tā dǎ nà ge hào mǎShe has asked the person at the front desk to connect her to that number.
  • 全心全意quán xīn quán yìwholeheartedly
    Dictionary senses(idiom) heart and soul
    • 我全心全意地支持你。wǒ quán xīn quán yì de zhī chí nǐI support you whole-heartedly.
    • 从那时起，他就全心全意地投入到他的工作。cóng nà shí qǐ tā jiù quán xīn quán yì de tóu rù dào tā de gōng zuòSince then he has put his soul into his work.

  • 共鸣gòng míngresonanceTraditional: 共鳴
    Dictionary senses(physics) to resonate; sympathetic response
  • 绷bēngto stretch tightTraditional: 綳
    Dictionary sensesto draw tight; to stretch taut; to tack (with thread or pin)
  • 假冒jiǎ màocounterfeit
    Dictionary sensesto impersonate; to pose as (someone else); to counterfeit; to palm off (a fake as a genuine)
  • 扳bānto pull, turn
    Dictionary sensesto pull; to turn (sth) around; to turn around (a situation); to recoup
  • 乞丐qǐ gàibeggar
    • 他很富有，但活得像个乞丐。tā hěn fù yǒu dàn huó de xiàng gè qǐ gàiHe is rich yet he lives like a beggar.
    • 我把身上的钱全都给了那个乞丐。wǒ bǎ shēn shàng de qián quán dōu gěi le nà ge qǐ gàiI gave the beggar what money I had.
  • 高空gāo kōnghigh altitude
    • 高空弹跳是恐怖还是好玩？gāo kōng dàn tiào shì kǒng bù hái shi hǎo wánIs bungee jumping frightening or fun?
  • 敬请jìng qǐngpleaseTraditional: 敬請

    • 敬请期待。jìng qǐng qī dàiIt's being arranged.
  • 放纵fàng zòngto indulgeTraditional: 放縱
    Dictionary sensesto pamper; to connive at; permissive; indulgent
  • 申报shēn bàoto declareTraditional: 申報
    Dictionary sensesto report (to the authorities); to declare (to customs)
    • 你有任何要申报的东西吗？nǐ yǒu rèn hé yào shēn bào de dōng xi maDo you have anything to declare?
  • 冒充mào chōngto pose as
    Dictionary sensesto feign; to pretend to be; to pass oneself off as
  • 流失liú shīto be lost
    Dictionary senses(of soil etc) to wash away; to be eroded; (fig.) (of talented staff, followers of a religious faith, investment funds etc) to go elsewhere; to fail to be retained
  • 高雅gāo yǎelegant
    Dictionary sensesdainty; elegance
  • 事态shì tàisituationTraditional: 事態
    Dictionary sensesexisting state of affairs
  • 驱动qū dòngto driveTraditional: 驅動
    Dictionary sensesto propel; drive (vehicle wheel); drive mechanism (tape or disk); device driver (computing software)
  • 恶性è xìngmalignantTraditional: 惡性

  • 担当dān dāngto take onTraditional: 擔當
    Dictionary sensesto take upon oneself; to assume
  • 差错chā cuòmistakeTraditional: 差錯
