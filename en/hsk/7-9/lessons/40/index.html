  • 照样zhào yàngas beforeTraditional: 照樣
    Dictionary senses(same) as usual; in the same manner; still; nevertheless
    • 虽然我知道从网上免费下载音乐是不对的，但我还是照样下载。suī rán wǒ zhī dào cóng wǎng shàng miǎn fèi xià zǎi yīn yuè shì bù duì de dàn wǒ hái shi zhào yàng xià zǎiI know that downloading music from the internet without paying is wrong, but I do it anyway.
  • 沉浸chén jìnto immerse
    Dictionary sensesto soak; to permeate
    • 他沉浸在阅读之中。tā chén jìn zài yuè dú zhī zhōngHe was absorbed in reading.
    • 他完全沉浸于家庭生活中，不想工作了。tā wán quán chén jìn yú jiā tíng shēng huó zhōng bù xiǎng gōng zuò leHe is totally immersed in family life and has no wish to work.
  • 从头cóng tóufrom the beginningTraditional: 從頭

    • 迷信之所以存在，是因为人无法接受巧合只是巧合而已。mí xìn zhī suǒ yǐ cún zài shì yīn wèi rén wú fǎ jiē shòu qiǎo hé zhǐ shì qiǎo hé ér yǐSuperstitions derive from the inability of men to acknowledge that coincidences are merely coincidences.

    • 大家同意先在这里住一晚，行程的事情明天再从长计议。dà jiā tóng yì xiān zài zhè li zhù yī wǎn xíng chéng de shì qing míng tiān zài cóng cháng jì yìEveryone agrees to stay here for the night and make long-term itinerary decisions tomorrow.
