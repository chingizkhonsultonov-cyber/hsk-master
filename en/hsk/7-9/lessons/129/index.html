    Dictionary senses(statistics) period on period (i.e. "month on month" or "quarter on quarter" etc, depending on the context)
  • 焕然一新huàn rán yì xīnbrand new lookTraditional: 煥然一新
    Dictionary sensesto look completely new (idiom); brand new; changed beyond recognition
    • 适度的运动让身心焕然一新。shì dù de yùn dòng ràng shēn xīn huàn rán yī xīnModerate exercise will refresh both mind and body.

    Dictionary sensesto wake sb; to rouse

    Dictionary sensesone of a sequence of contests (or subdivisions of a contest) between the same two opponents; round (boxing etc); rally (tennis etc); frame (billiards etc)
  • 挥舞huī wǔto waveTraditional: 揮舞
    Dictionary sensesto brandish; to wave sth
  • 绘制huì zhìto drawTraditional: 繪製

    Dictionary sensesnow hot, now cold; (of one's mood, affection etc) to alternate

    Dictionary sensesfig. sly and treacherous person
    • 狐狸吃母鸡。hú li chī mǔ jīFoxes eat hens.

    Dictionary sensesto argue; to reason with sb; to talk sense; to be reasonable

    Dictionary sensesrivers and mountains; landscape; country; state power

    • 植物学界众多争论之一是关于山核桃的问题——在我看来，它是水果，而不是坚果。zhí wù xué jiè zhòng duō zhēng lùn zhī yī shì guān yú shān hé tao de wèn tí zài wǒ kàn lai tā shì shuǐ guǒ ér bù shì jiān guǒOne of many botanical debates regards the question of the pecan — To me, it's a fruit, and not a nut.
