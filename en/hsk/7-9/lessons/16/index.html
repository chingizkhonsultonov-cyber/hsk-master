    Dictionary sensespad; mat; to pad out; to fill a gap
    • 这个床垫很硬。zhè ge chuáng diàn hěn yìngThis is a very hard mattress.
  • 席xímat; seat
    Dictionary senseswoven mat; banquet; place in a democratic assembly; classifier for banquets, conversations etc
  • 确切què qièexactTraditional: 確切
    Dictionary sensesdefinite; precise
    • 我想知道确切的时间。wǒ xiǎng zhī dào què qiè de shí jiānI'd like to know the exact time.
    • 我不知道确切的日期。wǒ bù zhī dào què qiè de rì qīI don't know the exact date.
  • 不惜bù xīnot spare
    Dictionary sensesnot stint; not hesitate (to do sth); not scruple (to do sth)
    • 战争必须不惜一切代价地去避免。zhàn zhēng bì xū bù xī yī qiè dài jià de qù bì miǎnWar must be avoided at all costs.
    • 他执意不惜一切代价获得成功。tā zhí yì bù xī yī qiè dài jià huò dé chéng gōngHe is determined to succeed at any price.
  • 揭晓jiē xiǎoto announceTraditional: 揭曉
    Dictionary sensesto announce publicly; to publish; to make known; to disclose
    • 真相总算在我们面前揭晓了。zhēn xiàng zǒng suàn zài wǒ men miàn qián jiē xiǎo leAt last, the truth was revealed to us.
    • 他们所有的秘密都已经被揭晓了。tā men suǒ yǒu de mì mì dōu yǐ jīng bèi jiē xiǎo leAll their secrets have been revealed.
  • 发誓fā shìto swearTraditional: 發誓
    Dictionary sensesto vow; to pledge
    • 我发誓我不会做这种事。wǒ fā shì wǒ bù huì zuò zhè zhǒng shìI swear I'll never do such a thing.
    • 我能发誓这箱子里有东西。wǒ néng fā shì zhè xiāng zi lǐ yǒu dōng xiI could swear there's something inside this box.
  • 安心ān xīnat ease
