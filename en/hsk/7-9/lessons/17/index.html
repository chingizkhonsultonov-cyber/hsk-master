  • 恶化è huàto worsenTraditional: 惡化
    • 经济情况恶化。jīng jì qíng kuàng è huàThe economic situation grew worse.
  • 口气kǒu qìtoneTraditional: 口氣
    Dictionary sensestone of voice; the way one speaks; manner of expression
  • 国民guó míncitizensTraditional: 國民
    Dictionary sensesnationals; people of a nation
    • 日本人是勤奋的国民。rì běn rén shì qín fèn de guó mínThe Japanese are an industrious people.
  • 指令zhǐ lìnginstruction
    Dictionary sensesorder; command
  • 勾gōuto tick; to evoke
    Dictionary sensesto attract; to arouse; to strike out; to delineate
  • 出局chū júto be out
    Dictionary senses(of a batter) to be put out (in baseball); to be dismissed (in cricket); (of a player or team) to be eliminated from a competition; (fig.) to be weeded out
  • 贵族guì zúaristocratTraditional: 貴族
    Dictionary senseslord; nobility; nobleman; noblewoman
    • 贵族住在故宫的东边和西边。guì zú zhù zài gù gōng de dōng biān hé xī biānAristocrats lived to the east and west of the imperial palace.
  • 制服zhì fúuniformMeasure word: 套
    Dictionary sensesto subdue; to check; to bring under control; (in former times) what one is allowed to wear depending on social status
    • 我没有穿制服。wǒ méi yǒu chuān zhì fúI wasn't wearing a uniform.
    • 他们都穿着制服。tā men dōu chuān zhuó zhì fúThey are all wearing uniforms.
  • 制服zhì fúto subdueMeasure word: 套
    Dictionary sensesto check; to bring under control; (in former times) what one is allowed to wear depending on social status; uniform (army, party, school etc)
  • 吉他jí tāguitarMeasure word: 把
    Dictionary senses(loanword) guitar
    • 谁在弹吉他？shéi zài dàn jí tāWho's playing the guitar?
    • 我没有吉他。wǒ méi yǒu jí tāI don't own a guitar.
  • 癌áicancer
    Dictionary senses(esp.) carcinoma
    • 她死于胃癌。tā sǐ yú wèi áiShe died of stomach cancer.
    • 他死于肺癌。tā sǐ yú fèi áiHe died of lung cancer.
  • 证人zhèng rénwitnessTraditional: 證人
  • 清新qīng xīnfresh
    Dictionary sensesfresh and clean
  • 征zhēngto levy; to recruit
    Dictionary sensesjourney; trip; expedition; to go on long campaign
    • 我去了公共游泳池应征当救生员。wǒ qù le gōng gòng yóu yǒng chí yīng zhēng dāng jiù shēng yuánI applied for a job as a lifeguard at the community pool.
    • 如果你有意应征，应该在面试前明白公司的主张以及公司如何对待员工。rú guǒ nǐ yǒu yì yīng zhēng yīng gāi zài miàn shì qián míng bai gōng sī de zhǔ zhāng yǐ jí gōng sī rú hé duì dài yuán gōngIf you are a potential employee then you should be aware of what the company stands for and how it treats its employees before attending an interview.
  • 坦克tǎn kètank
    Dictionary sensestank (military vehicle) (loanword)
    • 坦克失踪了。tǎn kè shī zōng leThe tank vanished.
  • 自称zì chēngto claimTraditional: 自稱
    Dictionary sensesto call oneself; to claim to be; to profess; to claim a title
    • 美国是一个自称没有社会阶层的国家。měi guó shì yī gè zì chēng méi yǒu shè huì jiē céng de guó jiāAmerica likes to claim that it is a "classless" society.
  • 毁灭huǐ mièto destroyTraditional: 毀滅
    Dictionary sensesto perish; to ruin
    • 人不是为失败而生的，一个人可以被毁灭，但不能给打败。rén bù shì wèi shī bài ér shēng de yī gè rén kě yǐ bèi huǐ miè dàn bù néng gěi dǎ bàiBut a man is not made for defeat. A man can be destroyed but not defeated.
    • 如果人类不好好保护大自然，大自然说不定会反过来把人类毁灭。rú guǒ rén lèi bù hǎo hǎo bǎo hù dà zì rán dà zì rán shuō bu dìng huì fǎn guò lai bǎ rén lèi huǐ mièIf mankind doesn't take care of the environment, the environment may eliminate mankind.
  • 乐意lè yìwillingTraditional: 樂意
    Dictionary sensesto be willing to do sth; to be ready to do sth; to be happy to do sth; content

    • 我会乐意帮你做。wǒ huì lè yì bāng nǐ zuòI'd be glad to do that for you.
  • 哭泣kū qìto weep
    • 她开始哭泣。tā kāi shǐ kū qìShe began crying.

  • 瞄准miáo zhǔnto aimTraditional: 瞄準
    Dictionary sensesto take aim at; to target
    • 警察瞄准了那个男人。jǐng chá miáo zhǔn le nà ge nán rénThe policeman aimed his gun at the man.
    • 猎人们用枪瞄准了大象。liè rén men yòng qiāng miáo zhǔn le dà xiàngThe hunters aimed at the elephant.
  • 脑海nǎo hǎimindTraditional: 腦海
    Dictionary sensesthe mind; the brain
    • 不要以为我已经忘了你，你在我的脑海中，但我抽不出时间给你写信。bú yào yǐ wéi wǒ yǐ jīng wàng le nǐ nǐ zài wǒ de nǎo hǎi zhōng dàn wǒ chōu bù chū shí jiān gěi nǐ xiě xìnDon't think I've forgotten you already, you've been on my mind, but I haven't been able to take the time to write to you.
    • 一个绝妙的想法浮现在他的脑海。yī gè jué miào de xiǎng fǎ fú xiàn zài tā de nǎo hǎiA wonderful idea floated to his mind.

  • 终止zhōng zhǐto terminateTraditional: 終止

  • 掩盖yǎn gàito cover upTraditional: 掩蓋

    • 她没有试图掩盖真相。tā méi yǒu shì tú yǎn gài zhēn xiàngShe didn't try to hide the truth.
  • 标签biāo qiānlabelTraditional: 標籤
    Dictionary sensestag; (computing) tab (GUI element)
    • 箱子的内容在标签上面写着。xiāng zi de nèi róng zài biāo qiān shàng miàn xiě zheThe contents of the box are listed on the label.
    • 我在我的行李上附上了有我名字的标签，但是它很快就剥落了。wǒ zài wǒ de xíng li shàng fù shàng le yǒu wǒ míng zi de biāo qiān dàn shì tā hěn kuài jiù bō luò leI attached my name-tag to my baggage, but it soon came off.

  • 艾滋病ài zī bìngAIDS

  • 陷阱xiàn jǐngtrap

    • 你认为它是陷阱吗？nǐ rèn wéi tā shì xiàn jǐng maDo you think it's a trap?
    • 一只狐狸不会两次掉入同一个陷阱里。yī zhī hú li bù huì liǎng cì diào rù tóng yī gè xiàn jǐng lǐA fox isn't caught twice in the same snare.
  • 胎tāifoetus; tyre

    • 来看看这些车胎。lái kàn kàn zhè xiē chē tāiTake a look at these tires.
    • 我的自行车的后轮爆胎了。wǒ de zì xíng chē de hòu lún bào tāi leMy bike's back tyre is flat.

  • 筒tǒngtube
