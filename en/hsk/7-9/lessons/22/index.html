  • 歧视qí shìto discriminateTraditional: 歧視
    Dictionary sensesto discriminate against; discrimination
    • 看起来不男不女的人常常受到议论和歧视。kàn qǐ lai bù nán bù nǚ de rén cháng cháng shòu dào yì lùn hé qí shìAndrogynous-looking people are often the subject of discussion and discrimination.
    • 他反对种族歧视。tā fǎn duì zhǒng zú qí shìHe opposes racism.
  • 放过fàng guòto let goTraditional: 放過
    Dictionary sensesto let off; to let slip by; to let sb get away with sth
  • 润rùnmoistTraditional: 潤
    Dictionary sensesglossy; sleek; to moisten; to lubricate
    • 润滑系统设计得不好。rùn huá xì tǒng shè jì de bù hǎoThe lubrication system was poorly designed.
  • 无意wú yìunintentionallyTraditional: 無意
    Dictionary sensesinadvertent; accidental; to have no intention of (doing sth)
    • 我无意打扰。wǒ wú yì dǎ rǎoI didn't mean to intrude.
    • 对不起，我无意打扰。duì bu qǐ wǒ wú yì dǎ rǎoSorry, I didn't mean to interrupt.
  • 裂lièto crack
    Dictionary sensesto split; to break open; to rend
    • 自来水管冻裂了。zì lái shuǐ guǎn dòng liè leThe water pipes froze and then burst.
    • 一条主要水管被地震震裂了。yī tiáo zhǔ yào shuǐ guǎn bèi dì zhèn zhèn liè leThe water mains burst due to the earthquake.
  • 效力xiào lìeffect
    Dictionary senseseffectiveness; positive effect; to serve (in some capacity)
    • 这份合同没有法律效力。zhè fèn hé tong méi yǒu fǎ lǜ xiào lìThis contract is null and void.
  • 侵略qīn lüèaggression
    Dictionary sensesto invade; invasion
    • 侵略者注定要被打败。qīn lüè zhě zhù dìng yào bèi dǎ bàiThe aggressors are bound to be defeated.
  • 宣称xuān chēngto declareTraditional: 宣稱
    Dictionary sensesto assert; to claim
  • 威力wēi lìpower
    Dictionary sensesmight; formidable power
  • 别说bié shuōlet aloneTraditional: 別說
    Dictionary sensesto say nothing of; not to mention
    • 别说这个。bié shuō zhè geDon't say that.
    • 别说了好吗？bié shuō le hǎo maCan you please just shut up already?
  • 施工shī gōngconstruction
    Dictionary sensesto carry out construction or large-scale repairs
  • 勿wùdo not
    • 请勿望您的物品。qǐng wù wàng nín de wù pǐnPlease mind your belongings.
    • 未经允许请勿进入这个房间。wèi jīng yǔn xǔ qǐng wù jìn rù zhè ge fáng jiānDon't enter the room without permission.
  • 激素jī sùhormone
    • 变性者需要不断服用激素来维持性别特征吗？biàn xìng zhě xū yào bù duàn fú yòng jī sù lái wéi chí xìng bié tè zhēng maDo transgender people need to continuously use hormones to maintain their sexual characteristics?
  • 隆重lóng zhònggrand
    Dictionary sensesprosperous; ceremonious; solemn
  • 破裂pò lièto rupture
    Dictionary sensesto burst; (of a relationship etc) to break down
    • 和平谈判再次破裂了。hé píng tán pàn zài cì pò liè leThe peace talks failed again.
  • 废物fèi wùwaste; good-for-nothingTraditional: 廢物
    Dictionary sensesrubbish; waste material; useless person
  • 迷失mí shīto lose (one's way)
    Dictionary sensesto lose (one's bearings); to get lost
    • 我迷失了。wǒ mí shī leI'm lost.
  • 体制tǐ zhìsystemTraditional: 體制
    Dictionary sensesorganization
  • 守护shǒu hùto guardTraditional: 守護
    Dictionary sensesto protect
  • 协商xié shāngto consultTraditional: 協商
    Dictionary sensesto consult with; to talk things over; agreement
  • 视野shì yěfield of visionTraditional: 視野
    Dictionary sensesfield of view; (fig.) outlook; perspective
  • 委员wěi yuáncommittee memberTraditional: 委員
  • 蹦bèngto jump
    Dictionary sensesto bounce; to hop
  • 雷达léi dáradarTraditional: 雷達
    Dictionary senses(loanword) radar
    • 这艘船没有雷达装备。zhè sōu chuán méi yǒu léi dá zhuāng bèiThe ship is not equipped with radar.
  • 样品yàng pǐnsampleTraditional: 樣品
    Dictionary sensesspecimen
  • 关节guān jiéjointTraditional: 關節
    Dictionary sensesjoint (physiology); key point; critical phase
    • 当天气变冷的时候，我的关节疼痛。dàng tiān qì biàn lěng de shí hou wǒ de guān jié téng tòngMy joints ache when it gets cold.
  • 边界biān jièborderTraditional: 邊界
    Dictionary sensesboundary
  • 嫌疑xián yísuspicion
    Dictionary sensesto have suspicions
  • 波动bō dòngto fluctuateTraditional: 波動
    Dictionary sensesto undulate; wave motion; rise and fall
  • 海盗hǎi dàopirateTraditional: 海盜
    • 黑胡子是最有名的海盗之一。hēi hú zi shì zuì yǒu míng de hǎi dào zhī yīBlackbeard is one of the most famous pirates.
  • 往事wǎng shìpast events
    Dictionary sensesformer happenings
  • 与否yǔ fǒuwhether or notTraditional: 與否
    Dictionary senseswhether or not (at the end of a phrase)
  • 传言chuán yánrumourTraditional: 傳言
    Dictionary sensesrumor; hearsay
  • 何况hé kuànglet aloneTraditional: 何況
    Dictionary sensesto say nothing of; besides; what's more
    • 宝宝连走都不会，更何况是跑。bǎo bǎo lián zǒu dōu bù huì gèng hé kuàng shì pǎoThe baby can't walk, much less run.
  • 传闻chuán wénrumourTraditional: 傳聞
    Dictionary sensesrumor
    • 传闻被爆是真的。chuán wén bèi bào shì zhēn deThe rumor turned out true.
    • 看来传闻是真的了。kàn lai chuán wén shì zhēn de leI think the rumor is true.
  • 简称jiǎn chēngabbreviationTraditional: 簡稱
    Dictionary sensesto be abbreviated to; short form
    • 科学幻想的简称是科幻。kē xué huàn xiǎng de jiǎn chēng shì kē huànScience-Fiction can be abbreviated as sci-fi.
  • 发泄fā xièto ventTraditional: 發洩
    Dictionary sensesto give vent to (one's feelings)
    • 我就是需要发泄一下。wǒ jiù shì xū yào fā xiè yí xiàI just want to rant a little bit.
  • 预言yù yánto predictTraditional: 預言
    Dictionary sensesprophecy
    • 她有预言的能力。tā yǒu yù yán de néng lìShe has a gift for prophecy.
    • 说来奇怪，他的预言竟然真的灵验了。shuō lái qí guài tā de yù yán jìng rán zhēn de líng yàn leStrange to say, his prediction has come true.
  • 失控shī kòngout of control
    Dictionary sensesto go out of control
    • 事情要失控了。shì qing yào shī kòng leThings are getting out of control.
    • 他醉酒后会失控。tā zuì jiǔ hòu huì shī kòngHe is out of control when drunk.
  • 出动chū dòngto set outTraditional: 出動
    Dictionary sensesto start out on a trip; to dispatch troops
