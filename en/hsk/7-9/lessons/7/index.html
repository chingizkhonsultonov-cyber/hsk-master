  • 诱惑yòu huòtemptationTraditional: 誘惑
    Dictionary sensesto entice; to lure; to induce; to attract

    • 没有男人能够抵挡女人的诱惑。méi yǒu nán rén néng gòu dǐ dǎng nǚ ren de yòu huòNo man can resist the lure of a woman.

  • 迹象jì xiàngsignTraditional: 跡象
