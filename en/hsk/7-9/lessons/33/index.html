  • 马桶mǎ tǒngtoiletTraditional: 馬桶
    Dictionary senseschamber pot; wooden pan used as toilet; toilet bowl
    • 我们刚刚清洗了马桶。wǒ men gāng gang qīng xǐ le mǎ tǒngWe just now cleaned the toilet.
    • 我一般会在马桶上看书。wǒ yī bān huì zài mǎ tǒng shàng kàn shūI usually read on the loo.

    Dictionary sensesto show; presentation; demonstration
  • 迷惑mí huòto confuse
    Dictionary sensesto puzzle; to baffle

    Dictionary sensesdaybreak
  • 步入bù rùto step into
    Dictionary sensesto enter
  • 屠杀tú shāto massacreTraditional: 屠殺
    Dictionary sensesto slaughter
    • 许多自然环境保护主义者担心持续屠杀鲸鱼正推动这些动物走向灭绝。xǔ duō zì rán huán jìng bǎo hù zhǔ yì zhě dān xīn chí xù tú shā jīng yú zhèng tuī dòng zhè xiē dòng wù zǒu xiàng miè juéMany conservationists fear the continuing slaughter of whales is pushing these animals towards extinction.
  • 琢磨zuó moto ponder
    Dictionary sensesto carve and polish (jade); to polish and refine a literary work

    Dictionary sensesto pay one's respects to; to pay tribute to; to pay homage to
  • 表态biǎo tàito state one's positionTraditional: 表態
    Dictionary sensesto declare one's position; to say where one stands
  • 心声xīn shēngheartfelt wishTraditional: 心聲
    Dictionary sensesinner voice; aspiration
  • 预见yù jiànto foreseeTraditional: 預見
    Dictionary sensesto predict; to forecast; to envision; foresight
  • 双胞胎shuāng bāo tāitwinsMeasure word: 对Traditional: 雙胞胎
    Dictionary sensestwin
    • 我的孩子是双胞胎。wǒ de hái zi shì shuāng bāo tāiMy children are twins.
    • 这对双胞胎长得一模一样。zhè duì shuāng bāo tāi cháng de yī mú yī yàngThose twins look like two peas in a pod.
  • 殴打ōu dǎto beat upTraditional: 毆打
    Dictionary sensesto come to blows; battery (law)
  • 支票zhī piàochequeMeasure word: 本
    Dictionary sensescheck (bank)
    • 支票是纸做的。zhī piào shì zhǐ zuò deCheques are made of paper.
    • 我们接受支票。wǒ men jiē shòu zhī piàoWe accept checks.
  • 宝石bǎo shígemMeasure word: 枚、颗Traditional: 寶石
    Dictionary sensesprecious stone
  • 领土lǐng tǔterritoryTraditional: 領土
  • 生前shēng qiánduring one's lifetime
    Dictionary senses(of a deceased) during one's life; while living
    • 父亲生前留下来的老古董，原来只是一堆不值钱的垃圾。fù qīn shēng qián liú xià lái de lǎo gǔ dǒng yuán lái zhǐ shì yī duī bù zhí qián de lā jīThe antiques my father left when he died turned out to be nothing but worthless junk.
  • 下落xià luòwhereabouts
    Dictionary sensesto drop; to fall
  • 无能为力wú néng wéi lìpowerlessTraditional: 無能為力
    Dictionary senses(idiom) powerless to do anything about it
    • 我们也无能为力。wǒ men yě wú néng wéi lìThere's nothing we can do to help.
  • 老人家lǎo rén jiāelderly person
    Dictionary sensespolite term for old woman or man
    • 不要捉弄老人家。bú yào zhuō nòng lǎo rén jiāDon't make fun of old people.
    • 这位老人家非常仁慈。zhè wèi lǎo rén jiā fēi cháng rén cíThe old man is very kind.

    Dictionary sensesto leap; to bound; to skip
  • 坠zhuìto fallTraditional: 墜
    Dictionary sensesto drop; to weigh down
    • 飞机坠毁了。fēi jī zhuì huǐ leThe plane crashed.
    • 多么漂亮的小吊坠啊！duō me piào liang de xiǎo diào zhuì aWhat a beautiful locket!
  • 本性běn xìngnature
    Dictionary sensesnatural instincts; inherent quality
    • 好奇是哲学家的本性。hào qí shì zhé xué jiā de běn xìngWonder is the true character of the philosopher.
  • 相识xiāng shíto know each otherTraditional: 相識
    Dictionary sensesto get to know each other; acquaintance
  • 惯例guàn lìconventionTraditional: 慣例
    Dictionary sensesusual practice
  • 荒唐huāng tángabsurd
    Dictionary sensesbeyond belief; preposterous; intemperate; dissipated
    • 你就是荒唐。nǐ jiù shì huāng tángYou're just being ridiculous.
    • 多么荒唐的故事！duō me huāng táng de gù shiWhat a bizarre tale!
  • 必定bì dìngcertainly
    Dictionary sensesto be bound to; to be sure to
    • 他的政权必定会崩溃。tā de zhèng quán bì dìng huì bēng kuìHis regime is bound to collapse.
    • 脚踏两条船，必定落空。jiǎo tà liǎng tiáo chuán bì dìng luò kōngIf you run after two hares, you will catch neither.
  • 事宜shì yímatters
    Dictionary sensesarrangements
  • 弦xiánstringMeasure word: 根
    Dictionary sensesbow string; string of musical instrument; watchspring; chord (segment of curve)
    • 我弄断了我吉他的一根弦。wǒ nòng duàn le wǒ jí tā de yī gēn xiánI broke a string on my guitar.
  • 迫不及待pò bù jí dàiimpatient
    Dictionary senses(idiom) unable to hold oneself back; eager to do sth without delay
    • 他迫不及待地想要知道结果。tā pò bù jí dài de xiǎng yào zhī dào jié guǒHe is anxious to know the result.
  • 囚犯qiú fànprisoner
    Dictionary sensesconvict
    • 国王下令把囚犯释放。guó wáng xià lìng bǎ qiú fàn shì fàngThe king ordered that the prisoner should be set free.
  • 虚伪xū wěihypocriticalTraditional: 虛偽
    Dictionary sensesfalse; artificial; sham
  • 分泌fēn mìto secrete
    Dictionary sensessecretion
  • 予以yǔ yǐto give
    Dictionary sensesto impose; to apply
  • 触摸chù mōto touchTraditional: 觸摸
    • 我可以触摸这个吗？wǒ kě yǐ chù mō zhè ge maCan I touch this one too?
  • 好比hǎo bǐto be like
    Dictionary sensesto be just like; can be compared to
    • 公园和城市的关系，就好比肺和动物的关系一样。gōng yuán hé chéng shì de guān xi jiù hǎo bǐ fèi hé dòng wù de guān xi yī yàngParks are for the city what lungs are for animals.
  • 起步qǐ bùto start
    Dictionary sensesto set out; to set in motion; the start (of some activity)
    • 空间科学还在起步阶段。kōng jiān kē xué hái zài qǐ bù jiē duànSpace science is still in its infancy.
    • 我们的乐队刚起步时，只有小城市里的小俱乐部雇我们。wǒ men de yuè duì gāng qǐ bù shí zhǐ yǒu xiǎo chéng shì lǐ de xiǎo jù lè bù gù wǒ menWhen we started out, our band could only find small clubs in small cities that would hire us.
  • 余地yú dìroom, leewayTraditional: 餘地
    Dictionary sensesmargin; leeway
    • 那时我没有选择的余地。nà shí wǒ méi yǒu xuǎn zé de yú dìI had no choice.

    Dictionary sensesto amend; to rectify
