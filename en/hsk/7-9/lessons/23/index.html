  • 妥协tuǒ xiéto compromiseTraditional: 妥協
    Dictionary sensesto reach terms; a compromise
  • 宁静níng jìngtranquilTraditional: 寧靜
    Dictionary sensestranquility; serenity
  • 瞪dèngto stare
    Dictionary sensesto open (one's eyes) wide; to stare at; to glare at
    • 大家都在瞪着。dà jiā dōu zài dèng zhePeople were staring at us.
  • 来回lái huíback and forthTraditional: 來回
    Dictionary sensesto make a round trip; return journey; to and fro; repeatedly
    • 你每次来回都是公司的车子对吧？nǐ měi cì lái huí dōu shì gōng sī de chē zi duì baYou always go there and back in the company car, don't you?
  • 终结zhōng jiéendTraditional: 終結
    Dictionary sensesconclusion; to come to an end; to terminate (sth)
    • 所有悲剧都由死亡终结。suǒ yǒu bēi jù dōu yóu sǐ wáng zhōng jiéAll tragedies end with a death.
  • 可恶kě wùhatefulTraditional: 可惡
    Dictionary sensesrepulsive; vile; abominable
    • 可恶，家的钥匙到底放哪去了？kě wù jiā de yào shi dào dǐ fàng nǎ qù leShit, where the fuck did I put my home keys?
  • 泡沫pào mòfoam; bubble
    Dictionary senses(soap) bubble; (economic) bubble
  • 谴责qiǎn zéto condemnTraditional: 譴責
    Dictionary sensesto denounce; to criticize; condemnation; criticism
    • 谴责种族主义很重要，但光是谴责种族主义是不够的。qiǎn zé zhǒng zú zhǔ yì hěn zhòng yào dàn guāng shì qiǎn zé zhǒng zú zhǔ yì shì bù gòu deIt is important to condemn racism, but that alone is not enough.
  • 分歧fēn qídifference
    Dictionary sensesdivergent; difference (of opinion, position); disagreement; (math.) bifurcation
    • 是在这点上我们的意见有分歧。shì zài zhè diǎn shàng wǒ men de yì jiàn yǒu fēn qíIt's on this point that our opinions differ.
  • 烟花yān huāfireworksTraditional: 煙花
    Dictionary sensesprostitute (esp. in Yuan theater)
  • 纠缠jiū chánto pesterTraditional: 糾纏
    Dictionary sensesto be in a tangle; to nag
  • 海报hǎi bàoposterTraditional: 海報
    Dictionary sensesplaybill; notice
    • 他停下脚步看起海报。tā tíng xià jiǎo bù kàn qǐ hǎi bàoHe paused to look at the poster.
    • 那些海报现在都到哪里去了？nà xiē hǎi bào xiàn zài dōu dào nǎ li qù leWhere are those posters now?
  • 机密jī mìsecretTraditional: 機密
    Dictionary sensesclassified (information)
    • 机密文件被藏起来了。jī mì wén jiàn bèi cáng qǐ lai leThe classified documents were hidden.
  • 绳子shéng ziropeMeasure word: 条Traditional: 繩子
    Dictionary sensescord; string
    • 抓住绳子。zhuā zhù shéng ziGrab hold of the rope.
    • 他抓住了绳子。tā zhuā zhù le shéng ziHe caught the cord.
  • 剧情jù qíngplotTraditional: 劇情
    Dictionary sensesstory line; drama (genre)
  • 灿烂càn lànbrilliantTraditional: 燦爛
    Dictionary sensesto glitter; splendid
    • 这个世界是灿烂的吗？zhè ge shì jiè shì càn làn de maIs it splendid, this world?
  • 自行zì xíngby oneself
    Dictionary sensesvoluntary; autonomous; self-
    • 公司为了削减成本，拒绝让员工自行加班。gōng sī wèi le xuē jiǎn chéng běn jù jué ràng yuán gōng zì xíng jiā bānIn order to cut down on costs, the company refuses to allow employees to work overtime.
  • 拘留jū liúto detain
    Dictionary sensesto detain (a prisoner); to keep sb in custody
    • 这个男人被警察拘留了。zhè ge nán rén bèi jǐng chá jū liú leThe man was held in police custody.
  • 孕妇yùn fùpregnant womanTraditional: 孕婦
  • 腔qiāngcavity; accent
    Dictionary sensestalk; tune; accent (in one's speech); (old) classifier for carcasses of slaughtered livestock
  • 复合fù hécompoundTraditional: 復合
    Dictionary senses(of people who were estranged) to be reconciled; (of a couple) to get back together; complex; composite
  • 下场xià chǎngfate, endTraditional: 下場
    Dictionary sensesthe end; to conclude
    • 球员们跑下场，在中场休息时开了一个会。qiú yuán men pǎo xià chang zài zhōng chǎng xiū xi shí kāi le yī gè huìThe players ran off the pitch and had a meeting during half-time.
  • 匹配pǐ pèito match
    Dictionary sensesto mate or marry; matching; compatible
    • 它不匹配的拉丁之一。tā bù pǐ pèi de lā dīng zhī yīIt doesn't match the Latin one.
  • 气象qì xiàngweather; atmosphereTraditional: 氣象
    Dictionary sensesmeteorological condition; meteorology; ambience
    • 根据气象预报，明天会是晴天。gēn jù qì xiàng yù bào míng tiān huì shì qíng tiānThe weather forecast says it will be fine tomorrow.
    • 气象学家做预报几乎总是错的。qì xiàng xué jiā zuò yù bào jī hū zǒng shì cuò deThe weatherman's forecast is almost certainly wrong.
  • 傲àoproud, arrogant
    Dictionary sensesproud; arrogant; to despise; unyielding
    • 他以他的工作为傲。tā yǐ tā de gōng zuò wèi àoHe takes pride in his work.
    • 世界只能容忍有成就的人的自傲。shì jiè zhǐ néng róng rěn yǒu chéng jiù de rén de zì àoThe world tolerates conceit from those who are successful, but not from anybody else.
  • 介意jiè yìto mind
    Dictionary sensesto care about; to take offense
    • 我知道你介意。wǒ zhī dào nǐ jiè yìI know you're bothered.
    • 你介意我抽烟吗？nǐ jiè yì wǒ chōu yān maDo you mind if I smoke?
  • 编号biān hàonumber; to numberTraditional: 編號
    Dictionary sensesnumbering; serial number
  • 一体yì tǐa wholeTraditional: 一體
    Dictionary sensesan integral whole; all concerned; everybody
  • 辩biànto debateTraditional: 辯
    Dictionary sensesto dispute; to argue; to discuss
  • 巧合qiǎo hécoincidence
    Dictionary sensescoincidental; to coincide
    • 可能只是巧合。kě néng zhǐ shì qiǎo héIt could be just a coincidence.
    • 这一定是个巧合。zhè yī dìng shì gè qiǎo héIt must be a coincidence.
  • 古怪gǔ guàistrange
    Dictionary sensesweird; eccentric; bizarre
    • 你有没有发现他说话的语气有点古怪？nǐ yǒu méi yǒu fā xiàn tā shuō huà de yǔ qì yǒu diǎn gǔ guàiDid you pick up on that strange tone in his voice?
    • 我们在一家植物园里找到了许多稀奇古怪的植物。wǒ men zài yī jiā zhí wù yuán lǐ zhǎo dào le xǔ duō xī qí gǔ guài de zhí wùWe found many strange plants in a botanical garden.
  • 岩石yán shírock
    Dictionary senses(geology) rock
    • 一块岩石从上面落下。yī kuài yán shí cóng shàng miàn luò xiàA rock fell from above.
    • 他的头撞倒了一块岩石。tā de tóu zhuàng dào le yī kuài yán shíHe hit his head against a rock.
  • 裹guǒto wrap
    Dictionary sensesto wrap around; bundle; parcel; package
  • 克隆kè lóngclone
    Dictionary senses(biology) (loanword) to clone; a clone; (fig.) to make an identical copy of (a website etc)
  • 皱zhòuto wrinkleTraditional: 皺
    Dictionary senseswrinkled; to crease
  • 豹bàoleopard
    Dictionary sensespanther
    • 我还不敢想象他们在那个动物园里有一个雪豹，我以为它们是一种濒危物种。wǒ hái bù gǎn xiǎng xiàng tā men zài nà ge dòng wù yuán lǐ yǒu yī gè xuě bào wǒ yǐ wéi tā men shì yī zhǒng bīn wēi wù zhǒngI still can't believe they had a snow leopard in that zoo. I thought they were an endangered species.
  • 直至zhí zhìuntil
    Dictionary senseslasting until; up till (the present)
    • 他一直坚持他的工作直至他成功了。tā yī zhí jiān chí tā de gōng zuò zhí zhì tā chéng gōng leHe persisted in his work until he succeeded.
  • 出身chū shēnfamily background
    Dictionary sensesto be born of; to come from; class origin
    • 但是歌手并不否认她的出身。dàn shì gē shǒu bìng bù fǒu rèn tā de chū shēnHowever, the singer does not deny her ancestry.
  • 头号tóu hàonumber oneTraditional: 頭號
    Dictionary sensesfirst rate; top rank
  • 王国wáng guókingdomTraditional: 王國
    Dictionary sensesrealm
