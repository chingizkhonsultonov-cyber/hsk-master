    Morphemes语素2
    • Prefix-like morphemes 类前缀后—、亲—Prefixes 后 and 亲

      后 + noun · 亲 + noun

      后 post-, after: 后现代 postmodern, 后疫情时代 post-pandemic era. 亲 pro-, close to, or one's own: 亲人 family, 亲美 pro-American.

      When to use it

      后 = post-, after (后现代, 后疫情时代); 亲 = pro-, close to (亲美), or blood relation (亲兄弟).

      • 在后疫情时代，很多人习惯了线上工作。Zài hòu yìqíng shídài, hěn duō rén xíguànle xiànshàng gōngzuò.In the post-pandemic era, many people have got used to working online.
      • 后疫情时代，人们的生活方式发生了变化。Hòu yìqíng shídài, rénmen de shēnghuó fāngshì fāshēng le biànhuà.In the post-pandemic era, people's lifestyles have changed.
    • Suffix-like morphemes 类后缀—观、—鬼、—界、—然、—坛、—为、—制Suffixes 观, 鬼, 界, 然, 坛, 为, 制

      世界观 · 胆小鬼 · 教育界 · 突然 · 文坛 · 认为 · 学分制

      观 view: 人生观 outlook on life. 鬼 (scornful) person: 胆小鬼 coward. 界 circles, field: 教育界. 然 adverb ending: 突然, 显然. 坛 world of: 文坛 literary world. 为 verb ending: 认为, 成为. 制 system: 学分制 credit system.

      When to use it

      Suffixes: 观 view (世界观), 鬼 person with a vice (酒鬼, 胆小鬼), 界 circle/field (教育界), 然 adverb-maker (突然, 显然), 坛 sphere (文坛, 体坛), 为 verb-maker (成为, 认为), 制 system (学分制).

      • 每个人的人生观都不一样。Měi gè rén de rénshēngguān dōu bù yíyàng.Everyone has a different outlook on life.
      • 别做胆小鬼！Bié zuò dǎnxiǎoguǐ!Don't be a coward!
      • 他是教育界的知名人士。Tā shì jiàoyùjiè de zhīmíng rénshì.He's a well-known figure in education.
      • 别当胆小鬼！Bié dāng dǎnxiǎoguǐ!Don't be a coward!
    Parts of speech词类23
    • Verbs 动词巴不得、并非、除外、犯不着、犯得着、恨不得、可否、可谓、免不了、所谓、有所、予以Verbs like 巴不得, 免不了, 所谓, 有所

      巴不得 · 恨不得 · 免不了 · 犯不着 · 并非 · 可谓 · 所谓 · 有所 · 予以 · 除外 · 可否

      巴不得 and 恨不得 long to, wish one could. 免不了 can't avoid. 犯不着 not worth it (犯得着 in questions). 并非 is not at all. 可谓 can be called. 所谓 so-called. 有所 to some extent (有所提高). 予以 give (formal: 予以支持). 除外 excepted. 可否 whether or not (formal).

      When to use it

      巴不得/恨不得 = be dying to; 并非 = is not at all; 除外 = excepted; 犯不着/犯得着 = (not) worth it; 可否 = may I/is it possible (formal); 可谓 = can be called; 免不了 = unavoidable; 所谓 = so-called; 有所 = to some extent (有所提高); 予以 = give (formal).

      • 我恨不得马上飞回家。Wǒ hènbude mǎshàng fēi huí jiā.I wish I could fly home right now.
      • 为这点小事生气，犯不着。Wèi zhè diǎn xiǎo shì shēngqì, fànbuzháo.It's not worth getting angry over such a small thing.
      • 他的成绩有所提高。Tā de chéngjì yǒusuǒ tígāo.His grades have improved somewhat.
      • 我恨不得马上飞回家。Wǒ hènbude mǎshàng fēi huí jiā.I wish I could fly home right now.
      • 为这点小事生气，犯不着。Wèi zhè diǎn xiǎo shì shēngqì, fàn bu zháo.It's not worth getting angry over such a trifle.
      • 今年的成绩有所提高。Jīnnián de chéngjì yǒusuǒ tígāo.Results have improved somewhat this year.

      Compare

      恨不得 is for wishes you can't act on; 巴不得 for something you hope will happen.

    • Pronouns 代词Personal pronouns 人称代词我我 meaning “our side”

      我 + 国 / 校 / 方 / 公司

      In formal writing 我 before an organisation means ours: 我国 our country, 我校 our school, 我方 our side.

      When to use it

      我 can mean “we, our side” in formal writing: 我国 our country, 我校 our school.

      • 我校今年招收了三千名新生。Wǒ xiào jīnnián zhāoshōule sānqiān míng xīnshēng.Our school admitted three thousand new students this year.
      • 我校今年招收了三千名新生。Wǒ xiào jīnnián zhāoshōu le sānqiān míng xīnshēng.Our school admitted 3,000 new students this year.
    • Pronouns 代词Demonstratives 指示代词彼、这么着、兹彼, 这么着, 兹

      彼 (that, the other) · 这么着 (like this) · 兹 (hereby)

      彼 that, the other side, in set phrases: 此起彼伏, 知己知彼. 这么着 do it this way, spoken. 兹 hereby, now, in official notices.

      When to use it

      彼 = that, the other (彼此, 此起彼伏); 这么着 = like this (spoken, often a decision); 兹 = hereby (official notices).

      • 这么着吧，你先回去，我明天给你打电话。Zhèmezhe ba, nǐ xiān huíqu, wǒ míngtiān gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.Let's do it this way: you go back first, and I'll call you tomorrow.
      • 兹定于本月十日召开会议。Zī dìng yú běn yuè shí rì zhàokāi huìyì.A meeting is hereby scheduled for the 10th of this month.
      • 这么着吧，我们明天再谈。Zhème zhe ba, wǒmen míngtiān zài tán.Tell you what — let's talk tomorrow.
      • 兹定于五月一日召开大会。Zī dìng yú wǔ yuè yī rì zhàokāi dàhuì.It is hereby decided to hold the general meeting on 1 May.
    • Pronouns 代词Question words 疑问代词何、怎么着何 and 怎么着

      何 (what, written) · 怎么着 (what about it, spoken)

      何 is classical what, why, how, in set phrases: 为何 why, 如何 how, 何时 when. 怎么着 is spoken: what's going on, what will you do, or a challenge: 我就不去，怎么着?

      When to use it

      何 = what/why (written: 何时, 何地, 为何); 怎么着 = what about it / what to do (spoken).

      • 你为何不早说？Nǐ wèihé bù zǎo shuō?Why didn't you say so earlier?
      • 我就不去，你能怎么着？Wǒ jiù bú qù, nǐ néng zěnmezhe?I'm not going. What are you going to do about it?
      • 你为何不早说？Nǐ wèihé bù zǎo shuō?Why didn't you say so earlier?
      • 我就不去，你想怎么着？Wǒ jiù bú qù, nǐ xiǎng zěnme zhe?I'm just not going — so what are you going to do?
    • Pronouns 代词Quantity pronouns 数量代词若干若干: a certain number

      若干 + noun

      若干 means several, a number of, in formal language.

      When to use it

      若干 = some, a number of (formal).

      • 报告提出了若干建议。Bàogào tíchūle ruògān jiànyì.The report put forward a number of suggestions.
      • 会议提出了若干建议。Huìyì tíchū le ruògān jiànyì.The meeting put forward a number of proposals.
    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词重、截、缕、码、枚、摊、丸、则、幢、宗Literary measure words

      重 · 截 · 缕 · 码 · 枚 · 摊 · 丸 · 则 · 幢 · 宗

      重 layers (重重). 截 a section cut off. 缕 a wisp (of smoke, hair). 码 kind, matter (两码事 two different things). 枚 small objects (coins, medals). 摊 a puddle or pile. 丸 pills. 则 items of news or text. 幢 buildings. 宗 cases, deals.

      When to use it

      重 layers (一重山); 截 sections (一截绳子); 缕 wisps (一缕阳光); 码 matters (两码事); 枚 small objects (一枚硬币); 摊 puddles (一摊水); 丸 pills; 则 items of news (一则新闻); 幢 buildings; 宗 cases/deals (一宗交易).

      • 他获得了两枚金牌。Tā huòdéle liǎng méi jīnpái.He won two gold medals.
      • 这是两码事。Zhè shì liǎng mǎ shì.These are two different things.
      • 一缕阳光照进了房间。Yì lǚ yángguāng zhào jìn le fángjiān.A ray of sunlight shone into the room.
      • 学习和考试是两码事。Xuéxí hé kǎoshì shì liǎng mǎ shì.Studying and exams are two different things.

      Compare

      两码事 = two different things: 这是两码事.

    • Measure words 量词Verbal measure words 动量词通通: a bout of

      verb + 一通

      通 counts a bout of an action, often noisy or unpleasant: 骂了一通 gave a scolding, 说了一通.

      When to use it

      通 (tòng) counts bouts of speech or noise: 骂了一通, 打一通电话.

      • 他把我批评了一通。Tā bǎ wǒ pīpíngle yí tòng.He gave me a good telling-off.
      • 他把我批评了一通。Tā bǎ wǒ pīpíng le yí tòng.He gave me a good telling-off.
    • Measure words 量词Compound measure words 复合量词人次人次: person-times

      number + 人次

      人次 counts visits or attendance, where one person may be counted more than once.

      When to use it

      人次 = person-times (counts visits; one person visiting twice = 2).

      • 博物馆今年接待游客一百万人次。Bówùguǎn jīnnián jiēdài yóukè yìbǎi wàn réncì.The museum received a million visits this year.
      • 博物馆今年接待游客一百万人次。Bówùguǎn jīnnián jiēdài yóukè yìbǎi wàn réncì.The museum received a million visits this year.
    • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词大为、分外、何等、极度、极力、略、略微、颇、稍稍、万分、微、尤为、愈加、越发Formal degree adverbs: 颇, 略, 万分, 愈加 …

      大为 · 分外 · 何等 · 极度 · 极力 · 略 / 略微 · 颇 · 稍稍 · 万分 · 微 · 尤为 · 愈加 / 越发

      颇 quite, rather. 略 and 略微 slightly. 万分 extremely (万分感谢). 大为 greatly. 分外 especially. 何等 how (exclamation). 极度 extreme. 极力 do one's utmost. 尤为 particularly. 愈加 and 越发 all the more.

      When to use it

      Written degree adverbs: 大为 greatly, 分外 especially, 何等 how (exclamation), 极度 extremely, 极力 do one's utmost, 略/略微/稍稍/微 slightly, 颇 quite, 万分 extremely, 尤为 particularly, 愈加/越发 even more.

      • 对您的帮助，我们万分感谢。Duì nín de bāngzhù, wǒmen wànfēn gǎnxiè.We are extremely grateful for your help.
      • 他的话颇有道理。Tā de huà pō yǒu dàoli.What he says makes a lot of sense.
      • 听了这话，他越发生气了。Tīng le zhè huà, tā yuèfā shēngqì le.Hearing this, he got even angrier.
      • 这本书颇受欢迎。Zhè běn shū pō shòu huānyíng.This book is quite popular.
      • 我们万分感谢您的帮助。Wǒmen wànfēn gǎnxiè nín de bāngzhù.We're extremely grateful for your help.

      Compare

      越发 = all the more (things keep increasing); often after a cause.

    • Adverbs 副词Scope adverbs 范围副词大体、概、皆、尽、均、通通、统统、唯、唯独、唯有、无非、一概Formal range adverbs: 皆, 均, 唯独, 一概 …

      皆 / 均 / 尽 (all) · 统统 / 通通 (all, spoken) · 唯 / 唯独 / 唯有 (only) · 一概 (without exception) · 无非 (nothing but) · 大体 (on the whole)

      皆, 均, 尽 all (written). 统统 and 通通 all of it (spoken). 唯独 and 唯有 only, alone. 一概 without exception. 无非 nothing more than. 大体 roughly, on the whole. 概 entirely (概不退换 no returns).

      When to use it

      Written scope adverbs: 大体 on the whole; 概/一概 without exception; 皆/均/尽 all; 通通/统统 all (spoken); 唯/唯独/唯有 only; 无非 nothing but.

      • 大家都来了，唯独他没来。Dàjiā dōu lái le, wéidú tā méi lái.Everyone came except him alone.
      • 本店商品，概不退换。Běn diàn shāngpǐn, gài bú tuìhuàn.Goods from this shop cannot be returned or exchanged.
      • 大家都同意了，唯独他反对。Dàjiā dōu tóngyì le, wéidú tā fǎnduì.Everyone agreed except him.
      • 过期商品一概不退。Guòqī shāngpǐn yígài bú tuì.Expired goods are not refundable, without exception.
    • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词姑且、即、即时、届时、尽早、就此、历来、尚、随即、向来、一经、暂且、早日Formal time adverbs: 尚, 届时, 随即, 向来 …

      姑且 / 暂且 · 即 / 即时 · 届时 · 尽早 / 早日 · 就此 · 历来 / 向来 · 尚 · 随即 · 一经

      姑且 and 暂且 for the time being. 即 immediately, namely. 届时 when the time comes. 尽早 and 早日 as early as possible. 就此 at this point. 历来 and 向来 always, historically. 尚 still, yet (尚未 not yet). 随即 immediately after. 一经 once (一经发现).

      When to use it

      Time adverbs: 姑且/暂且 for the time being; 即/随即/即时 immediately; 届时 at that time; 尽早/早日 as early as possible; 就此 at this point; 历来/向来 always; 尚 still; 一经 once (it's done).

      • 这个问题尚未解决。Zhège wèntí shàng wèi jiějué.This problem has not yet been solved.
      • 祝你早日康复！Zhù nǐ zǎorì kāngfù!Get well soon!
      • 会议下午三点开始，届时请准时参加。Huìyì xiàwǔ sān diǎn kāishǐ, jièshí qǐng zhǔnshí cānjiā.The meeting starts at 3 p.m.; please be on time.
      • 届时请准时参加。Jièshí qǐng zhǔnshí cānjiā.Please attend on time when the day comes.
      • 祝你早日康复！Zhù nǐ zǎorì kāngfù!Get well soon!
      • 这个问题尚未解决。Zhège wèntí shàng wèi jiějué.This problem has not yet been solved.
    • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词连连、屡次、屡屡、频、时而、时时、相继、一个劲（儿）、一连、再度Formal frequency adverbs: 屡次, 相继, 一连 …

      连连 · 屡次 / 屡屡 · 频 · 时而 · 时时 · 相继 · 一个劲儿 · 一连 · 再度

      连连 repeatedly in quick succession (连连点头). 屡次 and 屡屡 time and again. 时而 sometimes, now and then. 相继 one after another. 一个劲儿 persistently, non-stop. 一连 in a row (一连三天). 再度 once again.

      When to use it

      Frequency: 连连 repeatedly (连连点头); 屡次/屡屡 time after time; 频 frequently; 时而 now and then; 时时 constantly; 相继 one after another; 一个劲儿 non-stop; 一连 in a row; 再度 once again.

      • 他一连三天没来上班。Tā yìlián sān tiān méi lái shàngbān.He didn't come to work for three days in a row.
      • 孩子一个劲儿地哭。Háizi yígejìnr de kū.The child kept on crying.
      • 一连下了三天雨。Yìlián xià le sān tiān yǔ.It rained three days in a row.
      • 孩子一个劲儿地哭。Háizi yí ge jìnr de kū.The child kept crying non-stop.
    • Adverbs 副词Manner adverbs 方式副词按理、按说、百般、不由得、趁机、趁早、大肆、断然、公然、火速、截然、竭力、尽情、就近、刻意、拼命、强行、任意、日趋、如期、擅自、私自、特此、瞎、一并、一举、一手、毅然、有意、照常、照例、照样、执意、骤然、逐一、专程Manner adverbs: 趁机, 拼命, 照常, 专程 …

      adverb + verb

      A large group describing how something is done. Common ones: 按说 normally speaking. 不由得 can't help. 趁机 take the chance. 趁早 the sooner the better. 尽情 to one's heart's content. 拼命 desperately. 擅自 without permission. 照常 as usual. 照样 all the same. 逐一 one by one. 专程 make a special trip. 毅然 resolutely. 瞎 blindly, recklessly.

      When to use it

      Manner adverbs (formal and vivid): 按理/按说 normally, 百般 in every way, 不由得 can't help, 趁机/趁早 take the chance / early, 断然 resolutely, 公然 openly, 竭力/拼命 with all one's might, 尽情 to one's heart's content, 擅自/私自 without permission, 照常/照例/照样 as usual, 专程 on a special trip, 逐一 one by one, etc.

      • 他专程从北京来看我。Tā zhuānchéng cóng Běijīng lái kàn wǒ.He made a special trip from Beijing to see me.
      • 放假了，孩子们尽情地玩儿。Fàngjià le, háizimen jìnqíng de wánr.It's the holidays and the kids are playing to their hearts' content.
      • 别瞎说！Bié xiā shuō!Don't talk nonsense!
      • 按说他早该到了，怎么还没来？Ànshuō tā zǎo gāi dào le, zěnme hái méi lái?He should have arrived long ago — why isn't he here?
      • 假期里大家尽情地玩。Jiàqī li dàjiā jìnqíng de wán.Everyone played to their heart's content in the holidays.
      • 他专程来北京看望老师。Tā zhuānchéng lái Běijīng kànwàng lǎoshī.He made a special trip to Beijing to visit his teacher.

      Compare

      按说 introduces what should be, usually followed by a contrast: 按说他该到了，可是….

    • Adverbs 副词Negation adverbs 否定副词不见得、绝不、无从、无须、无可、勿Formal negatives: 不见得, 无须, 勿 …

      不见得 · 绝不 · 无从 · 无须 · 无可 · 勿

      不见得 not necessarily. 绝不 absolutely not. 无从 have no way to (无从知道). 无须 need not. 无可 cannot be (无可奈何). 勿 do not, on signs: 请勿吸烟.

      When to use it

      不见得 = not necessarily; 绝不 = absolutely not; 无从 = have no way to; 无须 = need not; 无可 = nothing can (无可奈何); 勿 = do not (signs).

      • 请勿吸烟。Qǐng wù xīyān.No smoking, please.
      • 他说的不见得对。Tā shuō de bú jiànde duì.What he says isn't necessarily right.
      • 请勿吸烟。Qǐng wù xīyān.No smoking, please.
      • 我绝不会放弃。Wǒ jué bú huì fàngqì.I will never give up.
    • Adverbs 副词Modal (attitude) adverbs 语气副词必定、必将、不妨、不免、反倒、非得、分明、怪不得、果真、好在、何必、竟、愣、莫非、难怪、宁可、宁愿、碰巧、偏偏、恰巧、切、情愿、势必、索性、万万、未免、务必、幸好、幸亏、终究、何不、何尝、何苦Tone adverbs: 何必, 难怪, 宁可, 幸亏, 务必 …

      adverb + verb / sentence

      A large group showing the speaker's attitude. Key ones: 不妨 might as well. 何必 why bother. 难怪 and 怪不得 no wonder. 宁可 would rather. 幸亏 and 幸好 luckily. 务必 must be sure to. 未免 rather too. 偏偏 just, contrary to wishes. 索性 simply, just. 莫非 could it be. 终究 after all. 万万 absolutely (with negative).

      When to use it

      Mood adverbs: 必定/必将/势必 surely; 不妨 might as well; 不免/未免 unavoidably / rather too; 反倒 instead; 非得 must; 怪不得/难怪 no wonder; 好在/幸好/幸亏 luckily; 何必/何苦 why bother; 莫非 could it be; 宁可/宁愿 would rather; 索性 simply; 务必 be sure to; 终究 after all; 何尝 never really.

      • 幸亏你提醒我，不然我就忘了。Xìngkuī nǐ tíxǐng wǒ, bùrán wǒ jiù wàng le.Luckily you reminded me, or I'd have forgotten.
      • 难怪他今天这么高兴。Nánguài tā jīntiān zhème gāoxìng.No wonder he's so happy today.
      • 你何必这么着急呢？Nǐ hébì zhème zháojí ne?Why get so worked up?
      • 怪不得他这么高兴，原来考上了。Guàibude tā zhème gāoxìng, yuánlái kǎo shàng le.No wonder he's so happy — he got in.
      • 你不妨试试这个办法。Nǐ bùfáng shìshi zhège bànfǎ.You might as well try this method.
      • 请务必按时到达。Qǐng wùbì ànshí dàodá.Please be sure to arrive on time.

      • 这件事非得你去才能解决。Zhè jiàn shì fěiděi nǐ qù cái néng jiějué.Only you going will sort this out.
      • 这件事非得你去才行。Zhè jiàn shì fēiděi nǐ qù cái xíng.Only you going will do.
    • Simple complex sentences 简单复句Contracted complex sentences 紧缩复句任……也……任……也: no matter how

      任 + person + verb，也 + result

      No matter what someone does, the result doesn't change: 任你怎么说，他也不听.

      When to use it

      任……也…… = no matter how (someone) …, still ….

      • 任你怎么劝，他也不听。Rèn nǐ zěnme quàn, tā yě bù tīng.No matter how you try to persuade him, he won't listen.
      • 任你怎么说，他也不听。Rèn nǐ zěnme shuō, tā yě bù tīng.No matter what you say, he won't listen.
    • Multi-clause sentences 多重复句多重并列复句Multi-layer parallel sentences

      A，B；C，D (several parallel parts)

      Several parallel clauses, sometimes grouped with semicolons, describe different aspects at the same level.

      When to use it

      Multiple coordinate clauses: several parallel parts, each may contain its own linked pair.

      • 这里春天开花，夏天下雨；秋天天高气爽，冬天很少下雪。Zhèlǐ chūntiān kāi huā, xiàtiān xià yǔ; qiūtiān tiān gāo qì shuǎng, dōngtiān hěn shǎo xià xuě.Here flowers bloom in spring and it rains in summer; autumn is clear and crisp, and it rarely snows in winter.
      • 他不但会说英语，而且会说汉语，还会说一点儿日语。Tā búdàn huì shuō Yīngyǔ, érqiě huì shuō Hànyǔ, hái huì shuō yìdiǎnr Rìyǔ.He speaks not only English but also Chinese, and a little Japanese.

      Compare

      Find the top-level link first, then read inside each part.

    • Multi-clause sentences 多重复句多重转折复句Multi-layer contrast sentences

      虽然 A，但是 B，可 C

      A contrast inside a contrast: one turn of thought leads to another.

      When to use it

      Multiple contrast: a 虽然…但是 contrast with another clause inside one part.

      • 虽然他很努力，但是成绩不好，不过老师还是表扬了他。Suīrán tā hěn nǔlì, dànshì chéngjì bù hǎo, búguò lǎoshī háishi biǎoyángle tā.Although he worked hard, his grades weren't good, but the teacher still praised him.
      • 虽然他很努力，也很聪明，但是这次还是没考好。Suīrán tā hěn nǔlì, yě hěn cōngming, dànshì zhè cì háishi méi kǎo hǎo.Although he works hard and is clever, he still didn't do well this time.
    • Multi-clause sentences 多重复句多重假设复句Multi-layer conditional sentences

      如果 A，就 B；如果 C，就 D

      Several conditions and results are combined in one sentence.

      When to use it

      Multiple conditional: an if-clause whose result contains another link.

      • 如果明天晴天，我们就去爬山；如果下雨，就在家看书。Rúguǒ míngtiān qíngtiān, wǒmen jiù qù pá shān; rúguǒ xià yǔ, jiù zài jiā kàn shū.If it's sunny tomorrow we'll go hiking; if it rains we'll read at home.
      • 如果明天不下雨，我们就去爬山，然后在山上吃饭。Rúguǒ míngtiān bú xià yǔ, wǒmen jiù qù pá shān, ránhòu zài shān shang chī fàn.If it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll go hiking and then eat on the mountain.
    • Multi-clause sentences 多重复句多重因果复句Multi-layer cause and effect

      因为 A，所以 B，因此 C

      A chain of causes and results.

      When to use it

      Multiple cause and result: a cause with several parts, or a result that leads to another.

      • 因为下大雪，所以路很滑，因此很多人迟到了。Yīnwèi xià dà xuě, suǒyǐ lù hěn huá, yīncǐ hěn duō rén chídào le.Because of heavy snow, the roads were slippery, so many people were late.
      • 因为路上堵车，又下着大雨，所以我们迟到了。Yīnwèi lù shang dǔ chē, yòu xià zhe dà yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen chídào le.Because of traffic and heavy rain, we were late.
    • Multi-clause sentences 多重复句多重递进复句Multi-layer additive sentences

      不但 A，而且 B，甚至 C

      Each clause goes a step further than the last.

      When to use it

      Multiple progressive: each step goes further than the last.

      • 他不但会说汉语，而且会写，甚至能翻译。Tā búdàn huì shuō Hànyǔ, érqiě huì xiě, shènzhì néng fānyì.He not only speaks Chinese but writes it, and can even translate.
      • 他不但自己学得好，而且帮助同学，甚至还给老师提建议。Tā búdàn zìjǐ xué de hǎo, érqiě bāngzhù tóngxué, shènzhì hái gěi lǎoshī tí jiànyì.He not only studies well but helps classmates and even gives the teacher suggestions.

    • 时间顺序Ordering a text by time

      首先 → 接着 → 然后 → 最后

      In longer texts, events are linked in time order with words like 先, 接着, 然后, 后来, 最后.

      When to use it

      Paragraph in time order: 先、然后、接着、最后 move the story forward.

      • 他先去了银行，接着去了超市，最后才回家。Tā xiān qùle yínháng, jiēzhe qùle chāoshì, zuìhòu cái huí jiā.He went to the bank first, then the supermarket, and only then home.
      • 早上他先跑步，然后吃早饭，接着去上班。Zǎoshang tā xiān pǎobù, ránhòu chī zǎofàn, jiēzhe qù shàngbān.In the morning he runs first, then has breakfast, then goes to work.
    • 空间顺序Ordering a text by space

      从外到里 · 从上到下 · 左边 … 右边 …

      Descriptions move through a place in order: outside to inside, top to bottom, left to right.

      When to use it

      Paragraph in space order: describe from near to far, outside to inside, etc. (前面、旁边、远处).

      • 走进大门，左边是图书馆，右边是食堂，前面是教学楼。Zǒujìn dàmén, zuǒbian shì túshūguǎn, yòubian shì shítáng, qiánmiàn shì jiàoxuélóu.Through the gate, the library is on the left, the canteen on the right and the teaching building ahead.
      • 门前是一条小河，河边种着柳树，远处是青山。Mén qián shì yì tiáo xiǎo hé, hé biān zhòng zhe liǔshù, yuǎnchù shì qīngshān.In front of the door is a stream, willows line its bank, and in the distance are green hills.
    • 逻辑顺序Ordering a text by logic

      首先 … 其次 … 再次 … 最后 / 因此

      Arguments move from points to conclusion using 首先, 其次, 此外, 因此, 总之.

      When to use it

      Paragraph in logical order: point → reason → example → conclusion (首先、其次、因此、总之).

      • 首先，运动对身体好；其次，运动能让人放松。因此，我们应该多运动。Shǒuxiān, yùndòng duì shēntǐ hǎo; qícì, yùndòng néng ràng rén fàngsōng. Yīncǐ, wǒmen yīnggāi duō yùndòng.First, exercise is good for the body; second, it helps you relax. So we should exercise more.
      • 运动很重要。首先，它让身体健康；其次，它能减轻压力。因此，我们应该每天运动。Yùndòng hěn zhòngyào. Shǒuxiān, tā ràng shēntǐ jiànkāng; qícì, tā néng jiǎnqīng yālì. Yīncǐ, wǒmen yīnggāi měi tiān yùndòng.Exercise matters. First, it keeps you healthy; second, it reduces stress. So we should exercise daily.
    • 话题延伸Extending a topic

      说到 … · 至于 … · 不仅如此 …

      Phrases like 说到, 关于这一点, 不仅如此 carry the topic further.

      When to use it

      Topic extension: the next sentence picks up something from the last and develops it.

      • 说到旅行，我最喜欢的地方是云南。Shuōdào lǚxíng, wǒ zuì xǐhuan de dìfang shì Yúnnán.Speaking of travel, my favourite place is Yunnan.
      • 我有一个朋友。这个朋友很喜欢旅行，他去过三十多个国家。Wǒ yǒu yí ge péngyou. Zhège péngyou hěn xǐhuan lǚxíng, tā qù guo sānshí duō ge guójiā.I have a friend. This friend loves travelling; he's been to over thirty countries.
    • 话题转换Changing the topic

      对了 · 另外 · 顺便说一下 · 言归正传

      对了, 另外, 顺便问一下 switch to a new topic; 言归正传 brings talk back to the main point.

      When to use it

      Topic shift: markers like 至于、另外、说到 move to a new topic.

      • 对了，你明天有空吗？Duì le, nǐ míngtiān yǒu kòng ma?Oh, by the way, are you free tomorrow?
      • 工作的事就这么定了。至于工资，我们下次再谈。Gōngzuò de shì jiù zhème dìng le. Zhìyú gōngzī, wǒmen xià cì zài tán.The job is settled. As for salary, we'll discuss it next time.
    • 承前省略Leaving out words already mentioned

      A + verb …，(A) + verb …

      Once the subject or object is known, later clauses drop it: 他来了，(他)坐下就吃.

      When to use it

      Forward ellipsis: a word already said is left out later (usually the subject).

      • 他走进教室，看了看，又出去了。Tā zǒujìn jiàoshì, kànle kàn, yòu chūqu le.He walked into the classroom, looked around and went out again.
      • 小王进了门，(他)放下书包，(他)就去做饭了。Xiǎo Wáng jìn le mén, fàng xià shūbāo, jiù qù zuò fàn le.Xiao Wang came in, put down his bag and went to cook.

      Compare

      Chinese drops a repeated subject much more freely than English or Russian.

    • 蒙后省略Leaving out words that come later

      (subject) verb …，subject + verb …

      Sometimes the first clause leaves out a subject that only appears in the next clause.

      When to use it

      Backward ellipsis: something is left out first and only named later.

      • 刚到中国的时候，我一句汉语都不会说。Gāng dào Zhōngguó de shíhou, wǒ yí jù Hànyǔ dōu bú huì shuō.When I first got to China, I couldn't speak a word of Chinese.
      • (他)一进门，(他)还没坐下，小李就开始抱怨。Yí jìn mén, hái méi zuò xià, Xiǎo Lǐ jiù kāishǐ bàoyuàn.No sooner in the door, not even sitting down, Xiao Li started complaining.
