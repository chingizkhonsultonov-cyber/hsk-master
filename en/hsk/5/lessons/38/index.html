  • 宝贵bǎo guìpreciousTraditional: 寶貴
    Dictionary sensesvaluable; to value; to treasure; to set store by
    • 宝贵的时间已失去。bǎo guì de shí jiān yǐ shī qùValuable time has been lost.
    • 时间是世界上最宝贵的东西。shí jiān shì shì jiè shàng zuì bǎo guì de dōng xiTime is the world's most valuable thing.
  • 进口jìn kǒuto importTraditional: 進口
    Dictionary sensesimported; entrance; inlet (for the intake of air, water etc)
    • 日本米市场禁止进口。rì běn mǐ shì chǎng jìn zhǐ jìn kǒuJapan's rice market is closed to imports.
    • 他想要减少进口品的税。tā xiǎng yào jiǎn shǎo jìn kǒu pǐn de shuìHe wanted to reduce the tax on imports.
