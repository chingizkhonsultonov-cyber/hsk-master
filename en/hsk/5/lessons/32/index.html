  • 约定yuē dìngto agree on; agreementTraditional: 約定
    Dictionary sensesto agree on sth (after discussion); to conclude a bargain; to arrange; to promise
  • 技能jì néngskill
    Dictionary sensestechnical ability
    • 控制一个班级需要你做老师的所有技能。kòng zhì yī gè bān jí xū yào nǐ zuò lǎo shī de suǒ yǒu jì néngTo control a class calls for all your skills as a teacher.
  • 消费xiāo fèito consume; consumptionTraditional: 消費
    Dictionary sensesto consume (goods and services, resources etc)
    • 在日本生活消费很高。zài rì běn shēng huó xiāo fèi hěn gāoLife in Japan is expensive.
  • 实行shí xíngto carry out, implementTraditional: 實行
    Dictionary sensesto implement; to carry out; to put into practice
    • 我们实行了那个计划。wǒ men shí xíng le nà ge jì huàWe carried out that plan.
    • 你们应该实行你们最初的计划。nǐ men yīng gāi shí xíng nǐ men zuì chū de jì huàYou must carry out your first plan.
  • 打扮dǎ banto dress up
    Dictionary sensesto decorate; to dress; to make up; to adorn
    • 他打扮得像女人一样。tā dǎ ban de xiàng nǚ ren yī yàngHe dressed up as a woman.
    • 我不在乎别人怎么看我的打扮。wǒ bù zài hu bié ren zěn me kàn wǒ de dǎ banI don't care what people think about the way I dress.
  • 乐趣lè qùpleasureTraditional: 樂趣
    Dictionary sensesdelight; joy
    • 无论读书的目的是什么，书总会带给我们乐趣和满足。wú lùn dú shū de mù dì shì shén me shū zǒng huì dài gěi wǒ men lè qù hé mǎn zúNo matter what your main purpose is in reading, books should never fail to provide contentment and satisfaction.
  • 知名zhī míngwell-known
    Dictionary sensesfamous
  • 裁判cái pànreferee
    Dictionary senses(law) to judge; to adjudicate; verdict; judgement
  • 从不cóng bùneverTraditional: 從不
    • 他从不喝酒。tā cóng bù hē jiǔHe never drinks alcohol.
    • 我从不喝啤酒。wǒ cóng bù hē pí jiǔI never drink beer.
  • 渐渐jiàn jiàngraduallyTraditional: 漸漸
    • 叫声渐渐地变弱了。jiào shēng jiàn jiàn de biàn ruò leThe sound of shouting grew faint.
    • 天渐渐黑了。我们回家吧。tiān jiàn jiàn hēi le wǒ men huí jiā baIt is getting dark. Let's go home.
  • 口味kǒu wèitaste
    Dictionary sensesa person's preferences; tastes (in food); flavor
    • 这个酸奶是什么口味的？zhè ge suān nǎi shì shén me kǒu wèi deWhat flavour is this yoghurt?
  • 具备jù bèito have; to possessTraditional: 具備
    Dictionary sensesequipped with; able to fulfill (conditions or requirements)
    • 孩子学钢琴要具备哪些条件？hái zi xué gāng qín yào jù bèi nǎ xiē tiáo jiànWhat are the prerequisites for a child to learn the piano?
  • 姿势zī shìpostureTraditional: 姿勢
    Dictionary sensesposition
  • 增强zēng qiángto strengthenTraditional: 增強
    Dictionary sensesto increase
  • 谨慎jǐn shèncautiousTraditional: 謹慎
    Dictionary sensesprudent
    • 我一生都谨慎行事。wǒ yī shēng dōu jǐn shèn xíng shìI've been careful my whole life.
    • 应该更谨慎地处理它。yīng gāi gèng jǐn shèn de chǔ lǐ tāIt must be done more carefully.
  • 握wòto hold, grasp
    Dictionary sensesto hold; to grasp; to clench (one's fist); classifier: a handful
    • 用双手握住球。yòng shuāng shǒu wò zhù qiúHold the ball in both hands.
    • 你在哪里和他们握的手？nǐ zài nǎ li hé tā men wò de shǒuWhere did you shake hands with them?
  • 尽力jìn lìto do one's bestTraditional: 盡力
    Dictionary sensesto strive one's hardest; to spare no effort
    • 尽力而为。jìn lì ér wèiDo your best.
    • 我们尽力了。wǒ men jìn lì leWe've done everything possible.
  • 陌生mò shēngunfamiliar
    Dictionary sensesstrange
    • 她对我来说是一个完全陌生的人。tā duì wǒ lái shuō shì yī gè wán quán mò shēng de rénShe's a complete stranger to me.
  • 新娘xīn niángbride
  • 车祸chē huòcar accidentMeasure word: 起Traditional: 車禍
    Dictionary sensestraffic accident; car crash
    • 车祸是怎么发生的？chē huò shì zěn me fā shēng deHow did the traffic accident happen?
    • 他在一次车祸中受伤了。tā zài yī cì chē huò zhōng shòu shāng leHe was hurt in a car accident.
  • 扶fúto support; to help up
    Dictionary sensesto support with the hand; to help sb up; to support oneself by holding onto something; to help
  • 下载xià zǎito downloadTraditional: 下載
    Dictionary sensesalso pr. [xia4zai4]
    • 假如你没有这个程序，你现在就可以去下载。jiǎ rú nǐ méi yǒu zhè ge chéng xù nǐ xiàn zài jiù kě yǐ qù xià zǎiIf you do not have this program, you can download it now.
  • 维修wéi xiūto repair, maintainTraditional: 維修
    Dictionary sensesmaintenance (of equipment); to protect and maintain
    • 这间房子要维修了。zhè jiān fáng zi yào wéi xiū leThis house must be done up.
  • 整整zhěng zhěngwhole, full
    Dictionary senseswhole; full
  • 构成gòu chéngto formTraditional: 構成
    Dictionary sensesto constitute; to compose; to make up; to configure (computing)
  • 口袋kǒu dàipocket
    Dictionary sensesbag; sack
    • 这件外套没有口袋。zhè jiàn wài tào méi yǒu kǒu dàiThis coat doesn't have pockets.
  • 不良bù liángbad
    Dictionary sensesharmful; unhealthy
    • 你吃不喜欢的食物会消化不良。nǐ chī bù xǐ huan de shí wù huì xiāo huà bù liángFood you eat that you don't like will not be digested well.
  • 无限wú xiànunlimited, infiniteTraditional: 無限
    Dictionary sensesunlimited; unbounded
  • 发言fā yánto speak; speechMeasure word: 个Traditional: 發言
    Dictionary sensesto make a speech; statement; utterance
    • 他突然发言了。tā tū rán fā yán leAll at once, he spoke out.
    • 通常女人都是最后发言有决定权的。tōng cháng nǚ ren dōu shì zuì hòu fā yán yǒu jué dìng quán deWomen will have the last word.
  • 真诚zhēn chéngsincereTraditional: 真誠
    Dictionary sensesgenuine; true
