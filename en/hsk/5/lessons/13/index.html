  • 尽快jǐn kuàias soon as possibleTraditional: 儘快
    Dictionary sensesas quickly as possible
    • 尽快来。jǐn kuài láiCome as fast as you can.
    • 我尽快需要。wǒ jǐn kuài xū yàoI need it ASAP.
  • 药物yào wùmedicationTraditional: 藥物
    Dictionary sensesmedicaments; pharmaceuticals; medicine; drug
    • 将你需要的药物写在这张纸上。jiāng nǐ xū yào de yào wù xiě zài zhè zhāng zhǐ shàngWrite down the medicine you need on this piece of paper.
    • 药物缓解了他的痛苦。yào wù huǎn jiě le tā de tòng kǔThe medicine decreased his pain.
  • 进一步jìn yí bùfurtherTraditional: 進一步
    Dictionary sensesto go a step further; (develop, understand, improve etc) more
    • 所以我们喜欢句子。更进一步来说，我们喜欢语言。suǒ yǐ wǒ men xǐ huan jù zi gèng jìn yī bù lái shuō wǒ men xǐ huan yǔ yánSo we love sentences. But, even more, we love languages.
  • 移动yí dòngto move; mobileTraditional: 移動
    Dictionary sensesmovement; migration; portable
    • 她慢慢移动向前。tā màn màn yí dòng xiàng qiánShe slowly moved forward.
    • 马停了下来，而且拒绝移动。mǎ tíng le xià lai ér qiě jù jué yí dòngThe horse stopped and wouldn't move.
  • 销售xiāo shòuto sell; salesTraditional: 銷售
    Dictionary sensesto market; sales (representative, agreement etc)
    • 谁负责销售部门？shéi fù zé xiāo shòu bù ménWho's in charge of the sales section?
  • 呼吸hū xīto breathe
    • 用你的鼻子呼吸。yòng nǐ de bí zi hū xīBreathe through your nose.
    • 我总是忘记呼吸。wǒ zǒng shì wàng jì hū xīI always forget to breathe.
  • 可怕kě pàterrible
    Dictionary sensesawful; dreadful; fearful; formidable
    • 这太可怕了。zhè tài kě pà leThat's terrible.
    • 但是地震仍然是最可怕的事。dàn shì dì zhèn réng rán shì zuì kě pà de shìBut earthquakes are still as frightening as ever.
  • 确认què rènto confirmTraditional: 確認
    Dictionary sensesto verify; confirmation
    • 但是所有这些数据都没有得到确认。dàn shì suǒ yǒu zhè xiē shù jù dōu méi yǒu dé dào què rènHowever, all these data were not confirmed.
  • 防fángto guard against
    Dictionary sensesto protect; to defend; to prevent
    • 这手表是防水的。zhè shǒu biǎo shì fáng shuǐ deThis watch is waterproof.
    • 以防下雨你最好带把伞。yǐ fáng xià yǔ nǐ zuì hǎo dài bǎ sǎnYou'd better take an umbrella with you in case it rains.
  • 舞台wǔ táistageTraditional: 舞臺
    Dictionary senses(lit. and fig.) stage; arena
    • 舞台上有著名音乐家吗？wǔ tái shàng yǒu zhù míng yīn yuè jiā maAre there any famous musicians on the stage?
  • 化huàto change; -ise
    Dictionary sensesto make into; to change into; -ization; to ... -ize
    • 雪化了。xuě huà leThe snow is melted.
    • 我们反对全球化。wǒ men fǎn duì quán qiú huàWe are against globalization.
  • 出色chū sèoutstanding
    Dictionary sensesremarkable
    • 他是位出色的作家。tā shì wèi chū sè de zuò jiāHe is a good writer.
    • 他在运动会上的表现十分出色。tā zài yùn dòng huì shàng de biǎo xiàn shí fēn chū sèHe was able to put up remarkable performances at the sports competition.
  • 调整tiáo zhěngto adjust; adjustmentMeasure word: 个Traditional: 調整
    Dictionary sensesrevision
  • 冰bīngto chill; icyMeasure word: 块
    Dictionary sensesice; to chill sth; (of an object or substance) to feel cold; (of a person) cold
  • 领lǐngto lead; to receive; collarTraditional: 領
    Dictionary sensesneck; classifier for clothes, mats, screens etc
    • 一领到工资就把钱还给你。yī lǐng dào gōng zī jiù bǎ qián hái gěi nǐI'll pay you back once I receive my salary.
    • 日本是世界高科技产业的领头人。rì běn shì shì jiè gāo kē jì chǎn yè de lǐng tóu rénJapan is a leader in the world's high-tech industry.
  • 滚gǔnto rollTraditional: 滾
    Dictionary sensesto boil; to take a hike; Get lost!
    • 我爱摇滚。wǒ ài yáo gǔnI love rock.
  • 结jiéknot; to tieTraditional: 結
    Dictionary sensessturdy; bond; to bind
    • 下周可能会结冰。xià zhōu kě néng huì jié bīngIt may freeze next week.
    • 水结冻后，变成冰。shuǐ jié dòng hòu biàn chéng bīngWhen water freezes it becomes ice.
  • 讲话jiǎng huàto speak; speechMeasure word: 个Traditional: 講話
    Dictionary sensesa speech; to talk; to address
    • 我妈妈讲话很慢。wǒ mā ma jiǎng huà hěn mànMy mother speaks slowly.
    • 不要这样对我讲话。bú yào zhè yàng duì wǒ jiǎng huàDon't talk to me like this.
  • 紧急jǐn jíurgentTraditional: 緊急
    Dictionary sensesemergency
    • 紧急出口在哪里？jǐn jí chū kǒu zài nǎ liWhere's the emergency exit?
    • 这件事非常紧急。zhè jiàn shì fēi cháng jǐn jíThis matter is very urgent.
  • 宣传xuān chuánto publicize; propagandaMeasure word: 个Traditional: 宣傳
    Dictionary sensesto disseminate; to give publicity to
    • 你不用担心宣传的事。nǐ bù yòng dān xīn xuān chuán de shìYou don't have to worry about publicity.
    • 我们在电视上宣传我们的产品。wǒ men zài diàn shì shàng xuān chuán wǒ men de chǎn pǐnWe advertise our products on TV.
  • 撞zhuàngto crash into
    Dictionary sensesto knock against; to bump into; to run into; to meet by accident
    • 你被车撞过吗？nǐ bèi chē zhuàng guò maHave you ever been hit by a car?
    • 我差点被车撞倒。wǒ chà diǎn bèi chē zhuàng dàoI was nearly hit by a car.
  • 优势yōu shìadvantageTraditional: 優勢
    Dictionary sensessuperiority; dominance
    • 这种科技的优势是什么？zhè zhǒng kē jì de yōu shì shì shén meWhat is the advantage of this technology?
  • 随时suí shíat any timeTraditional: 隨時
    Dictionary sensesat all times; at the right time; whenever necessary
    • 我随时可以开始。wǒ suí shí kě yǐ kāi shǐI can start anytime.
    • 它随时有可能发生。tā suí shí yǒu kě néng fā shēngIt may occur at any moment.
  • 付出fù chūto give; to pay
    Dictionary sensesto expend; to invest (energy or time)
    • 感谢你的付出。gǎn xiè nǐ de fù chūThanks for everything.
    • 付出什么，就会得到什么。fù chū shén me jiù huì dé dào shén meWhat you give, you will get it back.
  • 时期shí qīperiodMeasure word: 个Traditional: 時期
    Dictionary sensesphase
    • 那是困难时期。nà shì kùn nan shí qīThose were difficult times.
    • 非常时期需要非常措施。fēi cháng shí qī xū yào fēi cháng cuò shīDesperate times call for desperate measures.
  • 哎āihey; oh
    Dictionary senseshey!; (interjection used to attract attention or to express surprise or disapprobation)
    • 哎，马上就要考试了。我死定了！āi mǎ shàng jiù yào kǎo shì le wǒ sǐ dìng leAh, it's almost time for exams. I'm dead!
  • 好运hǎo yùngood luckTraditional: 好運
    • 祝您好运。zhù nín hǎo yùnI wish you luck.
    • 我祝您好运。wǒ zhù nín hǎo yùnI wish you luck.
  • 治zhìto treat; to govern
    Dictionary sensesto rule; to manage; to control; to harness (a river)
    • 这个药可能可以治到他的病。zhè ge yào kě néng kě yǐ zhì dào tā de bìngThis medicine may cure him.
  • 措施cuò shīmeasureMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesstep
  • 导演dǎo yǎndirector (film)Traditional: 導演
    Dictionary sensesto direct; director (film etc)
