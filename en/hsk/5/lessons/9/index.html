  • 协议xié yìagreementMeasure word: 项Traditional: 協議
    Dictionary sensespact; protocol
  • 满足mǎn zúto satisfyTraditional: 滿足
    Dictionary sensesto meet (the needs of); satisfied; content
    • 我不太确定我们是否能够满足你的要求。wǒ bù tài què dìng wǒ men shì fǒu néng gòu mǎn zú nǐ de yāo qiúI'm not quite sure if we can meet your requirements.
    • 无论读书的目的是什么，书总会带给我们乐趣和满足。wú lùn dú shū de mù dì shì shén me shū zǒng huì dài gěi wǒ men lè qù hé mǎn zúNo matter what your main purpose is in reading, books should never fail to provide contentment and satisfaction.
  • 配pèito match; to fit; to be worthy of
    Dictionary sensesto join; to mate; to mix
    • 我喜欢面包配汤。wǒ xǐ huan miàn bāo pèi tāngI like bread with my soup.
    • 这条领带跟我的西装不配。zhè tiáo lǐng dài gēn wǒ de xī zhuāng bù pèiThis tie doesn't go with my suit.
  • 土地tǔ dìland
    Dictionary senseslocal god; genius loci
    • 我的土地紧靠河边。wǒ de tǔ di jǐn kào hé biānMy land abuts on the river.
    • 他买土地目的是要造房子。tā mǎi tǔ di mù dì shì yào zào fáng ziHis goal in buying land is to build a house.
  • 威胁wēi xiéto threaten; threatTraditional: 威脅
    Dictionary sensesto menace
    • 你这是在威胁我吗？nǐ zhè shì zài wēi xié wǒ maAre you threatening me?
    • 他的生命受到了威胁。tā de shēng mìng shòu dào le wēi xiéHis life is in danger.
  • 老婆lǎo pówife
    Dictionary senses(coll.) wife
    • 他老婆。tā lǎo póHis wife.
    • 她是我老婆。tā shì wǒ lǎo póShe's my wife.
  • 等待děng dàito wait
    Dictionary sensesto wait for
    • 有时等待很难！yǒu shí děng dài hěn nánSometimes, waiting is very difficult!
    • 我们必须等待。wǒ men bì xū děng dàiWe must wait.
  • 挑战tiǎo zhànchallenge; to challengeTraditional: 挑戰
    • 这是一个巨大的挑战。zhè shì yī gè jù dà de tiǎo zhànThat's a huge challenge.
    • 她说她喜欢那个挑战。tā shuō tā xǐ huan nà ge tiǎo zhànShe said she enjoyed the challenge.
  • 占zhànto occupy, take upTraditional: 佔
    Dictionary sensesto take possession of; to occupy; to take up
    • 目前谁占上风？mù qián shéi zhàn shàng fēngWho has the edge now?
    • 床占了很多空间。chuáng zhàn le hěn duō kōng jiānThe bed took up a lot of space.
  • 小姐xiǎo jiěMiss; young ladyMeasure word: 个、位
    Dictionary sensesmiss; (slang) prostitute
    • 我看到一位漂亮小姐。wǒ kàn dào yī wèi piào liang xiǎo jieI saw a beautiful lady.
  • 秘密mì mìsecret
    Dictionary sensesprivate; confidential; clandestine; a secret (CL:個|个[ge4])
    • 它是个秘密。tā shì gè mì mìIt's a secret.
    • 你不能有秘密。nǐ bù néng yǒu mì mìYou can't keep a secret.
  • 超级chāo jísuperTraditional: 超級
    Dictionary sensessuper-; ultra-; hyper-
    • 今天超级冷。jīn tiān chāo jí lěngToday is very cold.
    • 超级市场在十点开门。chāo jí shì chǎng zài shí diǎn kāi ménThe supermarket opens at ten o'clock.
  • 猪zhūpigMeasure word: 口、头Traditional: 豬
    Dictionary senseshog; swine
    • 你这个猪头啊！nǐ zhè ge zhū tóu aYou blockhead!
    • 我昨天买了这头猪。wǒ zuó tiān mǎi le zhè tóu zhūI bought the pig yesterday.
  • 设备shè bèiequipmentMeasure word: 个Traditional: 設備
    Dictionary sensesfacilities; installations
    • 这间医院有很多新设备。zhè jiān yī yuàn yǒu hěn duō xīn shè bèiThis hospital has a lot of new equipment.
  • 意味着yì wèi zheto mean, signifyTraditional: 意味著
    Dictionary sensesto signify; to mean; to imply
    • 如果你能将它读出来，那就意味着你会说这门语言。rú guǒ nǐ néng jiāng tā dú chū lai nà jiù yì wèi zhe nǐ huì shuō zhè mén yǔ yánIf you can read this, it means that you can speak one language!
  • 保bǎoto protect; to keep
    Dictionary sensesto defend; to guarantee; to ensure
  • 团tuángroup; ball, lumpTraditional: 團
    Dictionary sensesround; lump; ball; to roll into a ball
    • 你有团子吗？nǐ yǒu tuán zi maDo you have some dango?
    • 明天第一次陪中国代表团，我紧张得不得了。míng tiān dì yī cì péi Zhōng guó dài biǎo tuán wǒ jǐn zhāng de bù dé liǎoTomorrow is the first time I will accompany the Chinese delegation, and I'm extremely nervous.
  • 制造zhì zàoto manufactureTraditional: 製造
    Dictionary sensesto make
    • 你一直在制造麻烦。nǐ yī zhí zài zhì zào má fanYou've been causing problems.
    • 这里制造的葡萄酒非常有名。zhè li zhì zào de pú tao jiǔ fēi cháng yǒu míngWine made here is very famous.
  • 紧jǐntightTraditional: 緊
    Dictionary sensesstrict; close at hand; near; urgent
    • 友谊紧紧联系着我们。yǒu yì jǐn jǐn lián xì zhe wǒ menWe are bound to each other by a close friendship.
    • 咬紧牙关。yǎo jǐn yá guānBite the bullet.
  • 抽chōuto draw; to smoke
    Dictionary sensesto draw out; to pull out from in between; to remove part of the whole; (of certain plants) to sprout or bud
    • 我不抽烟。wǒ bù chōu yānI don't smoke.
    • 她停下来抽烟。tā tíng xià lai chōu yānShe quit smoking.
  • 对手duì shǒuopponentTraditional: 對手
    Dictionary sensesrival; competitor; (well-matched) adversary; match
    • 他对自己的对手门很大度。tā duì zì jǐ de duì shǒu mén hěn dà dùHe is generous to his opponents.
    • 你的对手有四人。你一定会输的。nǐ de duì shǒu yǒu sì rén nǐ yī dìng huì shū deIt's four against you. You'll be beaten up.
  • 想象xiǎng xiàngto imagine; imagination
    Dictionary sensesto envision
    • 那只是你的想象。nà zhǐ shì nǐ de xiǎng xiàngIt's just your imagination.
    • 这只是你的想象。zhè zhǐ shì nǐ de xiǎng xiàngIt's just your imagination.
  • 当地dāng dìlocalTraditional: 當地
  • 背bèito carry on the back
    Dictionary sensesthe back of a body or object; to turn one's back; to hide something from; to learn by heart
  • 需xūto need
    Dictionary sensesto require; to want; necessity; need
    • 我无需工作。wǒ wú xū gōng zuòI don't need to work.
    • 每位会员每月需支付一万日元。měi wèi huì yuán měi yuè xū zhī fù yī wàn rì yuánEach member has to pay 10,000 yen a month.
  • 特殊tè shūspecial, unusual
    Dictionary sensesspecial; particular; unusual; extraordinary
  • 具体jù tǐspecific; concreteTraditional: 具體
    Dictionary sensesdetailed
    • 你具体需要些什么？nǐ jù tǐ xū yào xiē shén meWhat exactly do you need?
    • 可以说说您的具体要求吗？kě yǐ shuō shuō nín de jù tǐ yāo qiú maCould you tell me what your specific requirements are?
  • 合héto close; to combine
    Dictionary sensesto join; to fit; to be equal to; whole
    • 他太不合群。tā tài bù hé qúnHe's antisocial.
    • 鱼和红酒不合。yú hé hóng jiǔ bù héFish and red wine don't go together.
  • 风格fēng géstyleTraditional: 風格
    • 不是你的风格。bù shì nǐ de fēng géIt's not your style.
    • 她说话有自己的风格。tā shuō huà yǒu zì jǐ de fēng géShe had an individual style of speaking.
  • 建筑jiàn zhùbuilding; architectureMeasure word: 个Traditional: 建築
    Dictionary sensesto construct
    • 请在建筑的入口处等我。qǐng zài jiàn zhù de rù kǒu chù děng wǒPlease wait for me at the entrance of the building.
    • 这里现在有了许多新建筑。zhè li xiàn zài yǒu le xǔ duō xīn jiàn zhùThere are a lot of new buildings here now.
