  • 视为shì wéito regard asTraditional: 視為
    Dictionary sensesto view as; to see as; to consider to be; to deem
    • 他被视为村里最好的医生。tā bèi shì wéi cūn lǐ zuì hǎo de yī shēngHe is regarded as the best doctor in the village.
    • 我将水果视为最健康的食物。wǒ jiāng shuǐ guǒ shì wéi zuì jiàn kāng de shí wùI consider fruit to be the healthiest food.
