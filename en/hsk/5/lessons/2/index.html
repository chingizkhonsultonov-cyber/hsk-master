  • 呀ya(particle of surprise, like 啊)
    Dictionary senses(particle equivalent to 啊 after a vowel, expressing surprise or doubt)
    • 弄什么呀？nòng shén me yaWhat're you doing?
    • 没有关系呀。méi yǒu guān xi yaIt's OK.
  • 同tóngsame; with
    Dictionary senseslike; similar; together; alike
    • 我是女同。wǒ shì nǚ tóngI am lesbian.
    • 他和我同岁。tā hé wǒ tóng suìHe is as old as I.
  • 干gànto do; capableTraditional: 幹
    Dictionary sensestree trunk; main part of sth; to manage; to work
  • 如此rú cǐso, like this
    Dictionary senseslike this; so; such
    • 原来如此！yuán lái rú cǐSo that's how it is!
    • 原来如此。yuán lái rú cǐI see.
  • 处chùplace; departmentTraditional: 處
    Dictionary senseslocality; aspect; bureau; classifier for locations: spot, point
    • 到别处去做。dào bié chù qù zuòDo it somewhere else.
    • 你还是处男吗？nǐ hái shi chù nán maAre you still a virgin?
  • 处chǔto get along; to deal withTraditional: 處
    Dictionary senseslocality; aspect; department; bureau
  • 处理chǔ lǐto handleTraditional: 處理
    Dictionary sensesto deal with; to punish; to treat sth by a special process; to process
    • 她在处理问题。tā zài chǔ lǐ wèn tíShe is working on the problem.
    • 你想怎么处理？nǐ xiǎng zěn me chǔ lǐHow do you want this handled?
  • 政府zhèng fǔgovernmentMeasure word: 个
    • 能把经济管好的政府不多。néng bǎ jīng jì guǎn hǎo de zhèng fǔ bù duōFew governments can manage the economy successfully.
  • 其qíhis, her, its, their
    Dictionary senseshis; her; its; their
    • 我们除了走之外没有其它选择。wǒ men chú le zǒu zhī wài méi yǒu qí tā xuǎn zéWe have no choice but to go.
    • 谁也不能否定其事实。shéi yě bù néng fǒu dìng qí shì shíNo one can deny the fact.
  • 管guǎntube; pipeTraditional: 筦
    Dictionary sensesto take care (of); to control; to manage; to be in charge of
  • 曾céngonce (in the past)
    Dictionary sensesonce; already; ever (in the past); former
    • 他以前曾在北海道。tā yǐ qián céng zài běi hǎi dàoHe has been in Hokkaido before.
    • 我曾希望在那里遇见你。wǒ céng xī wàng zài nà li yù jiàn nǐI had hoped to meet you there.
  • 系统xì tǒngsystemMeasure word: 个Traditional: 系統
    • 你最喜欢什么作业系统？nǐ zuì xǐ huan shén me zuò yè xì tǒngWhat's your favorite operating system?
    • 你最喜欢的操作系统是哪个？nǐ zuì xǐ huan de cāo zuò xì tǒng shì nǎ geWhat's your favorite operating system?
  • 以及yǐ jías well as, and
    Dictionary sensesand; as well as
  • 控制kòng zhìto control
    • 控制住自己！kòng zhì zhù zì jǐControl yourself!
    • 控制住自己。kòng zhì zhù zì jǐControl yourself.
  • 组zǔto form; to put togetherTraditional: 組
    Dictionary sensesto organize; group; team; classifier for sets, series, groups of people, batteries
  • 组织zǔ zhīto organize; organizationMeasure word: 个Traditional: 組織
    Dictionary senses(biology) tissue; (textiles) weave
    • 组织很大。zǔ zhī hěn dàThe organization is big.
    • 谁组织的这场活动？shéi zǔ zhī de zhè chǎng huó dòngWho organised this event?
  • 根本gēn běnfundamental; at allMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesbasic; root; simply; absolutely (not)
    • 我根本不累。wǒ gēn běn bù lèiI'm not tired at all.
    • 它根本不是梦。tā gēn běn bù shì mèngIt's not a dream at all.
  • 面miànside; surface; aspect
    Dictionary sensesface; top
  • 面miànnoodles; flour
    Dictionary sensesface; side; surface; aspect
  • 重zhòngweight; to repeat
    Dictionary sensesheavy; serious; to attach importance to
  • 存在cún zàito exist
    Dictionary sensesto be; existence
    • 她把钱存在银行里。tā bǎ qián cún zài yín háng lǐShe put the money in the bank.
    • 没有空气，人无法存在。méi yǒu kōng qì rén wú fǎ cún zàiMen can not exist without air.
  • 靠kàoto lean on; to rely on
    Dictionary sensesto lean against or on; to stand by the side of; to come near to; to depend on
    • 全都靠它了。quán dōu kào tā leEverything depends on it.
    • 靠这点钱，我连自己的家庭都养不活。kào zhè diǎn qián wǒ lián zì jǐ de jiā tíng dōu yǎng bù huóWith this little money, I can't even support my family.
  • 现场xiàn chǎngscene, on-site; liveTraditional: 現場
    Dictionary sensesthe scene (of a crime, accident etc); (on) the spot; (at) the site
    • 警察到达了事故现场。jǐng chá dào dá le shì gù xiàn chǎngThe police got to the scene of the accident.
  • 个人gè rénindividual; personalTraditional: 個人
    Dictionary sensesoneself
    • 门口有个人。mén kǒu yǒu gè rénSomebody's at the door.
    • 他一个人生活。tā yī gè rén shēng huóHe lives alone.
  • 精神jīng shenspirit; energetic
    Dictionary sensesvigor; vitality; spirited; good-looking
    • 我们的生活不是由物质，而是由精神来决定的。wǒ men de shēng huó bù shì yóu wù zhì ér shì yóu jīng shen lái jué dìng deNot material, but spiritual things determine our life.
  • 保持bǎo chíto keep; to maintain
    Dictionary sensesto hold; to preserve
    • 保持安静！bǎo chí ān jìngKeep quiet!
    • 让我们保持联系。ràng wǒ men bǎo chí lián xìLet's keep in touch.
  • 代表dài biǎoto represent; representativeMeasure word: 位、个、名
    Dictionary sensesdelegate; to stand for; on behalf of
    • 绿色代表希望。lǜ sè dài biǎo xī wàngGreen stands for hope.
    • 那么，这代表更健康的城市吗？nà me zhè dài biǎo gèng jiàn kāng de chéng shì maSo, will this mean healthier cities?
  • 超chāoto exceed; super
    Dictionary sensesto overtake; to surpass; to transcend; to pass
    • 我被一辆车超了。wǒ bèi yī liàng chē chāo leI was overtaken by a car.
    • 我的钱不超一千日元。wǒ de qián bù chāo yī qiān rì yuánI didn't have more than 1,000 yen.
  • 确定què dìngto determine; definiteTraditional: 確定
    Dictionary sensescertain; fixed; to fix (on sth)
    • 你确定？nǐ què dìngReally?
    • 你确定吗？nǐ què dìng maAre you sure?
  • 中心zhōng xīncentre
    Dictionary sensescenter; heart; core
    • 当我离开购物中心，车子不在我停的地方。dāng wǒ lí kāi gòu wù zhōng xīn chē zi bù zài wǒ tíng de dì fangWhen I left the shopping center, the car wasn't where I had left it.
    • 图书馆位于城市中心。tú shū guǎn wèi yú chéng shì zhōng xīnThe library is in the center of the city.
