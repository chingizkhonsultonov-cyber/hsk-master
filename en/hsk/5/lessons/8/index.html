  • 测试cè shìtestTraditional: 測試
    Dictionary sensesto test (machinery etc); to test (a person's skill in a particular area); a test; (computing) beta (version of software)
    • 测试我考了低分。cè shì wǒ kǎo le dī fēnI got a low score on the test.
    • 我的意见是，你该再次参加测试。wǒ de yì jiàn shì nǐ gāi zài cì cān jiā cè shìI think you should take the test again.
