    Dictionary sensesdistinction; disparity
    • 两者之间只有很小的差别。liǎng zhě zhī jiān zhǐ yǒu hěn xiǎo de chā biéThere is only a marginal difference between the two.
  • 赞成zàn chéngto approve, agree withTraditional: 贊成
    Dictionary sensesto approve; to endorse; (literary) to assist
    • 我赞成你的计划。wǒ zàn chéng nǐ de jì huàI agree to your plan.
    • 他做的事情你赞成的吧？tā zuò de shì qing nǐ zàn chéng de baDo you approve of what he is doing?
  • 更新gēng xīnto update
    Dictionary sensesto replace the old with new; to renew; to renovate; to upgrade
    • 我必须更新我的护照。wǒ bì xū gēng xīn wǒ de hù zhàoI must renew my passport.
    • 你可以帮我更新这份资料吗？nǐ kě yǐ bāng wǒ gēng xīn zhè fèn zī liào maCould I get you to update this data for me?
  • 复制fù zhìto copyTraditional: 複製
    Dictionary sensesto duplicate; to make a copy of; to reproduce; to clone
  • 消化xiāo huàto digest
    Dictionary sensesto digest (food); (fig.) to absorb (information etc); to assimilate; to process
    • 喝酒帮助消化。hē jiǔ bāng zhù xiāo huàWine helps digest food.
    • 你吃不喜欢的食物会消化不良。nǐ chī bù xǐ huan de shí wù huì xiāo huà bù liángFood you eat that you don't like will not be digested well.
  • 录音lù yīnrecording; to recordTraditional: 錄音

  • 推动tuī dòngto promote, push forwardTraditional: 推動

  • 疑问yí wèndoubt, questionTraditional: 疑問

  • 适用shì yòngapplicableTraditional: 適用

    • 他们发起新任务。tā men fā qǐ xīn rèn wuThey launched a new mission.
  • 应用yìng yòngto apply; applicationTraditional: 應用
    Dictionary sensesto put to use; practical; applied (science, linguistics etc)
    • 这个应用好极了。zhè ge yìng yòng hǎo jí leThis application is awesome.

    • 培养政治意识要花费时间。péi yǎng zhèng zhì yì shí yào huā fèi shí jiānDeveloping political awareness takes time.

  • 大多dà duōmostly

    • 这儿的学生大多骑车上学。zhè r de xué sheng dà duō qí chē shàng xuéMost of the pupils here bike to school.

    • 你对什么有过敏吗？nǐ duì shén me yǒu guò mǐn maHave you any allergies?
