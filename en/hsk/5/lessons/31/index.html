    Dictionary sensesto love ardently; to adore
    • 我仍然热爱着网球运动。wǒ réng rán rè ài zhe wǎng qiú yùn dòngI am still keeping up my tennis craze.
  • 体现tǐ xiànto reflect, embodyTraditional: 體現
    Dictionary sensesto embody; to reflect; to incarnate
  • 生长shēng zhǎngto growTraditional: 生長
    Dictionary sensesto grow up; to be brought up
    • 这棵树停止生长了。zhè kē shù tíng zhǐ shēng zhǎng leThe tree stopped growing.
  • 论文lùn wénthesis; paperMeasure word: 篇Traditional: 論文
    Dictionary sensestreatise; to discuss a paper or thesis (old)
    • 你的论文写完了吗？nǐ de lùn wén xiě wán le maHave you finished writing your thesis?
    • 要翻译那篇论文，我最少需要三天时间。yào fān yì nà piān lùn wén wǒ zuì shǎo xū yào sān tiān shí jiānI'll need at least three days to translate that thesis.
  • 半夜bàn yèmidnight
    Dictionary sensesin the middle of the night
    • 他半夜打电话给我。tā bàn yè dǎ diàn huà gěi wǒHe rang me up at midnight.
    • 他半夜打了个电话给我。tā bàn yè dǎ le gè diàn huà gěi wǒHe gave me a ring at midnight.
  • 特色tè sèdistinctive feature
    Dictionary sensesa characteristic; a distinctive feature or quality
    • 这里的特色菜是什么？zhè li de tè sè cài shì shén meWhat is the specialty food here?
  • 用户yòng hùuserTraditional: 用戶
    Dictionary sensesconsumer; subscriber; customer
    • 我该用短一点的用户名的。wǒ gāi yòng duǎn yī diǎn de yòng hù míng deI should've chosen a shorter username.
    • 检查一下用户名和密码有没有填错。jiǎn chá yí xià yòng hù míng hé mì mǎ yǒu méi yǒu tián cuòCheck that your username and password are written correctly.
  • 从而cóng érthusTraditional: 從而

  • 综合zōng hécomprehensive; to synthesizeTraditional: 綜合
    Dictionary sensescomposite; synthesized; mixed; to sum up
  • 形容xíng róngto describe

    • 我不知道该怎么形容。wǒ bù zhī dào gāi zěn me xíng róngI can't really describe it.
  • 列车liè chētrainTraditional: 列車
    Dictionary senses(railway) train

  • 吸收xī shōuto absorb

  • 迎接yíng jiēto welcome, meet

    • 我要去机场迎接我的家人。wǒ yào qù jī chǎng yíng jiē wǒ de jiā rénI have to go to the airport to meet my family.
