  • 持续chí xùto continueTraditional: 持續
    Dictionary sensesto persist; to last; sustainable; preservation
    • 雪持续下了十天。xuě chí xù xià le shí tiānIt snowed for ten consecutive days.
    • 我已经持续读了一个小时了。wǒ yǐ jīng chí xù dú le yī gè xiǎo shí leI had been reading for an hour.
  • 具有jù yǒuto have
    Dictionary sensesto possess
    • 我们正在寻找具有客服工作经验的人。wǒ men zhèng zài xún zhǎo jù yǒu kè fú gōng zuò jīng yàn de rénWe're looking for someone who has experience in customer service.
    • 我们目前正在寻找具有客服工作经验的人员。wǒ men mù qián zhèng zài xún zhǎo jù yǒu kè fú gōng zuò jīng yàn de rén yuánWe are currently looking for individuals who have experience in customer service.
  • 医疗yī liáomedical treatmentTraditional: 醫療
    • 你有医疗保险吗？nǐ yǒu yī liáo bǎo xiǎn maDo you have medical insurance?
    • 急需医疗用品。jí xū yī liáo yòng pǐnThere's an urgent need for medical supplies.
  • 切qièto cut
    Dictionary sensesdefinitely; absolutely (not); (scoffing or dismissive interjection) Yeah, right.; Tut!
    • 她用刀子切苹果。tā yòng dāo zi qiè píng guǒShe cut the apple with a knife.
    • 厨师把肉切碎了。chú shī bǎ ròu qiè suì leThe cook chopped the meat.
  • 戏xìplay, drama; showMeasure word: 出、场、台Traditional: 戯
    Dictionary sensestrick; drama; play
    • 这星期有没有好戏看？zhè xīng qī yǒu méi yǒu hǎo xì kànAre there any good movies being shown this week?
    • 在我六岁的时候，我第一次被带去看马戏。zài wǒ liù suì de shí hou wǒ dì yī cì bèi dài qù kàn mǎ xìAt the age of six, I was taken to a circus for the first time.
  • 合同hé tongcontractMeasure word: 份
    Dictionary sensesagreement
    • 他们对合同很满意。tā men duì hé tong hěn mǎn yìThey are satisfied with the contract.
    • 别用铅笔签合同。bié yòng qiān bǐ qiān hé tongDon't sign the contract in pencil.
  • 发表fā biǎoto publishTraditional: 發表
    Dictionary sensesto issue
    • 它发表在哪里？tā fā biǎo zài nǎ liWhere was it published?
  • 处于chǔ yúto be in (a state)Traditional: 處於
    Dictionary sensesto be in (some state, position, or condition)
    • 那将会使你处于危险。nà jiāng huì shǐ nǐ chǔ yú wēi xiǎnThat'll put you in danger.
  • 相关xiāng guānrelated, relevantTraditional: 相關
    Dictionary sensesrelated; relevant; pertinent; to be interrelated
    • 相关调查正在进行中。xiāng guān diào chá zhèng zài jìn xíng zhōngThe investigation is under way.
    • 为了按期完成开发，我们请求暂时推迟其他不相关的任务。wèi le àn qī wán chéng kāi fā wǒ men qǐng qiú zàn shí tuī chí qí tā bù xiāng guān de rèn wuIn order to complete the development on schedule, we request that other unrelated tasks be delayed for the moment.
  • 简直jiǎn zhísimplyTraditional: 簡直
    Dictionary sensesreally
    • 我听到这个消息时，简直不能相信自己的耳朵。wǒ tīng dào zhè ge xiāo xi shí jiǎn zhí bù néng xiāng xìn zì jǐ de ěr duoI could hardly believe my ears when I heard the news.
  • 展示zhǎn shìto display, show

  • 退tuìto retreat; to return (goods)

    • 我今天可以早退吗？wǒ jīn tiān kě yǐ zǎo tuì maMay I leave school early today?
  • 情绪qíng xùmood, emotionsMeasure word: 种Traditional: 情緒
    Dictionary sensesmood; state of mind; moodiness
    • 她情绪多变。tā qíng xù duō biànShe has moods.
  • 究竟jiū jìngafter all; outcome
    Dictionary sensesto go to the bottom of a matter; when all is said and done; (in an interrogative sentence) finally
  • 强大qiáng dàpowerful, strongTraditional: 強大

    • 这使我们更强大。zhè shǐ wǒ men gèng qiáng dàThis makes us stronger.
  • 替tìto replace; for, on behalf of
    Dictionary sensesto substitute for; to take the place of; for; on behalf of
    • 你最后一次替别人擦眼泪是什么时候？nǐ zuì hòu yī cì tì bié ren cā yǎn lèi shì shén me shí houWhen did you last wipe someone's tears away?
    • 你今天下午去图书馆的话，可以顺道替我还两本书吗？nǐ jīn tiān xià wǔ qù tú shū guǎn de huà kě yǐ shùn dào tì wǒ hái liǎng běn shū maIf you are going to the library this afternoon, would you please return two books for me whilst you are there?
  • 掌握zhǎng wòto master, grasp
    Dictionary sensesto grasp (often fig.); to control; to seize (initiative, opportunity, destiny); to master
    • 掌握一门外语很难。zhǎng wò yī mén wài yǔ hěn nánMastering a foreign language is difficult.
    • 一个外国语言无法在一年左右就被掌握。yī gè wài guó yǔ yán wú fǎ zài yī nián zuǒ yòu jiù bèi zhǎng wòA foreign language can't be mastered in a year or so.
  • 证据zhèng jùevidenceTraditional: 證據
    Dictionary sensesproof; testimony
    • 我们没有证据。wǒ men méi yǒu zhèng jùWe have no proof.
    • 你有证据证明那真的发生了吗？nǐ yǒu zhèng jù zhèng míng nà zhēn de fā shēng le maDo you have any proof that that happened?
  • 幸运xìng yùnluckyTraditional: 幸運
    Dictionary sensesfortunate; good fortune; luck
    • 你们很幸运。nǐ men hěn xìng yùnYou're in luck.
    • 我今天看来很幸运。wǒ jīn tiān kàn lai hěn xìng yùnI seem to be unlucky today.
  • 制作zhì zuòto make, produceTraditional: 製作
    Dictionary sensesto make; to manufacture
  • 最佳zuì jiābest
    Dictionary sensesoptimum; optimal; peak; best (athlete, movie etc)
    • 跑步的最佳时间是什么时候？pǎo bù de zuì jiā shí jiān shì shén me shí houWhen's the best time to run?
    • 手术是最佳解决方案。shǒu shù shì zuì jiā jiě jué fāng ànSurgery is the best solution.
  • 记忆jì yìmemoryTraditional: 記憶
    Dictionary sensesto remember; to recall
    • 你的记忆力很好。nǐ de jì yì lì hěn hǎoYour memory is good.
    • 希望我的记忆力能好一点。xī wàng wǒ de jì yì lì néng hǎo yī diǎnI wish I had a better memory.
  • 资金zī jīnfundsTraditional: 資金
    Dictionary sensescapital
  • 员工yuán gōngstaff, employeeTraditional: 員工
    Dictionary sensesstaff; personnel; employee
    • 他们不是公司员工吗？tā men bù shì gōng sī yuán gōng maAre they not company employees?
    • 他和他的员工相处。tā hé tā de yuán gōng xiāng chǔHe is getting along with his employees.
  • 立即lì jíimmediately
    • 我很抱歉，但我无法立即回答。wǒ hěn bào qiàn dàn wǒ wú fǎ lì jí huí dáI'm sorry, but I can't answer right away.
  • 升shēngto rise, go up; to be promoted

    • 生活费升高了。shēng huó fèi shēng gāo leThe cost of living has risen.
    • 太阳升起来了。tài yang shēng qǐ lai leThe sun is rising.
  • 内部nèi bùinternalTraditional: 內部
    Dictionary sensesinterior; inside (part, section)
  • 经营jīng yíngto run (a business)Traditional: 經營
    Dictionary sensesto engage in (business etc); to run; to operate
    • 这家公司由一个外国人经营。zhè jiā gōng sī yóu yī gè wài guó rén jīng yíngThat company is managed by a foreigner.
  • 困kùnto trap; difficulty
    Dictionary sensesto surround; hard-pressed; stranded; destitute
  • 在于zài yúto lie in, depend onTraditional: 在於
    Dictionary sensesto rest with; to lie in; to be due to (a certain attribute); (of a matter) to be determined by
