  • 相似xiāng sìsimilar
    Dictionary sensesalike
    • 我们很相似。wǒ men hěn xiāng sìWe are cut from the same cloth.
    • 地球的外形和橙子相似。dì qiú de wài xíng hé chéng zi xiāng sìThe globe is similar in shape to an orange.
  • 担任dān rènto hold (a post)Traditional: 擔任
    Dictionary sensesto hold a governmental office or post; to assume office of; to take charge of; to serve as
    • 我叔叔让我担任翻译。wǒ shū shu ràng wǒ dān rèn fān yìMy uncle had me act as a translator.
    • 我们在学校担任教师。wǒ men zài xué xiào dān rèn jiào shīWe work as teachers in the school.
  • 广guǎngwideTraditional: 廣
    Dictionary sensesnumerous; to spread
    • 广东的夏天很长。guǎng dōng de xià tiān hěn chángGuangdong summers are long.
  • 宿舍sù shèdormitoryMeasure word: 间
    Dictionary sensesdorm room; living quarters; hostel
    • 他一放学就回宿舍了。tā yī fàng xué jiù huí sù shè leHe went back to the dorm as soon as he finished class.
    • 大学有教室，也有宿舍。dà xué yǒu jiào shì yě yǒu sù shèUniversities have classrooms and dormitories.
  • 线路xiàn lùline, route; circuitTraditional: 線路
    Dictionary senses(electricity) line; wire; (traffic) road; track
  • 沉chénto sink; heavyTraditional: 沈
    Dictionary sensesto submerge; to immerse; to keep down; to lower
    • 它沉了。tā chén leIt sank.
    • 雨让秋天的日子阴沉沉的。yǔ ràng qiū tiān de rì zi yīn chén chén deThe rain made the autumn day dismal.
  • 善良shàn liángkind-hearted
    Dictionary sensesgood and honest; kindhearted
  • 可靠kě kàoreliable
    • 他们不可靠。tā men bù kě kàoThey're unreliable.
  • 其余qí yúthe rest, remainingTraditional: 其餘
    Dictionary sensesthe rest; the others; remaining; remainder
  • 规模guī móscaleTraditional: 規模
    Dictionary sensesscope; extent
  • 决心jué xīndeterminationMeasure word: 个Traditional: 決心
    Dictionary sensesresolution; determined; firm and resolute; to make up one's mind
    • 他很快就下定了决心。tā hěn kuài jiù xià dìng le jué xīnHe made up his mind quickly.
    • 她决心要辞职离开公司。tā jué xīn yào cí zhí lí kāi gōng sīShe is determined to leave the company.
  • 乡村xiāng cūnvillage, countrysideTraditional: 鄉村
    Dictionary sensesrustic; village; countryside
  • 捡jiǎnto pick upTraditional: 撿
    Dictionary sensesto collect; to gather
    • 她捡了许多漂亮的小石头。tā jiǎn le xǔ duō piào liang de xiǎo shí touShe picked up beautiful stones.
  • 蔬菜shū càivegetables
    • 她只吃蔬菜。tā zhī chī shū càiShe eats nothing but vegetables.
    • 蔬菜不能说话。shū cài bù néng shuō huàVegetables can't talk.
  • 哪怕nǎ pàeven if
    Dictionary senseseven; even though; no matter how
  • 补充bǔ chōngto add; to supplementMeasure word: 个Traditional: 補充
    Dictionary sensesto replenish; to complement; additional; supplementary
    • 您对我说的话有什么要补充的吗？nín duì wǒ shuō de huà yǒu shén me yào bǔ chōng de maWould you like to add anything to what I've said?
  • 伙huǒgroup; partner
    Dictionary sensesmeals (abbr. for 伙食[huo3 shi2]); companion; classifier for groups of people
    • 我想要大家伙帮我个忙。wǒ xiǎng yào dà jiā huǒ bāng wǒ gè mángI want you guys to do me a favor.
  • 最初zuì chūinitial; at first
    Dictionary sensesfirst; primary; original
    • 我最初不怎么喜欢他。wǒ zuì chū bù zěn me xǐ huan tāAt first, I didn't like him.
    • 你们应该实行你们最初的计划。nǐ men yīng gāi shí xíng nǐ men zuì chū de jì huàYou must carry out your first plan.
  • 克服kè fúto overcome
    Dictionary senses(try to) overcome (hardships etc); to conquer; to put up with; to endure
    • 你必须克服困难。nǐ bì xū kè fú kùn nanYou must overcome the difficulties.
    • 他帮助我克服了困难。tā bāng zhù wǒ kè fú le kùn nanHe helped me to get over the difficulties.
  • 薄báothin
    Dictionary sensescold in manner; indifferent; weak; light
  • 装修zhuāng xiūto renovate, decorateTraditional: 裝修
    Dictionary sensesto decorate; interior decoration; to fit up; to renovate
    • 装修还不错。zhuāng xiū hái bú cuòThe decorating isn't bad.
    • 他把屋子重新装修了。tā bǎ wū zi chóng xīn zhuāng xiū leHe made over the interior of his house.
  • 填tiánto fill in
    Dictionary sensesto fill or stuff; (of a form etc) to fill in
    • 请你把这张表填一下，等我们的通知。qǐng nǐ bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo tián yí xià děng wǒ men de tōng zhīPlease fill out this form and wait for us to notify you.
    • 不要把寄件人、收件人地址的姓名填反了！bú yào bǎ jì jiàn rén shōu jiàn rén dì zhǐ de xìng míng tián fǎn leDon't flip-flop the addresses of the sender and recipient!
  • 成分chéng fèningredient
    Dictionary sensescomposition; element; component; one's social status
  • 同一tóng yīthe same
    Dictionary sensesidentical
    • 他们俩在同一个年龄。tā men liǎ zài tóng yī gè nián língThey are the same age.
  • 召开zhào kāito hold (a meeting)Traditional: 召開
    Dictionary sensesto convene (a conference or meeting); to convoke; to call together
    • 会议将在下周日召开。huì yì jiāng zài xià zhōu rì zhào kāiThe meeting will take place next Sunday.
  • 有限yǒu xiànlimited
    Dictionary sensesfinite
    • 我们公司是有限公司。wǒ men gōng sī shì yǒu xiàn gōng sīOur company is a limited company.
  • 代替dài tìto replace
    Dictionary sensesto take the place of
    • 他代替他的父亲去。tā dài tì tā de fù qīn qùHe left to replace his father.
    • 你会代替我去派对吗？nǐ huì dài tì wǒ qù pài duì maWill you go to the party instead of me?
  • 过于guò yútooTraditional: 過於
    Dictionary sensesexcessively
    • 别过于自信了。bié guò yú zì xìn leLet's not get over-confident.
  • 有利yǒu lìadvantageous
    Dictionary sensesfavorable
  • 博物馆bó wù guǎnmuseumTraditional: 博物館
    • 博物馆的入口在哪？bó wù guǎn de rù kǒu zài nǎWhere's the entrance to the museum?
    • 艺术博物馆在这附近吗？yì shù bó wù guǎn zài zhè fù jìn maIs the museum of art around here?
