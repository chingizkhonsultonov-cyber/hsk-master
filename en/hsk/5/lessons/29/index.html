    Dictionary sensesto amend; to alter; to modify
  • 冻dòngto freezeTraditional: 凍
    Dictionary sensesto feel very cold; aspic or jelly
    • 湖冻冰了。hú dòng bīng leThe lake was frozen.
    • 他们冻死了。tā men dòng sǐ leThey were frozen to death.
  • 辞职cí zhíto resignTraditional: 辭職
    • 他辞职了。tā cí zhí leHe quit.
    • 他有可能辞职吗？tā yǒu kě néng cí zhí maIs there any chance that he'll resign?
  • 遵守zūn shǒuto abide by
    Dictionary sensesto comply with; to respect (an agreement)
    • 你必须遵守法律。nǐ bì xū zūn shǒu fǎ lǜYou have to obey the law.
    • 我们必须遵守规则。wǒ men bì xū zūn shǒu guī zéWe must obey the rules.
  • 抓紧zhuā jǐnto grasp firmly; to hurryTraditional: 抓緊
    Dictionary sensesto keep a firm grip on; to pay close attention to; to lose no time in (doing sth)
  • 相处xiāng chǔto get along withTraditional: 相處

    • 他和他的员工相处。tā hé tā de yuán gōng xiāng chǔHe is getting along with his employees.
    • 他这个人很难相处。tā zhè ge rén hěn nán xiāng chǔHe is hard to get along with.
  • 建造jiàn zàoto build

  • 保存bǎo cúnto preserve; to save
    Dictionary sensesto conserve; to keep; to store; (computing) to save (a file etc)
