  • 批准pī zhǔnto approve, ratify
    Dictionary sensesto approve; to ratify
    • 她批准了我的方案。tā pī zhǔn le wǒ de fāng ànShe approved of my plan.
    • 经理批准了我们的计划。jīng lǐ pī zhǔn le wǒ men de jì huàThe manager approved our plan.
  • 前进qián jìnto advance, move forwardTraditional: 前進
    Dictionary sensesto go forward; to forge ahead; to advance; onward
    • 不断前进，不断向上。bù duàn qián jìn bù duàn xiàng shàngOnwards and upwards.
    • 人生就像骑单车一样，要保持平衡就得不停地前进。rén shēng jiù xiàng qí dān chē yī yàng yào bǎo chí píng héng jiù de bù tíng de qián jìnLife is like riding a bicycle. To keep your balance you must keep moving.
  • 他人tā rénothers, other people
    Dictionary sensesanother person; sb else; other people
    • 多多鼓励他人。duō duō gǔ lì tā rénEncourage others.
    • 他人很好，和你一样。tā rén hěn hǎo hé nǐ yī yàngHe is very kind, just like you.
  • 骂màto scoldMeasure word: 通、顿Traditional: 罵
    Dictionary sensesto abuse; to curse
    • 请别骂了。qǐng bié mà leStop swearing, please.
    • 我不是在骂你。wǒ bù shì zài mà nǐI was not scolding you.
  • 机器jī qìmachineMeasure word: 台、部、个Traditional: 機器
    • 机器发了很多电。jī qì fā le hěn duō diànThe machine generated large amounts of electricity.
    • 那台机器还能用吗？nà tái jī qì hái néng yòng maIs that machine still usable?
  • 守shǒuto guard; to keep (rules)
    Dictionary sensesto defend; to keep watch; to abide by the law; to observe (rules or ritual)
    • 要守时！yào shǒu shíBe on time.
    • 他是守门员。tā shì shǒu mén yuánHe's a goal keeper.
  • 滑huáslippery; to slide
    Dictionary sensesto slip; smooth; sly
    • 马路滑的。mǎ lù huá deThe street is slippery.
    • 车行道滑的。chē xíng dào huá deThe road is slippery.
  • 操作cāo zuòto operate
    Dictionary sensesto work; to manipulate
    • 你最喜欢的操作系统是哪个？nǐ zuì xǐ huan de cāo zuò xì tǒng shì nǎ geWhat's your favorite operating system?
    • 我不喜欢这个操作系统的页面。wǒ bù xǐ huan zhè ge cāo zuò xì tǒng de yè miànI didn't like the interface of that operating system.
  • 达成dá chéngto reach (an agreement)Traditional: 達成
    Dictionary sensesto accomplish
    • 你达成你的目标了吗？nǐ dá chéng nǐ de mù biāo le maHave you attained your goal?
  • 标志biāo zhìsign; symbolTraditional: 標誌
    Dictionary sensesmark; logo; to symbolize
  • 公布gōng bùto announceTraditional: 公佈
    Dictionary sensesto make public; to publish
  • 本人běn rénI myself; in person
    Dictionary sensesI; me; myself; oneself
    • 你是日本人吗？nǐ shì rì běn rén maAre you Japanese?
    • 你不是日本人。nǐ bù shì rì běn rénYou aren't Japanese.

  • 黄金huáng jīngoldTraditional: 黃金

  • 舞蹈wǔ dǎodance
    Dictionary sensesdance (performance art); dancing
    • 桌子上有一本关于舞蹈的书。zhuō zi shàng yǒu yī běn guān yú wǔ dǎo de shūThere is a book on dancing on the desk.
  • 伙伴huǒ bànpartner
    Dictionary sensescompanion; comrade
    • 你想要伙伴吗？nǐ xiǎng yào huǒ bàn maDo you want some company?
    • 无论书中的人物是真实的还是完全假想的，他们能成为你的伙伴和朋友。wú lùn shū zhōng de rén wù shì zhēn shí de hái shi wán quán jiǎ xiǎng de tā men néng chéng wéi nǐ de huǒ bàn hé péng youWhether the characters in a book are real or completely imaginary, they can become your friends and companions.
  • 因素yīn sùfactorMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary senseselement
    • 物价上涨是多方因素造成的。wù jià shàng zhǎng shì duō fāng yīn sù zào chéng deSeveral factors operated to help prices go up.
  • 发布fā bùto releaseTraditional: 發佈
    Dictionary sensesto issue; to announce; to distribute
    • 晚了！墙上杂志就要发布了！wǎn le qiáng shàng zá zhì jiù yào fā bù leToo late! The wall magazine is ready to be published!
  • 忍rěnto bear, endure
    Dictionary sensesto bear; to endure; to tolerate; to restrain oneself
    • 她忍不住笑了。tā rěn bù zhù xiào leShe cannot but laugh.
    • 我努力忍住哭。wǒ nǔ lì rěn zhù kūI tried not to cry.
  • 双方shuāng fāngboth sidesTraditional: 雙方
    Dictionary sensesbilateral; both parties involved
    • 首先，我要听听双方的意见。shǒu xiān wǒ yào tīng tīng shuāng fāng de yì jiànFirst, I should hear both sides.
  • 摸mōto touch
    Dictionary sensesto feel with the hand; to stroke; to grope; to steal
    • 谁也不要摸这个。shéi yě bú yào mō zhè geNobody wants to touch this.
  • 组成zǔ chéngto make up, formTraditional: 組成
    Dictionary sensesto form; to make up; to constitute
    • 社会是由个人组成的。shè huì shì yóu gè rén zǔ chéng deThe community is made up of individuals.
    • 一个足球队由十一个球员组成。yī gè zú qiú duì yóu shí yī gè qiú yuán zǔ chéngA soccer team consists of eleven players.
  • 离婚lí hūnto divorceTraditional: 離婚
    • 她打算离婚。tā dǎ suàn lí hūnShe intends to get divorced.
    • 她与丈夫离婚。tā yǔ zhàng fu lí hūnShe divorced her husband.

    Dictionary senseswarehouse; (file) library
  • 吵chǎonoisy; to quarrel
    Dictionary sensesto make a noise; to disturb by making a noise
