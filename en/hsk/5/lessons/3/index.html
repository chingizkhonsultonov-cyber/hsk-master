  • 行动xíng dòngaction; to actMeasure word: 个Traditional: 行動
    Dictionary sensesoperation; to move about; mobile
    • 现在是行动的时候了。xiàn zài shì xíng dòng de shí hou leNow is the time to act.
    • 开始行动之前，一定要清楚目标是什么。kāi shǐ xíng dòng zhī qián yī dìng yào qīng chu mù biāo shì shén meBefore taking action, make sure you are clear about what the goal is.
  • 合作hé zuòto cooperate
    Dictionary sensesto collaborate; to work together
    • 我想和贵公司合作。wǒ xiǎng hé guì gōng sī hé zuòI want to work with your company.
    • 所以别人都不愿意和你合作。suǒ yǐ bié ren dōu bù yuàn yì hé nǐ hé zuòThat's why no one wants to work with you.
  • 哈hāha (laughter)
    Dictionary senses(interj.) ha!; (onom. for laughter); (slang) to be infatuated with; to adore
  • 及jíand; to reach
    Dictionary sensesup to; in time for
    • 我被各国语言及文字的爱好者所围绕。wǒ bèi gè guó yǔ yán jí wén zì de ài hào zhě suǒ wéi ràoI'm surrounded by linguaphiles!
  • 报告bào gàoreport; to reportMeasure word: 篇、份、个Traditional: 報告
    Dictionary sensesto inform; to make known; speech
    • 你写好报告了吗？nǐ xiě hǎo bào gào le maDo you have the report yet?
    • 你拿到报告了吗？nǐ ná dào bào gào le maDo you have the report yet?
  • 治疗zhì liáoto treat; treatmentTraditional: 治療
    Dictionary sensesto treat (an illness); medical treatment; therapy
    • 应该接受治疗的是你自己！yīng gāi jiē shòu zhì liáo de shì nǐ zì jǐYou yourself need to be treated!
    • 没有药物能够治疗这种疾病。méi yǒu yào wù néng gòu zhì liáo zhè zhǒng jí bìngNo medicine can cure this disease.
  • 拥有yōng yǒuto have, possessTraditional: 擁有
    Dictionary sensesto have; to possess
    • 我终于拥有了一整套！wǒ zhōng yú yōng yǒu le yī zhěng tàoI've finally got the whole set!
    • 他拥有的书比我的多。tā yōng yǒu de shū bǐ wǒ de duōHe has more books than I do.
  • 根据gēn jùbasis; according toMeasure word: 个Traditional: 根據
    Dictionary sensesbased on; foundation
  • 台táiplatform; stage; station
    Dictionary senses(classical) you (in letters); desk; table; counter
  • 明显míng xiǎnobviousTraditional: 明顯
    Dictionary sensesclear; distinct
    • 那很明显。nà hěn míng xiǎnThat's quite obvious.
    • 他明显是队里最厉害的球员。tā míng xiǎn shì duì lǐ zuì lì hai de qiú yuánHe is clearly the team's best player.
  • 伤shāngto injure; woundTraditional: 傷
    Dictionary sensesinjury
    • 我以为你受了伤。wǒ yǐ wéi nǐ shòu le shāngI thought you were injured.
    • 脚上的伤是怎么回事？jiǎo shàng de shāng shì zěn me huí shìHow did you get this injury on your foot?
  • 套tàoset, suite (measure word); to cover
    Dictionary sensesto encase; cover; sheath; to overlap
    • 你对女人有一套。nǐ duì nǚ ren yǒu yī tàoYou're good with women.
    • 我要买一套漂亮的房子。wǒ yào mǎi yī tào piào liang de fáng ziI want to buy a beautiful house.
  • 曾经céng jīngonceTraditional: 曾經
    Dictionary sensesalready; former; previously; ever
    • 你曾经生过火吗？nǐ céng jīng shēng guò huǒ maHave you started a fire before?
    • 你曾经想过当个护士吗？nǐ céng jīng xiǎng guò dāng gè hù shi maHave you ever thought of becoming a nurse?
  • 黑hēiblack; dark
    Dictionary sensessinister; secret; shady
    • 她很怕黑。tā hěn pà hēiShe is very afraid of the dark.
    • 桥下很黑。qiáo xià hěn hēiIt was pretty dark under the bridge.
  • 行为xíng wéibehaviour, conductTraditional: 行為
    Dictionary sensesaction; conduct; behavior; activity
    • 人要为自己的行为负责。rén yào wèi zì jǐ de xíng wéi fù zéOne must be responsible for one's conduct.
    • 她必须对她的行为负责。tā bì xū duì tā de xíng wéi fù zéShe must answer for her actions.
  • 绝对jué duìabsolutelyTraditional: 絕對
    Dictionary sensesabsolute; unconditional
    • 绝对不可能。jué duì bù kě néngAbsolutely impossible.
    • 绝对将要下雨。jué duì jiāng yào xià yǔIt'll rain for sure.
  • 派pàito send, dispatch; school, faction
    Dictionary senses(literary) tributary; branch of a river; clique; school
    • 我爱派对。wǒ ài pài duìI love parties.
    • 我想做个派。wǒ xiǎng zuò gè pàiI want to make a pie.
  • 眼yǎneye; (measure word for looks, wells)
    Dictionary senseseye (CL:隻|只[zhi1],雙|双[shuang1]); (often used with 一[yi1]) a look; a glance; small hole
    • 不放在眼里。bù fàng zài yǎn lǐIgnore it.
    • 眼不见，心不烦。yǎn bù jiàn xīn bù fánOut of sight, out of mind.
  • 设计shè jìto design; designTraditional: 設計
    Dictionary sensesto plan; plan
  • 未来wèi láifutureMeasure word: 个Traditional: 未來
    Dictionary sensestomorrow; approaching; coming; pending
    • 你对未来有什么计划？nǐ duì wèi lái yǒu shén me jì huàWhat are your plans for the future?
    • 给我谈谈你未来的计划。gěi wǒ tán tán nǐ wèi lái de jì huàTell me about your program for the future.
  • 利用lì yòngto use
    Dictionary sensesto exploit; to make use of; to take advantage of; to utilize
    • 你可以利用他的图书馆。nǐ kě yǐ lì yòng tā de tú shū guǎnYou may make use of his library.
    • 你应该好好利用这个机会。nǐ yīng gāi hǎo hǎo lì yòng zhè ge jī huìYou had better make use of the opportunity.
  • 令lìngto make; to order
    Dictionary sensesto command; an order; warrant; writ
    • 城市是令人兴奋的地方，但也会让人产生压力。chéng shì shì lìng rén xīng fèn de dì fang dàn yě huì ràng rén chǎn shēng yā lìCities are exciting places, but also stressful.
    • 有些食物令人口渴。yǒu xiē shí wù lìng rén kǒu kěSome foods make you thirsty.
  • 倒dàoon the contrary; actually
    Dictionary sensesto invert; to place upside down or frontside back; to pour out; to tip out
  • 包括bāo kuòto include
    Dictionary sensesto comprise; to involve; to incorporate; to consist of
  • 红hóngred; popularTraditional: 紅
    Dictionary sensesrevolutionary; bonus
    • 你脸红了。nǐ liǎn hóng leYou're blushing.
    • 卖红酒吗？mài hóng jiǔ maSell wine?
  • 抓zhuāto grab; to catch
    Dictionary sensesto arrest; to snatch; to scratch
    • 抓住他。zhuā zhù tāGrab him.
    • 他抓住了她的胳膊。tā zhuā zhù le tā de gē boHe grabbed her arm.
  • 意义yì yìmeaning, significanceMeasure word: 个Traditional: 意義
    Dictionary sensessense; meaning; significance; importance
    • 这没有任何意义。zhè méi yǒu rèn hé yì yìThat doesn't make sense.
    • 你说的没有意义。nǐ shuō de méi yǒu yì yìWhat you're saying doesn't make sense.
  • 便biànthen; convenient
    Dictionary sensesplain; informal; suitable; opportune
    • 便后请洗手。biàn hòu qǐng xǐ shǒuPlease wash your hands after using the bathroom.
    • 数大便是美。shù dà biàn shì měiThe more, the better.
  • 力lìstrength
    Dictionary sensespower; force; ability; strenuously
    • 你的影响力是什么？nǐ de yǐng xiǎng lì shì shén meWhat are your influences?
    • 能够出一份力太好了。néng gòu chū yī fèn lì tài hǎo leI'm glad I could help.
  • 数shùnumberTraditional: 數
    Dictionary sensesfigure; several; a few
