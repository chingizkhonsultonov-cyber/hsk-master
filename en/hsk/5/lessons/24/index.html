  • 签名qiān míngto sign one's name; signatureTraditional: 簽名
    Dictionary sensesto sign (one's name with a pen etc); to autograph
    • 请在这里签名。qǐng zài zhè li qiān míngPlease sign here.
    • 他们有没有签名？tā men yǒu méi yǒu qiān míngDid they sign?
  • 先进xiān jìnadvancedTraditional: 先進
    Dictionary sensessophisticated; advanced (technology etc); meritorious; exemplary (deeds etc)
  • 摄影shè yǐngphotographyTraditional: 攝影
    Dictionary sensesto take a photograph; to shoot (a movie)
    • 我对摄影感兴趣。wǒ duì shè yǐng gǎn xìng qùI have an interest in photography.
  • 交换jiāo huànto exchangeTraditional: 交換
    Dictionary sensesto swap; to switch (telecom); commutative (math); to commute
    • 可以跟我交换房间吗？kě yǐ gēn wǒ jiāo huàn fáng jiān maCould you exchange rooms with me?
  • 描述miáo shùto describe
    Dictionary sensesdescription
    • 很难描述。hěn nán miáo shùIt's really difficult to describe.
  • 今日jīn rìtoday
  • 挡dǎngto blockTraditional: 擋
    Dictionary sensesto resist; to obstruct; to hinder; to keep off
    • 你挡我路？nǐ dǎng wǒ lùYou get in my way?
    • 你挡住了我的路。nǐ dǎng zhù le wǒ de lùYou're in my way.
  • 届jièsession; class (of graduates)Traditional: 屆
    Dictionary sensesto arrive at (place or time); period; to become due; classifier for events, meetings, elections, sporting fixtures, years (of graduation)
  • 认rènto recognize; to admitTraditional: 認
    Dictionary sensesto know
    • 我认我会留下。wǒ rèn wǒ huì liú xiàI think I'll stay.
    • 我马上就认出了他。wǒ mǎ shàng jiù rèn chū le tāI recognized him instantly.
  • 亿yìhundred millionTraditional: 億
    Dictionary senses100 million
    • 中国有超过十亿人口。Zhōng guó yǒu chāo guò shí yì rén kǒuMore than a billion people live in China.
  • 在场zài chǎngto be presentTraditional: 在場
    Dictionary sensesto be on the scene
  • 敏感mǐn gǎnsensitive
    Dictionary sensessusceptible
    • 我对热气很敏感。wǒ duì rè qì hěn mǐn gǎnI'm very sensitive to heat.
    • 猫的眼睛对光很敏感。māo de yǎn jing duì guāng hěn mǐn gǎnCats' eyes are very sensitive to light.
  • 运气yùn qìluckTraditional: 運氣
    Dictionary sensesluck (good or bad)
    • 我运气很好。wǒ yùn qi hěn hǎoI'm lucky.
    • 我今天运气很好。wǒ jīn tiān yùn qi hěn hǎoI'm lucky today.
  • 玩具wán jùtoy
    Dictionary sensesplaything
    • 他收起了他的玩具。tā shōu qǐ le tā de wán jùHe put away his toys.
    • 她为了个玩具在哭。tā wèi le gè wán jù zài kūShe was crying for a toy.
  • 防止fáng zhǐto prevent
    Dictionary sensesto guard against; to take precautions
    • 律师是防止别人拿你的钱的人。lǜ shī shì fáng zhǐ bié ren ná nǐ de qián de rénA lawyer is a person who prevents someone else from getting your money.
  • 年纪nián jìageMeasure word: 把、个Traditional: 年紀
    • 我比他年纪还大。wǒ bǐ tā nián jì hái dàI am yet older than he is.
    • 学习是不分年纪的。xué xí shì bù fēn nián jì deYou can study, no matter your age.
  • 设置shè zhìto set up; settingsTraditional: 設置
    Dictionary sensesto install
  • 智慧zhì huìwisdom
    Dictionary sensesintelligence
    • 他很有智慧。tā hěn yǒu zhì huìHe's very wise.
    • 她现在更加年长也更加有智慧。tā xiàn zài gèng jiā nián cháng yě gèng jiā yǒu zhì huìShe is older and wiser now.
  • 含hánto contain
    Dictionary sensesto keep in the mouth
  • 采用cǎi yòngto adoptTraditional: 採用
    Dictionary sensesto employ; to use
  • 夫妇fū fùmarried coupleMeasure word: 对Traditional: 夫婦
    Dictionary sensesa (married) couple; husband and wife
    • 我们是夫妇。wǒ men shì fū fùWe are a couple.
  • 摔shuāito fall; to drop and break
    Dictionary sensesto throw down
    • 他摔倒在地。tā shuāi dào zài deHe fell to the floor.
    • 小心别摔倒了。xiǎo xīn bié shuāi dào leBe careful not to fall.
  • 沟通gōu tōngto communicateTraditional: 溝通
    Dictionary sensesto join; to connect; to link up
    • 人和人之间沟通的方法有很多种。rén hé rén zhī jiān gōu tōng de fāng fǎ yǒu hěn duō zhǒngHuman beings communicate in many ways.
    • 以前人们以为只有人类才懂得用语言沟通。yǐ qián rén men yǐ wéi zhǐ yǒu rén lèi cái dǒng de yòng yǔ yán gōu tōngPeople used to think that only humans could use language.
  • 手指shǒu zhǐfingerMeasure word: 个、只
    • 用手指点别人不礼貌。yòng shǒu zhǐ diǎn bié ren bù lǐ màoIt's bad manners to point at people.
  • 结论jié lùnconclusionMeasure word: 个Traditional: 結論
    Dictionary sensesverdict; to conclude; to reach a verdict
    • 你们怎么得出这个结论的？nǐ men zěn me de chū zhè ge jié lùn deHow do you come to that conclusion?
  • 提升tí shēngto raise, improve; to promote
    Dictionary sensesto promote (to a higher-ranking position); to lift; to hoist; (fig.) to elevate
    • 我想找一种提升记忆力的方法。wǒ xiǎng zhǎo yī zhǒng tí shēng jì yì lì de fāng fǎI'd like to find a way to improve my memory.
  • 化学huà xuéchemistryTraditional: 化學
    Dictionary senseschemical
    • 我讨厌化学。wǒ tǎo yàn huà xuéI hate chemistry.
    • 你读化学吗？nǐ dú huà xué maAre you a chemistry student?
  • 总之zǒng zhīin shortTraditional: 總之
    Dictionary sensesin a word; in brief
  • 地面dì miànground; floor
    Dictionary sensessurface
    • 地面仍然很湿滑，看来比赛要被取消了。dì miàn réng rán hěn shī huá kàn lai bǐ sài yào bèi qǔ xiāo leThe ground is still wet. As likely as not, the game will be canceled.
  • 天空tiān kōngsky
    • 天空变得越来越暗了。tiān kōng biàn de yuè lái yuè àn leThe sky grew darker and darker.
    • 今天的天空是我看过最蓝的天空。jīn tiān de tiān kōng shì wǒ kàn guò zuì lán de tiān kōngThe sky today is the bluest that I've ever seen it.
