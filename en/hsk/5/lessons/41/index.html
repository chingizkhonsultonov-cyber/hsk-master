  • 字母zì mǔletter (of an alphabet)Measure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesletter (of the alphabet)
    • 写下你名字的大写字母。xiě xià nǐ míng zi de dà xiě zì mǔWrite your name in capitals.
    • 我们应该把学生的名字跟着字母顺序来安排。wǒ men yīng gāi bǎ xué sheng de míng zi gēn zhe zì mǔ shùn xù lái ān páiWe should sort the students' names alphabetically.
