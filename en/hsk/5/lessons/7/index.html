  • 朝cháotowards; dynasty
    Dictionary sensesimperial or royal court; government; reign of a sovereign or emperor; court or assembly held by a sovereign or emperor
    • 我是朝鲜人。wǒ shì cháo xiān rénI'm Korean.
    • 我想去朝鲜。wǒ xiǎng qù cháo xiānI want to go to North Korea.
  • 一旦yí dànonce, in case
    Dictionary sensesin case (sth happens); if; once (sth happens, then...); when
    • 一旦过了河，你就安全了。yī dàn guò le hé nǐ jiù ān quán leOnce across the river, you are safe.
    • 她一旦开始说话，就很难让她停下来。tā yī dàn kāi shǐ shuō huà jiù hěn nán ràng tā tíng xià laiOnce she starts talking, she is hard to stop.
  • 范围fàn wéirange; scopeMeasure word: 个Traditional: 範圍
    Dictionary senseslimit; extent
    • 专家就是一个能够把所有的错误控制在最小范围内的人。zhuān jiā jiù shì yī gè néng gòu bǎ suǒ yǒu de cuò wù kòng zhì zài zuì xiǎo fàn wéi nèi de rénAn expert is a person who has made all the mistakes that can be made in a very narrow field.
  • 副fùdeputy; vice-
    Dictionary sensessecondary; auxiliary; assistant
    • 这是个副词。zhè shì gè fù cíThis is an adverb.
    • 我有一副太阳眼镜。wǒ yǒu yī fù tài yang yǎn jìngI've got a pair of sunglasses.
  • 事业shì yècareer; cause, undertakingMeasure word: 个Traditional: 事業
    Dictionary sensesundertaking; project; activity; (charitable, political or revolutionary) cause
    • 她父亲把一生都贡献给科学事业了。tā fù qīn bǎ yī shēng dōu gòng xiàn gěi kē xué shì yè leHer father devoted his life to science.
    • 越来越多的女人拥有自己的事业并实现和丈夫的经济独立。yuè lái yuè duō de nǚ ren yōng yǒu zì jǐ de shì yè bìng shí xiàn hé zhàng fu de jīng jì dú lìMore and more women have their own careers and are economically independent of their husbands.
  • 承认chéng rènto admitTraditional: 承認
    Dictionary sensesto concede; to recognize; recognition (diplomatic, artistic etc); to acknowledge
    • 我承认我错了。wǒ chéng rèn wǒ cuò leI admit I'm wrong.
    • 他承认他错了吗？tā chéng rèn tā cuò le maDid he admit that he was wrong?
  • 执行zhí xíngto carry out, executeTraditional: 執行
    Dictionary sensesto implement; to carry out; to execute; to run
    • 计划被执行了。jì huà bèi zhí xíng leThe plan was executed.
    • 你执行你的计划了吗？nǐ zhí xíng nǐ de jì huà le maDid you carry out your plan?
  • 怪guàistrange; to blame
    Dictionary sensesbewildering; odd; uncanny; devil
    • 多怪啊！duō guài aHow weird!
    • 我不怪你。wǒ bù guài nǐI don't blame you.
  • 痛苦tòng kǔpain, suffering; painfulMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensespain; suffering
    • 我不能再承受这种痛苦。wǒ bù néng zài chéng shòu zhè zhǒng tòng kǔI can't take this pain anymore.
    • 药物缓解了他的痛苦。yào wù huǎn jiě le tā de tòng kǔThe medicine decreased his pain.
  • 空kōngin vain; empty
    Dictionary sensesair; sky
  • 显然xiǎn ránobviouslyTraditional: 顯然
    Dictionary sensesclearly; evidently
    • 显然地她生病了。xiǎn rán de tā shēng bìng leThat she is ill is obvious.
    • 他显然喜欢散步。tā xiǎn rán xǐ huan sàn bùThere's no doubt that he likes taking walks.
  • 传统chuán tǒngtraditionMeasure word: 个Traditional: 傳統
    Dictionary sensestraditional; convention; conventional
  • 状况zhuàng kuàngsituation, conditionMeasure word: 个Traditional: 狀況
    Dictionary sensescondition; state; situation
    • 他处理不好复杂的状况。tā chǔ lǐ bù hǎo fù zá de zhuàng kuàngHe can't cope with complicated situations.
    • 今天早上高速公路上的交通状况正常。jīn tiān zǎo shang gāo sù gōng lù shàng de jiāo tōng zhuàng kuàng zhèng chángTraffic conditions on the highway are normal this morning.
  • 分享fēn xiǎngto share
    Dictionary sensesto share (let others have some of sth good)
    • 我想跟你分享。wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ fēn xiǎngI want to share it with you.
    • 跟你兄弟分享。gēn nǐ xiōng dì fēn xiǎngShare with your brother.
  • 架jiàmeasure word for planes; frame; shelf
    Dictionary sensesto support; rack; framework; classifier for planes, large vehicles, radios etc
    • 她们在干架。tā men zài gàn jiàThey're fighting.
    • 那么上高架走。nà me shàng gāo jià zǒuWhich way to the elevated highway?
  • 经济jīng jìeconomicalTraditional: 經濟
    Dictionary senseseconomy; economic
  • 翻fānto turn over
    Dictionary sensesto flip over; to overturn; to rummage through; to translate
    • 翻开课本。fān kāi kè běnOpen your textbooks.
    • 价值翻倍了。jià zhí fān bèi leThe value doubled.
  • 时代shí dàiera, ageMeasure word: 个Traditional: 時代
    Dictionary sensesage; era; epoch; period (in one's life)
    • 我和他在学生时代是分不开的朋友。wǒ hé tā zài xué sheng shí dài shì fēn bù kāi de péng youHe and I were inseparable friends during our time together in school.
    • 她给我看了一张她母亲学生时代的照片。tā gěi wǒ kàn le yī zhāng tā mǔ qīn xué sheng shí dài de zhào piànShe showed me a picture of her mother as a schoolgirl.
  • 伟大wěi dàgreatTraditional: 偉大
    Dictionary senseshuge; grand; worthy of the greatest admiration; important (contribution etc)
    • 你能承认你错了是很伟大的。nǐ néng chéng rèn nǐ cuò le shì hěn wěi dà deIt's very big of you to admit you're wrong.
    • 他是世界上最伟大的科学家之一。tā shì shì jiè shàng zuì wěi dà de kē xué jiā zhī yīHe is one of the greatest scientists in the world.
  • 的确dí quèindeedTraditional: 的確
    Dictionary sensesreally
    • 电视的确有一些缺点。diàn shì dí què yǒu yì xiē quē diǎnIt is true that television also has some disadvantages.
    • 虽然听起来很奇怪，但是她说的的确是真的。suī rán tīng qǐ lai hěn qí guài dàn shì tā shuō de dí què shì zhēn deIt may sound strange, but what she said is true.
  • 颗kēmeasure word for small round thingsTraditional: 顆
    Dictionary sensesclassifier for small spheres, pearls, corn grains, teeth, hearts, satellites etc
    • 买几颗苹果。mǎi jǐ kē píng guǒBuy a few apples.
    • 给我一颗苹果！gěi wǒ yī kē píng guǒGive me an apple!
  • 准zhǔnto allow; standard
    Dictionary sensesto grant; in accordance with; in the light of; accurate
  • 身份shēn fènidentity; status
    Dictionary sensesaspect of one's identity (e.g. mayor, father, permanent resident); role; capacity (as in "in his capacity as ..." 以[yi3] + ... + 的身份[de5 shen1 fen4]); status (social, legal etc)
  • 充满chōng mǎnfull ofTraditional: 充滿
    Dictionary sensesbrimming with; very full; permeated
    • 这本书充满了错误。zhè běn shū chōng mǎn le cuò wùThis book is full of errors.
  • 人物rén wùfigure, character
    Dictionary sensesperson; personage; figure (esp. sb of importance); character (in a play, novel etc)
    • 无论书中的人物是真实的还是完全假想的，他们能成为你的伙伴和朋友。wú lùn shū zhōng de rén wù shì zhēn shí de hái shi wán quán jiǎ xiǎng de tā men néng chéng wéi nǐ de huǒ bàn hé péng youWhether the characters in a book are real or completely imaginary, they can become your friends and companions.
  • 系xìdepartment; to relate toTraditional: 係
    Dictionary sensesto connect; to tie up; to bind; to be (literary)
    • 那可能有关系。nà kě néng yǒu guān xìThat would be relevant.
    • 我是不会系领带的。wǒ shì bù huì xì lǐng dài deI won't be wearing a tie.
  • 系xìdepartment (university); to relate toTraditional: 係
    Dictionary sensesto connect; to tie up; to bind; to be (literary)
  • 直zhístraight; directly
    Dictionary sensesto straighten; fair and reasonable; frank; straightforward
    • 他是个直男。tā shì gè zhí nánHe's straight.
    • 船到桥头自然直。chuán dào qiáo tóu zì rán zhíIt'll come all right in the end.
  • 产品chǎn pǐnproductMeasure word: 个Traditional: 產品
    Dictionary sensesgoods; merchandise
    • 我们保证我们的产品一年。wǒ men bǎo zhèng wǒ men de chǎn pǐn yī niánWe guarantee our products for one year.
    • 这个国家的主要产品之一是咖啡。zhè ge guó jiā de zhǔ yào chǎn pǐn zhī yī shì kā fēiOne of the main products of this country is coffee.
  • 成员chéng yuánmemberTraditional: 成員
    • 他的所有家庭成员都很高。tā de suǒ yǒu jiā tíng chéng yuán dōu hěn gāoHis family members are all tall.
    • 六个成员中有三分之一是女性。liù gè chéng yuán zhōng yǒu sān fēn zhī yī shì nǚ xìngOne-third of the six members were women.
