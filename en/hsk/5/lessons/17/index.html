  • 念niànto read aloud; to studyTraditional: 唸
    Dictionary sensesto read; to study (a subject); to attend (a school); to give (sb) a tongue-lashing (CL:頓|顿[dun4])
    • 念这个。niàn zhè geRead this.
    • 这个字怎么念？zhè ge zì zěn me niànHow do you read this character?
  • 背景bèi jǐngbackground
    Dictionary sensesbackdrop; context; (fig.) powerful backer
  • 进步jìn bùprogress; to improveMeasure word: 个Traditional: 進步
    Dictionary sensesimprovement; to progress
    • 你在进步。nǐ zài jìn bùYou're making progress.
    • 今天我们取得了很多进步。jīn tiān wǒ men qǔ dé le hěn duō jìn bùWe've made a lot of progress today.
  • 对待duì dàito treatTraditional: 對待
    Dictionary sensestreatment
    • 你被不公平地对待。nǐ bèi bù gōng píng de duì dàiYou were treated unfairly.
    • 我不知道如何对待孩子。wǒ bù zhī dào rú hé duì dài hái ziI don't know how to handle children.
  • 大会dà huìgeneral meetingMeasure word: 个、届Traditional: 大會
    Dictionary sensesgeneral assembly; convention
    • 如果你是第一次到外国生活的话，加拿大会很适合你。rú guǒ nǐ shì dì yī cì dào wài guó shēng huó de huà jiā ná dà huì hěn shì hé nǐCanada is a good place to go if it's your first experience living abroad.
  • 或是huò shìor
    Dictionary senseseither one or the other
  • 现象xiàn xiàngphenomenonMeasure word: 个、种Traditional: 現象
    Dictionary sensesappearance
    • 酸雨不是自然现象。suān yǔ bù shì zì rán xiàn xiàngAcid rain isn't a natural phenomenon.
  • 实力shí lìstrength, capabilityTraditional: 實力
    Dictionary sensesstrength
  • 私人sī rénprivate, personal
    Dictionary sensesprivate; personal; interpersonal; sb with whom one has a close personal relationship
    • 他有私人飞机。tā yǒu sī rén fēi jīHe has a private jet.
  • 一路yí lùall the way
    Dictionary sensesthe whole journey; going the same way; going in the same direction; of the same kind
    • 祝你一路上玩得愉快。zhù nǐ yī lù shàng wán de yú kuàiHave a pleasant trip.
    • 一路平安！yī lù píng ānBon voyage!
  • 领域lǐng yùfield; domainTraditional: 領域
    Dictionary sensessphere; territory; area
  • 拼pīnto piece together; to go all out
    Dictionary sensesto put together; to pool (resources etc); to share; to risk all
    • 爱拼才会赢。ài pīn cái huì yíngIt is the dogged who wins.
    • 你的姓怎么拼？nǐ de xìng zěn me pīnHow do you spell your surname?
  • 湖húlake
    • 湖上有一些船。hú shàng yǒu yì xiē chuánThere were some boats on the lake.
    • 这湖里有许多鱼。zhè hú lǐ yǒu xǔ duō yúFish abound in this lake.
  • 主动zhǔ dòngon one's own initiativeTraditional: 主動
    Dictionary sensesto take the initiative; to do sth of one's own accord; spontaneous; active
  • 服装fú zhuāngclothingMeasure word: 身Traditional: 服裝
    Dictionary sensesdress; costume; clothes
    • 他穿中式服装。tā chuān zhōng shì fú zhuāngHe wears Chinese-style clothes.
  • 形式xíng shìformMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesouter appearance; shape; formality
  • 硬yìnghard, stiff
    Dictionary senseshard; stiff; solid; (fig.) strong
    • 那么硬卡怎么用呢？nà me yìng kǎ zěn me yòng neSo, how's the card used?
  • 毕竟bì jìngafter allTraditional: 畢竟
    Dictionary sensesall in all; when all is said and done; in the final analysis
    • 他毕竟是那个时代的人。tā bì jìng shì nà ge shí dài de rénHe's a product of his time.
  • 居民jū mínresident
    Dictionary sensesinhabitant
    • 这个城市里有多少居民？zhè ge chéng shì lǐ yǒu duō shao jū mínHow many residents live within this city?
    • 居民走后，房子就空了。jū mín zǒu hòu fáng zi jiù kōng leAfter the residents leave, the house is empty.
  • 限制xiàn zhìto limit; restrictionMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesto restrict; to confine; limit
    • 在市区内，数度被限制在每小时五十公里以内。zài shì qū nèi shù dù bèi xiàn zhì zài měi xiǎo shí wǔ shí gōng lǐ yǐ nèiIn towns, speed is limited to 50 km/h.
  • 恭喜gōng xǐcongratulations
    Dictionary sensesto congratulate; (interj.) congratulations!
    • 恭喜你！gōng xǐ nǐCongrats!
  • 万一wàn yījust in case; if by any chanceTraditional: 萬一
    Dictionary sensescontingency
    • 万一下雨，我就不去。wàn yī xià yǔ wǒ jiù bù qùIn case it rains, I won't go.
    • 万一你生病了，吃这个药。wàn yī nǐ shēng bìng le chī zhè ge yàoTake this medicine in case you get sick.
  • 如今rú jīnnowadays
    Dictionary sensesnow
    • 事到如今，也没有和她吵的必要了。shì dào rú jīn yě méi yǒu hé tā chǎo de bì yào leThere was no point in arguing with her at this point.
  • 前往qián wǎngto go to, head for
    Dictionary sensesto leave for; to proceed towards; to go to
    • 我在前往会议的路上。wǒ zài qián wǎng huì yì de lù shangI'm on my way to the meeting right now.
    • 国际救难队伍随时待命准备前往日本。guó jì jiù nán duì wǔ suí shí dài mìng zhǔn bèi qián wǎng rì běnNational rescue teams are on standby ready to fly to Japan.
  • 分手fēn shǒuto break up
    Dictionary sensesto part company; to split up
    • 我打算与她分手。wǒ dǎ suàn yǔ tā fēn shǒuI plan to break up with her.
    • 我要跟你分手了。wǒ yào gēn nǐ fēn shǒu leI want to break up with you.
  • 恋爱liàn àilove; to be in loveMeasure word: 个、场Traditional: 戀愛
    Dictionary senses(romantic) love; in love; to have an affair
    • 听起来像是我恋爱了吗？tīng qǐ lai xiàng shì wǒ liàn ài le maDo I sound in love?
  • 成立chéng lìto set up
    Dictionary sensesto establish; to be tenable; to hold water
  • 歌曲gē qǔsong
    • 他们非常喜欢那首歌曲。tā men fēi cháng xǐ huan nà shǒu gē qǔThey love that song.
  • 团队tuán duìteamTraditional: 團隊
    • 我觉得我们团队会继续变好。wǒ jué de wǒ men tuán duì huì jì xù biàn hǎoI think our team is going to continue to get better.
  • 模式mó shìmodel; pattern
    Dictionary sensesmode
    • 你很快就会习惯这个国家的生活模式了。nǐ hěn kuài jiù huì xí guàn zhè ge guó jiā de shēng huó mó shì leYou will soon get accustomed to living in this country.
