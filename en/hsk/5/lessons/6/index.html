  • 傻shǎfoolish, silly
    Dictionary sensesfoolish
    • 你傻啊！nǐ shǎ aYou're an idiot!
    • 你真傻。nǐ zhēn shǎYou're so stupid.
  • 权quánright; powerTraditional: 權
    Dictionary sensesauthority; (literary) to weigh; expedient
    • 你无权进入那里。nǐ wú quán jìn rù nà liYou are not authorized to enter there.
    • 先生，您无权把您的车停在这儿。xiān sheng nín wú quán bǎ nín de chē tíng zài zhè rSir, you are not allowed to park your car here.
  • 亲爱qīn àidearTraditional: 親愛
    Dictionary sensesbeloved; darling
  • 救jiùto save; to rescueTraditional: 捄
    Dictionary sensesto assist
    • 你救了我的命。nǐ jiù le wǒ de mìngYou saved my life.
    • 有人救了我的命。yǒu rén jiù le wǒ de mìngSomeone saved my life.
  • 恢复huī fùto recoverTraditional: 恢復
    Dictionary sensesto reinstate; to resume; to restore; to regain
    • 爸爸恢复了健康。bà ba huī fù le jiàn kāngFather recovered his health.
    • 不久后，他恢复了健康。bù jiǔ hòu tā huī fù le jiàn kāngBefore long, he had recuperated.
  • 棒bàngstick; great
    Dictionary sensesclub; cudgel; smart; capable
  • 或许huò xǔperhapsTraditional: 或許
    Dictionary sensesmaybe
    • 她或许会来。tā huò xǔ huì láiShe may come.
    • 那或许是真的。nà huò xǔ shì zhēn deMaybe it's true.
  • 现实xiàn shíreality; realisticTraditional: 現實
    Dictionary sensesactuality; real; actual
    • 现实点！xiàn shí diǎnBe realistic!
    • 那是现实。nà shì xiàn shíThat's the reality.
  • 班bānshift; measure word for buses and flightsMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesteam; class; grade; (military) squad
  • 笔bǐsum (of money); strokeTraditional: 筆
    Dictionary senseswriting brush; writing implement (pen, pencil etc) (CL:支[zhi1],枝[zhi1]); brushstroke; stroke (in painting or calligraphy); literary manner
  • 媒体méi tǐmediaTraditional: 媒體
    Dictionary sensesmedia, esp. news media
  • 意识yì shíconsciousness; to realizeTraditional: 意識
    Dictionary sensesawareness; (usu. followed by 到[dao4]) to be aware of
    • 我意识到了。wǒ yì shí dào leI'm aware of it.
    • 他已经意识到它了。tā yǐ jīng yì shí dào tā leHe already knew about it.
  • 追zhuīto chase
    Dictionary sensesto pursue; to look into; to investigate; to reminisce
    • 他跑着追上他哥哥。tā pǎo zhe zhuī shàng tā gē geHe ran to catch up to his brother.
    • 狗追兔子。gǒu zhuī tù ziThe dog chased the rabbit.
  • 导致dǎo zhìto lead toTraditional: 導致

  • 集中jí zhōngto concentrate
    Dictionary sensesto centralize; to focus; centralized; concentrated

    Dictionary sensesgrounds; justification

    • 那就是他生气的理由。nà jiù shì tā shēng qì de lǐ yóuThat's why he got angry.
  • 机构jī gòuorganisationMeasure word: 所Traditional: 機構

    Dictionary sensescontrary; in reverse; inside out or upside down; to reverse
    • 你把帽子戴反了。nǐ bǎ mào zi dài fǎn leYou've put your hat on backwards.
    • 他把衣服里外穿反了。tā bǎ yī fu lǐ wài chuān fǎn leHe wore his clothes inside out.

    • 太完美了。tài wán měi leIt was perfect.
    • 那是完美的。nà shì wán měi deThat's perfect.

    Dictionary sensesto spread; to transmit; to infect; to transfer

    • 茶叶是从中国传入的。chá yè shì cóng zhōng guó chuán rù deTea was introduced from China.

    • 他伤害了他的左手。tā shāng hài le tā de zuǒ shǒuHe hurt his left hand.

    • 这不是真实的爱情。zhè bù shì zhēn shí de ài qíngThis isn't true love.

  • 记录jì lùrecord; to recordMeasure word: 个Traditional: 記錄

    Dictionary sensesroom; (fig.) scope; leeway; (astronomy) outer space

    • 行与行之间多留些空间。xíng yǔ xíng zhī jiān duō liú xiē kōng jiānLeave more space between the lines.
  • 冲chōngto rush; to rinseTraditional: 沖
    Dictionary senses(of water) to dash against; to mix with water; to infuse; to flush

  • 期待qī dàito look forward to, expect
    Dictionary sensesto look forward to; to await; expectation
    • 我很期待哦。wǒ hěn qī dài oI look forward to it.
    • 他到我期待。tā dào wǒ qī dàiI've been anticipating his arrival.
  • 毛máowool; hair
    Dictionary sensesfeather; down; mildew
  • 政治zhèng zhìpolitics
