  • 齐qíneat, even; all presentTraditional: 齊
    Dictionary sensesneat; even; level with; identical
    • 齐里的故事发生在四月。qí lǐ de gù shi fā shēng zài sì yuèZiri's story took place in April.
    • 齐里独自跳舞。qí lǐ dú zì tiào wǔZiri dances alone.
  • 市民shì míncity residents
    Dictionary sensescity resident
  • 人口rén kǒupopulation
    Dictionary sensespeople
    • 人口稳定地增加。rén kǒu wěn dìng de zēng jiāThere was a steady increase in population.
    • 日本的人口是多少？rì běn de rén kǒu shì duō shaoWhat is the population of Japan?
  • 咨询zī xúnto consultTraditional: 咨詢
    Dictionary sensesto inquire; to seek advice from
    • 好的，您想咨询什么事情？hǎo de nín xiǎng zī xún shén me shì qingOK, what would you like advice on?
  • 表面biǎo miànsurface
    Dictionary sensesface; outside; appearance
    • 重力将一切带下地球表面。zhòng lì jiāng yī qiè dài xià dì qiú biǎo miànGravity brings everything down to Earth.
    • 光看表面是不足够的。guāng kàn biǎo miàn shì bù zú gòu deAll that glitters is not gold.
  • 地位dì wèistatusMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesposition; place
  • 青qīngblue-green; young
    Dictionary sensesgreen; blue; black; youth
  • 顺shùnalong; smoothTraditional: 順
    Dictionary sensesto obey; to follow; to arrange; to make reasonable
    • 你很顺眼。nǐ hěn shùn yǎnYou're very good-looking.
    • 请顺着这条路一直往前走。qǐng shùn zhe zhè tiáo lù yī zhí wǎng qián zǒuGo straight ahead along this street.
  • 登dēngto climb; to publish
    Dictionary sensesto scale (a height); to ascend; to mount; to publish or record
    • 请登入。qǐng dēng rùLog in.
    • 你有登山鞋吗？nǐ yǒu dēng shān xié maDo you have walking shoes?
  • 不幸bú xìngunfortunateMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesmisfortune; adversity; sad; unfortunately
    • 她看起来不幸福。tā kàn qǐ lai bù xìng fúShe looks unhappy.
    • 他不幸找不到工作。tā bù xìng zhǎo bù dào gōng zuòHe had no luck in finding work.
  • 结构jié gòustructureMeasure word: 座、个Traditional: 結構
    Dictionary sensescomposition; makeup; architecture
  • 妇女fù nǚwomanTraditional: 婦女
    • 妇女也是人。fù nǚ yě shì rénWomen are people.
    • 国际妇女节快乐！guó jì fù nǚ jié kuài lèHappy International Women's Day!
  • 领先lǐng xiānto leadTraditional: 領先
    Dictionary sensesto be in front
  • 矛盾máo dùncontradictionMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesconflicting views; contradictory
    • 他和老板有矛盾，这个我们知道就好。tā hé lǎo bǎn yǒu máo dùn zhè ge wǒ men zhī dào jiù hǎoBetween you and me, he's in trouble with the boss.
  • 上升shàng shēngto rise, go up
    Dictionary sensesto rise; to go up; to ascend
    • 如果她的体温上升，派人去请医生来。rú guǒ tā de tǐ wēn shàng shēng pài rén qù qǐng yī shēng láiIf her temperature goes up, send for the doctor.
    • 物价一直在稳定上升。wù jià yī zhí zài wěn dìng shàng shēngPrices have been rising steadily.
  • 围wéito surroundTraditional: 圍
    Dictionary sensesto encircle; all around; to wear by wrapping around (scarf, shawl)
    • 他被很多人围着，自然变得有点紧张。tā bèi hěn duō rén wéi zhe zì rán biàn de yǒu diǎn jǐn zhāngWith so many people around he naturally became a bit nervous.
  • 路线lù xiànrouteMeasure word: 条Traditional: 路線
    Dictionary sensesitinerary; political line (e.g. right revisionist road)
  • 完整wán zhěngcomplete, intact
    Dictionary sensescomplete; intact
    • 我们想要完整的句子。wǒ men xiǎng yào wán zhěng de jù ziWe want complete sentences.
  • 碰pèngto touch, bump; to run intoTraditional: 掽
    Dictionary sensesto touch; to meet with; to bump
    • 小心碰头。xiǎo xīn pèng tóuMind your head.
    • 可以碰吗？kě yǐ pèng maCan I touch it?
  • 煮zhǔto boilTraditional: 煑
    Dictionary sensesto cook
    • 你知道怎么煮肉吗？nǐ zhī dào zěn me zhǔ ròu maDo you know how to cook meat?
    • 我给我们煮了些咖啡。wǒ gěi wǒ men zhǔ le xiē kā fēiI've made us some coffee.
  • 在乎zài huto care about
    Dictionary sensesto rest with; to lie in; to be due to (a certain attribute); (often used in the negative) to care about
    • 我不在乎将来。wǒ bù zài hu jiāng láiI don't care a bit about the future.
    • 我真的不在乎。wǒ zhēn de bù zài huI really don't care.
  • 飞行fēi xíngto flyTraditional: 飛行
    Dictionary senses(of planes etc) to fly; flying; flight; aviation
    • 飞行愉快！fēi xíng yú kuàiHave a nice flight.
  • 较jiàorelatively; thanTraditional: 較
    Dictionary senses(literary) to dispute; compared to; (before an adjective) relatively; comparatively
    • 两相比较，他那边的形势更不利一些。liǎng xiāng bǐ jiào tā nà biān de xíng shì gèng bù lì yì xiēThe comparison between the two was to his disadvantage.
  • 请求qǐng qiúto request; requestTraditional: 請求
    Dictionary sensesto ask; request (CL:個|个[ge4])
    • 我请求她等一下。wǒ qǐng qiú tā děng yí xiàI asked her to wait a moment.
    • 你应该请求你的老师允许。nǐ yīng gāi qǐng qiú nǐ de lǎo shī yǔn xǔYou ought to ask for your teacher's permission.
  • 纪念jì niànto commemorateTraditional: 紀念
    Dictionary sensesto honor the memory of; memento; keepsake; souvenir
    • 周年纪念很重要。zhōu nián jì niàn hěn zhòng yàoAnniversaries are important.
  • 福fúgood fortune
    Dictionary senseshappiness; luck
    • 我好想吃大福。wǒ hǎo xiǎng chī dà fúI really want to eat some daifuku.
    • 他很有钱，但不幸福。tā hěn yǒu qián dàn bù xìng fúHe's rich, but he isn't happy.
  • 族zúethnic group
    Dictionary sensesrace; nationality; ethnicity; clan
    • 我只是一个普通的上班族。wǒ zhǐ shì yī gè pǔ tōng de shàng bān zúI'm just a plain office worker.
    • 日本上班族工作得非常辛苦。rì běn shàng bān zú gōng zuò de fēi cháng xīn kǔJapanese office workers work very hard.
  • 全新quán xīnbrand new
    Dictionary sensesall new; completely new
    • 这是全新的。zhè shì quán xīn deIt's brand new.
    • 您买了辆全新的车子？nín mǎi le liàng quán xīn de chē ziDid you buy a new car?
  • 插chāto insert
    Dictionary sensesstick in; pierce; to take part in; to interfere
    • 我讨厌插队的人。wǒ tǎo yàn chā duì de rénI hate people cutting queues.
  • 分别fēn biéseparately; to partTraditional: 分別
    Dictionary sensesto leave each other; to distinguish; to tell apart; difference
