    Official grammar list

    Morphemes语素1
    • Suffixes 后缀—头The suffix 头

      noun / verb / adjective + 头

      头 (light tone, tou) makes nouns: 石头 stone, 木头 wood, 前头 front. After a verb or adjective it means something worth doing or a benefit: 看头 something worth seeing, 甜头 a benefit, 没什么吃头 not worth eating.

      When to use it

      头 makes nouns: from nouns (石头, 木头), verbs (念头, 看头 something worth seeing) and adjectives (甜头 benefit). It is read in the neutral tone.

      • 这个电影没什么看头。Zhège diànyǐng méi shénme kàntou.This film isn't worth watching.
      • 桌子是用木头做的。Zhuōzi shì yòng mùtou zuò de.The table is made of wood.
      • 这部电影没什么看头。Zhè bù diànyǐng méi shénme kàntou.This film isn't worth watching.

      Compare

      As a real word 头 (tóu) means head; as a suffix (tou) it has no meaning of its own.

    Parts of speech词类19
    • Pronouns 代词Demonstratives 指示代词彼此、如此、本彼此, 如此, 本

      彼此 (each other) · 如此 (so, like this) · 本 + noun (this, our own)

      彼此 each other, both sides. 如此 so, in this way, written for 这样. 本 before a noun means this or our own: 本人 I myself, 本公司 this company.

      When to use it

      彼此 = each other/both sides; 如此 = so, like this (written); 本 + noun = this (本公司) or one's own (本人).

      • 我们彼此都很了解。Wǒmen bǐcǐ dōu hěn liǎojiě.We know each other well.
      • 事情原来如此。Shìqing yuánlái rúcǐ.So that's how it was.
      • 本店周一不营业。Běn diàn zhōuyī bù yíngyè.This shop is closed on Mondays.
      • 我们彼此都很了解。Wǒmen bǐcǐ dōu hěn liǎojiě.We know each other well.
    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词册、朵、届、颗、匹、行、架、群、支、根、批、束、副、集、周More measure words

      朵 · 颗 · 匹 · 架 · 群 · 支 · 根 · 批 · 束 · 副 · 集 · 册 · 届 · 行

      朵 flowers and clouds. 颗 small round things (teeth, stars). 匹 horses. 架 planes, pianos. 群 groups, crowds. 支 pens, songs, teams. 根 long thin things. 批 batches. 束 bunches. 副 pairs (glasses). 集 episodes. 册 volumes. 届 sessions, graduating classes. 行 lines, rows (háng).

      When to use it

      朵 flowers, clouds; 颗 small round things, stars, teeth; 匹 horses; 架 planes; 群 groups; 支 pens, songs, teams; 根 long thin things; 批 batches; 束 bunches; 副 pairs (glasses); 集 episodes; 册 volumes; 届 sessions, graduating years; 周 weeks; 行 lines.

      • 她送给我一束花。Tā sòng gěi wǒ yí shù huā.She gave me a bunch of flowers.
      • 天上有几朵白云。Tiān shang yǒu jǐ duǒ bái yún.There are a few white clouds in the sky.
      • 这个电视剧一共有四十集。Zhège diànshìjù yígòng yǒu sìshí jí.This TV series has forty episodes in all.
      • 他送给我一束花。Tā sòng gěi wǒ yí shù huā.He gave me a bunch of flowers.
      • 这部电视剧一共四十集。Zhè bù diànshìjù yígòng sìshí jí.This TV series has forty episodes.
    • Measure words 量词Verbal measure words 动量词眼眼: a glance

      看 + 一眼

      眼 counts glances: 看了一眼 took a look, glanced at.

      When to use it

      看 + 一眼 = take a glance; 眼 counts looks.

      • 他看了我一眼，没说话。Tā kànle wǒ yì yǎn, méi shuōhuà.He glanced at me and said nothing.
      • 他看了我一眼，没说话。Tā kàn le wǒ yì yǎn, méi shuōhuà.He glanced at me and said nothing.
    • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词过于、相当、较1、可2、格外、极其Degree adverbs: 相当, 格外, 极其, 过于

      相当 · 较 · 可 · 格外 · 极其 · 过于 + adjective

      相当 quite, considerably. 较 relatively (written 比较). 可 really, emphatic in speech: 可好了. 格外 especially, more than usual. 极其 extremely (written). 过于 too, excessively.

      When to use it

      相当 = quite, considerably; 格外 = unusually; 极其 = extremely (written); 过于 = too, excessively; 可 + adj = really (spoken, with feeling).

      • 这个问题相当复杂。Zhège wèntí xiāngdāng fùzá.This problem is quite complicated.
      • 雨后的空气格外新鲜。Yǔ hòu de kōngqì géwài xīnxiān.The air is especially fresh after rain.
      • 你不要过于担心。Nǐ búyào guòyú dānxīn.Don't worry too much.
      • 今天的月亮格外圆。Jīntiān de yuèliang géwài yuán.The moon is especially round tonight.

      Compare

      过于 is critical (too much); 非常 is neutral (very).

    • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词时刻、曾经、立刻、连忙、始终、早已、即将、急忙、渐渐、尽快、早晚Time adverbs: 曾经, 立刻, 渐渐, 始终, 尽快 …

      曾经 · 立刻 · 连忙 / 急忙 · 始终 · 早已 · 即将 · 渐渐 · 尽快 · 早晚 · 时刻

      曾经 once, at one time (past). 立刻 at once. 连忙 and 急忙 hurriedly. 始终 from start to finish, always. 早已 long since. 即将 about to (written). 渐渐 gradually. 尽快 as soon as possible. 早晚 sooner or later. 时刻 constantly.

      When to use it

      曾经 = once (in the past); 立刻 = immediately; 连忙/急忙 = hurriedly; 始终 = all along; 渐渐 = gradually; 尽快 = as soon as possible; 即将 = about to (written).

      Negative and questions

      Negative of 曾经: 不曾 / 没(有)…过.

      • 我曾经在上海住过两年。Wǒ céngjīng zài Shànghǎi zhùguo liǎng nián.I once lived in Shanghai for two years.
      • 天渐渐黑了。Tiān jiànjiàn hēi le.It gradually got dark.
      • 请尽快给我回电话。Qǐng jǐnkuài gěi wǒ huí diànhuà.Please call me back as soon as possible.
      • 请尽快给我回复。Qǐng jǐnkuài gěi wǒ huífù.Please reply as soon as possible.
    • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词老是、通常、时常老是, 通常, 时常

      老是 · 通常 · 时常 + verb

      老是 always, usually with complaint. 通常 usually, normally. 时常 often.

      When to use it

      老是 = always (complaining); 通常 = usually (neutral); 时常 = often.

      • 我通常七点起床。Wǒ tōngcháng qī diǎn qǐchuáng.I usually get up at seven.
      • 他老是忘带钥匙。Tā lǎoshì wàng dài yàoshi.He keeps forgetting his keys.
      • 你怎么老是迟到？Nǐ zěnme lǎoshì chídào?Why are you always late?

      Compare

      通常 can start a sentence; 常常 cannot.

    • Adverbs 副词Manner adverbs 方式副词尽量、亲自尽量 and 亲自

      尽量 + verb · 亲自 + verb

      尽量 as much as possible, do your best to. 亲自 in person, oneself.

      When to use it

      尽量 = do as much as possible; 亲自 = in person.

      • 我会尽量早点儿到。Wǒ huì jǐnliàng zǎo diǎnr dào.I'll try to get there as early as I can.
      • 校长亲自给我们上课。Xiàozhǎng qīnzì gěi wǒmen shàngkè.The headmaster taught us in person.
      • 经理亲自来机场接我们。Jīnglǐ qīnzì lái jīchǎng jiē wǒmen.The manager came to the airport in person to meet us.
    • Adverbs 副词Linking adverbs 关联副词便、一旦便 and 一旦

      subject + 便 + verb · 一旦 + condition，就 …

      便 is a written 就: then, right away. 一旦 once, if ever, for a moment that changes things.

      When to use it

      便 = written 就 (then, right away); 一旦 = once (something happens), often with 就.

      • 他听完便走了。Tā tīngwán biàn zǒu le.He left as soon as he'd heard it.
      • 一旦决定了，就不要改。Yídàn juédìng le, jiù búyào gǎi.Once you've decided, don't change it.
      • 一旦决定了，就不要改变。Yídàn juédìng le, jiù búyào gǎibiàn.Once you've decided, don't change your mind.
    • Adverbs 副词Manner and mood adverbs 情态副词似乎、仿佛似乎 and 仿佛: seem, as if

      似乎 + statement · 仿佛 + comparison

      似乎 it seems, apparently. 仿佛 as if, as though, often in descriptions.

      When to use it

      似乎 = seems (written 好像); 仿佛 = as if, often with 一样/似的.

      • 他似乎不太高兴。Tā sìhū bú tài gāoxìng.He seems not very happy.
      • 这里安静得仿佛没有人。Zhèlǐ ānjìng de fǎngfú méiyǒu rén.It's so quiet here, as if no one were around.
      • 他似乎不太高兴。Tā sìhū bú tài gāoxìng.He seems not very happy.
    • Adverbs 副词Modal (attitude) adverbs 语气副词毕竟、居然、反正、根本、果然、简直、绝对、的确、反而、白、刚好、可3Tone adverbs: 毕竟, 居然, 反正, 根本, 简直, 反而 …

      adverb + verb / adjective

      毕竟 after all. 居然 unexpectedly. 反正 anyway, in any case. 根本 at all (with negative). 果然 sure enough. 简直 simply, practically. 绝对 absolutely. 的确 indeed. 反而 on the contrary, instead. 白 in vain. 刚好 just right.

      When to use it

      毕竟 = after all; 居然 = unexpectedly; 反正 = anyway; 根本 + 不/没 = not at all; 简直 = simply, practically; 反而 = on the contrary; 果然 = as expected; 白 = in vain.

      • 他毕竟是孩子，别太生气。Tā bìjìng shì háizi, bié tài shēngqì.He is only a child after all, don't be too angry.
      • 我根本不认识他。Wǒ gēnběn bú rènshi tā.I don't know him at all.
      • 吃了药，病反而更重了。Chīle yào, bìng fǎn'ér gèng zhòng le.After taking the medicine, the illness got worse instead.
      • 我白跑了一趟。Wǒ bái pǎole yí tàng.I made the trip for nothing.
      • 我根本不认识他。Wǒ gēnběn bú rènshi tā.I don't know him at all.
      • 天气预报说今天有雨，果然下雨了。Tiānqì yùbào shuō jīntiān yǒu yǔ, guǒrán xià yǔ le.The forecast said rain, and sure enough it rained.

      Compare

      居然 = surprising; 果然 = as expected — opposite feelings.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing time or place 引出时间、处所自从自从: ever since

      自从 + past event (以后 / 以来)，…

      自从 marks a point in the past from which something has been true. It is only used for the past.

      When to use it

      自从 + past event: from then until now. Often with 以后/以来.

      • 自从来了中国，他的汉语进步很快。Zìcóng láile Zhōngguó, tā de Hànyǔ jìnbù hěn kuài.Since coming to China, his Chinese has improved fast.
      • 自从来了中国，他的汉语进步很快。Zìcóng lái le Zhōngguó, tā de Hànyǔ jìnbù hěn kuài.Since coming to China, his Chinese has improved fast.

      Compare

      自从 is only for the past; 从 can be for any time.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing direction or route 引出方向、路径朝、沿（着）朝 and 沿着: towards, along

      朝 + direction / person + verb · 沿 (着) + route + verb

      朝 facing, towards: 朝南 facing south, 朝他笑 smile at him. 沿着 along a road or river.

      When to use it

      朝 + direction/person = facing, towards; 沿着 + route = along.

      • 我的房间朝南。Wǒ de fángjiān cháo nán.My room faces south.
      • 沿着这条路一直走。Yánzhe zhè tiáo lù yìzhí zǒu.Keep going along this road.
      • 我们沿着河边散步。Wǒmen yánzhe hébiān sànbù.We walked along the river.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象替、同1、较2替, 同, 较: for, with, than

      替 + person + verb · 同 + person · A 较 B + adjective

      替 in someone's place, on their behalf: 替我问好 say hello for me. 同 is a written 跟/和. 较 is a written 比.

      When to use it

      替 + person = on behalf of; 同 = with (written); A 较 B + adj = A is more … than B (written).

      • 请替我向你父母问好。Qǐng tì wǒ xiàng nǐ fùmǔ wènhǎo.Please say hello to your parents for me.
      • 今年的收入较去年有所增加。Jīnnián de shōurù jiào qùnián yǒusuǒ zēngjiā.This year's income is somewhat higher than last year's.
      • 你替我向他问好。Nǐ tì wǒ xiàng tā wènhǎo.Say hello to him for me.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing means or basis 引出凭借、依据凭凭: relying on

      凭 + basis + verb

      凭 by, on the strength of: 凭票入场 entry by ticket, 凭经验 based on experience.

      When to use it

      凭 + basis = relying on, by virtue of.

      • 凭票入场。Píng piào rùchǎng.Admission by ticket only.
      • 他凭自己的努力考上了大学。Tā píng zìjǐ de nǔlì kǎoshangle dàxué.He got into university through his own effort.
      • 他凭自己的努力考上了大学。Tā píng zìjǐ de nǔlì kǎo shàng le dàxué.He got into university through his own effort.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing means or basis 引出凭借、依据据据: according to

      据 + source (说 / 报道 / 了解)，…

      据 introduces the source of information: 据说 it is said, 据报道 according to reports.

      When to use it

      据 + 说/报道/了解 = according to (reports).

      • 据说明天会下大雪。Jùshuō míngtiān huì xià dà xuě.They say there'll be heavy snow tomorrow.
      • 据说明天要降温。Jùshuō míngtiān yào jiàngwēn.They say it'll get colder tomorrow.
    • Prepositions 介词Introducing means or basis 引出凭借、依据依据依据: on the basis of

      依据 + rule / fact，…

      依据 is a formal according to, on the basis of, often about laws or rules.

      When to use it

      依据 = on the basis of (formal, rules and facts).

      • 依据规定，这里不能停车。Yījù guīdìng, zhèlǐ bù néng tíng chē.According to the rules, you can't park here.
      • 依据规定，这里不能停车。Yījù guīdìng, zhèlǐ bù néng tíng chē.According to the rules, you can't park here.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining words or phrases 连接词或词组以及、同2以及 and 同: and

      A、B 以及 C · A 同 B

      以及 and, as well as, joining the last item of a list, formal. 同 joins two nouns like 和.

      When to use it

      以及 joins the last item of a list (formal); 同 joins nouns (written 和).

      • 学生、老师以及家长都参加了。Xuésheng, lǎoshī yǐjí jiāzhǎng dōu cānjiā le.Students, teachers and parents all took part.
      • 会议讨论了工资、假期以及其他问题。Huìyì tǎolùn le gōngzī, jiàqī yǐjí qítā wèntí.The meeting discussed pay, holidays and other issues.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining clauses or sentences 连接分句或句子从而、可见、假如、总之从而, 可见, 假如, 总之

      从而 (thus) · 可见 (so it's clear) · 假如 (if) · 总之 (in short)

      从而 thereby, so that, formal. 可见 which shows that. 假如 if, supposing. 总之 in a word, to sum up.

      When to use it

      从而 = thereby; 可见 = this shows; 假如 = if (written); 总之 = in short.

      • 他每天都很晚才睡，可见工作很忙。Tā měitiān dōu hěn wǎn cái shuì, kějiàn gōngzuò hěn máng.He goes to bed late every day, which shows how busy work is.
      • 总之，这次旅行非常成功。Zǒngzhī, zhè cì lǚxíng fēicháng chénggōng.In short, the trip was a great success.
      • 总之，我们明天必须完成。Zǒngzhī, wǒmen míngtiān bìxū wánchéng.In short, we must finish tomorrow.
    • Particles 助词Other particles 其他助词似的似的: as if, like

      像 / 好像 + noun / clause + 似的

      似的 (shìde) ends a comparison: like, as if. It often pairs with 像 or 好像.

      When to use it

      像/好像 + noun/clause + 似的 = as if, like.

      • 他高兴得像个孩子似的。Tā gāoxìng de xiàng ge háizi shìde.He was as happy as a child.
      • 她好像什么都知道似的。Tā hǎoxiàng shénme dōu zhīdào shìde.She acts as if she knows everything.
      • 她高兴得像个孩子似的。Tā gāoxìng de xiàng ge háizi shìde.She was as happy as a child.
    Phrases短语12
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格A来A去A来A去: back and forth

      verb + 来 + verb + 去

      A verb repeated with 来 and 去 means doing it over and over, often without result: 想来想去 think it over and over.

      When to use it

      A来A去 = do something over and over, back and forth: 想来想去, 走来走去, 说来说去.

      • 他在房间里走来走去。Tā zài fángjiān li zǒu lái zǒu qù.He paced back and forth in the room.
      • 他在房间里走来走去，很着急。Tā zài fángjiān li zǒu lái zǒu qù, hěn zháojí.He paced up and down the room, anxious.
      • 我想来想去，还是决定留下。Wǒ xiǎng lái xiǎng qù, háishi juédìng liú xià.After thinking it over and over, I decided to stay.

      Compare

      Often followed by a conclusion: 想来想去，还是不去了.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格A着A着A着A着: while doing, then

      verb + 着 + verb + 着，…

      Repeating a verb with 着 means in the middle of doing it, something else happened: 说着说着哭了 started crying while talking.

      When to use it

      A着A着 + new event: while doing A, something else happens unexpectedly.

      • 孩子看着看着就睡着了。Háizi kànzhe kànzhe jiù shuìzháo le.The child fell asleep while watching.
      • 孩子哭着哭着就睡着了。Háizi kū zhe kū zhe jiù shuì zháo le.The child cried and cried and then fell asleep.
      • 我们走着走着，天就黑了。Wǒmen zǒu zhe zǒu zhe, tiān jiù hēi le.We kept walking, and it got dark.

      Compare

      The verb is repeated with 着; the second clause is a change, often with 就/突然.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格没A没B没A没B

      没 + A + 没 + B

      没 before two related words means lacking both: 没大没小 no respect for elders, 没完没了 endless.

      When to use it

      没A没B = without A or B; often critical: 没大没小 (disrespectful to elders), 没完没了 (endless).

      • 他说起来没完没了。Tā shuō qilai méi wán méi liǎo.Once he starts talking, he never stops.
      • 他说起话来没完没了。Tā shuō qǐ huà lái méi wán méi liǎo.Once he starts talking, he never stops.
      • 这孩子对爷爷说话没大没小的。Zhè háizi duì yéye shuōhuà méi dà méi xiǎo de.This child talks to grandpa with no respect.

      Compare

      无A无B is written and neutral (无忧无虑); 没A没B is spoken and often negative.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格说A就A说A就A: no sooner said than done

      说 + verb + 就 + verb

      Something happens the moment it's mentioned: 说走就走 off we go right away, 说来就来.

      When to use it

      说A就A = do it the moment it's mentioned, without delay.

      • 我们说走就走吧。Wǒmen shuō zǒu jiù zǒu ba.Let's just go, right now.
      • 这里的天气说变就变。Zhèlǐ de tiānqì shuō biàn jiù biàn.The weather here changes in an instant.
      • 他说走就走，谁也拦不住。Tā shuō zǒu jiù zǒu, shéi yě lán bu zhù.Once he says he's leaving, he's gone; no one can stop him.
      • 这里的天气说变就变。Zhèlǐ de tiānqì shuō biàn jiù biàn.The weather here can change in an instant.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他不得了不得了: extremely, terrible

      不得了 (alone) · adjective + 得不得了

      Alone, 不得了 means this is serious, terrible. After 得 it means extremely: 高兴得不得了 overjoyed.

      When to use it

      不得了 = terrible, extremely; also an exclamation “oh no!”. After 得 it means “extremely” (see row 43).

      • 不得了了，着火了！Bùdéliǎo le, zháohuǒ le!Oh no, there's a fire!
      • 听到这个消息，他高兴得不得了。Tīngdào zhège xiāoxi, tā gāoxìng de bùdéliǎo.He was overjoyed at the news.
      • 不得了了，着火了！Bùdéliǎo le, zháohuǒ le!Oh no, there's a fire!
      • 这件事要是让老板知道，可就不得了了。Zhè jiàn shì yàoshi ràng lǎobǎn zhīdào, kě jiù bùdéliǎo le.If the boss finds out about this, it'll be a disaster.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他用不着用不着: no need

      用不着 + verb / noun

      用不着 means there's no need for, it's unnecessary.

      When to use it

      用不着 = there's no need to; 用得着 = it's worth / needed (mostly in questions).

      Negative and questions

      Question: 用得着这么着急吗？ (Is there any need to rush?)

      • 你用不着担心。Nǐ yòng bu zháo dānxīn.There's no need for you to worry.
      • 这点儿小事，用不着麻烦你。Zhè diǎnr xiǎo shì, yòng bu zháo máfan nǐ.No need to trouble you over such a small thing.

      Compare

      用不着 sounds more natural in speech than 不用.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他从……来看从……来看: judging from

      从 + evidence + 来看，…

      从……来看 gives the evidence or angle a judgement is based on.

      When to use it

      从……来看 = judging from, looking at it from (a point of view).

      • 从他的表情来看，他不太满意。Cóng tā de biǎoqíng lái kàn, tā bú tài mǎnyì.Judging by his expression, he isn't very satisfied.

      Compare

      Also: 从……看来, 从……上看. 看来 alone = it seems.

      verb A + 的 + verb A，verb B + 的 + verb B

      This describes a group doing different things: 唱歌的唱歌，跳舞的跳舞 some singing, some dancing.

      When to use it

      A的A，B的B: describes a group where some do A and some do B.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他（自）……以来(自)……以来: since

      (自) + time / event + 以来

      以来 covers a period from a past point up to now: 自去年以来 since last year.

      When to use it

      (自)……以来 = since (a past time) up to now.

      • 自去年以来，他一直在学汉语。Zì qùnián yǐlái, tā yìzhí zài xué Hànyǔ.Since last year, he has been studying Chinese.

      以来 includes the present; 以后 just means after. 三年以来 = for the past three years.

      A + 由 + parts + 组成

      This says what a whole consists of.

      When to use it

      由……组成 = be made up of (parts or members).

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他X也不是，Y也不是X也不是，Y也不是: neither works

      A 也不是，B 也不是

      Both options feel wrong; you don't know what to do: 走也不是，留也不是.

      When to use it

      X也不是，Y也不是 = neither X nor Y works; at a loss.

      • 我站在那儿，走也不是，留也不是。Wǒ zhàn zài nàr, zǒu yě bú shì, liú yě bú shì.I stood there, unable to either leave or stay.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他X也X不得，Y也Y不得X也X不得，Y也Y不得: can't do either

      A 也 A 不得，B 也 B 不得

      Neither action is allowed or acceptable: 哭也哭不得，笑也笑不得 can neither cry nor laugh.

      When to use it

      X也X不得，Y也Y不得 = can't do X, can't do Y either: stuck.

      • 这件事说也说不得，问也问不得。Zhè jiàn shì shuō yě shuō bu de, wèn yě wèn bu de.You can neither talk about this nor ask about it.

      Compare

      Similar to row 30, but with verb + 不得 (must not).

    • X是它，Y也是它X是它，Y也是它: it's both

      A 是它，B 也是它

      The same thing is responsible for two opposite results.

      When to use it

      X是它，Y也是它 = the same thing causes both opposite results.

      • 让人高兴的是它，让人难过的也是它。Ràng rén gāoxìng de shì tā, ràng rén nánguò de yě shì tā.It's what makes people happy, and it's also what makes them sad.
      • 成功是它，失败也是它，关键看你怎么用。Chénggōng shì tā, shībài yě shì tā, guānjiàn kàn nǐ zěnme yòng.It can bring success or failure; it depends how you use it.
    • X着也是X着X着也是X着: might as well

      verb + 着也是 + verb + 着

      You're doing nothing useful anyway, so you might as well do something: 闲着也是闲着 I'm free anyway.

      When to use it

      X着也是X着 = you're doing X (idle) anyway, so you might as well.

      • 闲着也是闲着，我来帮你吧。Xiánzhe yě shì xiánzhe, wǒ lái bāng nǐ ba.I've nothing to do anyway, let me help you.

      • 车放着也是放着，你拿去用吧。Chē fàng zhe yě shì fàng zhe, nǐ ná qù yòng ba.The car is just sitting there; take it.
    • 不管怎样说不管怎样说: whatever you say

      不管怎样说，…

      This sets aside other points to state the main one: anyway, in any case.

      When to use it

      不管怎样说 = whatever one says; anyway, in any case.

      • 不管怎样说，他是你的朋友。Bùguǎn zěnyàng shuō, tā shì nǐ de péngyou.Whatever you say, he's your friend.
      • 不管怎样说，他是你哥哥。Bùguǎn zěnyàng shuō, tā shì nǐ gēge.Whatever you say, he's still your brother.
    • 真有你/他/她的真有你的: you're something else

      真有 + 你 / 他 / 她 + 的

      An exclamation of admiration, or sometimes of exasperation: you're amazing, only you could do that.

      When to use it

      真有你/他/她的 = you're really something! (praise or mock surprise).

      • 这么难的题你都做出来了，真有你的！Zhème nán de tí nǐ dōu zuò chulai le, zhēn yǒu nǐ de!You solved such a hard problem, you're amazing!
      • 这么难的题你都做出来了，真有你的！Zhème nán de tí nǐ dōu zuò chūlai le, zhēn yǒu nǐ de!You solved such a hard problem — you're amazing!
    • X什么XX什么X: what's there to X

      verb + 什么 + verb

      Putting 什么 between two copies of a verb tells someone to stop: 急什么急 what's the rush.

      When to use it

      X什么X = what's there to X? Tells someone not to do X.

      • 急什么急，时间还早呢。Jí shénme jí, shíjiān hái zǎo ne.What's the hurry? It's still early.
      • 哭什么哭，有话好好说。Kū shénme kū, yǒu huà hǎohǎo shuō.Stop crying; say what you mean calmly.
      • 急什么急，时间还早呢。Jí shénme jí, shíjiān hái zǎo ne.What's the hurry? It's still early.
    • 什么X不X（的）什么X不X(的): who cares about X

      什么 + A + 不 + A + (的)

      This dismisses a concern as unimportant: 什么钱不钱的 never mind about money.

      When to use it

      什么X不X(的) = who cares about X or not; X doesn't matter.

      • 什么钱不钱的，朋友之间别说这个。Shénme qián bu qián de, péngyou zhījiān bié shuō zhège.Forget about money, friends don't talk about that.
      • 什么贵不贵的，喜欢就买。Shénme guì bu guì de, xǐhuan jiù mǎi.Never mind whether it's expensive; if you like it, buy it.
    • 不X白不X不X白不X: why not, it's free

      不 + verb + 白不 + verb

      If you don't take the chance it's wasted, so take it: 不吃白不吃 might as well eat it.

      When to use it

      不X白不X = if you don't X, you lose out; X it since it's free.

      • 免费的，不吃白不吃。Miǎnfèi de, bù chī bái bù chī.It's free, so we might as well eat it.
      • 免费的，不吃白不吃。Miǎnfèi de, bù chī bái bù chī.It's free — you'd be silly not to eat it.
    • X来X去，都是/就是……X来X去，都是……: after all that

      verb + 来 + verb + 去，都是 / 就是 …

      However much you do it, it comes to the same thing: 说来说去 after all is said.

      When to use it

      X来X去，都是/就是…… = after all the X-ing, it's still the same thing.

      • 说来说去，还是钱的问题。Shuō lái shuō qù, háishi qián de wèntí.When all's said and done, it's about money.
      • 他说来说去，就是不想去。Tā shuō lái shuō qù, jiù shì bù xiǎng qù.For all his talk, he just doesn't want to go.
    • 动词+什么（就）是什么Verb + 什么 (就) 是什么: whatever

      verb + 什么 + (就) 是 + 什么

      Whatever is said or chosen is accepted as it is: 你说什么是什么 whatever you say goes.

      When to use it

      Verb + 什么(就)是什么 = whatever you verb, that's it (no choice or no fuss).

      • 今天你说什么就是什么。Jīntiān nǐ shuō shénme jiù shì shénme.Today, whatever you say goes.

    • Adverbial 状语多项状语Several adverbials in a row

      time + 都 / 也 + manner + 跟 / 在 + place / person + verb

      When several adverbials go before the verb, a common order is time, then adverbs like 都, 也, 已经, then manner, then prepositions with their objects: 他昨天也很认真地跟我们讨论了.

      When to use it

      Several adverbials in a row usually go: time → place/manner → 跟/对 + person → verb.

      • 他昨天也很认真地跟我们讨论了这个问题。Tā zuótiān yě hěn rènzhēn de gēn wǒmen tǎolùnle zhège wèntí.Yesterday he also discussed this question with us very seriously.

      Typical order: 他昨天在图书馆认真地跟同学讨论了问题.

    • Complement 补语Potential complements 2 可能补语2动词+得/不得Verb + 得 / 不得: allowed or not

      verb + 得 / 不得

      不得 after a verb means it must not be done or is unsafe: 吃不得 not edible, shouldn't eat. 得 alone is rarer: 吃得.

      When to use it

      Verb + 得/不得 = may / must not (allowed or advisable): 去不得, 吃不得.

      • 这种东西吃不得。Zhè zhǒng dōngxi chī bu de.You mustn't eat this kind of thing.
      • 这件事马虎不得。Zhè jiàn shì mǎhu bu de.You can't be careless about this.
      • 这个蘑菇有毒，吃不得。Zhège mógu yǒu dú, chī bu de.This mushroom is poisonous; you mustn't eat it.

      Compare

      吃不得 = shouldn't eat (bad for you); 吃不了 = can't finish.

    • Complement 补语Degree complements 2 程度补语2形容词/心理动词+得+不得了Adjective + 得不得了

      adjective / feeling verb + 得不得了

      得不得了 means extremely, to an incredible degree.

      When to use it

      adj / feeling verb + 得 + 不得了 = extremely.

      • 她紧张得不得了。Tā jǐnzhāng de bùdéliǎo.She was terribly nervous.
      • 听到这个消息，他高兴得不得了。Tīng dào zhège xiāoxi, tā gāoxìng de bùdéliǎo.He was overjoyed to hear the news.

      Compare

      Same meaning as 极了 or 得要命 but very spoken.

    • Complement 补语Direction complements 3 趋向补语3趋向补语的引申用法：表示状态意义：动词/形容词+下来/下去/起来/过来/过去Direction words for states: 下来, 下去, 起来, 过来, 过去

      adjective / verb + 下来 / 下去 / 起来 / 过来 / 过去

      After adjectives these show change of state. 起来 starting to become (热起来, 胖起来). 下来 settling down, often from strong to weak (安静下来, 黑下来). 下去 continuing to get worse (瘦下去). 过来 coming back to normal (醒过来). 过去 losing consciousness (晕过去).

      When to use it

      Direction complements for state: 下来 = settling, calming (静下来); 下去 = continuing (说下去); 起来 = starting (笑起来); 过来 = back to normal (醒过来); 过去 = losing consciousness (晕过去).

      • 天气热起来了。Tiānqì rè qilai le.It's getting hot.
      • 教室里慢慢安静下来了。Jiàoshì li mànmàn ānjìng xialai le.The classroom slowly quietened down.
      • 他终于醒过来了。Tā zhōngyú xǐng guolai le.He finally came round.
      • 教室里突然安静下来了。Jiàoshì li tūrán ānjìng xiàlai le.The classroom suddenly went quiet.
      • 病人终于醒过来了。Bìngrén zhōngyú xǐng guòlai le.The patient finally came round.
      • 你说下去，我在听。Nǐ shuō xiàqu, wǒ zài tīng.Go on, I'm listening.

      Compare

      天黑下来 = it gets dark (gradually settles); 天亮起来 = it starts getting light.

    • Complement 补语Complements of state 2 状态补语2（1）动词/形容词+得+动词短语得 + verb phrase

      verb / adjective + 得 + verb phrase

      After 得, a whole action can describe the result or degree: 累得走不动了 so tired I couldn't walk.

      When to use it

      Verb/adj + 得 + verb phrase: shows the result or degree: 累得走不动.

      verb / adjective + 得 + subject + predicate

      After 得, a small sentence with its own subject can show the effect: 他说得大家都笑了 he spoke so that everyone laughed.

      When to use it

      Verb/adj + 得 + subject-predicate clause: 疼得他直哭.

      • 他说得大家都笑了。Tā shuō de dàjiā dōu xiào le.The way he told it made everyone laugh.

      A four-character phrase can follow 得 to describe the degree vividly: 忙得团团转 run off one's feet.

      When to use it

    • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型把 sentences 3 “把”字句3（2）主语+把+宾语1+动词+宾语2把 + object 1 + verb + object 2

      subject + 把 + A + 当作 / 叫 / 翻译成 + B

      With verbs like 当 treat as, 叫 call, 翻译成 translate into, 把 introduces the first thing and the second follows the verb.

      When to use it

      Two verb phrases side by side can mean because, but or if without a linking word: 有病不去看 ill but doesn't see a doctor; 听了很高兴 happy because he heard it.

      When to use it

      Two verbs in a row can show cause, contrast or condition: 有病不去看 = ill but won't see a doctor.

      • 你有话就直说。Nǐ yǒu huà jiù zhí shuō.If you have something to say, say it.

      A + 大 / 小 / 高 + B + amount

      Without 比, an adjective can go between A and B with the difference after: 他大我三岁 he is three years older than me.

      When to use it

      A + adj + B + amount: A is (amount) more than B, without 比: 我大他两岁.
