Working from home

1. Pros and cons

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì你们公司现在还允许在家办公吗？Nǐmen gōngsī xiànzài hái yǔnxǔ zài jiā bàngōng ma?Does your company still allow working from home?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng每周可以在家两天。省了路上的时间，效率反而提高了。Měi zhōu kěyǐ zài jiā liǎng tiān. Shěng le lù shang de shíjiān, xiàolǜ fǎn'ér tígāo le.Two days a week. I save commuting time, and I'm actually more productive.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我倒觉得在家容易分心，工作和生活分不开。Wǒ dào juéde zài jiā róngyì fēnxīn, gōngzuò hé shēnghuó fēn bu kāi.I actually find it easy to get distracted at home; work and life blur together.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng关键是要给自己定规矩，比如固定上下班时间。Guānjiàn shì yào gěi zìjǐ dìng guīju, bǐrú gùdìng shàng xià bān shíjiān.The key is to set rules, like fixed start and finish times.

2. A video meeting

  1. 经理 · jīnglǐ李明，你那边声音断断续续的，听不清楚。Lǐ Míng, nǐ nàbian shēngyīn duànduàn xùxù de, tīng bu qīngchu.Li Ming, your sound keeps cutting out; I can't hear you clearly.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng抱歉，我换个房间试试……现在呢？Bàoqiàn, wǒ huàn ge fángjiān shìshi…… xiànzài ne?Sorry, let me try another room… How about now?
  3. 经理 · jīnglǐ好多了。请你接着汇报项目进度。Hǎo duō le. Qǐng nǐ jiēzhe huìbào xiàngmù jìndù.Much better. Please carry on with the project update.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好的，目前我们已经完成了百分之七十。Hǎo de, mùqián wǒmen yǐjīng wánchéng le bǎi fēn zhī qīshí.OK — so far we've completed seventy percent.

Starting a business

1. I want to start a company

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我打算辞职创业，开一家网上书店。Wǒ dǎsuàn cízhí chuàngyè, kāi yì jiā wǎng shang shūdiàn.I'm planning to quit and start up an online bookshop.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà想法不错，不过你做过市场调查吗？Xiǎngfǎ búcuò, búguò nǐ zuò guo shìchǎng diàochá ma?Nice idea, but have you done any market research?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng做了一些，发现专业书的需求挺大。Zuò le yìxiē, fāxiàn zhuānyè shū de xūqiú tǐng dà.Some — demand for specialist books seems quite strong.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà那就先做个小规模的试试，别一下子投入太多。Nà jiù xiān zuò ge xiǎo guīmó de shìshi, bié yíxiàzi tóurù tài duō.Then start small and test it; don't invest too much at once.

2. Looking for investors

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我们的产品已经有一万个用户了，增长很快。Wǒmen de chǎnpǐn yǐjīng yǒu yíwàn ge yònghù le, zēngzhǎng hěn kuài.Our product already has ten thousand users and is growing fast.
  2. 投资人 · tóuzīrén你们打算怎么赚钱？Nǐmen dǎsuàn zěnme zhuàn qián?How do you plan to make money?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng基础功能免费，高级功能收费。Jīchǔ gōngnéng miǎnfèi, gāojí gōngnéng shōufèi.Basic features are free; premium features are paid.
  4. 投资人 · tóuzīrén思路很清楚。下周把详细的计划书发给我看看。Sīlù hěn qīngchu. Xià zhōu bǎ xiángxì de jìhuàshū fā gěi wǒ kànkan.Clear thinking. Send me a detailed business plan next week.

City traffic

1. Stuck in traffic

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì师傅，前面怎么堵得这么厉害？Shīfu, qiánmiàn zěnme dǔ de zhème lìhai?Driver, why is the traffic so bad ahead?
  2. 司机 · sījī听说出了交通事故，一时半会儿走不了。Tīngshuō chū le jiāotōng shìgù, yìshí bànhuìr zǒu bu liǎo.I hear there's been an accident; we won't move for a while.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我九点要开会，来不及了。Wǒ jiǔ diǎn yào kāihuì, lái bu jí le.I have a meeting at nine; I won't make it.
  4. 司机 · sījī前面路口有地铁站，您要不要下车坐地铁？Qiánmiàn lùkǒu yǒu dìtiězhàn, nín yào bu yào xià chē zuò dìtiě?There's a metro station at the next crossing. Want to hop out and take the metro?

2. Should the city limit cars?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我觉得应该限制私家车，多发展公共交通。Wǒ juéde yīnggāi xiànzhì sījiāchē, duō fāzhǎn gōnggòng jiāotōng.I think private cars should be limited and public transport developed.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng可是对住得远的人来说，没有车很不方便。Kěshì duì zhù de yuǎn de rén lái shuō, méiyǒu chē hěn bù fāngbiàn.But for people who live far out, no car is very inconvenient.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng所以地铁要修到郊区去。Suǒyǐ dìtiě yào xiū dào jiāoqū qù.That's why the metro should extend to the suburbs.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这样的话，我同意。Zhèyàng de huà, wǒ tóngyì.In that case, I agree.

Caring for parents

1. Mum lives alone

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我爸去世以后，我妈一个人住，我很不放心。Wǒ bà qùshì yǐhòu, wǒ mā yí ge rén zhù, wǒ hěn bú fàngxīn.Since Dad passed away, Mum lives alone, and I worry a lot.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì为什么不把她接过来一起住？Wèishénme bù bǎ tā jiē guòlai yìqǐ zhù?Why not bring her to live with you?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà她舍不得离开老朋友，也怕给我添麻烦。Tā shěbude líkāi lǎo péngyou, yě pà gěi wǒ tiān máfan.She can't bear to leave her old friends and doesn't want to be a burden.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那至少多打电话，周末常回去看看。Nà zhìshǎo duō dǎ diànhuà, zhōumò cháng huíqu kànkan.Then at least call often and visit at weekends.

2. A carer for Grandpa

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng爷爷的腿不方便，我们是不是该请个保姆？Yéye de tuǐ bù fāngbiàn, wǒmen shì bu shì gāi qǐng ge bǎomǔ?Grandpa has trouble walking. Should we hire a carer?
  2. 妈妈 · māma我也这么想，可他不愿意让外人照顾。Wǒ yě zhème xiǎng, kě tā bú yuànyì ràng wàirén zhàogù.I think so too, but he doesn't want a stranger looking after him.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng要不先请个钟点工，每天来几个小时，让他慢慢习惯。Yàobù xiān qǐng ge zhōngdiǎngōng, měi tiān lái jǐ ge xiǎoshí, ràng tā mànmān xíguàn.How about a part-time helper for a few hours a day first, so he gets used to it?
  4. 妈妈 · māma好主意，我去跟他商量商量。Hǎo zhǔyi, wǒ qù gēn tā shāngliang shāngliang.Good idea; I'll talk it over with him.

Shopping habits

1. Shopping festival

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì“双十一”你又买了多少东西？“Shuāng Shíyī” nǐ yòu mǎi le duōshao dōngxi?How much did you buy on Singles' Day this time?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà别提了，买了一大堆，很多其实根本用不上。Bié tí le, mǎi le yí dà duī, hěn duō qíshí gēnběn yòng bu shàng.Don't ask — a whole pile, and lots of it I'll never use.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì打折的时候人总是觉得不买就亏了。Dǎzhé de shíhou rén zǒngshì juéde bù mǎi jiù kuī le.With discounts, people always feel they'd lose out by not buying.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà以后我得先列清单，只买需要的。Yǐhòu wǒ děi xiān liè qīngdān, zhǐ mǎi xūyào de.From now on I'll make a list and buy only what I need.

2. Second-hand

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我把不用的东西都放到二手网站上卖了。Wǒ bǎ bú yòng de dōngxi dōu fàng dào èrshǒu wǎngzhàn shang mài le.I've put everything I don't use on a second-hand site.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng卖得出去吗？Mài de chūqu ma?Do they sell?
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng卖得挺快的，既能赚点儿钱，又不浪费。Mài de tǐng kuài de, jì néng zhuàn diǎnr qián, yòu bú làngfèi.Pretty fast — I make a bit of money and nothing goes to waste.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng我家也有很多旧书，我也试试。Wǒ jiā yě yǒu hěn duō jiù shū, wǒ yě shìshi.I've got lots of old books at home; I'll try too.

School pressure

1. Too many extra classes

  1. 妈妈 · māma我给孩子报了英语、钢琴和数学三个班。Wǒ gěi háizi bào le Yīngyǔ, gāngqín hé shùxué sān ge bān.I've signed my child up for English, piano and maths classes.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà他才八岁，会不会太累了？Tā cái bā suì, huì bu huì tài lèi le?He's only eight. Won't that be too tiring?
  3. 妈妈 · māma别的孩子都在学，不学就落后了。Bié de háizi dōu zài xué, bù xué jiù luòhòu le.All the other kids are doing it; if he doesn't, he'll fall behind.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà我觉得童年也很重要，应该让他有时间玩儿。Wǒ juéde tóngnián yě hěn zhòngyào, yīnggāi ràng tā yǒu shíjiān wánr.I think childhood matters too; he should have time to play.

2. The gaokao

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你还记得高考那年吗？Nǐ hái jìde gāokǎo nà nián ma?Do you still remember the year of the university entrance exam?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng怎么会忘？每天学到半夜，压力大得要命。Zěnme huì wàng? Měi tiān xué dào bànyè, yālì dà de yàomìng.How could I forget? Studying till midnight every day, unbearable pressure.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng不过现在想想，那段经历也让我学会了坚持。Búguò xiànzài xiǎngxiang, nà duàn jīnglì yě ràng wǒ xuéhuì le jiānchí.But looking back, it taught me perseverance.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng确实，什么事都有两面。Quèshí, shénme shì dōu yǒu liǎng miàn.True — everything has two sides.

Traditional Chinese medicine

1. Trying acupuncture

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我的腰一直疼，西药吃了也没什么用。Wǒ de yāo yìzhí téng, xīyào chī le yě méi shénme yòng.My back keeps hurting, and Western medicine hasn't helped.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì你可以试试针灸，我妈做了几次就好多了。Nǐ kěyǐ shìshi zhēnjiǔ, wǒ mā zuò le jǐ cì jiù hǎo duō le.Try acupuncture; my mum felt much better after a few sessions.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà扎针疼不疼？我有点儿怕。Zhā zhēn téng bu téng? Wǒ yǒudiǎnr pà.Do the needles hurt? I'm a bit scared.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì一点儿也不疼，就是有点儿麻。Yìdiǎnr yě bù téng, jiùshì yǒudiǎnr má.Not at all; it just tingles a bit.

2. Herbal soup

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng大夫，我最近总觉得没力气，睡不好。Dàifu, wǒ zuìjìn zǒng juéde méi lìqi, shuì bu hǎo.Doctor, lately I always feel weak and sleep badly.
  2. 中医 · zhōngyī我看看你的舌头……你压力太大，身体有点儿虚。Wǒ kànkan nǐ de shétou…… nǐ yālì tài dà, shēntǐ yǒudiǎnr xū.Let me see your tongue… You're under too much stress; your body is a bit run-down.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng需要吃药吗？Xūyào chī yào ma?Do I need medicine?
  4. 中医 · zhōngyī我给你开几副中药，再注意早睡、少喝冷饮。Wǒ gěi nǐ kāi jǐ fù zhōngyào, zài zhùyì zǎo shuì, shǎo hē lěngyǐn.I'll prescribe some herbal medicine. Also go to bed early and cut down on cold drinks.

Tourism

1. Too many tourists

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng国庆节去长城的人太多了，根本看不到风景。Guóqìng Jié qù Chángchéng de rén tài duō le, gēnběn kàn bu dào fēngjǐng.There were so many people at the Great Wall over National Day you couldn't see the view at all.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà节假日出去玩儿就是这样，到处都是人。Jiéjiàrì chūqu wánr jiù shì zhèyàng, dàochù dōu shì rén.That's holidays for you — people everywhere.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng下次我要错峰出行，等人少了再去。Xià cì wǒ yào cuòfēng chūxíng, děng rén shǎo le zài qù.Next time I'll travel off-peak, when it's quieter.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà而且淡季的机票和酒店都便宜很多。Érqiě dànjì de jīpiào hé jiǔdiàn dōu piányi hěn duō.And off-season flights and hotels are much cheaper.

2. A guided tour

  1. 导游 · dǎoyóu各位游客，这座寺庙已经有一千多年的历史了。Gèwèi yóukè, zhè zuò sìmiào yǐjīng yǒu yìqiān duō nián de lìshǐ le.Ladies and gentlemen, this temple is over a thousand years old.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì请问，里面可以拍照吗？Qǐngwèn, lǐmiàn kěyǐ pāizhào ma?Excuse me, can we take photos inside?
  3. 导游 · dǎoyóu外面可以，大殿里面禁止拍照。Wàimiàn kěyǐ, dàdiàn lǐmiàn jìnzhǐ pāizhào.Outside yes; in the main hall photography is forbidden.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì明白了，谢谢。Míngbai le, xièxie.Understood, thanks.

Artificial intelligence

1. Will AI take our jobs?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng人工智能发展得这么快，以后很多工作会不会被代替？Réngōng zhìnéng fāzhǎn de zhème kuài, yǐhòu hěn duō gōngzuò huì bu huì bèi dàitì?AI is developing so fast — will lots of jobs be replaced?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì一些重复性的工作肯定会，但也会出现新的工作。Yìxiē chóngfùxìng de gōngzuò kěndìng huì, dàn yě huì chūxiàn xīn de gōngzuò.Some repetitive jobs certainly will, but new jobs will appear too.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng那我们应该怎么准备？Nà wǒmen yīnggāi zěnme zhǔnbèi?So how should we prepare?
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì不断学习，学会利用这些工具，而不是害怕它们。Búduàn xuéxí, xuéhuì lìyòng zhèxiē gōngjù, ér bú shì hàipà tāmen.Keep learning and learn to use these tools instead of fearing them.

2. AI for learning Chinese

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我最近用人工智能练习口语，效果不错。Wǒ zuìjìn yòng réngōng zhìnéng liànxí kǒuyǔ, xiàoguǒ búcuò.I've been practising speaking with AI lately; it works well.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng它能纠正你的发音吗？Tā néng jiūzhèng nǐ de fāyīn ma?Can it correct your pronunciation?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà能，还会告诉我哪个声调说错了。Néng, hái huì gàosu wǒ nǎge shēngdiào shuō cuò le.Yes, it even tells me which tone I got wrong.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng不过还是要多跟真人交流。Búguò háishi yào duō gēn zhēnrén jiāoliú.Still, you need to talk to real people a lot too.

A health check

1. Test results

  1. 医生 · yīshēng你的体检报告出来了，血压有点儿高。Nǐ de tǐjiǎn bàogào chūlai le, xuèyā yǒudiǎnr gāo.Your check-up results are in. Your blood pressure is a bit high.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng严重吗？我才三十五岁。Yánzhòng ma? Wǒ cái sānshíwǔ suì.Is it serious? I'm only thirty-five.
  3. 医生 · yīshēng现在还不用吃药，但是要少吃盐、多运动。Xiànzài hái búyòng chī yào, dànshì yào shǎo chī yán, duō yùndòng.No medication yet, but eat less salt and exercise more.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng好的，我一定改改生活习惯。Hǎo de, wǒ yídìng gǎigai shēnghuó xíguàn.OK, I'll definitely change my habits.

2. Annual check-up

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì公司下周组织体检，要空腹去。Gōngsī xià zhōu zǔzhī tǐjiǎn, yào kōngfù qù.The company is arranging check-ups next week; you have to go on an empty stomach.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà那早上连水也不能喝吗？Nà zǎoshang lián shuǐ yě bù néng hē ma?So not even water in the morning?
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì可以喝一点儿水，但是不能吃早饭。Kěyǐ hē yìdiǎnr shuǐ, dànshì bù néng chī zǎofàn.A little water is fine, but no breakfast.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà好，检查完我们一起去吃早餐吧。Hǎo, jiǎnchá wán wǒmen yìqǐ qù chī zǎocān ba.OK. After the tests, let's get breakfast together.

Asking for a raise

1. Talking to the boss

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng经理，我在公司已经工作三年了，想跟您谈谈工资的事。Jīnglǐ, wǒ zài gōngsī yǐjīng gōngzuò sān nián le, xiǎng gēn nín tántan gōngzī de shì.Manager, I've worked here three years; I'd like to talk about my salary.
  2. 经理 · jīnglǐ你说吧。Nǐ shuō ba.Go ahead.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这两年我负责的项目都完成得很好，而且工作量增加了不少。Zhè liǎng nián wǒ fùzé de xiàngmù dōu wánchéng de hěn hǎo, érqiě gōngzuòliàng zēngjiā le bù shǎo.My projects over the past two years have gone well, and my workload has grown a lot.
  4. 经理 · jīnglǐ你的表现大家都看得到。我向上面申请一下，尽快给你答复。Nǐ de biǎoxiàn dàjiā dōu kàn de dào. Wǒ xiàng shàngmiàn shēnqǐng yíxià, jǐnkuài gěi nǐ dáfù.Everyone can see your performance. I'll put in a request and get back to you soon.

2. A job offer

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng另一家公司给我开的工资比现在高百分之三十。Lìng yì jiā gōngsī gěi wǒ kāi de gōngzī bǐ xiànzài gāo bǎi fēn zhī sānshí.Another company has offered me thirty percent more than now.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那你准备跳槽吗？Nà nǐ zhǔnbèi tiàocáo ma?So are you going to switch?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng还在犹豫。现在的团队很好，我舍不得离开。Hái zài yóuyù. Xiànzài de tuánduì hěn hǎo, wǒ shěbude líkāi.I'm still hesitating. My current team is great; I'd hate to leave.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì钱不是唯一的标准，你要考虑发展机会。Qián bú shì wéiyī de biāozhǔn, nǐ yào kǎolǜ fāzhǎn jīhuì.Money isn't the only factor; think about career prospects.

Disagreements at work

1. Who's responsible?

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这次项目推迟了，经理说是我们组的问题。Zhè cì xiàngmù tuīchí le, jīnglǐ shuō shì wǒmen zǔ de wèntí.The project's been delayed, and the manager says it's our team's fault.

Cultural differences

1. Why so many questions?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà刚认识的人就问我多大、结婚没有、挣多少钱，我有点儿不习惯。Gāng rènshi de rén jiù wèn wǒ duō dà, jiéhūn méiyǒu, zhèng duōshao qián, wǒ yǒudiǎnr bù xíguàn.People I've just met ask my age, whether I'm married, how much I earn — I'm not used to it.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì在中国，这常常是表示关心，不是故意打听隐私。Zài Zhōngguó, zhè chángcháng shì biǎoshì guānxīn, bú shì gùyì dǎting yǐnsī.In China it's often a way of showing interest, not prying.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà原来如此。那我不想回答的时候怎么办？Yuánlái rúcǐ. Nà wǒ bù xiǎng huídá de shíhou zěnme bàn?I see. But what if I don't want to answer?
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì笑一笑，换个话题就行了。Xiào yi xiào, huàn ge huàtí jiù xíng le.Just smile and change the subject.

2. Splitting the bill

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng今天我请客，你别跟我抢。Jīntiān wǒ qǐngkè, nǐ bié gēn wǒ qiǎng.It's my treat today — don't fight me for it.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà在我们国家，朋友吃饭一般是AA制。Zài wǒmen guójiā, péngyou chī fàn yìbān shì AA zhì.In my country, friends usually split the bill.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng在中国，大家习惯轮流请客，这次我请，下次你请。Zài Zhōngguó, dàjiā xíguàn lúnliú qǐngkè, zhè cì wǒ qǐng, xià cì nǐ qǐng.In China, people take turns: I pay this time, you next time.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà那好，下次一定我来。Nà hǎo, xià cì yídìng wǒ lái.All right — next time it's definitely on me.

  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这就是全球变暖的结果。Zhè jiù shì quánqiú biàn nuǎn de jiéguǒ.That's the result of global warming.
