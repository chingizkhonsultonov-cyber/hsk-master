City and countryside

1. Moving back to the village

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我表哥辞掉了城里的工作，回老家搞起了农产品直播。Wǒ biǎogē cídiào le chéng li de gōngzuò, huí lǎojiā gǎo qǐ le nóngchǎnpǐn zhíbō.My cousin quit his city job and went home to sell farm produce via livestreams.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì现在这样的年轻人越来越多了，农村的机会其实不少。Xiànzài zhèyàng de niánqīngrén yuè lái yuè duō le, nóngcūn de jīhuì qíshí bù shǎo.There are more and more young people like that; the countryside actually has plenty of opportunities.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng他说虽然收入不稳定，但生活压力小多了，还能照顾父母。Tā shuō suīrán shōurù bù wěndìng, dàn shēnghuó yālì xiǎo duō le, hái néng zhàogù fùmǔ.He says his income is unstable, but life is far less stressful and he can look after his parents.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì关键是找到适合自己的生活方式。Guānjiàn shì zhǎo dào shìhé zìjǐ de shēnghuó fāngshì.The key is finding the way of life that suits you.

2. The price of city life

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà大城市机会多，可是房价高得离谱，通勤也要两个小时。Dà chéngshì jīhuì duō, kěshì fángjià gāo de lípǔ, tōngqín yě yào liǎng ge xiǎoshí.Big cities have opportunities, but housing is absurdly expensive and commuting takes two hours.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng所以不少人选择去二线城市发展，生活成本低，节奏也慢一些。Suǒyǐ bù shǎo rén xuǎnzé qù èrxiàn chéngshì fāzhǎn, shēnghuó chéngběn dī, jiézòu yě màn yìxiē.That's why many choose second-tier cities: lower costs and a slower pace.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà不过那里的工资和发展空间也有限。Búguò nàlǐ de gōngzī hé fāzhǎn kōngjiān yě yǒuxiàn.But salaries and career prospects there are limited too.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng有得必有失，就看你更看重什么了。Yǒu dé bì yǒu shī, jiù kàn nǐ gèng kànzhòng shénme le.Every gain has a cost; it depends what you value more.

Online privacy

1. How did they know?

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我刚跟朋友聊到想买跑步机，手机上就推送了广告，太可怕了。Wǒ gāng gēn péngyou liáo dào xiǎng mǎi pǎobùjī, shǒujī shang jiù tuīsòng le guǎnggào, tài kěpà le.I'd just told a friend I wanted a treadmill, and ads popped up on my phone. Scary.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng很多应用会收集我们的数据，有时候授权的时候根本没注意。Hěn duō yìngyòng huì shōují wǒmen de shùjù, yǒushíhou shòuquán de shíhou gēnběn méi zhùyì.Lots of apps collect our data; we often grant permissions without noticing.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那怎么保护个人隐私呢？Nà zěnme bǎohù gèrén yǐnsī ne?So how do we protect our privacy?
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng定期检查权限，不需要的就关掉，也别随便点陌生链接。Dìngqī jiǎnchá quánxiàn, bù xūyào de jiù guān diào, yě bié suíbiàn diǎn mòshēng liànjiē.Check permissions regularly, switch off what's unnecessary, and don't click unknown links.

2. A scam call

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng刚才有人打电话说我的银行卡被冻结了，让我把验证码告诉他。Gāngcái yǒu rén dǎ diànhuà shuō wǒ de yínhángkǎ bèi dòngjié le, ràng wǒ bǎ yànzhèngmǎ gàosu tā.Someone just called saying my bank card was frozen and asked me for the verification code.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà千万别说！银行不会在电话里要验证码，这肯定是骗子。Qiānwàn bié shuō! Yínháng bú huì zài diànhuà li yào yànzhèngmǎ, zhè kěndìng shì piànzi.Don't tell them! Banks never ask for codes by phone — that's definitely a scammer.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng幸亏我多了个心眼儿，直接挂了。Xìngkuī wǒ duō le ge xīnyǎnr, zhíjiē guà le.Luckily I was suspicious and hung up.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà做得对，最好再向警方举报这个号码。Zuò de duì, zuìhǎo zài xiàng jǐngfāng jǔbào zhège hàomǎ.Good call. Better report the number to the police too.

Freelancing

1. Being your own boss

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà我现在做自由翻译，时间完全由自己安排。Wǒ xiànzài zuò zìyóu fānyì, shíjiān wánquán yóu zìjǐ ānpái.I'm a freelance translator now; I set my own hours entirely.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì听起来很自由，但收入稳定吗？Tīng qǐlai hěn zìyóu, dàn shōurù wěndìng ma?Sounds free, but is the income stable?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà忙的时候接不过来，闲的时候一个月都没活儿。Máng de shíhou jiē bu guòlai, xián de shíhou yí ge yuè dōu méi huór.When it's busy I can't keep up; when it's quiet, there's no work for a month.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì那社保和医疗保险你得自己交，别忽视了。Nà shèbǎo hé yīliáo bǎoxiǎn nǐ děi zìjǐ jiāo, bié hūshì le.Then you have to pay social and health insurance yourself — don't neglect it.

2. Delivery riders

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng外卖骑手为了准时，经常闯红灯，太危险了。Wàimài qíshǒu wèile zhǔnshí, jīngcháng chuǎng hóngdēng, tài wēixiǎn le.Delivery riders often run red lights to be on time — it's so dangerous.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这背后是平台的算法压力，时间被压缩得越来越短。Zhè bèihòu shì píngtái de suànfǎ yālì, shíjiān bèi yāsuō de yuè lái yuè duǎn.Behind it is the platform's algorithm squeezing delivery times shorter and shorter.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng作为顾客，我们也可以多一点儿耐心。Zuòwéi gùkè, wǒmen yě kěyǐ duō yìdiǎnr nàixīn.As customers, we could be a bit more patient too.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng对，晚几分钟不是什么大事，安全更重要。Duì, wǎn jǐ fēnzhōng bú shì shénme dà shì, ānquán gèng zhòngyào.Right — a few minutes late is no big deal; safety matters more.

Mental health

1. Feeling burned out

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì最近我对什么都提不起兴趣，每天上班像在应付。Zuìjìn wǒ duì shénme dōu tí bu qǐ xìngqù, měi tiān shàngbān xiàng zài yìngfu.Lately nothing interests me; I'm just going through the motions at work.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà听起来你可能有点儿职业倦怠，要不要请几天假休息？Tīng qǐlai nǐ kěnéng yǒudiǎnr zhíyè juàndài, yào bu yào qǐng jǐ tiān jià xiūxi?Sounds like burnout. Why not take a few days off?
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我怕别人觉得我太脆弱。Wǒ pà biérén juéde wǒ tài cuìruò.I'm afraid people will think I'm weak.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà照顾好自己的情绪不是脆弱。如果持续下去，也可以找心理咨询师聊聊。Zhàogù hǎo zìjǐ de qíngxù bú shì cuìruò. Rúguǒ chíxù xiàqu, yě kěyǐ zhǎo xīnlǐ zīxúnshī liáoliao.Looking after your mental health isn't weakness. If it continues, talk to a counsellor.

2. Supporting a friend

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng小陈失恋以后一直不出门，我们该怎么帮他？Xiǎo Chén shīliàn yǐhòu yìzhí bù chūmén, wǒmen gāi zěnme bāng tā?Xiao Chen hasn't left home since his break-up. How can we help him?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng先别急着讲道理，多听他说就好。Xiān bié jí zhe jiǎng dàolǐ, duō tīng tā shuō jiù hǎo.Don't rush to lecture him; just listen.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng要不周末约他出去走走，换换心情？Yàobù zhōumò yuē tā chūqu zǒuzou, huànhuan xīnqíng?How about asking him out this weekend for a change of scene?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng可以，但别勉强他，让他知道我们一直都在。Kěyǐ, dàn bié miǎnqiǎng tā, ràng tā zhīdào wǒmen yìzhí dōu zài.Sure, but don't push him; let him know we're always here.

Cultural heritage

1. Saving old crafts

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà您做剪纸做了多少年了？Nín zuò jiǎnzhǐ zuò le duōshao nián le?How many years have you been making paper-cuts?
  2. 老艺人 · lǎo yìrén五十多年了。可惜现在愿意学的年轻人越来越少。Wǔshí duō nián le. Kěxī xiànzài yuànyì xué de niánqīngrén yuè lái yuè shǎo.Over fifty years. Sadly fewer and fewer young people want to learn.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我觉得可以通过短视频让更多人了解这门手艺。Wǒ juéde kěyǐ tōngguò duǎn shìpín ràng gèng duō rén liǎojiě zhè mén shǒuyì.I think short videos could introduce the craft to more people.
  4. 老艺人 · lǎo yìrén我孙女也这么说，她帮我拍的视频还真有不少人看。Wǒ sūnnǚ yě zhème shuō, tā bāng wǒ pāi de shìpín hái zhēn yǒu bù shǎo rén kàn.My granddaughter says the same; the videos she made of me actually got lots of views.

2. Rebuilding old streets

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng老街改造以后，商店都一样，失去了原来的味道。Lǎo jiē gǎizào yǐhòu, shāngdiàn dōu yíyàng, shīqù le yuánlái de wèidao.Since the old street was redeveloped, the shops are all the same; it's lost its character.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì保护和开发确实很难平衡。Bǎohù hé kāifā quèshí hěn nán pínghéng.Balancing preservation and development is genuinely hard.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我认为应该让原来的居民留下来，文化是靠人传下去的。Wǒ rènwéi yīnggāi ràng yuánlái de jūmín liú xiàlai, wénhuà shì kào rén chuán xiàqu de.I think the original residents should be allowed to stay; culture is passed on by people.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这个观点我很赞同。Zhège guāndiǎn wǒ hěn zàntóng.I fully agree with that.

Living with less

1. Decluttering

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì我最近开始断舍离，扔掉了一半的衣服。Wǒ zuìjìn kāishǐ duànshělí, rēng diào le yíbàn de yīfu.I've started decluttering and got rid of half my clothes.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà舍得吗？Shěde ma?Wasn't it hard to part with them?
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì一开始舍不得，后来发现很多根本没穿过，留着只是占地方。Yì kāishǐ shěbude, hòulái fāxiàn hěn duō gēnběn méi chuān guo, liú zhe zhǐshì zhàn dìfang.At first, yes, but I found lots I'd never worn; they were just taking up space.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà东西少了，心里也轻松了吧？Dōngxi shǎo le, xīn li yě qīngsōng le ba?Fewer things, lighter mind?

2. Need or want?

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng每次想买东西前，我都问自己：是需要还是想要？Měi cì xiǎng mǎi dōngxi qián, wǒ dōu wèn zìjǐ: shì xūyào háishi xiǎng yào?Before buying anything, I ask myself: do I need it or just want it?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng这个方法好，能避免冲动消费。Zhège fāngfǎ hǎo, néng bìmiǎn chōngdòng xiāofèi.Good method — it avoids impulse buying.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng而且省下来的钱可以用在旅行和学习上。Érqiě shěng xiàlai de qián kěyǐ yòng zài lǚxíng hé xuéxí shang.And the money saved can go on travel and learning.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng把钱花在体验上，比花在东西上更有意义。Bǎ qián huā zài tǐyàn shang, bǐ huā zài dōngxi shang gèng yǒu yìyì.Spending on experiences is more meaningful than on things.

Equality at work

1. An awkward interview question

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà面试的时候，对方问我打算什么时候要孩子。Miànshì de shíhou, duìfāng wèn wǒ dǎsuàn shénme shíhou yào háizi.At the interview they asked when I'm planning to have children.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这种问题本来就不应该问，跟能力没有关系。Zhè zhǒng wèntí běnlái jiù bù yīnggāi wèn, gēn nénglì méiyǒu guānxi.That question shouldn't be asked at all; it has nothing to do with ability.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我当时不知道该怎么回答。Wǒ dāngshí bù zhīdào gāi zěnme huídá.I didn't know how to answer at the time.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì可以礼貌地说，你更希望讨论和工作相关的问题。Kěyǐ lǐmào de shuō, nǐ gèng xīwàng tǎolùn hé gōngzuò xiāngguān de wèntí.You can politely say you'd rather discuss work-related matters.

2. Paternity leave

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我们公司开始给爸爸们放育儿假了。Wǒmen gōngsī kāishǐ gěi bàbamen fàng yù'érjià le.Our company has started giving fathers parental leave.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这是好事，照顾孩子本来就是父母双方的责任。Zhè shì hǎo shì, zhàogù háizi běnlái jiù shì fùmǔ shuāngfāng de zérèn.Good — caring for children is both parents' responsibility.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng不过有些同事担心请假会影响升职。Búguò yǒuxiē tóngshì dānxīn qǐngjià huì yǐngxiǎng shēngzhí.Though some colleagues worry taking leave will hurt their promotion chances.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng制度有了，观念的改变还需要时间。Zhìdù yǒu le, guānniàn de gǎibiàn hái xūyào shíjiān.The policy's there; changing attitudes still takes time.

Lifelong learning

1. Back to school at sixty

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì奶奶，听说您报了老年大学的书法班？Nǎinai, tīngshuō nín bào le lǎonián dàxué de shūfǎ bān?Grandma, I hear you've joined a calligraphy class at the senior university?
  2. 奶奶 · nǎinai是啊，年轻的时候没机会学，现在终于有时间了。Shì a, niánqīng de shíhou méi jīhuì xué, xiànzài zhōngyú yǒu shíjiān le.Yes — I never had the chance when I was young; now I finally have time.
  3. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì您真是活到老，学到老！Nín zhēn shì huó dào lǎo, xué dào lǎo!You really are a lifelong learner!
  4. 奶奶 · nǎinai学东西让我觉得自己还很年轻。Xué dōngxi ràng wǒ juéde zìjǐ hái hěn niánqīng.Learning makes me feel young.

2. New skills for a new job

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng行业变化太快了，我大学学的东西很多已经过时了。Hángyè biànhuà tài kuài le, wǒ dàxué xué de dōngxi hěn duō yǐjīng guòshí le.The industry changes so fast; much of what I learned at university is outdated.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà所以要不断更新自己的知识。Suǒyǐ yào búduàn gēngxīn zìjǐ de zhīshi.So you have to keep updating your knowledge.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng我报了一个数据分析的网课，每天晚上学一小时。Wǒ bào le yí ge shùjù fēnxī de wǎngkè, měi tiān wǎnshang xué yì xiǎoshí.I've signed up for an online data analysis course; an hour every evening.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà坚持下去，一定会有收获。Jiānchí xiàqu, yídìng huì yǒu shōuhuò.Keep at it; it'll pay off.

Doctors and patients

1. Searching symptoms online

  1. 医生 · yīshēng你别太相信网上查的，每个人的情况都不一样。Nǐ bié tài xiāngxìn wǎng shang chá de, měi ge rén de qíngkuàng dōu bù yíyàng.Don't trust what you find online too much; every case is different.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng可是网上说这个症状可能很严重，我一晚上没睡着。Kěshì wǎng shang shuō zhège zhèngzhuàng kěnéng hěn yánzhòng, wǒ yì wǎnshang méi shuì zháo.But online it said this symptom could be serious; I couldn't sleep all night.
  3. 医生 · yīshēng从检查结果看，只是普通的炎症，按时吃药就行。Cóng jiǎnchá jiéguǒ kàn, zhǐshì pǔtōng de yánzhèng, ànshí chī yào jiù xíng.From your tests, it's just ordinary inflammation; take your medicine on time.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng听您这么说，我就放心了。Tīng nín zhème shuō, wǒ jiù fàngxīn le.That's a relief to hear.

2. Long waits at hospitals

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà大医院挂号要排很久，看病只有几分钟。Dà yīyuàn guàhào yào pái hěn jiǔ, kànbìng zhǐ yǒu jǐ fēnzhōng.At big hospitals you queue for ages to register and see the doctor for a few minutes.
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì优质医疗资源太集中了，小医院又不太受信任。Yōuzhì yīliáo zīyuán tài jízhōng le, xiǎo yīyuàn yòu bú tài shòu xìnrèn.Good medical resources are too concentrated, and people don't trust small hospitals.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà现在推广的家庭医生制度也许能缓解这个问题。Xiànzài tuīguǎng de jiātíng yīshēng zhìdù yěxǔ néng huǎnjiě zhège wèntí.The family doctor system being rolled out may ease this.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì希望如此，小病在社区解决就好了。Xīwàng rúcǐ, xiǎo bìng zài shèqū jiějué jiù hǎo le.Hopefully; minor ailments should be handled in the community.

Fake news

1. Is it true?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng群里有人转发说喝某种茶能预防癌症，是真的吗？Qún li yǒu rén zhuǎnfā shuō hē mǒu zhǒng chá néng yùfáng áizhèng, shì zhēn de ma?Someone in the group chat forwarded a post saying a certain tea prevents cancer. True?
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng一看就是谣言，没有任何科学依据。Yí kàn jiù shì yáoyán, méiyǒu rènhé kēxué yījù.Obviously a rumour; there's no scientific basis.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng可是我爸妈已经信了，还买了好几盒。Kěshì wǒ bàmā yǐjīng xìn le, hái mǎi le hǎo jǐ hé.But my parents believe it and bought several boxes.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你可以找权威机构的辟谣文章发给他们看。Nǐ kěyǐ zhǎo quánwēi jīgòu de pìyáo wénzhāng fā gěi tāmen kàn.Find a debunking article from an authoritative source and send it to them.

2. Checking sources

  1. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì现在信息太多，真假难辨。Xiànzài xìnxī tài duō, zhēn jiǎ nán biàn.There's so much information now, it's hard to tell true from false.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà我的习惯是看消息来源，再找两三个不同的媒体对比一下。Wǒ de xíguàn shì kàn xiāoxi láiyuán, zài zhǎo liǎng sān ge bù tóng de méitǐ duìbǐ yíxià.I check the source, then compare with two or three different outlets.

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng这幅山水画留了这么多空白，有什么讲究吗？Zhè fú shānshuǐhuà liú le zhème duō kòngbái, yǒu shénme jiǎngjiu ma?This landscape painting leaves so much blank space. Is there a reason?
  2. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这叫“留白”，给观众留下想象的空间。Zhè jiào “liúbái”, gěi guānzhòng liú xià xiǎngxiàng de kōngjiān.It's called “leaving white”; it gives viewers room to imagine.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng怪不得看久了，觉得空白处好像有云雾。Guàibude kàn jiǔ le, juéde kòngbáichù hǎoxiàng yǒu yúnwù.No wonder — the longer I look, the more the empty part seems full of mist.
  4. 张丽 · Zhāng Lì这正是中国画追求的意境。Zhè zhèng shì Zhōngguóhuà zhuīqiú de yìjìng.That's exactly the mood Chinese painting aims for.
