    Morphemes语素2
    • Prefix-like morphemes 类前缀超—、多—、反—、无—、亚—、准—Prefixes 超, 多, 反, 无, 亚, 准

      超市 · 多功能 · 反作用 · 无线 · 亚军 · 准决赛

      超 super, beyond: 超市, 超大. 多 multi-: 多功能. 反 anti-, counter-: 反作用. 无 without, -less: 无线 wireless. 亚 second, sub-: 亚军 runner-up. 准 quasi-, almost: 准决赛 semi-final.

      When to use it

      Prefixes: 超 super- (超市, 超大), 多 multi- (多功能), 反 anti- (反对, 反作用), 无 without (无线), 亚 sub-/second (亚洲, 亚军), 准 quasi-/to-be (准新娘).

      • 这是一台多功能的机器。Zhè shì yì tái duō gōngnéng de jīqì.This is a multi-purpose machine.
      • 他们队得了亚军。Tāmen duì déle yàjūn.Their team came second.
      • 这是一款多功能手机。Zhè shì yì kuǎn duō gōngnéng shǒujī.This is a multi-function phone.
      • 他们队获得了亚军。Tāmen duì huòdé le yàjūn.Their team came second.
    • Suffix-like morphemes 类后缀—化、—式、—型、—性Suffixes 化, 式, 型, 性

      现代化 · 中式 · 大型 · 重要性

      化 -ise, -ification: 现代化 modernisation. 式 style: 中式 Chinese-style. 型 type, size: 大型 large-scale. 性 -ness, -ity: 重要性 importance.

      When to use it

      Suffixes: 化 -ise/-isation (现代化), 式 style (中式), 型 type/model (大型), 性 -ness/-ity (重要性).

      • 这是一家中式饭店。Zhè shì yì jiā Zhōngshì fàndiàn.This is a Chinese-style restaurant.
      • 大家都明白这件事的重要性。Dàjiā dōu míngbai zhè jiàn shì de zhòngyàoxìng.Everyone understands the importance of this.
      • 我们要认识到学习的重要性。Wǒmen yào rènshi dào xuéxí de zhòngyàoxìng.We must realise the importance of study.
      • 城市越来越现代化了。Chéngshì yuè lái yuè xiàndàihuà le.The city is becoming more and more modern.

      Compare

      化 turns a noun or adjective into a verb of change: 绿化 make green.

    Parts of speech词类18
    • Pronouns 代词Personal pronouns 人称代词人家人家: others, or I

      人家 = other people / a certain person / I

      人家 can mean other people (人家都去了), a specific person already mentioned, or, said playfully or sulkily, I myself (人家不想去嘛).

      When to use it

      人家 = other people; also a coy “I/me” (said by women and children); or a named third person.

      • 你看人家，多努力！Nǐ kàn rénjia, duō nǔlì!Look at others, how hard they work!
      • 人家不想去嘛。Rénjia bù xiǎng qù ma.But I don't want to go!
      • 人家都走了，你怎么还在这儿？Rénjia dōu zǒu le, nǐ zěnme hái zài zhèr?Everyone else has left; why are you still here?
      • 你别笑人家嘛！Nǐ bié xiào rénjia ma!Don't laugh at me!

      Compare

      别人 just means others; 人家 can also refer to the speaker playfully.

    • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词专用名量词：串、滴、枝、扇、卷1、卷2Measure words 串, 滴, 枝, 扇, 卷

      一串 · 一滴 · 一枝 · 一扇 · 一卷

      串 strings and bunches (keys, grapes). 滴 drops. 枝 stems, branches. 扇 doors and windows. 卷 rolls or volumes (juǎn / juàn).

      When to use it

      串 bunches/strings (一串葡萄), 滴 drops (一滴水), 枝 stems (一枝花), 扇 doors and windows (一扇门), 卷 rolls (一卷纸) or volumes (第一卷).

      • 我买了一串葡萄。Wǒ mǎile yí chuàn pútao.I bought a bunch of grapes.
      • 他一滴酒也不喝。Tā yì dī jiǔ yě bù hē.He doesn't drink a drop of alcohol.
      • 桌上放着一串钥匙。Zhuō shang fàng zhe yí chuàn yàoshi.There's a bunch of keys on the table.
      • 他推开了那扇窗户。Tā tuī kāi le nà shàn chuānghu.He pushed open that window.
    • Measure words 量词Verbal measure words 动量词番番: a round of effort

      verb + 一番

      番 counts actions that take time and effort: 研究了一番 studied it carefully, 打扮一番 get dressed up.

      When to use it

      番 counts rounds of effort or speech, usually with 一: 一番话, 努力一番; 一番 also means a kind (别有一番风味).

      • 他认真地研究了一番。Tā rènzhēn de yánjiūle yì fān.He studied it carefully.
      • 经过一番努力，他终于成功了。Jīngguò yì fān nǔlì, tā zhōngyú chénggōng le.After much effort, he finally succeeded.

      Compare

      番 is written and implies effort; 次 is neutral.

    • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词特、异常、较为特, 异常, 较为

      特 · 异常 · 较为 + adjective

      特 especially, very (written for 特别). 异常 unusually, extremely. 较为 relatively, comparatively (formal).

      When to use it

      特 = especially (spoken, 特好); 异常 = unusually (written); 较为 = relatively (written).

      • 今天异常冷。Jīntiān yìcháng lěng.It's unusually cold today.
      • 这个办法较为简单。Zhège bànfǎ jiàowéi jiǎndān.This method is relatively simple.
      • 今天天气异常炎热。Jīntiān tiānqì yìcháng yánrè.It's unusually hot today.
      • 这个方法较为简单。Zhège fāngfǎ jiàowéi jiǎndān.This method is relatively simple.
    • Adverbs 副词Scope adverbs 范围副词净、一同、大都净, 一同, 大都

      净 (nothing but) · 一同 (together) · 大都 (mostly)

      净 nothing but, all, spoken: 净说废话. 一同 together (formal). 大都 mostly, for the most part.

      When to use it

      净 = nothing but (spoken, often complaining); 一同 = together (written); 大都 = mostly.

      • 你别净玩儿手机。Nǐ bié jìng wánr shǒujī.Don't spend all your time on your phone.
      • 来的人大都是学生。Lái de rén dàdōu shì xuésheng.Most of the people who came were students.
      • 你别净玩手机。Nǐ bié jìng wán shǒujī.Don't do nothing but play on your phone.
      • 来参加的大都是年轻人。Lái cānjiā de dàdōu shì niánqīngrén.Most of those attending were young people.
    • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词一时、不时、仍旧、依旧、一向一时, 不时, 仍旧, 一向

      一时 · 不时 · 仍旧 / 依旧 · 一向

      一时 for the moment, temporarily: 一时想不起来. 不时 from time to time. 仍旧 and 依旧 still, as before. 一向 always, consistently.

      When to use it

      一时 = for a moment, temporarily; 不时 = from time to time; 仍旧/依旧 = still, as before; 一向 = always (all along).

      • 我一时想不起他的名字。Wǒ yìshí xiǎng bu qǐ tā de míngzi.I can't recall his name just now.
      • 他一向很认真。Tā yíxiàng hěn rènzhēn.He has always been conscientious.
      • 他的名字我一时想不起来了。Tā de míngzi wǒ yìshí xiǎng bu qǐlai le.I can't remember his name right now.
      • 他一向很守时。Tā yíxiàng hěn shǒushí.He has always been punctual.

      Compare

      一时想不起来 = can't recall right now; 一向 describes a long-standing habit.

    • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词一再、再三一再 and 再三: again and again

      一再 / 再三 + verb

      Both mean repeatedly, over and over.

      When to use it

      一再/再三 = again and again, repeatedly.

      • 我再三提醒他，他还是忘了。Wǒ zàisān tíxǐng tā, tā háishi wàng le.I reminded him again and again, but he still forgot.
      • 我再三提醒他，他还是忘了。Wǒ zàisān tíxǐng tā, tā háishi wàng le.I reminded him again and again, but he still forgot.

      Compare

      再三 can follow the verb (考虑再三); 一再 cannot.

    • Adverbs 副词Manner adverbs 方式副词不禁、赶忙、特地、特意、偷偷不禁, 赶忙, 特地, 偷偷

      不禁 · 赶忙 · 特地 / 特意 · 偷偷 + verb

      不禁 can't help doing. 赶忙 hurriedly. 特地 and 特意 specially, on purpose. 偷偷 secretly, stealthily.

      When to use it

      不禁 = can't help (doing); 赶忙 = hurriedly; 特地/特意 = specially, on purpose; 偷偷 = secretly.

      • 听了这个故事，她不禁哭了。Tīngle zhège gùshi, tā bùjīn kū le.Hearing the story, she couldn't help crying.
      • 这是我特地为你买的。Zhè shì wǒ tèdì wèi nǐ mǎi de.I bought this specially for you.
      • 看到这里，我不禁笑了起来。Kàn dào zhèlǐ, wǒ bùjīn xiào le qǐlai.Reading this, I couldn't help laughing.
      • 他特地从上海来看我。Tā tèdì cóng Shànghǎi lái kàn wǒ.He came all the way from Shanghai specially to see me.
    • Adverbs 副词Negation adverbs 否定副词未必未必: not necessarily

      未必 + verb / adjective

      未必 means not necessarily, may not be.

      When to use it

      未必 = not necessarily.

      • 贵的东西未必好。Guì de dōngxi wèibì hǎo.Expensive things aren't necessarily good.
      • 贵的东西未必就好。Guì de dōngxi wèibì jiù hǎo.Expensive things aren't necessarily good.

      Compare

      未必 = 不一定, but more written.

    • Adverbs 副词Modal (attitude) adverbs 语气副词恰好、倒是、干脆、明明、总算恰好, 倒是, 干脆, 明明, 总算

      adverb + verb / sentence

      恰好 just, by chance exactly. 倒是 actually, contrary to expectation, or softening. 干脆 simply, might as well. 明明 obviously, clearly (yet). 总算 at long last, finally.

      When to use it

      恰好 = just, by chance; 倒是 = actually (contrast); 干脆 = simply, just (decisive); 明明 = clearly (yet…); 总算 = at last.

      • 他明明知道，却说不知道。Tā míngmíng zhīdào, què shuō bù zhīdào.He clearly knows, yet says he doesn't.
      • 太晚了，干脆别回去了。Tài wǎn le, gāncuì bié huíqu le.It's too late, you might as well not go back.
      • 问题总算解决了。Wèntí zǒngsuàn jiějué le.The problem is finally solved.
      • 他明明在家，却说不在。Tā míngmíng zài jiā, què shuō bú zài.He's clearly at home, yet says he isn't.
      • 太晚了，干脆别回去了。Tài wǎn le, gāncuì bié huíqu le.It's too late; just don't go back.
      • 等了一个小时，车总算来了。Děng le yí ge xiǎoshí, chē zǒngsuàn lái le.After an hour's wait, the bus finally came.

      Compare

      明明 is followed by a contrary clause: 明明知道，却不说.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing time or place 引出时间、处所于于: at, in (written)

      verb + 于 + time / place

      于 is a written 在, often after the verb: 生于1990年 born in 1990, 位于 located in.

      When to use it

      于 = at, in, on (written; time or place): 生于1990年, 位于北京.

      • 他生于一九九零年。Tā shēng yú yī jiǔ jiǔ líng nián.He was born in 1990.
      • 长城位于中国北部。Chángchéng wèiyú Zhōngguó běibù.The Great Wall is in northern China.

      Compare

      于 can follow the verb (位于, 生于); 在 normally goes before it.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象至于至于: as for

      A …，至于 B，…

      至于 turns to another, related topic: as for, as to.

      When to use it

      至于 = as for (switching to a new topic).

      • 我先去，至于他去不去，我不知道。Wǒ xiān qù, zhìyú tā qù bu qù, wǒ bù zhīdào.I'll go first; as for whether he goes, I don't know.
      • 我先去，至于你，明天再说。Wǒ xiān qù, zhìyú nǐ, míngtiān zài shuō.I'll go first; as for you, we'll see tomorrow.

      Compare

      关于 = about (the topic itself); 至于 = turning to another point.

    • Prepositions 介词Introducing purpose or cause 引出目的、原因因因: because of (written)

      因 + reason + verb

      因 is a written 因为, often before a noun: 因病请假 take leave due to illness.

      When to use it

      因 + reason = because of (written 因为).

      • 他因病没来上课。Tā yīn bìng méi lái shàngkè.He missed class because of illness.
      • 比赛因雨推迟。Bǐsài yīn yǔ tuīchí.The match was postponed because of rain.
    • Prepositions 介词Expressing exclusion 表示排除除除: except (written)

      除 + A + 外，…

      除……外 is a written 除了……以外.

      When to use it

      除 + item (+ 外/以外) = except, besides (written 除了).

      • 除他外，大家都同意。Chú tā wài, dàjiā dōu tóngyì.Everyone agreed except him.
      • 除星期日外，每天都开门。Chú xīngqīrì wài, měi tiān dōu kāimén.Open every day except Sunday.
    • Conjunctions 连词Joining clauses or sentences 连接分句或句子不料不料: unexpectedly

      A，不料 B

      不料 introduces something unexpected that happened.

      When to use it

      不料 = unexpectedly, who would have thought.

      • 我以为他会来，不料他病了。Wǒ yǐwéi tā huì lái, búliào tā bìng le.I thought he'd come, but unexpectedly he fell ill.
      • 我本想早点儿到，不料路上堵车了。Wǒ běn xiǎng zǎo diǎnr dào, búliào lù shang dǔ chē le.I meant to arrive early, but unexpectedly there was a traffic jam.
    • Particles 助词Structural particles 结构助词所所 + verb

      (subject +) 所 + verb + 的 (+ noun)

      所 before a verb in a modifier marks what is acted on, in formal style: 我所知道的 what I know, 他所说的话 the words he said.

      When to use it

      所 + verb turns it into a noun phrase (written): 所说的话 what was said; 为……所 + verb = passive.

      • 这就是我所知道的。Zhè jiù shì wǒ suǒ zhīdào de.This is all I know.
      • 这就是我所知道的一切。Zhè jiù shì wǒ suǒ zhīdào de yíqiè.This is everything I know.
      • 他的话为大家所接受。Tā de huà wéi dàjiā suǒ jiēshòu.His words were accepted by everyone.

      Compare

      所 is optional and formal: 我(所)知道的 = what I know.

    • Particles 助词Modal particles 语气助词啦、嘛啦 and 嘛

      sentence + 啦 / 嘛

      啦 is 了 + 啊, lively: 好啦! 嘛 says this is obvious, or makes a request softer and a bit insistent: 本来就是嘛.

      When to use it

      啦 = 了 + 啊 (lively); 嘛 = “it's obvious” or a soft request.

      • 别哭啦，没事儿。Bié kū la, méi shìr.Stop crying, it's fine.
      • 你就去嘛！Nǐ jiù qù ma!Oh go on, just go!
      • 我们到啦！Wǒmen dào la!We're here!
      • 有问题就问嘛。Yǒu wèntí jiù wèn ma.If you have a question, just ask.
    • Particles 助词Other particles 其他助词也好也好: fine, may as well

      A 也好，B 也好 · … 也好

      也好 accepts a situation as acceptable. Twice, it means whether A or B: 你去也好，不去也好.

      When to use it

      ……也好 = it's fine either way / it's just as well.

      • 不去也好，在家休息吧。Bú qù yěhǎo, zài jiā xiūxi ba.It's just as well not to go; rest at home.
      • 你来也好，不来也好，都告诉我一声。Nǐ lái yěhǎo, bù lái yěhǎo, dōu gàosu wǒ yì shēng.Whether you come or not, let me know.
      • 下雨了也好，可以在家休息。Xià yǔ le yěhǎo, kěyǐ zài jiā xiūxi.It's raining — just as well, I can rest at home.

      Compare

      A也好，B也好 = whether A or B.

    Phrases短语6
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格A这A那A这A那: this and that

      verb + 这 + verb + 那

      Describes doing all sorts of things, often fussing: 问这问那 ask about everything.

      When to use it

      A这A那 = A this and A that: listing many things, often complaining.

      • 孩子一直问这问那。Háizi yìzhí wèn zhè wèn nà.The child keeps asking about this and that.
      • 妈妈总是问这问那。Māma zǒngshì wèn zhè wèn nà.Mum is always asking about this and that.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Four-character expressions 四字格左A右B左A右B: over and over

      左 + verb + 右 + verb

      Means doing something repeatedly, from every side: 左想右想 think it over every which way.

      When to use it

      左A右B = doing something again and again: 左思右想 think over and over, 左看右看.

      • 我左等右等，他都没来。Wǒ zuǒ děng yòu děng, tā dōu méi lái.I waited and waited, but he never came.
      • 我左思右想，还是不明白。Wǒ zuǒ sī yòu xiǎng, háishi bù míngbai.I thought it over again and again but still didn't get it.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他好（不）容易好(不)容易: with great difficulty

      好容易 / 好不容易 + 才 + verb

      Both 好容易 and 好不容易 mean with great difficulty, finally. Usually followed by 才.

      When to use it

      好容易 and 好不容易 both mean “with great difficulty”.

      • 我好不容易才找到他家。Wǒ hǎo bù róngyì cái zhǎodào tā jiā.It took me ages to find his home.
      • 我好不容易才买到票。Wǒ hǎo bù róngyì cái mǎi dào piào.I managed to get a ticket only with great difficulty.

      Compare

      Here 不 doesn't negate: 好容易 = 好不容易.

      Exam note: Here 好容易 does not mean very easy.

    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他那倒（也）是那倒(也)是: that's true

      那倒 (也) 是

      Agrees with someone's point, sometimes reluctantly: well, that's true.

      When to use it

      那倒(也)是 = that's true, fair enough (conceding a point).

      • “太晚了，明天再说吧。”“那倒也是。”“Tài wǎn le, míngtiān zài shuō ba.” “Nà dào yě shì.”“It's too late, let's talk tomorrow.” “That's true.”
      • ——坐地铁比打车快。——那倒也是。— Zuò dìtiě bǐ dǎchē kuài. — Nà dào yě shì.“The subway is faster than a taxi.” “Fair enough.”
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他算了算了: forget it

      算了 (吧)

      算了 drops a matter: never mind, forget it, let it go.

      When to use it

      算了 = forget it, let it go.

      • 算了，我不去了。Suàn le, wǒ bú qù le.Forget it, I won't go.
      • 算了，别生气了。Suàn le, bié shēngqì le.Forget it, don't be angry.
    • Fixed phrases 固定短语Other 其他得了得了: enough

      得了 (吧) · … 就得了

      得了 (déle) stops something or agrees impatiently: that's enough, fine. 就得了 at the end: just … and that's it.

      When to use it

      得了 (déle) = that's enough, stop it; or OK, fine (agreeing impatiently).

      • 得了吧，你别说了。Déle ba, nǐ bié shuō le.Oh come on, stop talking.
      • 你告诉他就得了。Nǐ gàosu tā jiù déle.Just tell him and that's that.
      • 得了，别说了，我都知道。Dé le, bié shuō le, wǒ dōu zhīdào.Enough, stop talking; I know it all.
      • 得了，就这么办吧。Dé le, jiù zhème bàn ba.Fine, let's do it this way.

      Compare

      得了 (déle) ends talk; 得了 (deliǎo) after a verb means can manage (row in HSK 4).

    Set patterns固定格式13
    • A一+量词，B一+量词A一 + measure word，B一 + measure word

      verb A + 一 + measure word，verb B + 一 + measure word

      Describes scattered or alternating actions: 东一个西一个 one here, one there.

      When to use it

      A一 + MW，B一 + MW: dividing or sharing out: 你一个，我一个.

      • 他高一脚低一脚地走着。Tā gāo yì jiǎo dī yì jiǎo de zǒuzhe.He walked along unsteadily.
      • 苹果你一个，我一个，正好。Píngguǒ nǐ yí ge, wǒ yí ge, zhènghǎo.One apple for you, one for me — just right.
      • 大家你一句，我一句，讨论得很热烈。Dàjiā nǐ yí jù, wǒ yí jù, tǎolùn de hěn rèliè.Everyone chipped in, and the discussion was lively.
    • 东一A，西一A东一A，西一A: here and there

      东 + 一 + measure word，西 + 一 + measure word

      Things scattered untidily: 东一件西一件 clothes thrown here and there.

      When to use it

      东一A，西一A = scattered here and there.

      • 他的衣服东一件西一件的。Tā de yīfu dōng yí jiàn xī yí jiàn de.His clothes are thrown all over the place.
      • 他的衣服东一件，西一件，到处都是。Tā de yīfu dōng yí jiàn, xī yí jiàn, dàochù dōu shì.His clothes are scattered all over the place.
    • 到……为止到……为止: up to

      到 + time / point + 为止

      到……为止 marks an end point: up to now, until.

      When to use it

      到……为止 = up to, until (a point in time or a limit).

      • 到现在为止，一切都很顺利。Dào xiànzài wéizhǐ, yíqiè dōu hěn shùnlì.Up to now, everything has gone smoothly.
      • 今天就到这儿为止。Jīntiān jiù dào zhèr wéizhǐ.That's it for today.
      • 到目前为止，一切都很顺利。Dào mùqián wéizhǐ, yíqiè dōu hěn shùnlì.So far, everything has gone smoothly.
      • 今天就学到这儿为止。Jīntiān jiù xué dào zhèr wéizhǐ.Let's stop here for today.
    • X到Y头上来了X到Y头上来了: picking on Y

      verb + 到 + person + 头上来了

      Complains that something bad has now reached even me: 欺负到我头上来了 now you're bullying me too.

      When to use it

      X到Y头上来了 = X has gone as far as targeting Y (indignation).

      • 你怎么骗到我头上来了？Nǐ zěnme piàn dào wǒ tóu shang lai le?How could you try to fool even me?
      • 你骗到我头上来了！Nǐ piàn dào wǒ tóu shang lái le!You're even trying to cheat me now!
    • 不X不……，一X……不X不……，一X……: once you do

      不 + verb + 不知道，一 + verb + 吓一跳

      Before doing it you don't realise; once you do, you're surprised: 不看不知道，一看吓一跳.

      When to use it

      不X不……，一X…… = you don't realise until you X: 不看不知道，一看吓一跳.

      • 不看不知道，一看吓一跳。Bú kàn bù zhīdào, yí kàn xià yí tiào.You'd never know till you look, and then you get a shock.
      • 不比不知道，一比吓一跳。Bù bǐ bù zhīdào, yì bǐ xià yí tiào.You don't know until you compare — then you're shocked.
    • 好你个X好你个X: well, well, you X

      好你个 + noun / name

      A playful or angry scolding: 好你个小李 well, look at you, Xiao Li.

      When to use it

      好你个X = “why, you X!” (mock anger or surprise).

      • 好你个小张，怎么不早说？Hǎo nǐ ge Xiǎo Zhāng, zěnme bù zǎo shuō?Well, Xiao Zhang, why didn't you say so earlier?
      • 好你个小王，原来是你干的！Hǎo nǐ ge Xiǎo Wáng, yuánlái shì nǐ gàn de!Why, you Xiao Wang — so it was you!
    • 早（也）不X，晚（也）不X早不X，晚不X: of all times

      早 (也) 不 + verb，晚 (也) 不 + verb，就 …

      Complains that something happened at the worst possible moment.

      When to use it

      早(也)不X，晚(也)不X，偏偏…… = of all times, it had to happen now.

      • 早不下雨，晚不下雨，就今天下雨。Zǎo bú xià yǔ, wǎn bú xià yǔ, jiù jīntiān xià yǔ.It didn't rain before or after, it had to rain today of all days.
      • 早不来晚不来，偏偏这时候来。Zǎo bù lái wǎn bù lái, piānpiān zhè shíhou lái.Of all times, he had to come now.
    • 看/瞧+把+宾语（施事）+X得看把 + person + X得: look how X they are

      看 / 瞧 + 把 + person + adjective + 得

      An exclamation about how strongly someone is affected: 看把你高兴得 look how happy you are.

      When to use it

      看/瞧 + 把 + person + X得……: “look how X it made them!”

      • 看把你急得！Kàn bǎ nǐ jí de!Look how worked up you are!
      • 看把你高兴得！Kàn bǎ nǐ gāoxìng de!Look how happy you are!
      • 瞧把孩子累得，快让他休息吧。Qiáo bǎ háizi lèi de, kuài ràng tā xiūxi ba.Look how tired the child is; let him rest.
    • 放着X不Y放着X不Y: ignoring X

      放着 + A + 不 + verb，…

      Criticises someone for ignoring an obvious option: 放着好工作不做 leaving a good job undone.

      When to use it

      放着X不Y = has X available but won't use it (criticism).

      • 放着大路不走，非要走小路。Fàngzhe dà lù bù zǒu, fēi yào zǒu xiǎo lù.He ignores the main road and insists on the path.
      • 放着好好的工作不做，你想干什么？Fàng zhe hǎohǎo de gōngzuò bú zuò, nǐ xiǎng gàn shénme?You have a perfectly good job and won't do it — what do you want?
    • X了就X了，（没）有……X了就X了: what's done is done

      verb + 了就 + verb + 了，没什么 …

      Accepts something that has happened: 丢了就丢了 if it's lost, it's lost.

      When to use it

      X了就X了，(没)有…… = what's done is done; no big deal.

      • 丢了就丢了，没什么大不了的。Diū le jiù diū le, méi shénme dàbuliǎo de.If it's lost, it's lost, no big deal.
      • 丢了就丢了，有什么大不了的。Diū le jiù diū le, yǒu shénme dàbuliǎo de.So it's lost — it's no big deal.
    • 这/那也不X，那/这也不Y这也不X，那也不Y: picky

      这也不 + verb，那也不 + verb

      Describes someone refusing everything: 这也不吃，那也不吃 won't eat this or that.

      When to use it

      这也不X，那也不Y = won't do this, won't do that (fussy).

      • 这孩子这也不吃，那也不吃。Zhè háizi zhè yě bù chī, nà yě bù chī.This child won't eat anything.
      • 这也不吃，那也不吃，你到底想吃什么？Zhè yě bù chī, nà yě bù chī, nǐ dàodǐ xiǎng chī shénme?You won't eat this or that — what do you actually want?
    • X归X，Y归YX归X，Y归Y: separate matters

      A 归 A，B 归 B

      Keeps two things apart: 朋友归朋友，生意归生意 friendship is one thing, business another.

      When to use it

      X归X，Y归Y = X is X and Y is Y; keep them separate.

      • 朋友归朋友，生意归生意。Péngyou guī péngyou, shēngyi guī shēngyi.Friendship is one thing, business another.
      • 朋友归朋友，钱的事要说清楚。Péngyou guī péngyou, qián de shì yào shuō qīngchu.Friends are friends, but money must be made clear.

      Compare

      Similar to X是X，Y是Y (HSK 4), slightly more emphatic.

    • 看你X的/瞧他X的看你X的: look at you

      看 / 瞧 + 你 / 他 + adjective / verb + 的

      Comments on someone's state, often teasing: 看你高兴的 look how pleased you are.

      When to use it

      看你X的 / 瞧他X的 = look how X you are (teasing or criticising).

      • 瞧他累的，快让他休息吧。Qiáo tā lèi de, kuài ràng tā xiūxi ba.Look how tired he is, let him rest.
      • 看你急的，慢慢说。Kàn nǐ jí de, mànmān shuō.Look how worked up you are — slow down.

    • Complement 补语Degree complements 3 程度补语3动词/形容词+透+了透了: thoroughly

      verb / adjective + 透了

      透了 means completely, through and through, often negative: 坏透了 rotten, 湿透了 soaked.

      When to use it

      Verb/adj + 透了 = thoroughly, completely (often negative): 坏透了, 湿透了.

      • 衣服湿透了。Yīfu shī tòu le.My clothes are soaked through.
      • 这件事糟糕透了。Zhè jiàn shì zāogāo tòu le.This is a total mess.
      • 我全身都湿透了。Wǒ quánshēn dōu shī tòu le.I'm soaked through.
      • 这个人坏透了。Zhège rén huài tòu le.This person is rotten to the core.
    Sentence types句子的类型10
    • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型把 sentences 4 “把”字句4（1）主语（非生物体）+把+宾语+动词+其他成分把 with a non-living subject

      thing / event + 把 + person + verb + result

      The subject of a 把 sentence can be an event or thing that causes the result: 这件事把我累坏了 this wore me out.

      When to use it

      A non-living subject can use 把: 这场雨把衣服淋湿了 (the rain soaked the clothes).

      • 这场雨把我的衣服都打湿了。Zhè cháng yǔ bǎ wǒ de yīfu dōu dǎshī le.This rain soaked all my clothes.
      • 大风把树刮倒了。Dà fēng bǎ shù guā dǎo le.The strong wind blew the tree down.
    • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型把 sentences 4 “把”字句4（2）主语+把+宾语（施事）+动词+其他成分把 + the doer

      subject + 把 + person (who acts) + verb + result

      Sometimes the noun after 把 is the one who experiences or does the action: 把孩子哭醒了 made the child wake up crying; 跑了一天，把我累坏了.

      When to use it

      把 + the doer (not the object) + verb: the subject is a cause: 这件事把他急坏了.

      • 跑了一天，把我累坏了。Pǎole yì tiān, bǎ wǒ lèi huài le.After running around all day, I'm exhausted.
      • 这件事把他急坏了。Zhè jiàn shì bǎ tā jí huài le.This matter drove him frantic.
      • 一句话把大家都说笑了。Yí jù huà bǎ dàjiā dōu shuō xiào le.One remark made everyone laugh.
    • Complex sentences 复句Coordinate clauses 并列复句一时……一时……一时……一时: now … now

      一时 A，一时 B

      Things keep changing back and forth, like 一会儿……一会儿.

      When to use it

      一时……一时…… = one moment …, the next … (changing quickly).

      • 他一时高兴，一时难过。Tā yìshí gāoxìng, yìshí nánguò.One moment he's happy, the next sad.
      • 他一时哭，一时笑。Tā yìshí kū, yìshí xiào.One moment he cries, the next he laughs.
    • Complex sentences 复句Alternative clauses 选择复句要么……，要么……要么……要么: either … or

      要么 A，要么 B

      Offers two choices, one must be taken.

      When to use it

      要么……，要么…… = either … or … (choose one).

      • 要么你来，要么我去。Yàome nǐ lái, yàome wǒ qù.Either you come here or I go there.
      • 要么你去，要么我去。Yàome nǐ qù, yàome wǒ qù.Either you go or I go.

      Compare

      要么 offers a choice to make; 或是…或是… just lists possibilities.

    • Complex sentences 复句Contrast clauses 转折复句虽……，但/可/却/也……虽……但 / 却: although (written)

      subject + 虽 A，但 / 可 / 却 / 也 B

      A shorter written 虽然. 虽 must go after the subject.

      When to use it

      虽……，但/可/却/也…… = although (written 虽然).

      • 他虽年轻，却很有经验。Tā suī niánqīng, què hěn yǒu jīngyàn.Though young, he is very experienced.
      • 他虽年轻，但经验很丰富。Tā suī niánqīng, dàn jīngyàn hěn fēngfù.Although young, he's very experienced.

      Compare

      虽 must follow the subject: 他虽年轻，但…; 虽然 can come first.

    • Complex sentences 复句Conditional clauses 条件复句凡是……，都……凡是……都: all

      凡是 + group，都 …

      凡是 means every single one of a category, without exception.

      When to use it

      凡是……，都…… = all, every (without exception).

      • 凡是来过的人，都说这里好。Fánshì láiguo de rén, dōu shuō zhèlǐ hǎo.Everyone who has been here says it's great.
      • 凡是去过那儿的人都说好。Fánshì qù guo nàr de rén dōu shuō hǎo.Everyone who has been there says it's good.
    • Complex sentences 复句Conditional clauses 条件复句除非……，才……除非……才: only if

      除非 A，才 B

      除非 gives the only condition under which B happens.

      When to use it

      除非……，才…… = only if …, will ….

      • 除非你去，他才会去。Chúfēi nǐ qù, tā cái huì qù.He'll only go if you go.
      • 除非你亲自去，他才会同意。Chúfēi nǐ qīnzì qù, tā cái huì tóngyì.He'll agree only if you go in person.

      Compare

      除非 A，才 B = 只有 A，才 B.

    • Complex sentences 复句Conditional clauses 条件复句除非……，否则/不然……除非……否则: unless

      除非 A，否则 / 不然 B

      Unless A happens, B will follow.

      When to use it

      除非……，否则/不然…… = unless …, otherwise ….

      • 除非下大雨，否则比赛照常进行。Chúfēi xià dà yǔ, fǒuzé bǐsài zhàocháng jìnxíng.Unless it pours, the match goes ahead as planned.
      • 除非下大雨，否则比赛照常进行。Chúfēi xià dà yǔ, fǒuzé bǐsài zhàocháng jìnxíng.Unless it pours, the match goes ahead.
    • Complex sentences 复句Concessive clauses 让步复句就算……，也……就算……也: even if

      就算 A，(subject) 也 B

      A spoken even if, like 即使.

      When to use it

      就算……，也…… = even if (spoken 即使).

      • 就算你不同意，我也要去。Jiùsuàn nǐ bù tóngyì, wǒ yě yào qù.Even if you don't agree, I'm going.
      • 就算你不说，我也知道。Jiùsuàn nǐ bù shuō, wǒ yě zhīdào.Even if you don't say, I know.
    • Complex sentences 复句Purpose clauses 目的复句……，以便……以便: so that

      A，以便 B

      以便 gives the purpose of the first action, formal.

      When to use it

      ……，以便…… = so that, in order to (written).

      • 请留下电话，以便我们联系你。Qǐng liúxià diànhuà, yǐbiàn wǒmen liánxì nǐ.Please leave your number so that we can contact you.
      • 请留下电话，以便我们联系你。Qǐng liú xià diànhuà, yǐbiàn wǒmen liánxì nǐ.Please leave your number so we can contact you.

      Compare

      以便 introduces a convenient goal; 以免 = so as to avoid.
