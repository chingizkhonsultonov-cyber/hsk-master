  • 过时guò shíoutdatedTraditional: 過時
    Dictionary sensesold-fashioned; out of date; to be later than the time stipulated or agreed upon
    • 他的衣服过时了。tā de yī fu guò shí leHis clothes are out of fashion.
    • 长发现在过时了。cháng fā xiàn zài guò shí leLong hair is out of fashion now.
  • 路程lù chéngjourney, distance
    Dictionary sensesroute; path traveled; distance traveled; course (of development)
    • 坐公车从这里到机场只不过是十五分钟的路程。zuò gōng chē cóng zhè li dào jī chǎng zhī bù guò shì shí wǔ fēn zhōng de lù chéngIt's only a fifteen minute bus ride from here to the airport.

  • 成效chéng xiàoeffect, result
    Dictionary senseseffect; result
  • 众所周知zhòng suǒ zhōu zhīas everyone knowsTraditional: 眾所周知
    Dictionary senses(idiom) as everyone knows
    • 他的名字众所周知。tā de míng zi zhòng suǒ zhōu zhīEveryone knows his name.
    • 他的名字是众所周知的。tā de míng zi shì zhòng suǒ zhōu zhī deHis name is known to all.

    • 我匆忙地吃了午饭。wǒ cōng máng de chī le wǔ fànI ate lunch in a hurry.
  • 要素yào sùkey element
    Dictionary sensesessential factor; key constituent
    • 自信是伟大事业的第一要素。zì xìn shì wěi dà shì yè de dì yī yào sùSelf-confidence is the principal element of any great endeavor.
    • 所以，友情也是幸福的一个要素。suǒ yǐ yǒu qíng yě shì xìng fú de yī gè yào sùSo friendship is also a requirement for happiness.

  • 景观jǐng guānlandscapeTraditional: 景觀
    • 这里的自然景观的特点是山非常多，而且像画一样特别美。zhè li de zì rán jǐng guān de tè diǎn shì shān fēi cháng duō ér qiě xiàng huà yī yàng tè bié měiThe natural landscape is characterised by its large amount of mountains, and it looks as truly beautiful as a painting.
  • 淹没yān mòto submergeTraditional: 淹沒
    Dictionary sensesto drown; to flood; to drown out (also fig.)
    • 由于全球气候变暖，城市可能被完全地淹没。yóu yú quán qiú qì hòu biàn nuǎn chéng shì kě néng bèi wán quán de yān mòDue to global warming, cities could be completely submerged.
    • 住在海边的人可能失去他们的家，并且城市可能会被完全淹没。zhù zài hǎi biān de rén kě néng shī qù tā men de jiā bìng qiě chéng shì kě néng huì bèi wán quán yān mòPeople living on coastlines might lose their homes, and cities might be completely submerged.
  • 背心bèi xīnvest, sleeveless topMeasure word: 件
    Dictionary sensessleeveless garment (vest, waistcoat, singlet, tank top etc)
    • 背心反了，要翻一个面。bèi xīn fǎn le yào fān yī gè miànYou need to reverse your vest. It's inside-out!
    • 我找不到我三件套的背心。wǒ zhǎo bù dào wǒ sān jiàn tào de bèi xīnI can't find the waistcoat of my three piece suit.
