  • 当选dāng xuǎnto be electedTraditional: 當選
    Dictionary sensesto be selected
    • 谁将当选主席呢？shéi jiāng dāng xuǎn zhǔ xí neWho'll be elected chairman?
    • 每个人都希望他当选州长。měi gè rén dōu xī wàng tā dāng xuǎn zhōu chángEverybody wished he had been elected governor.
  • 散发sàn fāto give off; to distributeTraditional: 散發
    Dictionary sensesto emit; to issue
    • 这朵花散发出强烈的香味。zhè duǒ huā sàn fā chū qiáng liè de xiāng wèiThis flower gives off a strong fragrance.
  • 艺人yì rénperformerTraditional: 藝人
    Dictionary sensesperforming artist; actor
    • 谁是你最喜欢的乡村音乐艺人呢？shéi shì nǐ zuì xǐ huan de xiāng cūn yīn yuè yì rén neWho's your favorite country artist?
  • 出名chū míngfamous; to become famous
    Dictionary senseswell-known for sth; to become well known; to make one's mark; to lend one's name (to an event, endeavor etc)
    • 我不出名。wǒ bù chū míngI'm not famous.
    • 她想出名。tā xiǎng chū míngShe wanted to be famous.
  • 项链xiàng liànnecklaceMeasure word: 条Traditional: 項鏈
    • 这条项链是我奶奶送给我的。zhè tiáo xiàng liàn shì wǒ nǎi nai sòng gěi wǒ deMy nan gave me this necklace.
    • 我至今还珍惜你十年前给我的项链。wǒ zhì jīn hái zhēn xī nǐ shí nián qián gěi wǒ de xiàng liànI still cherish the necklace you gave me ten years ago.
  • 杰出jié chūoutstandingTraditional: 傑出
    Dictionary sensesdistinguished; remarkable; prominent; illustrious
  • 鼓舞gǔ wǔto inspire
    Dictionary sensesto encourage; to hearten; to boost (morale)
    • 那真令人鼓舞。nà zhēn lìng rén gǔ wǔThat's encouraging.
    • 我借由听音乐来鼓舞我自己。wǒ jiè yóu tīng yīn yuè lái gǔ wǔ wǒ zì jǐI cheered myself up by listening to music.
  • 抄chāoto copy
    Dictionary sensesto make a copy; to plagiarize; to search and seize; to raid
  • 棍gùnstick
    Dictionary sensesrod; truncheon; scoundrel; villain
    • 他用一根棍子把老鼠打死了。tā yòng yī gēn gùn zi bǎ lǎo shǔ dǎ sǐ leThe rat was killed by him with a stick.
  • 佩服pèi fúto admire
    • 我就是佩服你。wǒ jiù shì pèi fú nǐI'm simply amazed by you.
    • 我佩服他的勇气。wǒ pèi fú tā de yǒng qìI admire his courage.
  • 树立shù lìto establish, set upTraditional: 樹立
    Dictionary sensesto set up; to establish
    • 上海人要树立上海新形象。shàng hǎi rén yào shù lì shàng hǎi xīn xíng xiàngThe Shanghainese people must build up Shanghai's new image.
    • 你应该给你的孩子们树立好榜样。nǐ yīng gāi gěi nǐ de hái zi men shù lì hǎo bǎng yàngYou should set a good example for your children.
  • 妨碍fáng àito hinderTraditional: 妨礙
    Dictionary sensesto obstruct
  • 消耗xiāo hàoto consume
    Dictionary sensesto use up; to deplete; to expend; (old) news
    • 我们消耗了所有的自然资源。wǒ men xiāo hào le suǒ yǒu de zì rán zī yuánWe have consumed all the natural resources.
  • 以免yǐ miǎnso as to avoid
    Dictionary sensesin order to avoid; so as not to
    • 我们小声谈话以免被人听到。wǒ men xiǎo shēng tán huà yǐ miǎn bèi rén tīng dàoWe talked in a low voice so as not to be heard.
  • 平凡píng fánordinary
    Dictionary sensescommonplace; mediocre
    • 我们过着十分平凡的生活。wǒ men guò zhe shí fēn píng fán de shēng huóWe lead a very ordinary life.
  • 花生huā shēngpeanutMeasure word: 粒
    • 他对花生过敏吧？tā duì huā shēng guò mǐn baHe's allergic to peanuts, isn't he?
    • 这些花生长在温暖的国家。zhè xiē huā shēng cháng zài wēn nuǎn de guó jiāThese flowers grow in warm countries.
  • 听话tīng huàobedientTraditional: 聽話
    Dictionary sensesto do what one is told
    • 他很会教育人所以他的孩子都很听话。tā hěn huì jiào yù rén suǒ yǐ tā de hái zi dōu hěn tīng huàHe's a very good teacher, so his children really listen to him.
  • 言语yán yǔspeech, wordsTraditional: 言語
    Dictionary sensesto speak; to tell
    • 言语无法形容我有多爱你。yán yu wú fǎ xíng róng wǒ yǒu duō ài nǐWords cannot describe how much I love you.
    • 我难以用言语表达我的感情。wǒ nán yǐ yòng yán yu biǎo dá wǒ de gǎn qíngI find it hard to express my feelings in words.
  • 前景qián jǐngprospect
    Dictionary sensesforeground; vista; (future) prospects; perspective
  • 传真chuán zhēnfaxTraditional: 傳真
    Dictionary sensesto fax; a fax; (literary) to portray vividly in painting
    • 我不知道如何传真。wǒ bù zhī dào rú hé chuán zhēnI don't know how to send a fax.
    • 我前几天收到了你的传真。wǒ qián jǐ tiān shōu dào le nǐ de chuán zhēnI got your fax the other day.
  • 片刻piàn kèa moment
    Dictionary sensesshort period of time
  • 隐私yǐn sīprivacyTraditional: 隱私
    Dictionary sensessecrets; private business
  • 庞大páng dàhugeTraditional: 龐大
    Dictionary sensesenormous; tremendous
    • 观众人数庞大。guān zhòng rén shù páng dàThe audience was very large.
  • 极为jí wéiextremelyTraditional: 極為
    Dictionary sensesexceedingly
    • 电脑可以做极为复杂的工作。diàn nǎo kě yǐ zuò jí wéi fù zá de gōng zuòComputers are capable of doing extremely complicated work.
  • 糖果táng guǒsweets, candyMeasure word: 粒
    Dictionary sensescandy
    • 他们卖糖果。tā men mài táng guǒThey sell candy.
    • 他在吃糖果。tā zài chī táng guǒHe's eating sweets.
  • 智力zhì lìintelligence
    Dictionary sensesintellect
  • 液体yè tǐliquidTraditional: 液體
    • 水是液体。shuǐ shì yè tǐWater is liquid.
    • 水是液体。冻起来就成了固体。shuǐ shì yè tǐ dòng qǐ lai jiù chéng le gù tǐWater is liquid. When it freezes, it becomes solid.
  • 资本zī běncapitalTraditional: 資本
    Dictionary sensescapital (economics)
    • 我们需要资本。wǒ men xū yào zī běnWe need capital.
  • 券quànticket, coupon
    Dictionary sensesbond (esp. document split in two, with each party holding one half); contract; deed (i.e. title deeds); ticket
    • 可以给我优惠券吗？kě yǐ gěi wǒ yōu huì quàn maMay I have coupon tickets?
  • 误解wù jiěto misunderstandTraditional: 誤解
    Dictionary sensesmisunderstanding
    • 别误解我了。bié wù jiě wǒ leDon't get me wrong.
    • 他误解了我。tā wù jiě le wǒHe got me wrong.
