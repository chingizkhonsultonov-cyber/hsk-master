  • 被迫bèi pòto be forced
    Dictionary sensesto be compelled
    • 我被迫吃了药。wǒ bèi pò chī le yàoI was forced to take medicine.
    • 他被迫在文件上签字。tā bèi pò zài wén jiàn shàng qiān zìHe was forced to sign the document.
  • 争夺zhēng duóto fight overTraditional: 爭奪

  • 扫描sǎo miáoto scanTraditional: 掃描

    Dictionary sensesto flee from sth; to escape
    • 我试图逃跑。wǒ shì tú táo pǎoI tried to escape.
    • 我会帮你逃跑的。wǒ huì bāng nǐ táo pǎo deI'll help you escape.

  • 铜tóngcopperMeasure word: 块Traditional: 銅

  • 原始yuán shǐoriginal; primitive
    Dictionary sensesfirst; original (document etc)

  • 活跃huó yuèactive, livelyTraditional: 活躍

    • 他习惯在公众场合说话。tā xí guàn zài gōng zhòng chǎng hé shuō huàHe's used to speaking in public.
  • 收购shōu gòuto purchase, acquireTraditional: 收購

  • 串chuànto string together; string (measure word)
    Dictionary sensesto skewer; to connect wrongly; to gang up; to rove

    • 我喜欢吃烤羊肉串。wǒ xǐ huan chī kǎo yáng ròu chuànI like to eat lamb kebabs.
  • 横hénghorizontalTraditional: 橫
    Dictionary sensesacross; crosswise; horizontal stroke (in Chinese characters); to place (sth) flat (on a surface)
    • 横街的那端有一个幼儿园。héng jiē de nà duān yǒu yī gè yòu ér yuánThere is a kindergarten at the other end of the street.
    • 你从这里过去到那里，穿过第二条横马路就是。nǐ cóng zhè li guò qu dào nà li chuān guò dì èr tiáo héng mǎ lù jiù shìYou can get there by crossing the street at the second intersection.
