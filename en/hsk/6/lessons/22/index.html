  • 看好kàn hǎoto be optimistic about
    Dictionary sensesto regard as having good prospects
    • 把包包看好。bǎ bāo bāo kàn hǎoKeep an eye on the bags.
  • 眼光yǎn guāngvision, eye (judgement)
    Dictionary sensesgaze; insight; foresight; vision
    • 他很有审美眼光。tā hěn yǒu shěn měi yǎn guāngHe has an eye for the beautiful.
  • 占据zhàn jùto occupyTraditional: 佔據
    Dictionary sensesto hold
  • 夸张kuā zhāngto exaggerateTraditional: 誇張
    Dictionary sensesoverstated; exaggerated; hyperbole; (coll.) excessive
    • 不要再夸张了。bú yào zài kuā zhāng leDon't exaggerate.
    • 在我看来，她有一种夸张的倾向。zài wǒ kàn lai tā yǒu yī zhǒng kuā zhāng de qīng xiàngIt seems to me that she has a tendency to exaggerate.
  • 一流yì liúfirst-class
    Dictionary sensestop quality; front ranking
    • 他们这里提供一流的美食。tā men zhè li tí gōng yī liú de měi shíThey serve excellent food here.
  • 管道guǎn dàopipeline
    Dictionary sensestubing; (fig.) channel; means
  • 效应xiào yìngeffectTraditional: 效應
    Dictionary senseseffect (scientific phenomenon)
  • 射击shè jīshootingTraditional: 射擊
    Dictionary sensesto shoot; to fire (a gun)
  • 掏tāoto take out (from a pocket)
    Dictionary sensesto fish out (from pocket); to scoop
    • 男子从口袋中掏出枪，吓了我一跳。nán zǐ cóng kǒu dài zhōng tāo chū qiāng xià le wǒ yī tiàoTo my horror, the man took a gun out of his pocket.
  • 认可rèn kěto approveTraditional: 認可
    Dictionary sensesapproval; acknowledgment; OK
    • 翻译不代表认可。fān yì bù dài biǎo rèn kěA translation is not an endorsement.
    • 我不认可他的行为。wǒ bù rèn kě tā de xíng wéiI don't approve of his conduct.
  • 中毒zhòng dúto be poisoned
    • 那台电脑中毒了。nà tái diàn nǎo zhòng dú leThe computer has a virus.
    • 您曾经食物中毒过吗？nín céng jīng shí wù zhòng dú guò maHave you ever had food poisoning?
  • 家属jiā shǔfamily membersTraditional: 家屬
    Dictionary sensesfamily member; (family) dependent
  • 坚决jiān juéresoluteTraditional: 堅決
    Dictionary sensesfirm; determined
  • 外表wài biǎoappearance
    Dictionary sensesexternal; outside; outward appearance
    • 外表并不是全部。wài biǎo bìng bù shì quán bùAppearance isn't all.
    • 他的外表欺骗了我。tā de wài biǎo qī piàn le wǒHis appearance deceived me.
  • 十足shí zúcomplete, full
    Dictionary sensesample; complete; hundred percent; a pure shade (of some color)
  • 窗口chuāng kǒuwindow; counter
    Dictionary sensesopening providing restricted access (e.g. customer service window); computer operating system window; fig. medium; intermediary
    • 我从窗口爬进来的。wǒ cóng chuāng kǒu pá jìn lai deI climbed in through the window.
    • 站在窗口的是你们的老板吗？zhàn zài chuāng kǒu de shì nǐ men de lǎo bǎn maIs that your boss by the window?
  • 老实lǎo shihonestTraditional: 老實
    Dictionary sensessincere; well-behaved; naive; gullible
    • 我是个老实人。wǒ shì gè lǎo shi rénI'm an honest person.
    • 我觉得她很老实。wǒ jué de tā hěn lǎo shiI think that she's honest.
  • 势力shì lìinfluence, forceTraditional: 勢力
    Dictionary sensespower; influence; a force (military, political etc)
  • 抑制yì zhìto restrain
    Dictionary sensesto inhibit; to keep down; to suppress
  • 精心jīng xīnmeticulous
    Dictionary senseswith utmost care; fine; detailed
  • 极端jí duānextremeTraditional: 極端
    • 别走极端。bié zǒu jí duānDon't go to extremes.
    • 青年人总爱挑战极端。qīng nián rén zǒng ài tiǎo zhàn jí duānYoung people are apt to go to extremes.
  • 理性lǐ xìngrational
    Dictionary sensesreason; rationality
    • 保持理性的思维。bǎo chí lǐ xìng de sī wéiMaintain rational thought.
    • 人的行为不总是理性的。rén de xíng wéi bù zǒng shì lǐ xìng dePeople don't always behave rationally.
  • 服用fú yòngto take (medicine)
    • 你经常服用任何药物吗？nǐ jīng cháng fú yòng rèn hé yào wù maAre you taking any medicine regularly?
  • 遗产yí chǎnheritage; inheritanceMeasure word: 笔Traditional: 遺產

    • 他将会继承大笔的遗产。tā jiāng huì jì chéng dà bǐ de yí chǎnHe will come into a large fortune.
  • 捐赠juān zèngto donateTraditional: 捐贈
    Dictionary sensesto contribute (as a gift); benefaction
  • 皇帝huáng dìemperorMeasure word: 个
    • 皇帝的生日在星期日。huáng dì de shēng rì zài xīng qī rìThe Emperor's Birthday fell on Sunday.
  • 交谈jiāo tánto converseTraditional: 交談
    Dictionary sensesto discuss; chat; discussion
    • 和她交谈很愉快。hé tā jiāo tán hěn yú kuàiIt was nice to converse with her.
    • 我没有参与交谈。wǒ méi yǒu cān yù jiāo tánI didn't take part in the conversation.
  • 取代qǔ dàito replace
    Dictionary sensesto supersede; to supplant; (chemistry) substitution
    • 你无可取代。nǐ wú kě qǔ dàiYou're irreplaceable.
    • 车取代了自行车。chē qǔ dài le zì xíng chēThe car replaced the bicycle.
  • 模拟mó nǐto simulate; mockTraditional: 模擬
    Dictionary sensesimitation; to imitate; analog (device, as opposed to digital)
  • 住宅zhù zháiresidence
    Dictionary sensesdwelling; abode
