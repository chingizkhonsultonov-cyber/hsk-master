  • 则zéthen (literary)Traditional: 則
    Dictionary senses(literary) (conjunction used to express contrast with a previous clause) but; then; norm; (literary) to imitate
    • 动物需要食物和饮料，而植物则需要雨水和阳光。dòng wù xū yào shí wù hé yǐn liào ér zhí wù zé xū yào yǔ shuǐ hé yáng guāngAs food and drink is to animals, so are rain and sunshine to plants.
    • 一些在我的花园里的玫瑰是白色的，其他的则是红色的。yì xiē zài wǒ de huā yuán lǐ de méi guī shì bái sè de qí tā de zé shì hóng sè deSome of the roses in my garden are white, and others are red.
  • 清qīngclear
    Dictionary senses(of water etc) clear; clean; quiet; still
    • 能看清吧？néng kàn qīng baCan you see it clearly?
    • 我是清白的。wǒ shì qīng bái deI'm innocent.
  • 轮lúnwheel; round, turnTraditional: 輪
    Dictionary sensesdisk; ring; steamship; to take turns
    • 轮到您了。lún dào nín leIt's your turn.
    • 再来一轮怎么样？zài lái yī lún zěn me yàngHow about another round?
  • 投票tóu piàoto vote
    Dictionary sensesvote
    • 我们要投票。wǒ men yào tóu piàoWe want to vote.
  • 逼bīto forceTraditional: 偪
    Dictionary sensesto compel; to pressure; to force (sb to do sth); to press for
    • 别逼我！bié bī wǒDon't force me!
    • 我永远不会逼你跟他结婚。wǒ yǒng yuǎn bù huì bī nǐ gēn tā jié hūnI will never force you to marry him.
  • 待dàito treat; to stay

  • 尽jǐnto the utmostTraditional: 儘

    • 我们尽我们所能。wǒ men jǐn wǒ men suǒ néngWe do the best we can.
    • 尽可能多点查字典。jǐn kě néng duō diǎn chá zì diǎnRefer to the dictionary as often as possible.
  • 投资tóu zīto invest; investmentTraditional: 投資
    • 谁来为风险投资提供资金？shéi lái wèi fēng xiǎn tóu zī tí gōng zī jīnWho will provide capital for the venture?
    • 他把钱投资于股票。tā bǎ qián tóu zī yú gǔ piàoHe invested his money in stocks.

  • 副fùdeputy, vice-

    • 光传播得比声音快，所以我们先看到闪电，然后才会听见雷声。guāng chuán bō de bǐ shēng yīn kuài suǒ yǐ wǒ men xiān kàn dào shǎn diàn rán hòu cái huì tīng jiàn léi shēngIt is because light travels faster than sound that we see lightning before we hear thunder.
