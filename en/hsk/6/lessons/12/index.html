    Dictionary sensesnucleus
    • 这个章节是这部小说的核心。zhè ge zhāng jié shì zhè bù xiǎo shuō de hé xīnThis chapter is the heart of the novel.
  • 扎zhāto prick; to tie
    Dictionary sensesto run or stick (a needle etc) into; mug or jug used for serving beer (loanword from "jar"); (of troops) to be stationed (at); Taiwan pr. [zha2]
  • 保障bǎo zhàngto guarantee; guarantee
    Dictionary sensesto ensure; to safeguard
  • 资产zī chǎnassetsTraditional: 資產
    Dictionary sensesproperty
  • 运行yùn xíngto run, operateTraditional: 運行
    Dictionary senses(of celestial bodies etc) to move along one's course; (fig.) to function; to be in operation; (of a train service etc) to operate
  • 局面jú miànsituation
    Dictionary sensesaspect; phase
  • 期望qī wàngexpectation
    Dictionary sensesto have expectations; to earnestly hope; hope
    • 我对他期望很高。wǒ duì tā qī wàng hěn gāoI expect a lot from him.
    • 我们期望能再见到你。wǒ men qī wàng néng zài jiàn dào nǐWe look forward to seeing you again.
  • 供应gōng yìngto supplyTraditional: 供應
    Dictionary sensesto provide (goods, services etc)
    • 供应和需求之间的关系十分密切。gōng yìng hé xū qiú zhī jiān de guān xi shí fēn mì qièThere is close relationship between supply and demand.
  • 一时yì shífor a while; momentarilyTraditional: 一時
    Dictionary sensesa period of time; a while; for a short while; temporary
    • 十一时了，你该上床睡觉了。shí yī shí le nǐ gāi shàng chuáng shuì jiào leIt's already eleven. It's high time you were in bed.
    • 现在已经十一时了。我一定要走了。xiàn zài yǐ jīng shí yī shí le wǒ yī dìng yào zǒu leIt's already eleven o'clock. I must be leaving now.
  • 温柔wēn róugentleTraditional: 溫柔
    Dictionary sensesgentle and soft; tender; sweet (commonly used to describe a girl or woman)
    • 她很温柔。tā hěn wēn róuShe has a tender heart.
    • 她温柔地唱了。tā wēn róu de chàng leShe sang softly.
  • 补偿bǔ chángto compensate; compensationTraditional: 補償
    Dictionary sensesto make up
  • 抛pāoto throwTraditional: 拋
    Dictionary sensesto toss; to fling; to cast; to abandon
  • 显xiǎnto show; obviousTraditional: 顯
    Dictionary sensesto make visible; to reveal; prominent; conspicuous
    • 他看上去显老。tā kàn shàng qu xiǎn lǎoHe is not as young as he looks.
    • 她显出不同于他人的平静。tā xiǎn chū bù tóng yú tā rén de píng jìngShe showed more composure than others.
  • 脖子bó zineckMeasure word: 个
    • 我的脖子还疼。wǒ de bó zi hái téngMy neck still hurts.
    • 他伸长了脖子，希望能听到她们低声说的话。tā shēn cháng le bó zi xī wàng néng tīng dào tā men dī shēng shuō de huàHe craned his neck in the hope of hearing what they were whispering.
  • 战胜zhàn shèngto defeatTraditional: 戰勝
    Dictionary sensesto prevail over; to surmount
    • 最大的胜利是战胜自己。zuì dà de shèng lì shì zhàn shèng zì jǐThe biggest victory is to win over yourself.
  • 年度nián dùyear (annual)

  • 检验jiǎn yànto test, inspectTraditional: 檢驗
    Dictionary sensesto inspect; to examine; to test
  • 寂寞jì mòlonely
    Dictionary senseslonesome; (of a place) quiet; silent
    • 我最近很寂寞。wǒ zuì jìn hěn jì mòI feel very lonely these days.
    • 我最近非常寂寞。wǒ zuì jìn fēi cháng jì mòI feel very lonely these days.
  • 排除pái chúto rule out
    Dictionary sensesto eliminate; to remove; to exclude
    • 他把问题从脑子里排除了。tā bǎ wèn tí cóng nǎo zi lǐ pái chú leHe cast off the problem from his mind.
  • 国王guó wángkingTraditional: 國王
    • 他是国王。tā shì guó wángHe is the king.
    • 他像国王一样开心。tā xiàng guó wáng yī yàng kāi xīnHe's as happy as a king.
  • 发行fā xíngto issue, releaseTraditional: 發行
    Dictionary sensesto publish; to issue; to release; to distribute
    • 新邮票将于下个月发行。xīn yóu piào jiāng yú xià gè yuè fā xíngNew stamps will be issued next month.
  • 密mìdense; close; secret
    Dictionary sensesthick; intimate; confidential
  • 播bōto broadcast; to sow
    Dictionary sensesto spread (ideas, rumors etc); to broadcast (radio signals etc); Taiwan pr. [bo4]
    • 那个节目现在正在播出。nà ge jié mù xiàn zài zhèng zài bō chūThe program is on the air.
  • 社shèsociety, agency
    Dictionary sensesorganization; agency; (old) god of the land
    • 我参加戏剧社。wǒ cān jiā xì jù shèI belong to the drama club.
    • 我参加游泳社。wǒ cān jiā yóu yǒng shèI belong to the swimming club.
  • 当天dàng tiānthe same dayTraditional: 當天
  • 井jǐngwellMeasure word: 口
    Dictionary sensesa well; neat; orderly

    • 这口井里没有水。zhè kǒu jǐng lǐ méi yǒu shuǐThere's no water in the well.
  • 王子wáng zǐprince
    Dictionary sensesson of a king
    • 一位王子骑着白马到来了。yī wèi wáng zǐ qí zhe bái mǎ dào lái leA prince arrived, riding a white horse.

    Dictionary sensesto glue; to paste; to adhere; to stick to
  • 钻zuānto drill; to get intoTraditional: 鑽
