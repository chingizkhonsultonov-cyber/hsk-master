  • 自我zì wǒself
    Dictionary sensesself-; ego (psychology)
    • 你永远都不能太自我。nǐ yǒng yuǎn dōu bù néng tài zì wǒYou can never have too much ego.
    • 请允许我自我介绍一下。qǐng yǔn xǔ wǒ zì wǒ jiè shào yí xiàLet me introduce myself.
  • 犯fànto commit (a crime, mistake)
    Dictionary sensesto violate; to offend; to assault; criminal
    • 他怕犯错。tā pà fàn cuòHe's afraid of making mistakes.
    • 你老是犯错。nǐ lǎo shi fàn cuòYou're always making mistakes.
  • 疼téngto ache; to love dearly
    Dictionary senses(it) hurts; sore
  • 服fúclothes; to obey; to take (medicine)
    Dictionary sensesdress; garment; to serve (in the military, a prison sentence etc)
    • 穿上衣服。chuān shàng yī fúDress up.
    • 你会穿这件和服吗？nǐ huì chuān zhè jiàn hé fú maWill you put on this kimono?
  • 布bùcloth; to arrangeTraditional: 佈
    Dictionary sensesto announce; to spread; to declare; to make known
    • 他住在布哈拉。tā zhù zài bù hā lāHe lives in Bukhara.
    • 剪刀、石头、布。jiǎn dāo shí tou bùRock, paper, scissors.
  • 样yàngkind, type (measure word)Traditional: 樣
    Dictionary sensesmanner; pattern; way; appearance
    • 别同时做两样事。bié tóng shí zuò liǎng yàng shìDon't do two things at once.
    • 他有各种各样的书。tā yǒu gè zhǒng gè yàng de shūHe has all kinds of books.
  • 理lǐto tidy; reason
    Dictionary sensestexture; grain (of wood); inner essence; intrinsic order
    • 不用理他。bù yòng lǐ tāDon't pay attention to him.
    • 为什么你不理我？wèi shén me nǐ bù lǐ wǒWhy are you ignoring me?
  • 恨hènto hate
    Dictionary sensesto regret
    • 不要恨我。bú yào hèn wǒDon't hate me!
    • 我不恨你。wǒ bù hèn nǐI don't hate you.
  • 射shèto shootTraditional: 䠶
    Dictionary sensesto launch; to allude to; radio- (chemistry)
    • 我要射了！wǒ yào shè leI am going to shoot!
    • 她不小心射中了他。tā bù xiǎo xīn shè zhōng le tāShe accidentally shot him.
  • 时尚shí shàngfashion; fashionableTraditional: 時尚
    Dictionary sensesfad
  • 剑jiànswordMeasure word: 口、把Traditional: 劍
    Dictionary sensesdouble-edged sword; classifier for blows of a sword
    • 我没有剑。wǒ méi yǒu jiànI don't have a sword.
    • 剑在哪里？jiàn zài nǎ liWhere's the sword?
  • 和平hé píngpeace
    Dictionary sensespeaceful
    • 我们希望世界和平。wǒ men xī wàng shì jiè hé píngWe want peace in the world.
    • 谁都希望世界和平。shéi dōu xī wàng shì jiè hé píngWe are all longing for peace in the world.
  • 爆炸bào zhàexplosion; to explode
    Dictionary sensesto blow up; to detonate
    • 爆炸的原因是什么？bào zhà de yuán yīn shì shén meWhat was the cause of the explosion?
    • 化学工厂一着火就爆炸了。huà xué gōng chǎng yī zháo huǒ jiù bào zhà leOnce the chemical plant caught fire, it exploded.
  • 偷tōuto steal
    Dictionary sensesto pilfer; to snatch; thief; stealthily
    • 你在偷听。nǐ zài tōu tīngYou were eavesdropping.
    • 小偷跑了。xiǎo tōu pǎo leThe thief ran away.
  • 基地jī dìbase
    Dictionary sensesbase (of operations, industrial, military, research etc)
  • 黄huángyellow; to fall throughTraditional: 黃
    Dictionary sensespornographic
    • 我们有黄苹果。wǒ men yǒu huáng píng guǒWe have yellow apples.
    • 请把黄油递给我。qǐng bǎ huáng yóu dì gěi wǒPlease pass me the butter.
  • 岛dǎoislandMeasure word: 个、座Traditional: 㠀
    • 我是冰岛人。wǒ shì bīng dǎo rénI'm Icelandic.
    • 他们来自冰岛。tā men lái zì bīng dǎoThey're from Iceland.
  • 战斗zhàn dòubattle; to fightMeasure word: 场、次Traditional: 戰鬥
    Dictionary sensesto engage in combat; struggle
    • 我会战斗至死。wǒ huì zhàn dòu zhì sǐI'll fight to the death.
    • 她们应该战斗。tā men yīng gāi zhàn dòuThey should fight.
  • 官guānofficialMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesgovernment official; governmental; public; organ of the body
    • 是的，长官。shì de cháng guānAye aye, sir.
    • 我做不到，长官。wǒ zuò bù dào cháng guānI can't do that, sir.
  • 公开gōng kāipublic, openTraditional: 公開
    Dictionary sensesopen; overt; public; to make public
    • 秘密的礼物公开的奖励。mì mì de lǐ wù gōng kāi de jiǎng lìSecret gifts are openly rewarded.
    • 工作越公开，批评就越多。gōng zuò yuè gōng kāi pī píng jiù yuè duōThe more public the work, the more criticism you receive.
  • 混hùnto mix; to muddle along
    Dictionary sensesto mingle; muddled; to drift along
    • 把米饭和鸡蛋及酱油混一下。bǎ mǐ fàn hé jī dàn jí jiàng yóu hùn yí xiàMix the rice, egg and soy sauce.
    • 不要把欲望和爱情混为一谈。bú yào bǎ yù wàng hé ài qíng hùn wèi yī tánDon't confuse desire with love.
  • 人家rén jiāothers; household
    Dictionary sensesother people; sb else; he, she or they; I, me (referring to oneself as "one" or "people")
    • 你为什么要骂人家？nǐ wèi shén me yào mà rén jiaWhy do you go about being cross at people?
    • 他是有钱人家的儿子。tā shì yǒu qián rén jia de ér ziHe is a son of a wealthy family.
  • 摆bǎito place, arrange; to swayTraditional: 擺
    Dictionary sensesto arrange; to exhibit; to move to and fro; a pendulum
    • 美雪为派对摆好桌子。měi xuě wèi pài duì bǎi hǎo zhuō ziMiyuki set the table for the party.
    • 那件家具不适合摆在客厅。nà jiàn jiā jù bù shì hé bǎi zài kè tīngThat piece of furniture is not fitting for the living room.
  • 吓xiàto frightenTraditional: 嚇
    Dictionary sensesto scare
    • 我吓坏了。wǒ xià huài leI'm scared shitless.
    • 我吓一跳。wǒ xià yī tiàoI was shocked.

    Dictionary sensesto ascend; to rise; to promote; to elevate
  • 军队jūn duìarmyMeasure word: 支、个Traditional: 軍隊
    Dictionary sensesarmed forces; troops
    • 军队意外地被攻击了。jūn duì yì wài de bèi gōng jī leThe army was unexpectedly attacked.
    • 日本的军队在当时很强。rì běn de jūn duì zài dāng shí hěn qiángJapan's army was very powerful.
  • 基金jī jīnfund
    • 国际货币基金组织不考虑再借款给该国。guó jì huò bì jī jīn zǔ zhī bù kǎo lǜ zài jiè kuǎn gěi gāi guóThe IMF ruled out any new loans to that country.

    • 你是怎么学会说话的？nǐ shì zěn me xué huì shuō huà deHow did you learn to speak?
  • 部队bù duìarmy, troopsMeasure word: 个Traditional: 部隊
    Dictionary sensesarmy; armed forces; troops; force
