  • 土tǔsoil, earth; rustic
    Dictionary sensesearth; dust; clay; local
  • 本身běn shēnitself
    Dictionary sensesin itself; per se
    • 这个想法本身还可以。zhè ge xiǎng fǎ běn shēn hái kě yǐThe idea is not in itself a bad one.
    • 创新创新本身就提升了创新。chuàng xīn chuàng xīn běn shēn jiù tí shēng le chuàng xīnCreating creativity itself is improving creativity.
  • 端duānto carry (level); end
    Dictionary sensesextremity; facet; proper; to hold sth level with both hands
    • 能不能请你拿着这端？néng bù néng qǐng nǐ ná zhe zhè duānWill you please hold this edge?
    • 亲爱的，给客人端点咖啡吧。qīn ài de gěi kè rén duān diǎn kā fēi baPlease serve the guests some coffee, dear.
  • 时光shí guāngtime, daysTraditional: 時光
    Dictionary sensestime; era; period of time
    • 我度过了美好的时光。wǒ dù guò le měi hǎo de shí guāngI had a wonderful time.
    • 希望你会享受在中国的时光。xī wàng nǐ huì xiǎng shòu zài Zhōng guó de shí guāngI hope you will enjoy your time in China.
  • 扣kòuto button; to deduct
    Dictionary sensesto fasten; button; buckle; knot
    • 包裹被海关扣了。bāo guǒ bèi hǎi guān kòu leThe package was seized by customs.
  • 狼lángwolfMeasure word: 匹、只、条
    • 狼靠腿吃饭。láng kào tuǐ chī fànA wolf is fed by its legs.
  • 道德dào démoralityMeasure word: 种
    Dictionary sensesvirtue; ethics
    • 必须判断一个行为是否符合道德标准。bì xū pàn duàn yī gè xíng wéi shì fǒu fú hé dào dé biāo zhǔnIt needs to be judged on whether it meets the ethical standards.
    • 这个学校对学生的道德标准要求很高。zhè ge xué xiào duì xué sheng de dào dé biāo zhǔn yāo qiú hěn gāoThis school sets high moral standards for pupils.
  • 编辑biān jíto edit; editorTraditional: 編輯
    Dictionary sensesto compile; compiler
    • 这家报社的新闻编辑室在哪里？zhè jiā bào shè de xīn wén biān jí shì zài nǎ liWhere's the newsroom of this newspaper?
  • 挖wāto dig
    Dictionary sensesto excavate; to scoop out
    • 他们正在挖一个洞。tā men zhèng zài wā yī gè dòngThey're digging a hole.
    • 你可以在地上挖土。nǐ kě yǐ zài de shàng wā tǔYou can dig dirt from the ground.
  • 证实zhèng shíto confirmTraditional: 證實
    Dictionary sensesto confirm (sth to be true); to verify
    • 他被证实是个理想丈夫。tā bèi zhèng shí shì gè lǐ xiǎng zhàng fuHe proved to be an ideal husband.
    • 我自己能够证实那是真的。wǒ zì jǐ néng gòu zhèng shí nà shì zhēn deI can verify that that's the truth myself.
  • 要么yào meeither ... orTraditional: 要麼
    Dictionary sensesor; either one or the other
    • 我要么去看电影，要么和朋友聊天。wǒ yào me qù kàn diàn yǐng yào me hé péng you liáo tiānI am either going to watch a movie, or chat with my friends.
  • 恶心ě xīnnauseous; disgustingTraditional: 噁心
    Dictionary sensesnausea; to feel sick; disgust; nauseating
    • 真恶心！zhēn ě xīnSo gross!
    • 我有点恶心。wǒ yǒu diǎn ě xīnI feel sick.
  • 将军jiāng jūngeneral (army)Traditional: 將軍
    Dictionary sensesgeneral; high-ranking military officer; to check or checkmate; fig. to embarrass
  • 透露tòu lùto reveal
    Dictionary sensesto leak out; to divulge
    • 他透露了我的秘密。tā tòu lù le wǒ de mì mìHe disclosed my secret.
    • 我不能再向你多说什么了。我已经透露太多了。wǒ bù néng zài xiàng nǐ duō shuō shén me le wǒ yǐ jīng tòu lù tài duō leI can't tell you any more. I've already said too much.
  • 兵bīngsoldierMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensessoldiers; a force; an army; weapons
    • 他在当兵。tā zài dāng bīngHe's in the army.
    • 我住在兵库县。wǒ zhù zài bīng kù xiànI live in Hyogo.
  • 侧cèsideTraditional: 側
    Dictionary sensesthe side; to incline towards; to lean; inclined
    • 自行车从右侧超过汽车是不合法的。zì xíng chē cóng yòu cè chāo guò qì chē shì bù hé fǎ deIt is illegal for bicycles to pass on the right of cars.
    • 自行车在汽车的右侧行驶是不合法的。zì xíng chē zài qì chē de yòu cè xíng shǐ shì bù hé fǎ deBicycles traveling to the right of automobiles is illegal.
  • 闭bìto close, shutTraditional: 閉
    Dictionary sensesto close; to stop up; to shut; to obstruct
    • 她双眼紧闭。tā shuāng yǎn jǐn bìShe kept her eyes closed.
    • 闭上你的嘴。bì shàng nǐ de zuǐZip your lips.
  • 异常yì chángabnormal; extremelyTraditional: 異常
    Dictionary sensesunusual; exceptionally
    • 环境污染正造成异常的天气情况。huán jìng wū rǎn zhèng zào chéng yì cháng de tiān qì qíng kuàngEnvironmental pollution is causing abnormal weather conditions.
  • 粉fěnpowder; pink; fan (of a star)
    Dictionary sensescosmetic face powder; food prepared from starch; noodles or pasta made from any kind of flour; to turn to powder
    • 我对花粉过敏。wǒ duì huā fěn guò mǐnI am allergic to pollen.
    • 玫瑰是粉红色的。méi guī shì fěn hóng sè deThe rose is pink.
  • 足zúfoot; enough
    Dictionary sensesleg; sufficient; ample; as much as
    • 我们打算明天去远足。wǒ men dǎ suàn míng tiān qù yuǎn zúWe plan to go hiking tomorrow.
    • 他对自己感到心满意足。tā duì zì jǐ gǎn dào xīn mǎn yì zúHe felt perfectly content.
  • 曲qūbent, crooked
    Dictionary sensesbent; crooked; wrong; fermentation starter
    • 你听过这首曲子吗？nǐ tīng guò zhè shǒu qū zi maHave you listened to this song?
    • 这首曲子叫什么名字？zhè shǒu qū zi jiào shén me míng ziWhat's the name of this tune?
  • 档案dàng ànfile, archiveTraditional: 檔案
    Dictionary sensesfile; record; archive
  • 协会xié huìassociationMeasure word: 个、家Traditional: 協會
    Dictionary sensesan association; a society
    • 我申请成为协会会员。wǒ shēn qǐng chéng wéi xié huì huì yuánI applied for membership in the association.
    • 这个协会有三十个会员。zhè ge xié huì yǒu sān shí gè huì yuánThe association counts thirty members.
  • 公gōngpublic; male (animal)
    Dictionary sensescollectively owned; common; international (e.g. high seas, metric system, calendar); make public
    • 公车来了。gōng chē lái leThe bus has arrived.
    • 他下了公车。tā xià le gōng chēHe got off the bus.
  • 细胞xì bāocellTraditional: 細胞
    Dictionary sensescell (biology)
    • 每一个细胞都是由另一个细胞产生的。měi yī gè xì bāo dōu shì yóu lìng yī gè xì bāo chǎn shēng deEvery cell comes from a cell.
    • 这个团体内的每个人，像是一个健康的人体内免疫系统里的细胞。zhè ge tuán tǐ nèi de měi gè rén xiàng shì yī gè jiàn kāng de rén tǐ nèi miǎn yì xì tǒng lǐ de xì bāoEvery person in this community is like a cell in the immune system of a healthy human body.
  • 症状zhèng zhuàngsymptomTraditional: 症狀
    Dictionary sensessymptom (of an illness)
    • 你有什么症状？nǐ yǒu shén me zhèng zhuàngWhat symptoms do you have?
    • 这种症状在我身上将会持续多久？zhè zhǒng zhèng zhuàng zài wǒ shēn shàng jiāng huì chí xù duō jiǔFor how long will I continue to have these symptoms?
  • 试图shì túto attemptTraditional: 試圖
    Dictionary sensesto try
    • 我试图解决这个问题。wǒ shì tú jiě jué zhè ge wèn tíI attempted to solve the problem.
    • 试图劝她是没有用的。shì tú quàn tā shì méi yǒu yòng deIt's no use trying to persuade her.
  • 患者huàn zhěpatient
    Dictionary sensessufferer
  • 艘sōu(measure word for ships)
    Dictionary sensesclassifier for ships; Taiwan pr. [sao1]
    • 我有一艘小船。wǒ yǒu yī sōu xiǎo chuánI have a boat.
    • 那艘船上有多少人？nà sōu chuán shàng yǒu duō shao rénHow many people were aboard that ship?
  • 财产cái chǎnpropertyMeasure word: 笔Traditional: 財產
    Dictionary sensesassets; estate
    • 他留给他女儿一大笔财产。tā liú gěi tā nǚ ér yī dà bǐ cái chǎnHe left his daughter a great fortune.
    • 他把财产留给了他的儿子。tā bǎ cái chǎn liú gěi le tā de ér ziHe left his fortune to his son.
