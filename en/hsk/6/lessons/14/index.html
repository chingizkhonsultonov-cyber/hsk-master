  • 扇shānto fan

  • 笑容xiào róngsmileMeasure word: 副
    Dictionary sensessmiling expression
    • 她被他的笑容迷住了。tā bèi tā de xiào róng mí zhù leShe was bewitched by his smile.
    • 好运向他露出了笑容。hǎo yùn xiàng tā lù chū le xiào róngFortune beamed on him.
  • 消除xiāo chúto eliminate
    Dictionary sensesto remove
  • 寻求xún qiúto seekTraditional: 尋求
    Dictionary sensesto look for
    • 他寻求他的朋友的帮助。tā xún qiú tā de péng you de bāng zhùHe asked his friends for help.
    • 你也许要寻求他的帮助。nǐ yě xǔ yào xún qiú tā de bāng zhùPerhaps you will seek his aid.
  • 通讯tōng xùncommunication; news reportMeasure word: 个Traditional: 通訊
    Dictionary sensescommunications; news story; dispatch

    Dictionary sensesto conceal; to mask; to shelter; to harbor (i.e. keep sth hidden)

    Dictionary sensesto lag (in technology etc); to retrogress
    • 骄傲使人落后。jiāo ào shǐ rén luò hòuPride goes before a fall.
    • 我们落后于原定计划。wǒ men luò hòu yú yuán dìng jì huàWe're behind schedule.

    • 那路当然挺远的，我们打的去算了。nà lù dāng rán tǐng yuǎn de wǒ men dǎ de qù suàn leThat way is very far, so we took a taxi.
  • 狠hěnruthless; firmly
    Dictionary sensesfierce; ferocious; determined; resolute
    • 算你狠。suàn nǐ hěnYou win.
    • 今天热得狠。jīn tiān rè de hěnIt's very hot today.
  • 策划cè huàto plan, plotTraditional: 策劃
    Dictionary sensesto plot; to scheme; to bring about; to engineer
    • 他在策划部工作。tā zài cè huà bù gōng zuòHe works in the planning section.
  • 蹲dūnto squat
    Dictionary sensesto crouch; to stay (somewhere)
  • 冒险mào xiǎnto take a risk; adventureTraditional: 冒險
    Dictionary sensesto take risks; to take chances; foray
    • 他喜欢冒险。tā xǐ huan mào xiǎnHe likes adventure.
    • 我渴望冒险。wǒ kě wàng mào xiǎnI've a yen for adventures.
  • 牵qiānto lead (by the hand)Traditional: 牽
    Dictionary sensesto lead along; to pull (an animal on a tether); to draw in
    • 我们牵过一次手。wǒ men qiān guò yī cì shǒuWe held hands, once.
    • 我可以牵着你的手吗？wǒ kě yǐ qiān zhe nǐ de shǒu maCan I hold your hand?

  • 技巧jì qiǎotechnique, skill
    Dictionary sensesskill; technique

    Dictionary sensesland under heaven; the whole world; the whole of China; realm

  • 公正gōng zhèngfair, just
    Dictionary sensesjust; fair; equitable
    • 公正的代价很高。gōng zhèng de dài jià hěn gāoJustice is expensive.
    • 他们受到的对待公正吗？tā men shòu dào de duì dài gōng zhèng maAre they treated fairly?

    • 他完全不会游泳，但却是一个滑雪高手。tā wán quán bù huì yóu yǒng dàn què shì yī gè huá xuě gāo shǒuHe can't swim at all, but when it comes to skiing, he is the best.

    • 天气逐渐回暖了。tiān qì zhú jiàn huí nuǎn leIt is getting warmer and warmer.
  • 规划guī huàplan; to planTraditional: 規劃
    Dictionary sensesto draw up a plan; to map out a program; a plan; a program
  • 不许bù xǔnot allowedTraditional: 不許
    Dictionary sensesnot to allow; must not; can't
