    • 目前，我妹妹在一家超市当收银员。mù qián wǒ mèi mei zài yī jiā chāo shì dāng shōu yín yuánCurrently, my sister works as a cashier at a supermarket.
  • 刺cìthorn; to prick
    Dictionary sensessting; thrust; to pierce
    • 她有刺青吗？tā yǒu cì qīng maDoes she have any tattoos?

  • 至于zhì yúas for; to go so far asTraditional: 至於
    Dictionary sensesas to; to go so far as to

    • 至于该文件，我在保管。zhì yú gāi wén jiàn wǒ zài bǎo guǎnAs for the papers, I have custody of them.

    • 他们的牛都很肥。tā men de niú dōu hěn féiTheir cattle are all fat.
