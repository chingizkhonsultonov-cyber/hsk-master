  • 可怜kě liánpitifulTraditional: 可憐
    Dictionary sensespathetic; to have pity on
    • 你真可怜。nǐ zhēn kě liánIt must suck to be you.
    • 可怜可怜我吧！kě lián kě lián wǒ baHave pity on me.
  • 为何wèi héwhyTraditional: 為何
    • 你为何想当一个老师？nǐ wèi hé xiǎng dāng yī gè lǎo shīWhy did you want to become a teacher?
    • 你为何不现在就回家？nǐ wèi hé bù xiàn zài jiù huí jiāWhy don't you go home now?
  • 爆bàoto burst, explode
    Dictionary sensesto explode or burst; to quick fry or quick boil
  • 毒dúpoison; poisonous
    Dictionary sensesto poison; malicious; cruel
    • 它有毒吗？tā yǒu dú maIs it poisonous?
    • 这条鱼没有毒。zhè tiáo yú méi yǒu dúThis fish is free from poison.
  • 吐tǔto spit
    Dictionary sensesto send out (silk from a silkworm, bolls from cotton flowers etc); to say; to pour out (one's grievances)
    • 我刚才吐了。wǒ gāng cái tǔ leI just threw up.
    • 有点恶心，但是吐不出来。yǒu diǎn ě xīn dàn shì tǔ bù chū laiI feel a little nauseous, but I'm unable to throw up.
  • 吐tùto vomit
    Dictionary sensesto spit; to send out (silk from a silkworm, bolls from cotton flowers etc); to say; to pour out (one's grievances)
  • 中央zhōng yāngcentral
    Dictionary sensesmiddle; center; central authorities (of a state)
    • 图书馆位于市中央。tú shū guǎn wèi yú shì zhōng yāngThe library is in the middle of the city.
    • 城市中央有一座公园。chéng shì zhōng yāng yǒu yī zuò gōng yuánThere is a park in the middle of the city.
  • 州zhōustate, prefecture
    Dictionary sensesprefecture; (old) province; (old) administrative division; state (e.g. of US)
    • 我住在广州。wǒ zhù zài guǎng zhōuI live in Canton.
    • 你去过九州吗？nǐ qù guò jiǔ zhōu maHave you ever been to Kyushu?
  • 解jiěto untie; to solve
    Dictionary sensesto divide; to break up; to split; to separate
    • 解封，解封！jiě fēng jiě fēngUnblock, unblock!
    • 你用什么方法解压？nǐ yòng shén me fāng fǎ jiě yāHow do you relieve stress?
  • 罪zuìcrime
    Dictionary sensesguilt; fault; blame; sin
    • 这是战争罪。zhè shì zhàn zhēng zuìThis is a war crime.
    • 他承认他有罪。tā chéng rèn tā yǒu zuìHe admitted his guilt.
  • 显著xiǎn zhùremarkableTraditional: 顯著
    Dictionary sensesoutstanding; notable; statistically significant
    • 这几年，科学取得了显著的进步。zhè jǐ nián kē xué qǔ dé le xiǎn zhù de jìn bùIn recent years, science has made remarkable progress.
  • 承诺chéng nuòpromise; to promiseTraditional: 承諾
    Dictionary sensesto undertake to do something; commitment
    • 承诺是不够的。chéng nuò shì bù gòu dePromises aren't enough.
    • 她总是信守承诺。tā zǒng shì xìn shǒu chéng nuòShe always keeps her promises.
  • 感染gǎn rǎnto infect; infection
    Dictionary senses(fig.) to influence
  • 启动qǐ dòngto start, launchTraditional: 啟動
    Dictionary sensesto start (a machine); (fig.) to set in motion; to launch (an operation); to activate (a plan)
    • 你需要重新启动电脑。nǐ xū yào chóng xīn qǐ dòng diàn nǎoYou need to reboot your computer.
    • 你必须重新启动你的电脑。nǐ bì xū chóng xīn qǐ dòng nǐ de diàn nǎoYou must reboot your computer.
  • 一一yī yīone by one
    Dictionary sensesone after another
  • 化huà-ize (suffix); to change
    Dictionary sensesto make into; to change into; -ization; to ... -ize
  • 算是suàn shìcan be considered
    Dictionary sensesconsidered to be; at last
    • 他算是男孩子中最高的了。tā suàn shì nán hái zi zhōng zuì gāo de leHe is the tallest of all boys.
    • 从严格的科学观点来看，历史不能算是一门科学。cóng yán gé de kē xué guān diǎn lái kàn lì shǐ bù néng suàn shì yī mén kē xuéFrom a strictly scientific point of view, history cannot be called a science.
  • 滴dīto drip; drop (measure word)
    Dictionary sensesto let drip; to apply (eye drops etc); classifier for drops of liquid
    • 没有剩下一滴水。méi yǒu shèng xià yī dī shuǐThere's not a drop of water left.
    • 两周没有下过一滴雨了。liǎng zhōu méi yǒu xià guò yī dī yǔ leThere hasn't been a drop of rain in two weeks.
  • 结jiéto tie; knotTraditional: 結
    Dictionary sensessturdy; bond; to bind
  • 逃táoto escape
    Dictionary sensesto run away; to flee
    • 他偶尔逃学。tā ǒu ěr táo xuéHe is sometimes absent from school.
    • 我们逃课吧！wǒ men táo kè baLet's cut class!
  • 婚姻hūn yīnmarriageMeasure word: 桩、次
    Dictionary sensesmatrimony
    • 有些人害怕婚姻。yǒu xiē rén hài pà hūn yīnMarriage frightens some people.
    • 能告诉我你的婚姻状况吗？néng gào su wǒ nǐ de hūn yīn zhuàng kuàng maCan you tell me about your marital status?
  • 打击dǎ jīto strike, crack down onTraditional: 打擊
    Dictionary sensesto hit; to strike; to attack; to crack down on sth
    • 这会打击我们的生意。zhè huì dǎ jī wǒ men de shēng yiThis could hurt our business.
    • 他的去世让他的太太大受打击。tā de qù shì ràng tā de tài tai dà shòu dǎ jīHis death was great shock to his wife.
  • 转移zhuǎn yíto shiftTraditional: 轉移
    Dictionary sensesto relocate; to transfer; (fig.) to shift (attention); to change (the subject etc)
  • 印yìnto print; mark
    Dictionary sensesto mark; to engrave; a seal; a print
    • 印度万岁。yìn dù wàn suìLong live India!
    • 我去过印度。wǒ qù guò yìn dùI have been to India.
  • 卷juǎnto roll up
    Dictionary senses(fig.) to sweep up; to engulf; to drag into (a situation); (slang) to compete to an unhealthy degree
    • 卷心菜可以生吃。juǎn xīn cài kě yǐ shēng chīCabbage can be eaten raw.
    • 这张考卷太简单了。zhè zhāng kǎo juǎn tài jiǎn dān leThis test is too easy.
  • 卷juànvolume (of a book); exam paper
    Dictionary sensesto roll up; (fig.) to sweep up; to engulf; to drag into (a situation)
  • 透tòuto penetrate; thoroughly
    Dictionary sensesto seep through; to tell secretly; to leak; through and through
    • 天气糟透了。tiān qì zāo tòu leThe weather is really bad.
    • 我看那糟透了。wǒ kàn nà zāo tòu leI think that's awful.
  • 酷kùcool
    Dictionary sensesruthless; strong (e.g. of wine); (loanword) cool; hip
    • 我很酷。wǒ hěn kùI'm cool.
    • 我酷爱电视。wǒ kù ài diàn shìI really like TV.
  • 人士rén shìperson, personage
    Dictionary sensesperson; figure; public figure
    • 我正在寻找可以帮我修改这篇论文的母语人士。wǒ zhèng zài xún zhǎo kě yǐ bāng wǒ xiū gǎi zhè piān lùn wén de mǔ yǔ rén shìI am looking for a native speaker who can correct this paper for me.
  • 拖tuōto drag; to delayTraditional: 拕

    • 他在拖。tā zài tuōHe is towing.
