  • 弟弟dì diyounger brotherMeasure word: 个、位
    • 弟弟问我要钱。dì di wèn wǒ yào qiánMy little brother asked me for money.
    • 我弟弟在看电视。wǒ dì di zài kàn diàn shìMy brother is watching TV.
  • 百bǎihundred
    Dictionary sensesnumerous; all kinds of
    • 五百年前是一家。wǔ bǎi nián qián shì yī jiāFive hundred years ago it was a house.
  • 好吃hǎo chītasty; delicious
    • 好吃吗？hǎo chī maIs it tasty?
    • 真好吃。zhēn hǎo chīIt's so yummy.
  • 明年míng niánnext year
  • 再见zài jiàngoodbyeTraditional: 再見
    • 我想说再见。wǒ xiǎng shuō zài jiànI want to say goodbye.
    • 我不会再见她。wǒ bù huì zài jiàn tāI won't see her again.
  • 这儿zhè rhereTraditional: 這兒
    • 我住在这儿。wǒ zhù zài zhè rI live here.
    • 我明天不在这儿。wǒ míng tiān bù zài zhè rI won't be here tomorrow.
  • 哪儿nǎ rwhereTraditional: 哪兒
    Dictionary senseswhere?; wherever; anywhere; somewhere
    • 学校在哪儿？xué xiào zài nǎ rWhere is the school?
    • 你想去哪儿？nǐ xiǎng qù nǎ rWhere do you want to go?
  • 苹果píng guǒappleMeasure word: 个、颗Traditional: 蘋果
    • 我吃苹果。wǒ chī píng guǒI eat apples.
    • 他有苹果。tā yǒu píng guǒHe has apples.
  • 中午zhōng wǔnoonMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesmidday
    • 我们在中午吃了饭。wǒ men zài zhōng wǔ chī le fànWe took lunch at noon.
  • 起床qǐ chuángto get up
    Dictionary sensesto get out of bed
    • 你几点起床？nǐ jǐ diǎn qǐ chuángWhen do you get up?
    • 她晚了起床。tā wǎn le qǐ chuángShe got up late.
  • 开车kāi chēto drive (a car)Traditional: 開車
    Dictionary sensesto drive a car (or train etc); (slang) to post sexual content online; to make a sexual joke
    • 她会开车。tā huì kāi chēShe can drive a car.
    • 他会开车。tā huì kāi chēHe can drive.
  • 出租车chū zū chētaxiTraditional: 出租車
    Dictionary senses(Tw) rental car
    • 出租车到了。chū zū chē dào leThe cab's here.
    • 坐出租车吧。zuò chū zū chē baLet's take a cab.
  • 这边zhè biānthis side; hereTraditional: 這邊
  • 火车huǒ chētrainMeasure word: 列、节、班Traditional: 火車
    • 火车来了！huǒ chē lái leThe train is coming!
    • 看！火车来了！kàn huǒ chē lái leLook! The train has arrived!
  • 便宜pián yicheap
    Dictionary sensesinexpensive; a petty advantage; to let sb off lightly
    • 它真的很便宜。tā zhēn de hěn pián yiIt is really cheap.
    • 可以便宜一点吗？kě yǐ pián yi yī diǎn maCan you cut it down a bit?
