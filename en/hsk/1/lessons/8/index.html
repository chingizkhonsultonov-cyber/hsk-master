  • 水shuǐwater
    Dictionary senses(after a name) ... River; to swim (used mostly in 會水|会水[hui4 shui3] and 水性[shui3 xing4]); (coll.) lacking in substance; shoddy
    • 没有水。méi yǒu shuǐThere's no water.
    • 这水很好喝。zhè shuǐ hěn hǎo hēThe water is tasty.
  • 写xiěto writeTraditional: 寫
    • 你写吗？nǐ xiě maDo you write?
    • 写点什么。xiě diǎn shén meWrite something.
  • 您nínyou (polite)
    Dictionary sensesyou (courteous, as opposed to informal 你[ni3])
    • 我爱您。wǒ ài nínI love you.
    • 您好吗？nín hǎo maHow are you?
  • 电话diàn huàtelephone; phone callMeasure word: 部、通Traditional: 電話
    Dictionary sensesphone number
    • 有你的电话。yǒu nǐ de diàn huàThere is a call for you.
  • 女nǚfemale
    Dictionary senseswoman; daughter
  • 分fēnminute; cent (fen); to divide
    Dictionary sensesto separate; to distribute; to allocate; to distinguish (good and bad)
    • 现在是七点四十五分。xiàn zài shì qī diǎn sì shí wǔ fēnIt's 7:45.
  • 小时xiǎo shíhourMeasure word: 个Traditional: 小時
    • 我们三小时后见吧。wǒ men sān xiǎo shí hòu jiàn baWe'll meet in three hours.
    • 我只睡了两小时。wǒ zhī shuì le liǎng xiǎo shíI slept just two hours.
  • 有些yǒu xiēsome; somewhat
  • 日rìday; date
    Dictionary sensessun; date, day of the month
    • 是十月三日。shì shí yuè sān rìIt's October the third.
    • 你喜欢日本菜吗？nǐ xǐ huan rì běn cài maDo you like Japanese food?
  • 玩wánto play; to have fun
    Dictionary sensesto trifle with; toy; sth used for amusement
    • 我想去玩。wǒ xiǎng qù wánI want to play.
    • 我不想和他玩。wǒ bù xiǎng hé tā wánI don't want to play with him.
  • 正在zhèng zàiin the middle of (doing)
    Dictionary sensesto be in the process of (doing sth); to be currently ...-ing
    • 你正在想什么？nǐ zhèng zài xiǎng shén meWhat're you thinking?
    • 我正在吃东西。wǒ zhèng zài chī dōng xiI'm eating.
  • 喝hēto drink
    • 你想喝什么？nǐ xiǎng hē shén meWhat would you like to drink?
    • 你喝了什么？nǐ hē le shén meWhat did you drink?
  • 穿chuānto wear; to put on
    Dictionary sensesto dress; to bore through; to pierce
    • 你穿点东西。nǐ chuān diǎn dōng xiGet dressed.
    • 我穿三十七号。wǒ chuān sān shí qī hàoI wear a size 37.
  • 学校xué xiàoschoolMeasure word: 所Traditional: 學校
    • 我去学校。wǒ qù xué xiàoI go to school.
    • 你怎么去学校？nǐ zěn me qù xué xiàoHow do you go to school?
  • 坐zuòto sit; to take (a bus, plane)
    Dictionary sensesto take a seat; to take (a bus, airplane etc); to bear fruit
    • 我觉得你想坐下。wǒ jué de nǐ xiǎng zuò xiàI think you're going to want to sit down.
    • 请坐一下。qǐng zuò yí xiàPlease sit down.
