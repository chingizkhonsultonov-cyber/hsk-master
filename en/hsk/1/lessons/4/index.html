  • 现在xiàn zàinowTraditional: 現在
    Dictionary sensesat present; currently
    • 我们要现在做。wǒ men yào xiàn zài zuòWe want to do it now.
    • 你现在在做什么？nǐ xiàn zài zài zuò shén meWhat are you doing?
  • 年niányearMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesgrain; harvest (old)
    • 他想在中国工作两年。tā xiǎng zài Zhōng guó gōng zuò liǎng niánHe wants to work in China for two years.
    • 我们认识有好些年了。wǒ men rèn shi yǒu hǎo xiē nián leWe've known each other for years.
  • 多duōmany; much
    Dictionary sensesmore; a lot of; too many
    • 你想多了！nǐ xiǎng duō leYou're overthinking it!
    • 你想太多了。nǐ xiǎng tài duō leYou think too much.
  • 两liǎngtwo (with measure words)Traditional: 兩
    Dictionary sensestwo; both; some; a few
    • 他有两只猫。tā yǒu liǎng zhī māoHe has two cats.
    • 我有两只猫。wǒ yǒu liǎng zhī māoI have two cats.
  • 吗maquestion particle (yes/no)Traditional: 嗎
    Dictionary senses(question particle for "yes-no" questions)
    • 有人吗？yǒu rén maAnybody here?
    • 你在吗？nǐ zài maAre you there?
  • 天tiānday; sky
    Dictionary sensesheaven
    • 我知道这天要来。wǒ zhī dào zhè tiān yào láiI knew this day was coming.
    • 天很冷。tiān hěn lěngIt's cold.
  • 太tàitoo; extremely
    Dictionary senseshighest; greatest; too (much); very
    • 太早了。tài zǎo leIt's so early.
    • 太贵了！tài guì leToo expensive!
  • 再zàiagain
    Dictionary sensesonce more; re-; further; beyond this point of time
    • 我想再看看。wǒ xiǎng zài kàn kànI want to see it again.
    • 我不想再做了。wǒ bù xiǎng zài zuò leI don't want to do it again.
  • 呢nequestion particle (and …?; where is …?)
    Dictionary sensesparticle indicating that a previously asked question is to be applied to the preceding word ("What about ...?", "And ...?"); particle for inquiring about location ("Where is ...?"); particle signaling a pause, to emphasize the preceding words and allow the listener time to take them on board ("ok?", "are you with me?"); (at the end of a declarative sentence) particle indicating continuation of a state or action
    • 你在说什么呢？nǐ zài shuō shén me neWhat're you saying?
    • 我很好。你呢？wǒ hěn hǎo nǐ neI’m fine. And you?
  • 只zhīmeasure word for animals
    Dictionary sensesonly; merely; just
    • 我是一只狗。wǒ shì yī zhī gǒuI'm a dog.
    • 我是一只猫。wǒ shì yī zhī māoI'm a cat.
  • 小xiǎosmall; little; young
    Dictionary sensestiny; few
    • 有一个小问题。yǒu yī gè xiǎo wèn tíThere's a small problem.
    • 这本书很小。zhè běn shū hěn xiǎoThis book is very small.
  • 知道zhī dàoto know
    Dictionary sensesto become aware of; also pr. [zhi1dao5]
    • 我不知道。wǒ bù zhī dàoI don't know.
    • 我们知道。wǒ men zhī dàoWe know.
  • 时候shí houtime; whenTraditional: 時候
    Dictionary senseslength of time; moment; period
    • 什么时候？shén me shí houWhen?
    • 你什么时候来？nǐ shén me shí hou láiWhen will you come?
  • 问题wèn tíquestion; problemMeasure word: 个Traditional: 問題
    Dictionary sensesissue; topic
    • 好问题。hǎo wèn tíGood question!
    • 有问题？yǒu wèn tíProblem?
  • 工作gōng zuòto work; jobMeasure word: 个、份、项
    Dictionary senses(of a machine) to operate; work; task
    • 你做什么工作？nǐ zuò shén me gōng zuòWhat's your job?
    • 她做什么工作？tā zuò shén me gōng zuòWhat's her job?
