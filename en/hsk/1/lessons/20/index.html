  • 中学生zhōng xué shēngsecondary school studentTraditional: 中學生
    Dictionary sensesmiddle-school student; high school student
  • 星期日xīng qī rìSunday
    • 今天是星期日。jīn tiān shì xīng qī rìToday is Sunday.
  • 汉语Hàn yǔChinese (language)Traditional: 漢語
    Dictionary sensesChinese language
    • 我学习汉语。wǒ xué xí Hàn yǔI'm learning Chinese.
    • 我会说汉语。wǒ huì shuō Hàn yǔI speak Chinese.
  • 包子bāo zisteamed stuffed bunMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesbaozi; bao (steamed stuffed bun)
  • 不客气bú kè qiyou're welcomeTraditional: 不客氣
    Dictionary sensesdon't mention it; impolite; rude; blunt
    • 不客气。bú kè qiYou're welcome.
  • 汉字Hàn zìChinese characterTraditional: 漢字
    Dictionary sensesChinese character(s)
    • 我在学汉字。wǒ zài xué Hàn zìI'm studying hanzi.
    • 汉字有多少个？Hàn zì yǒu duō shao gèHow many hanzi are there?
  • 好玩儿hǎo wán rfunTraditional: 好玩兒
    Dictionary senseserhua variant of 好玩[hao3 wan2]
  • 没事méi shìit's nothing; never mindTraditional: 沒事
    Dictionary sensesit's not important; to have nothing to do; to be free
    • 我没事。wǒ méi shìI'm OK.
    • 他没事。tā méi shìThere is nothing wrong with him.
  • 那个nà gethat oneTraditional: 那個
    Dictionary sensesthat thing; that (as opposed to this); (used before a verb or adjective for emphasis); (used to humorously or indirectly refer to sth embarrassing, funny etc, or when one can't think of the right word)
    • 那个人是谁？nà ge rén shì shéiWho's that person?
    • 他也看见那个。tā yě kàn jiàn nà geHe saw it also.
  • 哪个nǎ gewhich oneTraditional: 哪個
    Dictionary senseswhich; who
    • 哪个是我的？nǎ ge shì wǒ deWhich is mine?
    • 说你想要哪个。shuō nǐ xiǎng yào nǎ geSay which you would like.
  • 你好nǐ hǎohello
    Dictionary senseshi
    • 你好吗？nǐ hǎo maHow are you?
    • 你好！我叫小雨。nǐ hǎo wǒ jiào xiǎo yǔHello! I'm called Xiaoyu.
  • 一下yí xià(after a verb) a bit; once
    Dictionary sensesa little (indicating brief duration, or softening the tone, or suggesting giving sth a try); all at once; suddenly
    • 请坐一下。qǐng zuò yí xiàPlease sit down.
    • 现在休息一下。xiàn zài xiū xi yí xiàTry resting for now.
  • 这个zhè gethis oneTraditional: 這個
    Dictionary senses(pronoun) this; (adjective) this
    • 听这个！tīng zhè geListen to this!
    • 这个多少？zhè ge duō shaoHow much is it?
  • 面条儿miàn tiáo rnoodlesTraditional: 麵條兒
    Dictionary senseserhua variant of 麵條|面条[mian4 tiao2]
  • 有一点儿yǒu yì diǎn ra bit (usually negative)Traditional: 有一點兒Syllabus form: 有（一）点儿
    Dictionary sensesa bit; a little
