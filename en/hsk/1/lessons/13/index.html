  • 漂亮piào liangpretty; beautiful
    • 她很漂亮。tā hěn piào liangShe's pretty.
    • 她很漂亮，不是吗？tā hěn piào liang bù shì maShe's very pretty, isn't she?
  • 高兴gāo xìnghappy; gladTraditional: 高興
    Dictionary senseswilling (to do sth); in a cheerful mood
    • 他很高兴。tā hěn gāo xìngHe's happy.
    • 我好高兴。wǒ hǎo gāo xìngI'm so happy.
  • 去年qù niánlast year
    • 去年下了很多雪。qù nián xià le hěn duō xuěIt snowed a lot last year.
  • 热rèhotTraditional: 熱
    Dictionary sensesto warm up; to heat up; hot (of weather); heat
    • 天很热。tiān hěn rèIt's hot.
    • 今天很热。jīn tiān hěn rèToday is very warm.
  • 睡觉shuì jiàoto sleep; to go to bedTraditional: 睡覺
    • 我在睡觉。wǒ zài shuì jiàoI'm sleeping.
    • 猫在睡觉。māo zài shuì jiàoThe cat's asleep.
  • 电脑diàn nǎocomputerMeasure word: 台Traditional: 電腦
    • 你的电脑怎么了？nǐ de diàn nǎo zěn me leWhat happened to your computer?
    • 我对电脑知道的不多。wǒ duì diàn nǎo zhī dào de bù duōI don't know much about computers.
  • 第dìordinal prefix (first, second…)
    Dictionary senses(prefix indicating ordinal number, as in 第六[di4 liu4] "sixth"); (literary) grades in which successful candidates in the imperial examinations were placed; (old) residence of a high official; (literary) but
    • 你是第一。nǐ shì dì yīYou're the first.
    • 第一天怎么样？dì yī tiān zěn me yàngHow's the first day?
  • 读dúto read (aloud); to studyTraditional: 讀
    Dictionary sensesto read out; to read aloud; to read; to attend (school)
    • 我想读。wǒ xiǎng dúI want to read.
    • 他在读大学。tā zài dú dà xuéHe is in college.
  • 冷lěngcold
    • 天很冷。tiān hěn lěngIt's cold.
    • 这里很冷。zhè li hěn lěngIt's very cold here.
  • 飞机fēi jīairplaneMeasure word: 架Traditional: 飛機
    • 那飞机真大！nà fēi jī zhēn dàThat airplane is huge!
    • 飞机上可以吃东西吗？fēi jī shàng kě yǐ chī dōng xi maCan I eat on the plane?
  • 早上zǎo shangmorningMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesearly morning
    • 早上好！zǎo shang hǎoGood morning!
    • 大家早上好。dà jiā zǎo shang hǎoGood morning, everyone.
  • 猫māocatTraditional: 貓
    Dictionary sensescat (CL:隻|只[zhi1]); (dialect) to hide oneself; (loanword) (coll.) modem
    • 我是猫。wǒ shì māoI'm a cat.
    • 我没有猫。wǒ méi yǒu māoI don't have a cat.
  • 打电话dǎ diàn huàto make a phone callTraditional: 打電話
    Dictionary sensesto make a telephone call
    • 打电话回家！dǎ diàn huà huí jiāCall home!
    • 打电话回家。dǎ diàn huà huí jiāRing home.
  • 休息xiū xito rest
    Dictionary sensesrest
    • 我没有休息的时间。wǒ méi yǒu xiū xi de shí jiānI have no time to have a rest.
    • 现在休息一下。xiàn zài xiū xi yí xiàTry resting for now.
