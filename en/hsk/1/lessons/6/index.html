  • 今天jīn tiāntoday
    Dictionary sensesthe present time; now
    • 你今天要做什么？nǐ jīn tiān yào zuò shén meWhat do you want to do today?
    • 今天来了很多人。jīn tiān lái le hěn duō rénA lot of people came today.
  • 觉得jué deto think; to feelTraditional: 覺得
    Dictionary sensesto think that ...; to feel that ...; to feel (uncomfortable etc)
    • 我觉得他会来。wǒ jué de tā huì láiI think he'll come.
    • 我觉得他是对的。wǒ jué de tā shì duì deI have a feeling he is right.
  • 月yuèmonth; moonMeasure word: 个、轮
    Dictionary sensesmonthly
    • 四月到了。sì yuè dào leIt's April.
    • 他下个月要去那里。tā xià gè yuè yào qù nà liHe will go there next month.
  • 几jǐhow many (small number); a fewTraditional: 幾
    Dictionary senseshow much; how many; several
    • 几点了？jǐ diǎn leWhat time is it?
    • 现在几点？xiàn zài jǐ diǎnWhat time is it?
  • 见jiànto see; to meetTraditional: 見
    Dictionary sensesto appear (to be sth); to interview; opinion
    • 见到你真好。jiàn dào nǐ zhēn hǎoGood to see you.
    • 好想见到你。hǎo xiǎng jiàn dào nǐI miss you so much.
  • 车chēcar; vehicleMeasure word: 辆Traditional: 車
    Dictionary sensesmachine; to shape with a lathe; Kangxi radical 159
    • 上车吧！shàng chē baClimb aboard!
    • 你有车吗？nǐ yǒu chē maDo you have a car?
  • 一些yì xiēsome
    Dictionary sensesa few; a little; (following an adjective) slightly ...er
    • 我有一些钱。wǒ yǒu yì xiē qiánI have some money.
    • 请再给我一些水。qǐng zài gěi wǒ yì xiē shuǐI'd like some more water.
  • 大家dà jiāeverybody
    Dictionary senseseveryone; influential family; great expert
    • 大家好。dà jiā hǎoHi everybody.
    • 大家好！dà jiā hǎoHello all!
  • 听tīngto listenTraditional: 聽
    Dictionary sensesto listen to; to hear; to heed; to obey
    • 谁在听？shéi zài tīngWho's listening?
    • 你们在听吗？nǐ men zài tīng maAre you listening?
  • 非常fēi chángvery; extremely
    Dictionary sensesreally; unusual; extraordinary
    • 我非常想你。wǒ fēi cháng xiǎng nǐI miss you badly.
    • 我非常想见到他。wǒ fēi cháng xiǎng jiàn dào tāI want to see him very much.
  • 开kāito open; to driveTraditional: 開
    Dictionary sensesto open (transitive or intransitive); (of ships, vehicles, troops etc) to start; to turn on; to put in operation
  • 叫jiàoto be called; to call
    Dictionary sensesto shout; to order; to ask
    • 她叫什么？tā jiào shén meWhat's her name?
    • 我叫了你。wǒ jiào le nǐI called you.
  • 孩子hái zichild
    • 他没有孩子。tā méi yǒu hái ziHe has no children.
    • 你有孩子吗？nǐ yǒu hái zi maDo you have a child?
  • 这里zhè lihereTraditional: 這裏
    • 在这里吗？zài zhè li maIs it here?
    • 我家在这里。wǒ jiā zài zhè liMy house is here.
  • 请qǐngplease; to inviteTraditional: 請
    Dictionary sensesto ask; please (do sth); to treat (to a meal etc); to request
    • 请给我打电话。qǐng gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huàPlease call me.
    • 她请了我吃晚饭。tā qǐng le wǒ chī wǎn fànShe invited me to dinner.
