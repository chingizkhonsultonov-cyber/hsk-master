  • 电影diàn yǐngfilm; movieMeasure word: 部、片、场Traditional: 電影
    • 我在看电影。wǒ zài kàn diàn yǐngI'm watching a movie.
    • 我喜欢看电影。wǒ xǐ huan kàn diàn yǐngI like movies.
  • 哪nǎwhich
    Dictionary senses(emphatic sentence-final particle, used instead of 啊[a5] after a word ending in "n")
    • 我的书在哪？wǒ de shū zài nǎWhere is my book?
    • 你妈妈在哪？nǐ mā ma zài nǎWhere's your mom?
  • 昨天zuó tiānyesterday
    • 她昨天去了医院。tā zuó tiān qù le yī yuànShe went to the hospital yesterday.
    • 你昨天几点睡的？nǐ zuó tiān jǐ diǎn shuì deWhen did you go to bed last night?
  • 晚wǎnlate; evening
    Dictionary sensesnight
    • 很晚了。hěn wǎn leIt's late.
    • 她晚了起床。tā wǎn le qǐ chuángShe got up late.
  • 书shūbookMeasure word: 本、册、部Traditional: 書
    Dictionary sensesletter; document; to write
    • 他在看书。tā zài kàn shūHe reads.
    • 我没有书。wǒ méi yǒu shūI have no books.
  • 五wǔfive
    Dictionary senses5
    • 我五点在家。wǒ wǔ diǎn zài jiāI will be at home at five.
    • 再给我五分钟。zài gěi wǒ wǔ fēn zhōngGive me five more minutes.
  • 先生xiān shengMr; sir; husbandMeasure word: 位
    Dictionary sensesteacher; gentleman; mister (Mr.)
  • 男nánmale
    Dictionary sensesbaron, the lowest of the five ranks of nobility 五等爵位[wu3 deng3 jue2 wei4]
  • 卖màito sellTraditional: 賣
    Dictionary sensesto betray; to spare no effort; to show off or flaunt
    • 卖车是我的工作。mài chē shì wǒ de gōng zuòSelling cars is my job.
    • 这是要卖的东西吗？zhè shì yào mài de dōng xi maIs this for sale?
  • 医院yī yuànhospitalMeasure word: 所、家、座Traditional: 醫院
    • 他在医院。tā zài yī yuànHe's in hospital.
    • 这是医院。zhè shì yī yuànThis is a hospital.
  • 些xiēsome; a few
    Dictionary sensesclassifier indicating a small amount or small number greater than 1: some, a few, several
    • 他今天好些了吗？tā jīn tiān hǎo xiē le maIs he better today?
    • 我知道些东西的！wǒ zhī dào xiē dōng xi deI know something!
  • 狗gǒudogMeasure word: 只、条
    • 狗不会叫。gǒu bù huì jiàoThe dog couldn't bark.
    • 这是我的狗。zhè shì wǒ de gǒuHere's my dog.
  • 口kǒumouth; measure word for family members
    Dictionary sensesclassifier for things with mouths (people, domestic animals, cannons, wells etc); classifier for bites or mouthfuls
    • 你家有几口人？nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rénHow many people are in your family?
    • 我们家有五口人。wǒ men jiā yǒu wǔ kǒu rénWe are a family of five.
  • 学生xué shengstudentTraditional: 學生
    Dictionary sensesschoolchild
    • 你是学生。nǐ shì xué shengYou're a student.
    • 我是学生。wǒ shì xué shengI'm a pupil.
  • 二èrtwo
    Dictionary senses2; (Beijing dialect) stupid
    • 一，二，三，四，五。yī èr sān sì wǔOne, two, three, four, five.
    • 我三十二岁。wǒ sān shí èr suìI'm 32 years old.
