  • 哥哥gē geolder brotherMeasure word: 个、位
    • 你哥哥不会买它。nǐ gē ge bù huì mǎi tāYour brother won't buy it.
    • 你的哥哥在哪里？nǐ de gē ge zài nǎ liWhere's your brother?
  • 天气tiān qìweatherTraditional: 天氣
    • 天气冷了。tiān qì lěng leThe weather is cold now.
    • 天气很冷。tiān qì hěn lěngIt's cold.
  • 千qiānthousand
    • 她有两千本书。tā yǒu liǎng qiān běn shūShe has 2,000 books.
    • 五千元是很多钱。wǔ qiān yuán shì hěn duō qián5000 yuan is a lot of money.
  • 同学tóng xuéclassmateMeasure word: 位、个Traditional: 同學
    Dictionary sensesto study at the same school; fellow student
    • 我们是同学。wǒ men shì tóng xuéWe're classmates.
  • 好看hǎo kàngood-looking; interesting (film, book)
    Dictionary sensesnice-looking; (of a movie, book, TV show etc) good; in an embarrassing situation
    • 我好看吗？wǒ hǎo kàn maAm I pretty?
  • 茶cháteaMeasure word: 杯、壶
    Dictionary sensestea plant
    • 请喝茶！qǐng hē cháPlease drink tea.
    • 你喝茶了吗？nǐ hē chá le maDid you drink tea?
  • 雨yǔrainMeasure word: 阵、场
    • 昨天下大雨。zuó tiān xià dà yǔIt rained heavily yesterday.
    • 你好！我叫小雨。nǐ hǎo wǒ jiào xiǎo yǔHello! I'm called Xiaoyu.
  • 星期xīng qīweekMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesday of the week; Sunday
    • 昨天星期几？zuó tiān xīng qī jǐWhat day was yesterday?
    • 今天是星期一。jīn tiān shì xīng qī yīIt's Monday.
  • 雪xuěsnowMeasure word: 场
    Dictionary senses(literary) to wipe away (a humiliation etc)
    • 下雪了。xià xuě leIt snowed.
    • 今天下雪。jīn tiān xià xuěIt's snowy today.
  • 九jiǔnine
    Dictionary senses9
    • 你九点上班。nǐ jiǔ diǎn shàng bānYou work at nine o'clock.
    • 我今年十九岁。wǒ jīn nián shí jiǔ suìI'm 19.
  • 妹妹mèi meiyounger sisterMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesyoung woman
    • 我是你的妹妹。wǒ shì nǐ de mèi meiI'm your sister.
    • 这是我的妹妹。zhè shì wǒ de mèi meiThis is my sister.
  • 女士nǚ shìlady; MsMeasure word: 个、位
    Dictionary sensesmadam; Miss
  • 上午shàng wǔmorning (before noon)Measure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesmorning
    • 上午你有时间吗？shàng wǔ nǐ yǒu shí jiān maDo you have time this morning?
    • 今天上午你们不在医院。jīn tiān shàng wǔ nǐ men bù zài yī yuànYou are not at the hospital this morning.
  • 贵guìexpensiveTraditional: 貴
    Dictionary sensesprecious; of high rank; (prefix) (honorific) your
    • 太贵了！tài guì leToo expensive!
    • 太贵了。tài guì leIt's too expensive.
  • 那边nà biānover thereTraditional: 那邊
    Dictionary sensesyonder
    • 那边有人吗？nà biān yǒu rén maIs anybody there?
