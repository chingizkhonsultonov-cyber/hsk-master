    • 他是我儿子。tā shì wǒ ér ziHe's my son.
    • 你有几个儿子？nǐ yǒu jǐ gè ér ziHow many sons do you have?
  • 歌gēsong
    Dictionary sensessong (CL:首[shou3],支[zhi1])
    • 请唱歌。qǐng chàng gēPlease sing.
    • 我喜欢唱歌。wǒ xǐ huan chàng gēI like to sing.
  • 半bànhalf
    Dictionary sensessemi-; incomplete; (after a number) and a half
    • 我今天早上六点半起床。wǒ jīn tiān zǎo shang liù diǎn bàn qǐ chuángI got up at six-thirty this morning.
  • 中国Zhōng guóChinaTraditional: 中國
    • 我在中国。wǒ zài Zhōng guóI'm in China.
    • 我不是中国人。wǒ bù shì Zhōng guó rénI'm not Chinese.
  • 店diànshop
    Dictionary sensesinn; old-style hotel (CL:家[jia1]); store
  • 元yuányuan
    Dictionary sensescurrency unit (esp. Chinese yuan); original; primary; fundamental
    • 五千元是很多钱。wǔ qiān yuán shì hěn duō qián5000 yuan is a lot of money.
  • 哪里nǎ liwhereTraditional: 哪裏
    Dictionary senseswhere?; somewhere; anywhere; wherever

    • 哪里有问题？nǎ li yǒu wèn tíWhat is the problem?
  • 那里nà lithereTraditional: 那裏
    Dictionary sensesthat place

    • 我想去那里。wǒ xiǎng qù nà liI want to go there.
  • 电视diàn shìtelevisionMeasure word: 台、部Traditional: 電視

    • 你有电视吗？nǐ yǒu diàn shì maDo you have a TV?
  • 下午xià wǔafternoonMeasure word: 个
    Dictionary sensesp.m.

  • 学习xué xíto study; to learnTraditional: 學習

  • 六liùsix
    Dictionary senses6

  • 边biānside; edgeTraditional: 邊
