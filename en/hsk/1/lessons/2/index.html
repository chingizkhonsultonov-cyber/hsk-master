  • 要yàoto want; will; need
    Dictionary sensesto need; to ask for; shall
    • 我要睡了。wǒ yào shuì leI'm going to bed.
    • 你想要什么？nǐ xiǎng yào shén meWhat do you want?
  • 好hǎogood; well
    Dictionary sensesappropriate; proper; all right!; (before a verb) easy to
    • 我是好人。wǒ shì hǎo rénI'm a nice guy.
    • 他是个好人。tā shì gè hǎo rénHe's a good man.
  • 一yīone
    Dictionary sensessingle; a (article); as soon as; entire
    • 你是一个好人。nǐ shì yī gè hǎo rénYou're a good person.
    • 我是一个好人。wǒ shì yī gè hǎo rénI'm a good guy.
  • 会huìcan; to know how toTraditional: 會
    Dictionary sensesto have the skill; to be likely to; to be sure to
    • 他会来的。tā huì lái deHe'll come.
    • 我会很想你的。wǒ huì hěn xiǎng nǐ deI'll miss you very much.
  • 也yěalso; too
    Dictionary sensesas well; (not ...) either; (used after a verbal or nominal expression X to indicate that X is an extreme or unexpected case) even (X)
    • 我也是。wǒ yě shìMe too.
    • 我也去了。wǒ yě qù leI also went.
  • 都dōuall; both
    Dictionary sensesentirely; (used for emphasis) even; already
    • 我一点都不好。wǒ yī diǎn dōu bù hǎoI'm not well at all.
    • 我的朋友人都很好。wǒ de péng you rén dōu hěn hǎoMy friends are all really nice.
  • 对duìright; correct; toTraditional: 對
    Dictionary sensesat; for
    • 你是对的。nǐ shì duì deYou're right.
    • 这是对的。zhè shì duì deIt's true.
  • 到dàoto arrive; to reach; to
    Dictionary sensesto leave for; to go to; to (a place)
    • 我到了。wǒ dào leI came.
    • 我们到了！wǒ men dào leHere we are!
  • 上shàngon; above; to go up
    Dictionary sensesupper; first (of multiple parts); to climb
    • 我爱上她了。wǒ ài shàng tā leI'm in love with her.
    • 上车吧！shàng chē baClimb aboard!
  • 来láito comeTraditional: 來
    Dictionary senses(used as a substitute for a more specific verb); hither (directional complement for motion toward the speaker, as in 回來|回来[hui2 lai5]); ever since (as in 自古以來|自古以来[zi4 gu3 yi3 lai2]); for the past (amount of time)
    • 他来了。tā lái leHe came.
    • 我来了。wǒ lái leI came.
  • 很hěnvery
    Dictionary sensesquite; (also, often used before an adjective without intensifying its meaning, i.e. as a meaningless syntactic element)
    • 他很忙。tā hěn mángHe's busy.
    • 很晚了。hěn wǎn leIt's late.
  • 还háistill; alsoTraditional: 還
    Dictionary sensesstill in progress; still more; yet; even more
    • 还没有。hái méi yǒuNot yet.
    • 我还在看。wǒ hái zài kànI'm still reading.
  • 去qùto go
    Dictionary sensesto go to (a place); (of a time etc) last; just passed; to send
    • 我不去。wǒ bù qùI will not go.
    • 我想去。wǒ xiǎng qùI want to go.
  • 她tāshe; her
    • 我爱她。wǒ ài tāI love her.
    • 她很忙。tā hěn mángShe's busy.
  • 想xiǎngto think; to want; to miss
    Dictionary sensesto think (about); to think of; to devise; to think (that)
    • 我想你。wǒ xiǎng nǐI miss you.
    • 我想你！wǒ xiǎng nǐI miss you!
