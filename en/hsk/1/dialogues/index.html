Greetings and introductions

1. Meeting a new classmate

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你好！Nǐ hǎo!Hi!
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà你好！你叫什么名字？Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?Hi! What's your name?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我叫王明。你呢？Wǒ jiào Wáng Míng. Nǐ ne?I'm Wang Ming. And you?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà我叫安娜。我是新同学。Wǒ jiào Ānnà. Wǒ shì xīn tóngxué.I'm Anna. I'm a new classmate.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng你是哪国人？Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?Which country are you from?
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà我是俄国人。认识你很高兴！Wǒ shì Éguó rén. Rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng!I'm Russian. Nice to meet you!
  7. 王明 · Wáng Míng认识你，我也很高兴！Rènshi nǐ, wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng!Nice to meet you too!

2. Saying goodbye

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng现在几点了？Xiànzài jǐ diǎn le?What time is it now?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà五点半。我要回家了。Wǔ diǎn bàn. Wǒ yào huí jiā le.Half past five. I need to go home.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng好，明天见！Hǎo, míngtiān jiàn!OK, see you tomorrow!
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà明天见！你晚上做什么？Míngtiān jiàn! Nǐ wǎnshang zuò shénme?See you tomorrow! What are you doing tonight?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng我在家看书。再见！Wǒ zài jiā kàn shū. Zàijiàn!I'm reading at home. Bye!
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà再见！Zàijiàn!Bye!

Family

1. Looking at a family photo

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà这是你家人吗？Zhè shì nǐ jiārén ma?Is this your family?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng是。这是我爸爸，这是我妈妈。Shì. Zhè shì wǒ bàba, zhè shì wǒ māma.Yes. This is my dad, and this is my mum.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà这个男孩子是谁？Zhège nán háizi shì shéi?Who's this boy?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng他是我弟弟，今年十岁。Tā shì wǒ dìdi, jīnnián shí suì.He's my little brother; he's ten this year.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà你家有几口人？Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?How many people are in your family?
  6. 王明 · Wáng Míng我家有四口人。Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.There are four people in my family.

2. Brothers and sisters

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你有哥哥姐姐吗？Nǐ yǒu gēge jiějie ma?Do you have older brothers or sisters?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我有一个姐姐，没有哥哥。Wǒ yǒu yí ge jiějie, méiyǒu gēge.I have an older sister, no older brother.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà你姐姐多大了？Nǐ jiějie duō dà le?How old is your sister?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng她二十三岁，是大学生。Tā èrshísān suì, shì dàxuéshēng.She's twenty-three and at university.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà她漂亮吗？Tā piàoliang ma?Is she pretty?
  6. 王明 · Wáng Míng很漂亮！她还很会唱歌。Hěn piàoliang! Tā hái hěn huì chàng gē.Very! And she sings really well.

At a restaurant

1. Ordering at a restaurant

  1. 服务员 · fúwùyuán您好！请问几个人？Nín hǎo! Qǐngwèn jǐ ge rén?Hello! How many people, please?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng两个人。Liǎng ge rén.Two.
  3. 服务员 · fúwùyuán请坐。你们想吃什么？Qǐng zuò. Nǐmen xiǎng chī shénme?Please sit down. What would you like to eat?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng我们要一个菜和米饭。Wǒmen yào yí ge cài hé mǐfàn.We'd like one dish and some rice.
  5. 服务员 · fúwùyuán喝什么？Hē shénme?Anything to drink?
  6. 王明 · Wáng Míng两杯茶，谢谢。Liǎng bēi chá, xièxie.Two cups of tea, thanks.

2. What do you like to eat?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你喜欢吃什么？Nǐ xǐhuan chī shénme?What do you like to eat?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我喜欢吃饺子。你呢？Wǒ xǐhuan chī jiǎozi. Nǐ ne?I like dumplings. And you?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我喜欢吃面条儿和包子。Wǒ xǐhuan chī miàntiáor hé bāozi.I like noodles and steamed buns.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng我知道一个饭店，饺子很好吃。Wǒ zhīdào yí ge fàndiàn, jiǎozi hěn hǎochī.I know a restaurant with really good dumplings.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà太好了！我们明天中午去吧。Tài hǎo le! Wǒmen míngtiān zhōngwǔ qù ba.Great! Let's go tomorrow at noon.

Shopping

1. Buying fruit

  1. 卖水果的 · mài shuǐguǒ de您要买什么？Nín yào mǎi shénme?What would you like?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà苹果多少钱一个？Píngguǒ duōshao qián yí ge?How much is an apple?
  3. 卖水果的 · mài shuǐguǒ de五块钱一个。Wǔ kuài qián yí ge.Five yuan each.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà有点儿贵。四块，可以吗？Yǒudiǎnr guì. Sì kuài, kěyǐ ma?A bit expensive. Four yuan, OK?
  5. 卖水果的 · mài shuǐguǒ de好吧，您要几个？Hǎo ba, nín yào jǐ ge?All right. How many?
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà两个。给你八块钱。Liǎng ge. Gěi nǐ bā kuài qián.Two. Here's eight yuan.

2. At the supermarket

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng我们家没有米了。Wǒmen jiā méiyǒu mǐ le.We've run out of rice at home.
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà我们去超市买吧。还要买什么？Wǒmen qù chāoshì mǎi ba. Hái yào mǎi shénme?Let's go to the supermarket. What else do we need?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng鸡蛋、牛奶，还有一些水果。Jīdàn, niúnǎi, hái yǒu yìxiē shuǐguǒ.Eggs, milk and some fruit.
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà超市在哪儿？Chāoshì zài nǎr?Where's the supermarket?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng在学校外边，走五分钟。Zài xuéxiào wàibian, zǒu wǔ fēnzhōng.Outside the school, a five-minute walk.

Clothes

1. Buying clothes

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà这件衣服很好看。多少钱？Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn hǎokàn. Duōshao qián?This item of clothing looks nice. How much is it?
  2. 店员 · diànyuán三百块。Sānbǎi kuài.Three hundred yuan.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà太贵了！有便宜一点儿的吗？Tài guì le! Yǒu piányi yìdiǎnr de ma?Too expensive! Do you have anything cheaper?
  4. 店员 · diànyuán那件一百五十块，也很漂亮。Nà jiàn yìbǎi wǔshí kuài, yě hěn piàoliang.That one is 150 yuan, and it's pretty too.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà好，我买那件。Hǎo, wǒ mǎi nà jiàn.OK, I'll buy that one.

2. What to wear today

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà今天很冷，我穿什么？Jīntiān hěn lěng, wǒ chuān shénme?It's cold today. What should I wear?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng穿这件大衣服吧。Chuān zhè jiàn dà yīfu ba.Wear this big coat.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà这件不好看。Zhè jiàn bù hǎokàn.This one doesn't look nice.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng好看不好看没关系，不冷就好。Hǎokàn bù hǎokàn méi guānxi, bù lěng jiù hǎo.Never mind how it looks; as long as you're not cold.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà好吧，我听你的。Hǎo ba, wǒ tīng nǐ de.All right, I'll do as you say.

Days, dates and birthdays

1. What day is it today?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà今天几月几号？Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng今天十月二十号。Jīntiān shí yuè èrshí hào.Today is 20 October.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà星期几？Xīngqī jǐ?What day of the week?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng星期五。明天不上课。Xīngqīwǔ. Míngtiān bú shàngkè.Friday. No classes tomorrow.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà太好了！我明天想睡觉。Tài hǎo le! Wǒ míngtiān xiǎng shuìjiào.Great! Tomorrow I want to sleep.

2. When is your birthday?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你的生日是几月几号？Nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?When is your birthday?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà三月八号。你呢？Sān yuè bā hào. Nǐ ne?8 March. And yours?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我的生日是明天！Wǒ de shēngrì shì míngtiān!My birthday is tomorrow!
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà真的吗？你多大了？Zhēn de ma? Nǐ duō dà le?Really? How old will you be?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng明天我二十岁。Míngtiān wǒ èrshí suì.Tomorrow I'll be twenty.
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà我们晚上一起吃饭吧！Wǒmen wǎnshang yìqǐ chī fàn ba!Let's have dinner together in the evening!

Weekend plans

1. Making a plan for the weekend

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng星期天你有时间吗？Xīngqītiān nǐ yǒu shíjiān ma?Are you free on Sunday?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà有。你想做什么？Yǒu. Nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?Yes. What do you want to do?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我们去看电影，好吗？Wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng, hǎo ma?Shall we go and see a film?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà好！几点？Hǎo! Jǐ diǎn?Sure! What time?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng下午三点，在电影院前边见。Xiàwǔ sān diǎn, zài diànyǐngyuàn qiánbian jiàn.Three in the afternoon; meet in front of the cinema.
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà好，星期天见！Hǎo, xīngqītiān jiàn!OK, see you on Sunday!

2. Visiting a friend

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng明天你来我家玩儿吧。Míngtiān nǐ lái wǒ jiā wánr ba.Come over to my place tomorrow.
  2. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好！你家在哪儿？Hǎo! Nǐ jiā zài nǎr?Great! Where do you live?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng在学校后边，大学医院那边。Zài xuéxiào hòubian, dàxué yīyuàn nàbian.Behind the school, near the university hospital.
  4. 李明 · Lǐ Míng几点去？Jǐ diǎn qù?What time should I come?
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng上午十点。我们一起做饭。Shàngwǔ shí diǎn. Wǒmen yìqǐ zuò fàn.Ten in the morning. We'll cook together.
  6. 李明 · Lǐ Míng好，明天见！Hǎo, míngtiān jiàn!OK, see you tomorrow!

Weather

1. Talking about the weather

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà今天天气怎么样？Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?What's the weather like today?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng很冷，下午会下雪。Hěn lěng, xiàwǔ huì xià xuě.Very cold; it'll snow this afternoon.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà我很喜欢下雪！Wǒ hěn xǐhuan xià xuě!I love snow!
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng你多穿点儿衣服，不要生病。Nǐ duō chuān diǎnr yīfu, búyào shēngbìng.Wear more clothes so you don't get ill.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà知道了，谢谢！Zhīdào le, xièxie!Got it, thanks!

2. It's raining

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà外边下雨了。Wàibian xià yǔ le.It's raining outside.
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng下得大吗？Xià de dà ma?Is it heavy?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà很大。我们今天不出去了吧。Hěn dà. Wǒmen jīntiān bù chūqu le ba.Very. Let's not go out today.
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng好，在家看电视。Hǎo, zài jiā kàn diànshì.OK, we'll watch TV at home.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà我去做一点儿吃的。Wǒ qù zuò yìdiǎnr chī de.I'll go and make something to eat.

Getting around

1. Taking a taxi

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà你好，我去大学。Nǐ hǎo, wǒ qù dàxué.Hi, I'm going to the university.
  2. 司机 · sījī好。您几点上课？Hǎo. Nín jǐ diǎn shàngkè?OK. What time is your class?
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà十一点。现在去，可以吗？Shíyī diǎn. Xiànzài qù, kěyǐ ma?Eleven. Can we make it if we go now?
  4. 司机 · sījī可以，要二十分钟。Kěyǐ, yào èrshí fēnzhōng.Yes, it takes twenty minutes.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà太好了，谢谢！Tài hǎo le, xièxie!Great, thanks!

2. Train or plane?

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你明年去中国吗？Nǐ míngnián qù Zhōngguó ma?Are you going to China next year?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà去。我想坐火车去。Qù. Wǒ xiǎng zuò huǒchē qù.Yes. I want to go by train.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng坐火车要很多天！Zuò huǒchē yào hěn duō tiān!The train takes many days!
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà没关系，我想看看外边。Méi guānxi, wǒ xiǎng kànkan wàibian.That's fine; I want to see the views.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng我喜欢坐飞机，只要几个小时。Wǒ xǐhuan zuò fēijī, zhǐ yào jǐ ge xiǎoshí.I like flying; it only takes a few hours.

Asking the way

1. Asking the way

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng请问，书店在哪儿？Qǐngwèn, shūdiàn zài nǎr?Excuse me, where's the bookshop?
  2. 女士 · nǚshì在那边，医院后边。Zài nàbian, yīyuàn hòubian.Over there, behind the hospital.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng要几分钟？Yào jǐ fēnzhōng?How many minutes does it take?
  4. 女士 · nǚshì五分钟。Wǔ fēnzhōng.Five minutes.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng谢谢您！Xièxie nín!Thank you!
  6. 女士 · nǚshì不客气。Bú kèqi.You're welcome.

2. Where's the hospital?

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà先生，请问医院在哪儿？Xiānsheng, qǐngwèn yīyuàn zài nǎr?Excuse me, sir, where's the hospital?
  2. 先生 · xiānsheng在前边，商店那边。Zài qiánbian, shāngdiàn nàbian.Up ahead, by the shop.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà前边那个大楼吗？Qiánbian nàge dà lóu ma?That big building ahead?
  4. 先生 · xiānsheng对，就是那个。Duì, jiù shì nàge.Yes, that's the one.
  5. 安娜 · Ānnà谢谢！Xièxie!Thanks!

At school

1. Saying hello to a teacher

  1. 学生 · xuésheng老师，早上好！Lǎoshī, zǎoshang hǎo!Good morning, teacher!
  2. 老师 · lǎoshī早上好！你今天来得很早。Zǎoshang hǎo! Nǐ jīntiān lái de hěn zǎo.Good morning! You're early today.
  3. 学生 · xuésheng是，今天上午有汉语课。Shì, jīntiān shàngwǔ yǒu Hànyǔ kè.Yes, there's a Chinese class this morning.
  4. 老师 · lǎoshī你喜欢学汉语吗？Nǐ xǐhuan xué Hànyǔ ma?Do you like learning Chinese?
  5. 学生 · xuésheng非常喜欢！汉字很好玩儿。Fēicháng xǐhuan! Hànzì hěn hǎowánr.I love it! Chinese characters are really fun.
  6. 老师 · lǎoshī很好！我们上课吧。Hěn hǎo! Wǒmen shàngkè ba.Great! Let's start the class.

2. I don't understand

  1. 学生 · xuésheng老师，这个字怎么读？Lǎoshī, zhège zì zěnme dú?Teacher, how do you read this character?
  2. 老师 · lǎoshī读“hǎo”，好。Dú “hǎo”, hǎo.It's read “hǎo” — 好, good.
  3. 学生 · xuésheng这个字呢？Zhège zì ne?And this one?
  4. 老师 · lǎoshī这是“学”，学习的“学”。Zhè shì “xué”, xuéxí de “xué”.This is “xué”, as in 学习, to study.
  5. 学生 · xuésheng谢谢老师！我会了。Xièxie lǎoshī! Wǒ huì le.Thank you! Now I've got it.

Jobs and work

1. Parents' jobs

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng你爸爸做什么工作？Nǐ bàba zuò shénme gōngzuò?What does your dad do?
  2. 安娜 · Ānnà他是医生，在医院工作。Tā shì yīshēng, zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.He's a doctor; he works at a hospital.
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng你妈妈呢？Nǐ māma ne?And your mum?
  4. 安娜 · Ānnà我妈妈是老师，在中学上课。Wǒ māma shì lǎoshī, zài zhōngxué shàngkè.My mum is a teacher; she teaches at a secondary school.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng他们很忙吗？Tāmen hěn máng ma?Are they very busy?
  6. 安娜 · Ānnà很忙，晚上七点回家。Hěn máng, wǎnshang qī diǎn huí jiā.Very busy; they get home at seven in the evening.

2. Where do you work?

  1. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你在哪儿工作？Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?Where do you work?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我在一家公司工作。Wǒ zài yì jiā gōngsī gōngzuò.I work at a company.
  3. 李明 · Lǐ Míng你每天几点上班？Nǐ měi tiān jǐ diǎn shàngbān?What time do you start work every day?
  4. 王明 · Wáng Míng九点上班，六点下班。Jiǔ diǎn shàngbān, liù diǎn xiàbān.I start at nine and finish at six.
  5. 李明 · Lǐ Míng工作忙不忙？Gōngzuò máng bu máng?Is the work busy?
  6. 王明 · Wáng Míng很忙，可是我很喜欢。Hěn máng, kěshì wǒ hěn xǐhuan.Very busy, but I really like it.

On the phone

1. A phone call

  1. 王明 · Wáng Míng喂，请问李先生在吗？Wéi, qǐngwèn Lǐ xiānsheng zài ma?Hello, is Mr Li there, please?
  2. 李先生 · Lǐ xiānsheng是我。您是谁？Shì wǒ. Nín shì shéi?Speaking. Who's calling?
  3. 王明 · Wáng Míng我是王明。您明天上午在公司吗？Wǒ shì Wáng Míng. Nín míngtiān shàngwǔ zài gōngsī ma?This is Wang Ming. Will you be at the office tomorrow morning?
  4. 李先生 · Lǐ xiānsheng在。你十点来吧。Zài. Nǐ shí diǎn lái ba.Yes. Come at ten.
  5. 王明 · Wáng Míng好的，谢谢！明天见。Hǎo de, xièxie! Míngtiān jiàn.OK, thank you! See you tomorrow.

2. Calling a friend

  1. 安娜 · Ānnà喂，王明，你在做什么？Wéi, Wáng Míng, nǐ zài zuò shénme?Hello, Wang Ming, what are you doing?
  2. 王明 · Wáng Míng我在睡觉。Wǒ zài shuìjiào.I'm sleeping.
  3. 安娜 · Ānnà现在是下午两点！Xiànzài shì xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn!It's two in the afternoon!
