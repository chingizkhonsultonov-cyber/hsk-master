  1. 1 · 是字句

    Sentences with 是 (to be)

    Structure

    A + 是 + B　·　A + 不是 + B

    是 links two nouns: “A is B”. It is not used before adjectives. To negate it, put 不 before 是 (never 没).

    Examples
    • 我是学生。Wǒ shì xuésheng.I am a student.
    • 她不是老师，她是医生。Tā bú shì lǎoshī, tā shì yīshēng.She isn't a teacher, she's a doctor.

    Exam note: “我是很高兴” is wrong. Adjectives take 很 without 是: 我很高兴.

  2. 2 · 吗字疑问句

    Yes/no questions with 吗

    Structure

    Statement + 吗？

    Add 吗 to the end of a statement to make a yes/no question. Word order does not change. Answer by repeating the verb or adjective, with or without 不.

    Examples
    • 你是中国人吗？Nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma?Are you Chinese?
    • 你喜欢喝茶吗？——喜欢。Nǐ xǐhuan hē chá ma? — Xǐhuan.Do you like tea? — Yes.

    Exam note: Never use 吗 together with a question word: “你叫什么名字吗？” is wrong.

  3. 3 · 疑问代词

    Question words

    Structure

    谁 · 什么 · 哪 · 哪儿 · 几 · 多少 · 怎么 · 怎么样

    A question word goes in the same place as the answer would. 谁 who, 什么 what, 哪 which, 哪儿 where, 几 how many (usually under 10, with a measure word), 多少 how many/much (any number), 怎么 how, 怎么样 how is it.

    Examples
    • 他是谁？Tā shì shéi?Who is he?
    • 你家有几个人？Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén?How many people are in your family?
    • 这个杯子多少钱？Zhège bēizi duōshao qián?How much is this cup?
  4. 4 · 呢

    Follow-up questions with 呢

    Structure

    Noun + 呢？

    呢 after a noun or pronoun asks “and what about …?”, repeating the previous question for someone else. 呢 can also ask where someone or something is.

    Examples
    • 我很好，你呢？Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?I'm fine, and you?
    • 我的书呢？Wǒ de shū ne?Where is my book?
  5. 5 · 结构助词“的”

    Possession with 的

    Structure

    Owner + 的 + thing

    的 links an owner or description to a noun, like English 's. With close family members and one's own country or school, 的 is usually dropped: 我妈妈, 我们学校.

    Examples
    • 这是我的电脑。Zhè shì wǒ de diànnǎo.This is my computer.
    • 那是老师的书。Nà shì lǎoshī de shū.That is the teacher's book.
  6. 6 · 形容词谓语句

    Adjectives as predicates with 很

    Structure

    Subject + 很 + adjective

    Chinese adjectives work like verbs, so no 是 is needed. A plain adjective sounds like a comparison, so 很 is added; here it is often unstressed and means little more than “is”.

    Examples
    • 今天很冷。Jīntiān hěn lěng.It's cold today.
    • 这个苹果不大。Zhège píngguǒ bú dà.This apple isn't big.

    Exam note: Negation uses 不 and drops 很: 不冷, not 很不冷 (which means “very not cold”).

  7. 7 · 不和没

    Negation: 不 and 没

    Structure

    不 + verb/adjective　·　没(有) + verb　·　没有 + noun

    不 negates the present, the future, habits and wishes. 没 negates the past (something did not happen) and 有 (to have): always 没有, never 不有.

    Examples
    • 我明天不去。Wǒ míngtiān bú qù.I'm not going tomorrow.
    • 昨天我没去学校。Zuótiān wǒ méi qù xuéxiào.I didn't go to school yesterday.
    • 我没有猫。Wǒ méiyǒu māo.I don't have a cat.

    Exam note: 不 changes to bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì, 不去 bú qù.

  8. 8 · “有”和“在”

    Existence and location: 有 and 在

    Structure

    Place + 有 + thing　·　Thing/person + 在 + place

    有 says what exists in a place (“there is”). 在 says where a known person or thing is. 在 + place can also come before a verb to say where an action happens.

    Examples
    • 桌子上有一本书。Zhuōzi shang yǒu yì běn shū.There is a book on the table.
    • 妈妈在家。Māma zài jiā.Mum is at home.
    • 我在医院工作。Wǒ zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.I work at a hospital.
  9. 9 · 数词和量词

    Numbers and measure words

    Structure

    Number + measure word + noun　·　这/那 + measure word + noun

    A number cannot go straight before a noun; a measure word comes in between. 个 is the general one. HSK 1 also uses 本 (books), 块 (yuan), 岁 (age), 些 (some). 二 becomes 两 before a measure word.

    Examples
    • 我有两个女儿。Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè nǚ'ér.I have two daughters.
    • 这本书三十块。Zhè běn shū sānshí kuài.This book is 30 yuan.

    Exam note: “两个人”, not “二个人”. 二 is used in counting, in phone numbers and in numbers like 十二.

  10. 10 · 时间的表达

    Dates and times

    Structure

    年 → 月 → 日/号 → 星期 → 上午/下午 → 点 → 分

    Chinese goes from the largest unit to the smallest. Time words come before the verb, either before or after the subject, never at the end of the sentence.

    Examples
    • 今天是九月八号，星期三。Jīntiān shì jiǔ yuè bā hào, xīngqīsān.Today is Wednesday, 8 September.
    • 我下午三点去商店。Wǒ xiàwǔ sān diǎn qù shāngdiàn.I'm going to the shop at 3 pm.
  11. 11 · “了”表示完成或变化

    了 for completed actions and change

    Structure

    Verb + 了 (+ object)　·　Sentence + 了

    了 after a verb marks an action as completed. 了 at the end of a sentence marks a new situation (“now”). The negative of a completed action is 没 + verb, without 了.

    Examples
    • 我买了一个杯子。Wǒ mǎi le yí gè bēizi.I bought a cup.
    • 下雨了。Xià yǔ le.It's started raining.

    Exam note: “我没去了” is wrong. Say 我没去.

  12. 12 · 能愿动词：会、能、想

    Modal verbs 会, 能, 想

    Structure

    Subject + 会/能/想 + verb

    会 is a learned skill or a likely future (“can, will”). 能 is ability or permission in a situation (“be able to, may”). 想 is a wish (“would like to”). All three are negated with 不.

    Examples
    • 我会说汉语。Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.I can speak Chinese.
    • 明天你能来吗？Míngtiān nǐ néng lái ma?Can you come tomorrow?
    • 我想喝水。Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.I'd like some water.
  13. 13 · 太……了

    太 … 了 (too, so)

    Structure

    太 + adjective + 了

    太…了 expresses a strong feeling: “too …” when something is excessive, or “so …!” as praise. 不太 + adjective softens a negative: “not very”.

    Examples
    • 太贵了！Tài guì le!That's too expensive!
    • 你的衣服太漂亮了。Nǐ de yīfu tài piàoliang le.Your clothes are so beautiful.
  14. 14 · “和”与“都”

    和 (and) and 都 (all, both)

    Structure

    Noun + 和 + noun　·　Subject + 都 + verb

    和 joins nouns and pronouns, not verbs or sentences. 都 is an adverb, so it goes after the subject and before the verb.

    Examples
    • 我和我朋友都是学生。Wǒ hé wǒ péngyou dōu shì xuésheng.My friend and I are both students.

    Exam note: “都我们是学生” is wrong: 都 never comes before the subject.

Morphemes语素2
  • Prefixes 前缀小—、第—Prefixes 小 and 第

    小 + name/noun · 第 + number

    小 before a surname is a friendly way to address a younger person: 小王, 小李. 第 before a number makes an ordinal: 第一 first, 第二 second.

    When to use it

    小 + surname is used among friends and colleagues for someone younger or of the same age; for older people use 老 + surname (老王) or a title (王老师). 第 + number answers “which one in order?”: 第几？ which number?

    Negative and questions

    Ask the order with 第几: 你是第几个来的？ Negate the sentence, not 第: 他不是第一.

    • 小王是我的同学。Xiǎo Wáng shì wǒ de tóngxué.Xiao Wang is my classmate.
    • 今天是我第一天上班。Jīntiān shì wǒ dì yī tiān shàngbān.Today is my first day at work.
    • 小李，你好！Xiǎo Lǐ, nǐ hǎo!Hi, Xiao Li!
    • 今天是星期几？我们学第几课？Jīntiān shì xīngqī jǐ? Wǒmen xué dì jǐ kè?What day is it? Which lesson are we on?

    Compare

    一 alone is a count (one book: 一本书); 第一 is an order (the first book: 第一本书).

    Exam note: 第 needs a measure word before a noun: 第一个人, not 第一人 in everyday speech.

  • Suffixes 后缀—们、—边Suffixes 们 and 边

    pronoun/person + 们 · direction + 边

    们 makes pronouns and words for people plural: 我们, 老师们. 边 turns a direction into a place word: 里边 inside, 前边 in front.

    When to use it

    们 is only for people (and pronouns), not for things: 书们 is wrong. 边 makes place words from directions; 这边/那边 mean “this side/over there”.

    • 同学们，你们好！Tóngxuémen, nǐmen hǎo!Hello, classmates!
    • 医院在学校前边。Yīyuàn zài xuéxiào qiánbian.The hospital is in front of the school.
    • 朋友们都来了。Péngyoumen dōu lái le.My friends have all come.
    • 请到这边来。Qǐng dào zhèbiān lái.Please come over here.

    Compare

    里边 = 里面 = 里: all mean “inside”. The short 里 is used right after a noun (家里); 里边 can also stand alone (在里边).

    Exam note: Don't add 们 after a number: 三个学生, not 三个学生们.

Parts of speech词类25
  • Nouns 名词Location nouns 方位名词上、下、里、外、前、后Direction words 上, 下, 里, 外, 前, 后

    noun + 上/里/下/前/后/外

    These words go after a noun to say where something is: 桌子上 on the table, 家里 at home. Chinese puts them after the noun, the opposite of English “on the table”.

    When to use it

    Use them after a noun to make a place: 书上, 家里, 门外. Used alone after 在 they need 边/面: 在上边, not 在上 (except in set phrases).

    Negative and questions

    Ask “where?” with 在哪儿: 书在哪儿？书在桌子上。 Negative: 书不在桌子上。

    • 杯子在桌子上。Bēizi zài zhuōzi shang.The cup is on the table.
    • 妈妈在家里。Māma zài jiā li.Mum is at home.
    • 你的手机在书下边。Nǐ de shǒujī zài shū xiàbian.Your phone is under the book.
    • 他们在外边说话。Tāmen zài wàibian shuōhuà.They're talking outside.

    Compare

    Countries and cities don't take 里: 在中国, not 在中国里. Most other nouns need it: 在学校里 or 在学校 (both fine), 在桌子上 (上 is required).

    Exam note: Say 在桌子上, not 在上桌子.

  • Verbs 动词Modal verbs 能愿动词会、能Modal verbs 会 and 能

    subject + 会/能 + verb

    会 means “can” for a learned skill: 我会说汉语. 能 means “can, be able to” because conditions allow it: 明天我能来. Negate both with 不.

    When to use it

    会 also predicts the future: 明天会下雨 it will rain tomorrow. 能 also covers physical ability and permission in some contexts: 我能吃很多.

    Negative and questions

    Questions: 你会说汉语吗？ / 你会不会说汉语？ Short answers: 会 / 不会, 能 / 不能.

    • 我会做中国菜。Wǒ huì zuò Zhōngguó cài.I can cook Chinese food.
    • 今天我不能去学校。Jīntiān wǒ bù néng qù xuéxiào.I can't go to school today.
    • 你会写汉字吗？会一点儿。Nǐ huì xiě Hànzì ma? Huì yìdiǎnr.Can you write characters? A little.
    • 明天你能来吗？Míngtiān nǐ néng lái ma?Can you come tomorrow?

    Compare

    会 = know how (learned). 能 = be able now (conditions). 可以 = be allowed. 我会开车，但是今天不能开，我喝酒了 combines the first two.

    Exam note: For a learned skill use 会: 我会开车, not 我能开车 when you mean you know how.

  • Verbs 动词Modal verbs 能愿动词想、要Modal verbs 想 and 要

    subject + 想/要 + verb

    想 + verb is “would like to”, a soft wish. 要 + verb is “want to, be going to”, a firm wish or plan. The negative of both is usually 不想.

    When to use it

    想 is used for wishes and plans, and is polite when ordering: 我想要一杯茶. 要 is firmer and also means “need to”: 我要上班.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 你想去吗？ / 你想不想去？ The negative of both 想 and 要 is 不想; 不要 means “don't!”.

    • 我想喝茶。Wǒ xiǎng hē chá.I'd like some tea.
    • 明天我要去北京。Míngtiān wǒ yào qù Běijīng.Tomorrow I'm going to Beijing.
    • 我很想你。Wǒ hěn xiǎng nǐ.I miss you a lot.
    • 你想吃什么？我想吃米饭。Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme? Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn.What would you like to eat? Rice.

    Compare

    想 + person is “miss someone”: 我想妈妈. 要 + noun is “want something”: 我要这个.

    Exam note: 不要 + verb means “don't”, not “don't want”: 不要说话 = Don't talk. To say you don't want to, use 不想.

  • Verbs 动词Modal verbs 能愿动词可以Modal verb 可以

    subject + 可以 + verb (+ 吗？)

    可以 means “may, can” in the sense of permission. 可以…吗？ asks for permission. A short refusal is 不可以 or 不行.

    When to use it

    可以 asks or gives permission and makes polite requests. It can also mean “not bad, OK”: 这个菜还可以.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 我可以…吗？ / 可以不可以…？ Yes: 可以. No: 不可以 (strict) or 不行 (spoken).

    • 我可以坐这儿吗？Wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?May I sit here?
    • 你可以看我的书。Nǐ kěyǐ kàn wǒ de shū.You can read my book.
    • 这儿可以打电话吗？不可以。Zhèr kěyǐ dǎ diànhuà ma? Bù kěyǐ.Can I make a call here? No.
    • 你可以坐出租车去。Nǐ kěyǐ zuò chūzūchē qù.You can take a taxi there.

    Compare

    To ask for permission use 可以; 能 in a question often asks if something is possible: 你能来吗？ (Is it possible for you to come?).

  • Verbs 动词Separable verbs 离合词看病、睡觉、说话、上课、下课、上班、下班、生病Separable verbs (睡觉, 上课…)

    verb part + (了/time/number) + object part

    Words like 睡觉, 说话, 上课 are a verb plus an object. Other words can go between the two parts: 睡了一个小时觉. They don't take a second object: 见面 someone is wrong.

    When to use it

    Learn these as two-part words. Aspect words and durations go between the parts: 睡了觉, 睡一会儿觉, 上了一天班. 生病 and 看病 work the same way: 生了一次病.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没睡觉, 不上课. Question: 你睡觉了吗？

    • 我十一点睡觉。Wǒ shíyī diǎn shuìjiào.I go to bed at eleven.
    • 我们八点上课，十二点下课。Wǒmen bā diǎn shàngkè, shí'èr diǎn xiàkè.Our classes start at eight and end at twelve.
    • 我昨天生病了，没上班。Wǒ zuótiān shēngbìng le, méi shàngbān.I was ill yesterday and didn't go to work.
    • 上课的时候不要说话。Shàngkè de shíhou búyào shuōhuà.Don't talk during class.

    Compare

    To say “with someone” put the person before the verb with 跟/和: 我和他说话, not 我说话他.

    Exam note: Say 跟他说话, not 说话他.

  • Pronouns 代词Question words 疑问代词多、多少、几、哪、哪儿、哪里、哪些、什么、谁、怎么、怎么样Question words

    question word in the place of the answer

    Put the question word where the answer will go; the word order doesn't change and no 吗 is added. 什么 what, 谁 who, 哪儿 where, 几 how many (under 10), 多少 how many/much, 怎么 how, 怎么样 how is it.

    When to use it

    几 expects a small number and needs a measure word (几个人); 多少 is for any number and may drop it (多少钱). 怎么 + verb asks how to do something; 怎么样 asks for an opinion. 多 + adjective asks “how…?”: 多大 how old.

    • 你叫什么名字？Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?What's your name?
    • 你家有几口人？Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?How many people are in your family?
    • 这个多少钱？Zhège duōshao qián?How much is this?
    • 去医院怎么走？Qù yīyuàn zěnme zǒu?How do I get to the hospital?
    • 你觉得这个电影怎么样？Nǐ juéde zhège diànyǐng zěnmeyàng?What did you think of the film?

    Compare

    哪 + measure word = which (哪个); 哪儿/哪里 = where. 什么 + noun = what kind (什么书).

    Exam note: Never add 吗 to a question that has a question word: 你去哪儿？, not 你去哪儿吗？

  • Pronouns 代词Personal pronouns 人称代词我、你、您、他、她、我们、你们、他们、她们、它、它们、大家Personal pronouns

    我 · 你/您 · 他/她/它 + 们

    我 I, 你 you, 您 you (polite), 他 he, 她 she, 它 it (animals, things). Add 们 for the plural. 大家 means “everyone”. Pronouns don't change form: 我 is both “I” and “me”.

    When to use it

    Use 您 to be polite to older people, teachers and customers. 它/它们 are for animals and things. In speech 他, 她, 它 all sound tā.

    • 您好！您是王老师吗？Nín hǎo! Nín shì Wáng lǎoshī ma?Hello! Are you Teacher Wang?
    • 大家好，我叫李月。Dàjiā hǎo, wǒ jiào Lǐ Yuè.Hi everyone, my name is Li Yue.
    • 它是我的小猫。Tā shì wǒ de xiǎo māo.It's my kitten.
    • 他们是我的同学。Tāmen shì wǒ de tóngxué.They're my classmates.

    Compare

    我们 can include or exclude the listener; 咱们 (HSK 2) always includes them.

    Exam note: 您 has no plural 您们 in everyday use; say 你们 or 大家.

  • Pronouns 代词Demonstratives 指示代词这、那、这儿、那儿、这里、那里、这些、那些、有的、这个、那个、有些Demonstratives 这 and 那

    这/那 + (number) + measure word + noun

    这 this, 那 that. Before a noun they need a measure word: 这个人, 那本书. 这儿/这里 here, 那儿/那里 there, 这些/那些 these/those, 有的/有些 some.

    When to use it

    这/那 + 是 can stand alone as a subject: 这是我的书. Before a noun they need number + measure word: 这两本书. 这儿/那儿 are spoken, 这里/那里 slightly more formal.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 哪个？ which one? 哪儿？ where? Negative: 这不是…

    • 这本书是我的。Zhè běn shū shì wǒ de.This book is mine.
    • 有的人喜欢喝茶，有的人喜欢喝水。Yǒude rén xǐhuan hē chá, yǒude rén xǐhuan hē shuǐ.Some people like tea, some like water.
    • 那个人是谁？Nàge rén shì shéi?Who is that person?
    • 这些都是我的书。Zhèxiē dōu shì wǒ de shū.These are all my books.

    Compare

    这些/那些 make plurals: 这些书 these books. 有的/有些 mean “some (of them)”: 有的学生.

    Exam note: Don't say 这书; say 这本书.

  • Numerals 数词一、二/两、三、四、五、六、七、八、九、零、十、百、半、千Numbers; 二 and 两

    两 + measure word · 二 in counting and numbers

    Numbers are built logically: 十一 11, 二十 20, 九十九 99, 一百 100, 一千 1000. Use 两 before a measure word (两个人, 两本书) and 二 when counting or in numbers (第二, 十二). 半 is “half”: 半个小时.

    When to use it

    Read big numbers from the largest unit: 一千二百 1,200, 三百零五 305 (零 fills a gap). 两 is also common before 百 and 千: 两千.

    Negative and questions

    Ask a number with 几 (small) or 多少 (any).

    • 我有两个哥哥。Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge gēge.I have two older brothers.
    • 他二十八岁。Tā èrshíbā suì.He is twenty-eight.
    • 我们学校有一千二百个学生。Wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu yìqiān èrbǎi ge xuésheng.Our school has 1,200 students.
    • 我等了半个小时。Wǒ děng le bàn ge xiǎoshí.I waited half an hour.

    Compare

    半 before a measure word is “half a”: 半个小时; after it is “and a half”: 一个半小时.

    Exam note: 两个, never 二个.

  • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（1）专用名量词：本、个、家、口、块、件、只、元Measure words for nouns

    number/这/那 + measure word + noun

    Between a number (or 这/那) and a noun there must be a measure word. 个 is the general one. 本 books, 家 shops and companies, 口 family members, 块 pieces and money, 件 clothes and matters, 只 animals, 元 yuan.

    When to use it

    Each noun has its usual measure word, and 个 works when you're unsure. 块 also counts money in speech (五块钱). 口 is only for counting family members (三口人).

    Negative and questions

    Ask with 几 + measure word: 几本书？ 几口人？

    • 我买了三本书。Wǒ mǎi le sān běn shū.I bought three books.
    • 他家有一只小狗。Tā jiā yǒu yì zhī xiǎo gǒu.His family has a puppy.
    • 这件衣服很漂亮。Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn piàoliang.This piece of clothing is lovely.
    • 我家有四口人。Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.There are four people in my family.

    Compare

    The measure word can stand for the noun once it's known: 你要几个？我要两个。

    Exam note: 一书 is wrong: 一本书.

  • Measure words 量词Noun measure words 名量词（2）借用名量词：杯Containers as measure words: 杯

    number + 杯 + drink

    A container can work as a measure word: 一杯水 a glass of water, 两杯茶 two cups of tea. 杯子 is the noun “cup”; as a measure word it is just 杯.

    When to use it

    Any container can count its contents: 一杯水, 一杯茶. The thing itself is counted with 个: 一个杯子 one cup.

    Negative and questions

    Ask with 几杯: 你喝了几杯？

    • 请给我一杯水。Qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi shuǐ.A glass of water, please.
    • 他喝了两杯茶。Tā hē le liǎng bēi chá.He drank two cups of tea.
    • 你要几杯茶？两杯。Nǐ yào jǐ bēi chá? Liǎng bēi.How many teas would you like? Two.
    • 我买了两个杯子。Wǒ mǎi le liǎng ge bēizi.I bought two cups.

    Compare

    一杯水 = a glass of water (the water); 一个杯子 = one glass (the object).

    Exam note: Say 一杯茶, not 一个杯子茶.

  • Measure words 量词Time measure words 时量词日、号、岁、点、分、年、天Time words: 日/号, 岁, 点, 分, 年, 天

    number + 年/天/岁/点/分/号

    These words count time directly, without 个: 三天 three days, 两年 two years, 八岁 eight years old, 九点十分 9:10, 五号 the 5th. 号 is spoken, 日 is written.

    When to use it

    天 and 年 are counted directly (三天). 小时 needs 个 (三个小时). 岁 gives age, 点 and 分 the clock time, 号/日 the date.

    Negative and questions

    Ask: 几天？ 几岁？ (children) / 多大？ (adults) 几点？ 几号？

    • 我在中国住了两年。Wǒ zài Zhōngguó zhù le liǎng nián.I lived in China for two years.
    • 今天是三月八号。Jīntiān shì sān yuè bā hào.Today is March 8th.
    • 你女儿几岁了？Nǐ nǚ'ér jǐ suì le?How old is your daughter?
    • 我学了三个小时汉语。Wǒ xué le sān ge xiǎoshí Hànyǔ.I studied Chinese for three hours.

    Compare

    三点 = 3 o'clock (a point in time); 三个小时 = three hours (a length of time).

    Exam note: 三天, not 三个天; 两年, not 两个年.

  • Adverbs 副词Degree adverbs 程度副词非常、很、太、真、有（一）点儿Degree adverbs: 很, 非常, 太, 真, 有点儿

    degree adverb + adjective

    They go before an adjective: 很好 very good, 非常好 extremely good, 太好了 great!, 真好 really good. 有（一）点儿 means “a bit” and usually describes something unwelcome: 有点儿冷.

    When to use it

    很 is often neutral and just completes the sentence; 非常 really means “very”. 太…了 is “too” or an exclamation. 真 adds feeling.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不太 + adjective “not very”: 不太好. 很不 + adjective is “very un-”: 很不好.

    • 今天太热了！Jīntiān tài rè le!It's so hot today!
    • 这个菜有点儿贵。Zhège cài yǒudiǎnr guì.This dish is a bit expensive.
    • 我今天不太忙。Wǒ jīntiān bú tài máng.I'm not very busy today.
    • 这个苹果非常好吃。Zhège píngguǒ fēicháng hǎochī.This apple is delicious.

    Compare

    不太好 (not very good) is softer than 很不好 (very bad).

    Exam note: 有点儿 goes before the adjective (有点儿冷); 一点儿 goes after it in comparisons (冷一点儿).

  • Adverbs 副词Scope adverbs 范围副词都1都 (all, both)

    plural subject + 都 + verb

    都 sums up the people or things mentioned before it, so it comes after the subject and before the verb: 我们都是学生.

    When to use it

    都 refers back to everything named before it, including time words: 他每天都来. It can't refer to something after it.

    Negative and questions

    不都 = not all: 我们不都是学生. 都不 = none: 我们都不是学生.

    • 我们都是中国人。Wǒmen dōu shì Zhōngguó rén.We are all Chinese.
    • 他和她都喜欢看电影。Tā hé tā dōu xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.He and she both like watching films.
    • 我们都不喜欢下雨。Wǒmen dōu bù xǐhuan xià yǔ.None of us like rain.
    • 这些书我都看了。Zhèxiē shū wǒ dōu kàn le.I've read all these books.

    Compare

    Position changes meaning: 都不去 nobody goes; 不都去 not everyone goes.

    Exam note: 都 never comes before the subject: 都我们 is wrong.

  • Adverbs 副词Time adverbs 时间副词在、正在在 and 正在 (in progress)

    subject + (正)在 + verb (+ 呢)

    在 or 正在 before a verb shows an action happening right now: 他在吃饭 he is eating. 正在 stresses “right at this moment”.

    When to use it

    在 + verb works for now, the past or the future, as long as the action is in progress at that time: 昨天八点我在看电视.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没在 + verb. Question: 你在做什么？

    • 妈妈在做饭。Māma zài zuò fàn.Mum is cooking.
    • 我正在看书，你明天再来吧。Wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū, nǐ míngtiān zài lái ba.I'm reading right now, come back tomorrow.
    • 你在做什么？我在写字。Nǐ zài zuò shénme? Wǒ zài xiě zì.What are you doing? I'm writing.
    • 昨天晚上八点我在看电视。Zuótiān wǎnshang bā diǎn wǒ zài kàn diànshì.At eight last night I was watching TV.

    Compare

    在 + place = location; 在 + verb = in progress. 他在家 he is at home; 他在吃饭 he is eating.

    Exam note: Negate with 没（在）: 他没在睡觉, not 他不在睡觉.

  • Adverbs 副词Frequency adverbs 频率副词再1再 (again, then)

    再 + verb

    再 before a verb means the action will happen again or later: 再说一次 say it once more, 明天再来 come again tomorrow. It is for future actions.

    When to use it

    Use 再 for a repeat or a delay in the future, and in requests: 再来一个 one more, 再见 see you.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不再 “no longer, not again”: 我不再去了.

    • 这个菜很好吃，我想再吃一点儿。Zhège cài hěn hǎochī, wǒ xiǎng zài chī yìdiǎnr.This dish is tasty, I'd like a bit more.
    • 我们明天再去吧。Wǒmen míngtiān zài qù ba.Let's go tomorrow instead.
    • 再见！Zàijiàn!Goodbye!
    • 我们吃饭了再去吧。Wǒmen chī fàn le zài qù ba.Let's eat first and then go.

    Compare

    再 = again (future); 又 = again (already happened); 还 = still/also.

    Exam note: For a repeat that already happened, HSK 2 uses 又. 再 is for what hasn't happened yet.

  • Adverbs 副词Linking adverbs 关联副词还1、也还 and 也 (also)

    subject + 也/还 + verb

    也 “also, too” links a second subject to the same action: 我也是学生. 还 adds another action or thing: 我想喝茶，还想吃米饭. Both go before the verb.

    When to use it

    也 always comes after the subject and before the verb or 不. 还 adds something more, and can also mean “still”.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 也不/也没: 我也不去. Question: 你也去吗？

    • 他是老师，我也是老师。Tā shì lǎoshī, wǒ yě shì lǎoshī.He is a teacher, and so am I.
    • 我想喝茶，还想吃米饭。Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, hái xiǎng chī mǐfàn.I'd like some tea, and some rice too.
    • 他不喝茶，我也不喝。Tā bù hē chá, wǒ yě bù hē.He doesn't drink tea, and neither do I.
    • 你还要什么？Nǐ hái yào shénme?What else would you like?

    Compare

    也 = same thing, different person (他去，我也去). 还 = same person, one more thing (我去北京，还去上海).

    Exam note: 也 goes before the verb, never at the end of the sentence: 我也去, not 我去也.

  • Adverbs 副词Negation adverbs 否定副词不、没（有）、不要Negatives 不, 没（有）, 不要

    不 + verb/adj · 没（有）+ verb · 不要 + verb

    不 negates the present, future, habits and adjectives: 我不去, 不贵. 没（有） negates the past and 有: 我没去, 没有钱. 不要 + verb tells someone not to do something.

    When to use it

    不 is for things you don't do or won't do, and with adjectives; 没 for things that didn't happen; 不要 for telling someone not to.

    Negative and questions

    Short answers: 不 / 没有. 不 changes to bú before 4th tone: 不是 bú shì.

    • 昨天我没去学校。Zuótiān wǒ méi qù xuéxiào.I didn't go to school yesterday.
    • 不要在这儿打电话。Búyào zài zhèr dǎ diànhuà.Don't make calls here.
    • 我不喝酒。Wǒ bù hē jiǔ.I don't drink alcohol.
    • 他今天没有来。Tā jīntiān méiyǒu lái.He didn't come today.

    Compare

    我不去 = I'm not going / I don't go. 我没去 = I didn't go.

    Exam note: 有 is only negated with 没: 没有, never 不有.

  • Prepositions 介词Introducing time or place 引出时间、处所在在 + place before the verb

    subject + 在 + place + verb

    When 在 + place tells where an action happens, it comes before the verb: 我在家吃饭. English puts the place last; Chinese doesn't.

    When to use it

    在 + place + verb answers “where does it happen?”. After some verbs like 住 and 坐 it can also come after: 住在北京.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 你在哪儿工作？ Negative goes before 在: 我不在家吃饭.

    • 我在学校学习汉语。Wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.I study Chinese at school.
    • 他在医院工作。Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.He works at a hospital.
    • 你在哪儿工作？Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?Where do you work?
    • 我今天不在家吃饭。Wǒ jīntiān bú zài jiā chī fàn.I won't be eating at home today.

    Compare

    我在北京住 and 我住在北京 both mean “I live in Beijing”; the second is more common.

    Exam note: 我吃饭在家 is wrong; say 我在家吃饭.

  • Prepositions 介词Introducing the target 引出对象和1、对和 and 对: who you do it with or to

    和 + person + verb · 对 + person + verb/adj

    和 + person + verb: doing something with someone (我和朋友去). 对 + person: towards or to someone (他对我很好 he is good to me).

    When to use it

    和 + person + 一起 is common: 我和朋友一起去. 对 + person + adjective describes an attitude: 对我很好. 对 + thing can mean “for”: 对身体好.

    Negative and questions

    Negative goes before 和/对: 我不和他说话; 他对我不好 (before the adjective).

    • 我想和你说话。Wǒ xiǎng hé nǐ shuōhuà.I'd like to talk with you.
    • 老师对我们很好。Lǎoshī duì wǒmen hěn hǎo.The teacher is very kind to us.
    • 我和妈妈一起去商店。Wǒ hé māma yìqǐ qù shāngdiàn.I'm going to the shop with Mum.
    • 喝水对身体很好。Hē shuǐ duì shēntǐ hěn hǎo.Drinking water is good for you.
  • Conjunctions 连词Joining words or phrases 连接词语或短语和2和 joining words

    noun + 和 + noun

    和 means “and” between nouns or pronouns: 我和你, 苹果和米饭. It does not join two sentences or two verbs; then you just use a comma or 也/还.

    When to use it

    和 joins nouns and pronouns. With three or more, put 和 only before the last: 我、你和他.

    • 爸爸和妈妈都在家。Bàba hé māma dōu zài jiā.Dad and Mum are both at home.
    • 我喜欢猫和狗。Wǒ xǐhuan māo hé gǒu.I like cats and dogs.
    • 我、你和他都是学生。Wǒ, nǐ hé tā dōu shì xuésheng.You, he and I are all students.
    • 桌子上有书和杯子。Zhuōzi shang yǒu shū hé bēizi.There are books and cups on the table.

    Compare

    和 = “and” only between nouns. For “and” between actions or sentences use a comma, 也 or 还.

    Exam note: “我吃饭和喝茶” sounds wrong; say 我吃饭，也喝茶.

  • Particles 助词Structural particles 结构助词的1的 (possession and description)

    owner/description + 的 + noun

    的 links a word to the noun it describes: 我的书 my book, 很大的学校 a big school. With family and close relations it is often left out: 我妈妈, 我们学校.

    When to use it

    的 can also end a phrase with the noun left out: 这本书是我的 this book is mine; 红的 the red one.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 这是谁的？ whose is this?

    • 这是我朋友的电脑。Zhè shì wǒ péngyou de diànnǎo.This is my friend's computer.
    • 坐在前边的那个人是我姐姐。Zuò zài qiánbian de nàge rén shì wǒ jiějie.The person sitting in front is my older sister.
    • 这个杯子是谁的？是我的。Zhège bēizi shì shéi de? Shì wǒ de.Whose cup is this? It's mine.
    • 我喜欢大的，不喜欢小的。Wǒ xǐhuan dà de, bù xǐhuan xiǎo de.I like the big one, not the small one.

    Compare

    我的妈妈 and 我妈妈 both work; with family, friends and institutions 的 is usually dropped.

  • Particles 助词Aspect particles 动态助词了1了 after the verb (completed action)

    verb + 了 + (number) + object

    了 right after a verb shows the action is done, usually with a number or detail: 我买了三本书. Negate with 没 and drop 了: 我没买书.

    When to use it

    Verb + 了 is used when a completed action has some detail: a number, a describing word or another clause: 吃了饭再去.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没 + verb (no 了). Question: verb + 了 + 吗？ or verb + 没（有）？: 你吃了没有？

    • 我喝了一杯茶。Wǒ hē le yì bēi chá.I drank a cup of tea.
    • 他昨天看了一个电影。Tā zuótiān kàn le yí ge diànyǐng.He watched a film yesterday.
    • 你买了什么？我买了两本书。Nǐ mǎi le shénme? Wǒ mǎi le liǎng běn shū.What did you buy? Two books.
    • 你吃了没有？还没吃。Nǐ chī le méiyǒu? Hái méi chī.Have you eaten? Not yet.

    Compare

    了 is not a past tense: habits in the past don't take it: 以前我常常去 (no 了).

    Exam note: 我没买了 is wrong: 没 and 了 don't go together.

  • Particles 助词Modal particles 语气助词吧1、了2 、吗、呢1、呢2、呢3Sentence-final particles 吧, 了, 吗, 呢

    sentence + 吧/了/吗/呢

    吗 makes a yes/no question. 吧 makes a suggestion or checks a guess (我们走吧; 你是学生吧？). 了 at the end shows a change (下雨了). 呢 asks “and…?” (你呢？), asks where (我的书呢？) or marks an ongoing state (他在睡觉呢).

    When to use it

    吧 also softens orders (快走吧). 了 at the end means something new is true. 呢 after a noun asks “what about…?” or “where is…?”.

    • 我很好，你呢？Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?I'm fine, and you?
    • 我们去吃饭吧。Wǒmen qù chī fàn ba.Let's go and eat.
    • 你是王老师吧？Nǐ shì Wáng lǎoshī ba?You must be Teacher Wang?
    • 我的手机呢？Wǒ de shǒujī ne?Where's my phone?

    Compare

    吗 = yes/no question; 吧 = “I suppose so, right?”; 呢 = “and what about…?”.

    Exam note: One particle per question: 你是学生吗？ or 你是不是学生？, never both.

  • Interjections 叹词喂Interjection 喂

    喂 + ，…

    喂 is “hello” on the phone, or a way to get someone's attention. On the phone it is usually said wéi.

    When to use it

    喂 starts a phone call or calls out to someone. With a falling tone (wèi) it can sound rude face to face.

    • 喂，你好！请问王先生在吗？Wéi, nǐ hǎo! Qǐngwèn Wáng xiānsheng zài ma?Hello! Is Mr Wang there, please?
    • 喂，你在哪儿呢？Wéi, nǐ zài nǎr ne?Hello, where are you?
    • 喂，是李先生吗？Wéi, shì Lǐ xiānsheng ma?Hello, is that Mr Li?

    Exam note: 喂 is informal; don't use it to greet a teacher face to face. Say 老师好.

  • Structure types 结构类型Basic structures 基本结构类型（1）联合短语Coordinate phrases (A 和 B)

    A + 和 + B · A + B

    Two words of the same kind side by side, equal in rank: 爸爸妈妈, 我和你, 老师和学生.

    When to use it

    The two parts are equal and can usually swap places: 老师和学生 = 学生和老师.

    • 老师和学生都来了。Lǎoshī hé xuésheng dōu lái le.The teacher and the students have all come.
    • 爸爸妈妈在家看电视。Bàba māma zài jiā kàn diànshì.Mum and Dad are watching TV at home.
    • 我和他是好朋友。Wǒ hé tā shì hǎo péngyou.He and I are good friends.
  • Structure types 结构类型Basic structures 基本结构类型（2）偏正短语Modifier + head phrases

    describing word + (的) + noun · adverb + verb/adj

    The describing word always comes first: 中国菜 Chinese food, 我的朋友 my friend, 很好 very good, 都去 all go.

    When to use it

    Order: number + measure word + description + noun: 一个很好的朋友.

    • 我喜欢中国菜。Wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài.I like Chinese food.
    • 这是一个很大的商店。Zhè shì yí ge hěn dà de shāngdiàn.This is a big shop.
    • 他是一个很好的老师。Tā shì yí ge hěn hǎo de lǎoshī.He's a very good teacher.

    Exam note: Unlike Russian or French, the describer never comes after the noun: 菜中国 is wrong.

  • Structure types 结构类型Basic structures 基本结构类型（3）动宾短语Verb + object phrases

    verb + object

    The object comes right after the verb: 吃饭 eat, 看书 read, 喝茶 drink tea, 打电话 make a call.

    When to use it

    Many everyday actions are verb + object phrases, and the object is often general: 吃饭 to eat (a meal), 写字 to write.

    • 我晚上看书。Wǒ wǎnshang kàn shū.I read in the evening.
    • 他在打电话。Tā zài dǎ diànhuà.He's on the phone.
    • 你会开车吗？Nǐ huì kāichē ma?Can you drive?
  • Structure types 结构类型Basic structures 基本结构类型（4）主谓短语Subject + predicate phrases

    subject + predicate

    A small “who does what” unit can act as one part of a bigger sentence: 他来 “him coming”, 天气很好 “the weather being good”. At HSK 1 you mostly meet it as a whole sentence.

    When to use it

    Common after verbs of thinking and feeling: 觉得, 知道, 喜欢 + subject-predicate phrase.

    • 我觉得天气很好。Wǒ juéde tiānqì hěn hǎo.I think the weather is nice.
    • 我觉得他很好。Wǒ juéde tā hěn hǎo.I think he's great.
    • 我知道他是老师。Wǒ zhīdào tā shì lǎoshī.I know he's a teacher.
  • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（1）数量短语Number + measure word phrases

    number + measure word (+ noun)

    A number and a measure word together form one unit that can stand before a noun or alone: 三个人, 一杯, 五块.

    When to use it

    The phrase can come before a noun, or alone when the noun is clear: 我要两个.

    • 我要一杯茶。Wǒ yào yì bēi chá.I'd like a cup of tea.
    • 苹果多少钱？五块一个。Píngguǒ duōshao qián? Wǔ kuài yí ge.How much are the apples? Five yuan each.
    • 我要两个。Wǒ yào liǎng ge.I'll take two.
  • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（2）介宾短语Preposition phrases (在 + place)

    preposition + noun, placed before the verb

    A preposition and its noun form one block that goes before the verb: 在家, 在学校, 和朋友, 对我.

    When to use it

    The preposition phrase usually sits between the subject and the verb.

    • 我在北京工作。Wǒ zài Běijīng gōngzuò.I work in Beijing.
    • 他和朋友去看电影了。Tā hé péngyou qù kàn diànyǐng le.He went to a film with a friend.
    • 我对中国菜很有兴趣。Wǒ duì Zhōngguó cài hěn yǒu xìngqù.I'm very interested in Chinese food.

    Exam note: The block goes before the verb, not after: 我在北京工作.

  • Structure types 结构类型Other structures 其他结构类型（3）方位短语Location phrases (noun + 上/里…)

    noun + direction word

    A noun plus a direction word names a place: 桌子上, 学校里, 椅子下边, 商店前边. Often used after 在.

    When to use it

    Location phrases answer 在哪儿？ and can be subjects too: 学校里有很多人.

    • 猫在椅子下边。Māo zài yǐzi xiàbian.The cat is under the chair.
    • 学校里有很多学生。Xuéxiào li yǒu hěn duō xuésheng.There are many students in the school.
    • 房间里有两张桌子。Fángjiān li yǒu liǎng zhāng zhuōzi.There are two tables in the room.
Sentence elements句子成分7
  • Subject 主语名词、代词或名词性短语作主语Subjects

    noun/pronoun/noun phrase + predicate

    The subject comes first and is a noun, pronoun or noun phrase: 我, 我妈妈, 那个人. It can be dropped when it's clear from context.

    When to use it

    Time words often come before or after the subject: 明天我去 = 我明天去.

    • 那个人是我的老师。Nàge rén shì wǒ de lǎoshī.That person is my teacher.
    • 我妈妈很喜欢喝茶。Wǒ māma hěn xǐhuan hē chá.My mother loves tea.
    • 我们都喜欢学汉语。Wǒmen dōu xǐhuan xué Hànyǔ.We all like learning Chinese.
  • Predicate 谓语名词、代词、数词或数量短语作谓语Nouns and numbers as the predicate

    subject + date/age/price (no 是)

    For dates, times, ages and prices the predicate can be a noun or number with no verb: 今天星期一, 他八岁, 这个三块钱. In the negative you need 不是.

    When to use it

    Only in positive statements about age, dates, times, prices, origin and the like. Add 是 when you want to stress or negate.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不是 + noun: 今天不是星期一.

    • 明天星期六。Míngtiān xīngqīliù.Tomorrow is Saturday.
    • 我女儿五岁。Wǒ nǚ'ér wǔ suì.My daughter is five.
    • 这个苹果五块。Zhège píngguǒ wǔ kuài.This apple is five yuan.

    Exam note: Negative: 今天不是星期一, not 今天不星期一.

  • Predicate 谓语动词或动词性短语、形容词或形容词性短语作谓语Verbs and adjectives as the predicate

    subject + verb (+ object) · subject + 很 + adjective

    Most predicates are a verb (我喝茶) or an adjective. An adjective is the predicate by itself, with no 是, usually with 很: 天气很好.

    When to use it

    An adjective predicate needs a degree word like 很; a bare adjective sounds like a contrast: 他高，我不高.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不 + adjective (不贵). Question: adj + 吗 or adj + 不 + adj (贵不贵？).

    • 我喜欢学汉语。Wǒ xǐhuan xué Hànyǔ.I like learning Chinese.
    • 这个菜很好吃。Zhège cài hěn hǎochī.This dish is delicious.
    • 他的汉语很好。Tā de Hànyǔ hěn hǎo.His Chinese is very good.

    Exam note: 这个菜是好吃 is wrong; use 很: 这个菜很好吃.

  • Object 宾语名词、代词或名词性短语作宾语Objects

    verb + noun/pronoun/noun phrase

    The object follows the verb: 我看书, 我认识他, 我喜欢那个红杯子.

    When to use it

    Some verbs take a whole action as the object: 喜欢看书, 想去中国.

    • 我认识他。Wǒ rènshi tā.I know him.
    • 她想买一件新衣服。Tā xiǎng mǎi yí jiàn xīn yīfu.She wants to buy a new piece of clothing.
    • 我喜欢看书。Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū.I like reading.
  • Attributive 定语名词性词语、形容词性短语、数量短语作定语Attributes (words before a noun)

    noun/adjective phrase/number + measure + (的) + noun

    Anything describing a noun goes in front of it: a noun (中国菜), an adjective phrase (很大的商店) or a number with a measure word (三本书).

    When to use it

    Several describers stack before the noun: 我的两个中国朋友 my two Chinese friends.

    • 他在一个很大的商店工作。Tā zài yí ge hěn dà de shāngdiàn gōngzuò.He works in a big shop.
    • 我有三个中国朋友。Wǒ yǒu sān ge Zhōngguó péngyou.I have three Chinese friends.
    • 这是我的两个中国朋友。Zhè shì wǒ de liǎng ge Zhōngguó péngyou.These are my two Chinese friends.

    Exam note: With 很 + adjective you need 的: 很大的商店, not 很大商店.

  • Adverbial 状语副词、形容词作状语表示时间、处所的词语作状语Adverbials: time and place before the verb

    subject + time + place + verb

    Adverbs and time and place words go before the verb. The usual order is: who + when + where + do what. Time can also go before the subject.

    When to use it

    Order of adverbials: time, then 都/也, then place: 我们明天都在家.

    • 我明天在家休息。Wǒ míngtiān zài jiā xiūxi.I'll rest at home tomorrow.
    • 下午我们去商店买东西。Xiàwǔ wǒmen qù shāngdiàn mǎi dōngxi.This afternoon we're going shopping.
    • 我们明天都在家。Wǒmen míngtiān dōu zài jiā.We'll all be at home tomorrow.

    Exam note: Time never goes at the end: 我去北京明天 is wrong.

  • Complement 补语Quantity complements 1 数量补语1动词+动量补语How many times: verb + number + 次

    verb + number + 次 (+ object)

    After a verb, number + 次 says how many times it happens: 去了两次, 说一次. With a place or thing as object, the object usually goes after: 去过一次北京.

    When to use it

    次 counts how many times something happens. With a pronoun object, the pronoun comes before 次: 我见过他一次.

    Negative and questions

    Ask: 几次？ 你去过几次？

    • 请再说一次。Qǐng zài shuō yí cì.Please say it once more.
    • 我去了两次北京。Wǒ qù le liǎng cì Běijīng.I've been to Beijing twice.
    • 你去过几次中国？Nǐ qù guo jǐ cì Zhōngguó?How many times have you been to China?
    • 我看了他一次。Wǒ kàn le tā yí cì.I visited him once.

    Exam note: Not 两次去; the count comes after the verb.

Sentence types句子的类型20
  • Sentence patterns 句型Simple sentences 单句主谓句1：动词谓语句Verb-predicate sentences

    subject + verb + object

    The most common sentence: who + does + what. 我学习汉语, 他吃米饭.

    When to use it

    Add time and place before the verb and the object after it: 我明天在学校学汉语.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不/没 before the verb (or before the 在 phrase). Question: … 吗？

    • 我学习汉语。Wǒ xuéxí Hànyǔ.I study Chinese.
    • 他们在饭店吃饭。Tāmen zài fàndiàn chī fàn.They're eating at a restaurant.
    • 我不在家吃饭。Wǒ bú zài jiā chī fàn.I don't eat at home.
  • Sentence patterns 句型Simple sentences 单句主谓句2：形容词谓语句Adjective-predicate sentences

    subject + 很/太/非常 + adjective

    The adjective is the predicate, with no 是. A plain statement normally needs 很; without it the sentence sounds like a comparison.

    When to use it

    Adjectives work like verbs in Chinese: they are the predicate without 是.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不 + adjective. Question: 好不好？ / 好吗？

    • 今天很冷。Jīntiān hěn lěng.It's cold today.
    • 这个电脑太贵了。Zhège diànnǎo tài guì le.This computer is too expensive.
    • 今天冷不冷？不太冷。Jīntiān lěng bu lěng? Bú tài lěng.Is it cold today? Not very.

    Exam note: 我是很忙 is wrong; say 我很忙.

  • Sentence patterns 句型Simple sentences 单句主谓句3：名词谓语句Noun-predicate sentences

    subject + date/age/price/origin

    For age, date, day, time and price, the noun is the predicate without 是: 今天八号, 他二十岁, 现在三点.

    When to use it

    Only in short positive statements. For the negative, 是 comes back: 今天不是十五号.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 不是. Question: 今天几号？

    • 现在三点十分。Xiànzài sān diǎn shí fēn.It's ten past three now.
    • 今天几号？今天十五号。Jīntiān jǐ hào? Jīntiān shíwǔ hào.What's the date? It's the 15th.
    • 他今年二十岁。Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.He's twenty this year.
  • Sentence patterns 句型Simple sentences 单句非主谓句Sentences without a subject

    verb/word alone

    Some sentences have no subject at all: weather (下雨了), greetings (你好！), signs and short answers (谢谢！对！).

    When to use it

    Typical: weather (下雪了), greetings, thanks and apologies, notices (请坐), and one-word answers.

    • 下雨了。Xià yǔ le.It's raining.
    • 对不起！没关系。Duìbuqǐ! Méi guānxi.Sorry! That's all right.
    • 谢谢！不客气。Xièxie! Bú kèqi.Thank you! You're welcome.
  • Sentence functions 句类Statements 陈述句—Statements

    sentence + 。

    A statement tells a fact and ends with 。. It can be positive (我是学生) or negative (我不是学生).

    When to use it

    A statement keeps the basic order: subject + time + place + verb + object.

    Negative and questions

    Make it negative with 不/没; make it a question with 吗 or a question word.

    • 我是中国人。Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.I'm Chinese.
    • 他今天没来学校。Tā jīntiān méi lái xuéxiào.He didn't come to school today.
    • 我每天晚上看书。Wǒ měi tiān wǎnshang kàn shū.I read every evening.
  • Sentence functions 句类Questions 疑问句（1）是非问句Yes/no questions with 吗

    statement + 吗？

    Add 吗 to the end of a statement to ask yes or no. Answer by repeating the verb: 是 / 不是, 去 / 不去.

    When to use it

    吗 questions keep the statement word order. To answer “yes”, repeat the verb or adjective: 你忙吗？忙。

    Negative and questions

    A negative question: 你不去吗？ “Aren't you going?”. Answer about the fact: 对，我不去 (No, I'm not).

    • 你是学生吗？是。Nǐ shì xuésheng ma? Shì.Are you a student? Yes.
    • 你喜欢喝茶吗？Nǐ xǐhuan hē chá ma?Do you like tea?
    • 你不喜欢喝茶吗？对，我不喜欢。Nǐ bù xǐhuan hē chá ma? Duì, wǒ bù xǐhuan.Don't you like tea? No, I don't.

    Compare

    Don't answer with 是 unless the question used 是. 你喜欢吗？ → 喜欢, not 是.

  • Sentence functions 句类Questions 疑问句（2）特指问句Questions with a question word

    question word in place of the answer, no 吗

    Put 谁, 什么, 哪儿, 几, 多少 etc. where the answer goes. Don't add 吗.

    When to use it

    Several question words can't share one sentence at HSK 1; ask one thing at a time.

    • 你去哪儿？我去商店。Nǐ qù nǎr? Wǒ qù shāngdiàn.Where are you going? To the shop.
    • 他是谁？他是我哥哥。Tā shì shéi? Tā shì wǒ gēge.Who is he? He's my older brother.
    • 你什么时候去北京？Nǐ shénme shíhou qù Běijīng?When are you going to Beijing?

    Compare

    Question word = you want information; 吗 = you want yes or no.

  • Sentence functions 句类Questions 疑问句（3）正反问句Affirmative-negative questions (是不是)

    verb/adj + 不 + verb/adj？

    Say the verb or adjective twice, positive then negative: 你去不去？, 贵不贵？. No 吗 is added.

    When to use it

    With a two-syllable verb you can shorten the first one: 喜不喜欢？ The object can go at the end: 你去不去学校？

    Negative and questions

    Past: verb + 没 + verb or … 了没有？: 你去没去？ / 你去了没有？

    • 你是不是老师？Nǐ shì bu shì lǎoshī?Are you a teacher?
    • 这个菜好吃不好吃？Zhège cài hǎochī bu hǎochī?Is this dish tasty?
    • 你喜不喜欢这个电影？Nǐ xǐ bu xǐhuan zhège diànyǐng?Do you like this film?

    Compare

    A-not-A sounds more direct than 吗 and is common in speech.

    Exam note: 你去不去吗？ is wrong: no 吗 in this pattern.

  • Sentence functions 句类Commands and requests 祈使句—Requests and commands

    (请) + verb · 不要/别 + verb

    Ask or tell someone to do something with just the verb, made politer with 请 (请坐) or softer with 吧. 不要 + verb means “don't”.

    When to use it

    请 + verb is polite; verb + 吧 is friendly; a bare verb is direct. 不要/别 tell someone not to.

    • 请坐，请喝茶。Qǐng zuò, qǐng hē chá.Please sit down and have some tea.
    • 不要看电视了，睡觉吧。Búyào kàn diànshì le, shuìjiào ba.Stop watching TV and go to bed.
    • 请进！Qǐng jìn!Come in, please!
  • Sentence functions 句类Exclamations 感叹句—Exclamations

    太 + adj + 了！· 真 + adj！

    To show strong feeling use 太…了 or 真… and end with ！: 太好了！, 真漂亮！

    When to use it

    Other exclamations: 好…啊！ and 多…啊！ (HSK 2).

    • 太好了！明天不上课！Tài hǎo le! Míngtiān bú shàngkè!Great! No classes tomorrow!
    • 你的女儿真漂亮！Nǐ de nǚ'ér zhēn piàoliang!Your daughter is really pretty!
    • 这儿的菜真好吃！Zhèr de cài zhēn hǎochī!The food here is really good!
  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型是 sentences 1 “是”字句1表示等同或类属是 sentences: A is B

    A + 是 + B · A + 不是 + B

    是 links two nouns to say what or who something is: 我是学生, 这是我的书. Negate it with 不是.

    When to use it

    是 also gives a job, nationality or identity: 我是医生, 他是中国人. In 是 sentences adverbs go before 是: 他也是, 我们都是.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 你是…吗？ / 你是不是…？ / 他是谁？

    • 他是我的汉语老师。Tā shì wǒ de Hànyǔ lǎoshī.He is my Chinese teacher.
    • 这不是我的杯子。Zhè bú shì wǒ de bēizi.This isn't my cup.
    • 你是不是中国人？Nǐ shì bu shì Zhōngguó rén?Are you Chinese?

    Compare

    是 links nouns; adjectives need no 是. 他是高 is wrong, 他很高 is right.

    Exam note: 是 doesn't go before adjectives: 我很好, not 我是好.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型有 sentences 1 “有”字句1表示领有有 sentences: to have

    subject + 有 + object · 没有

    有 means “to have”: 我有一个哥哥. The only negative is 没有.

    When to use it

    有 is for things and people you have: 我有一个姐姐, 他有很多书. Don't use it for age: 他二十岁, not 他有二十岁.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 你有…吗？ / 你有没有…？

    • 我有两个中国朋友。Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge Zhōngguó péngyou.I have two Chinese friends.
    • 我今天没有时间。Wǒ jīntiān méiyǒu shíjiān.I don't have time today.
    • 你有没有中国朋友？Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu Zhōngguó péngyou?Do you have any Chinese friends?

    Exam note: 不有 does not exist; say 没有.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型Existential sentences 1 存现句1（1）所处+是+名词Place + 是 + thing

    place + 是 + noun

    “In this place is X”: tells what exactly is at a place, often the only thing there: 我家前边是一个商店.

    When to use it

    Use 是 when you identify the one thing in that place; use 有 when you just say something exists there.

    • 学校后边是一个医院。Xuéxiào hòubian shì yí ge yīyuàn.Behind the school is a hospital.
    • 桌子上是我的书。Zhuōzi shang shì wǒ de shū.What's on the table is my book.
    • 我家左边是一个饭店。Wǒ jiā zuǒbian shì yí ge fàndiàn.To the left of my home is a restaurant.

    Compare

    前边是医院 = what's in front is the hospital. 前边有一个医院 = there is a hospital in front.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型Existential sentences 1 存现句1（2）处所+有+数量短语+名词Place + 有 + number + thing

    place + 有 + number + measure + noun

    “There is/are … at a place”: the place comes first, then 有: 桌子上有三本书. Negative: 没有.

    When to use it

    The thing after 有 is new information, usually with a number: 有一个人, 有很多书.

    Negative and questions

    Question: 桌子上有什么？ Negative: 桌子上没有书.

    • 桌子上有一个杯子。Zhuōzi shang yǒu yí ge bēizi.There is a cup on the table.
    • 我们学校有很多中国学生。Wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu hěn duō Zhōngguó xuésheng.There are many Chinese students at our school.
    • 房间里有人吗？没有人。Fángjiān li yǒu rén ma? Méiyǒu rén.Is anyone in the room? No one.

    Compare

    To say where a known thing is, use 在: 我的书在桌子上. For “there is a…” use 有: 桌子上有一本书.

    Exam note: Don't start with 在: 在桌子上有书 is wrong; say 桌子上有书.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型Serial verb sentences 1 连动句1（1）前后动作先后发生，后一动作是前一动作的目的Verb series: go somewhere to do something

    subject + 去/来 + place + verb + object

    Two actions in time order; the second is the purpose of the first: 我去商店买东西 I go to the shop to buy things.

    When to use it

    The first verb is usually 去 or 来 + place. 了 goes after the second verb: 我去商店买了一本书.

    Negative and questions

    Negative before the first verb: 我不去商店买东西. Question: 你去哪儿买东西？

    • 我去医院看病。Wǒ qù yīyuàn kànbìng.I'm going to the hospital to see a doctor.
    • 他来北京学习汉语。Tā lái Běijīng xuéxí Hànyǔ.He came to Beijing to study Chinese.
    • 明天你来我家吃饭吧。Míngtiān nǐ lái wǒ jiā chī fàn ba.Come over for dinner tomorrow.

    Exam note: No word for “to/in order to” is needed between the verbs.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型Serial verb sentences 1 连动句1（2）前一动作是后一动作的方式Verb series: how you do it (坐/开…)

    subject + 坐/开 + noun + verb

    The first action is the way you do the second: 坐飞机去北京 go to Beijing by plane, 开车去 go by car.

    When to use it

    Typical first verbs: 坐 (a vehicle you ride in), 开 (drive), 用 (use, HSK 3). Ask how with 怎么去？

    Negative and questions

    Negative before the first verb: 我不坐飞机去.

    • 我坐出租车去医院。Wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù yīyuàn.I'm taking a taxi to the hospital.
    • 他们坐飞机去北京。Tāmen zuò fēijī qù Běijīng.They're flying to Beijing.
    • 你怎么去学校？我坐出租车去。Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào? Wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù.How do you get to school? By taxi.

    Exam note: The way comes first: 我去北京坐飞机 means you go to Beijing and then take a plane.

  • Special sentence patterns 特殊句型Double-object sentences 1 双宾语句1主语+动词+宾语1+宾语2Two objects: give someone something

    subject + 给/问/叫 + person + thing

    Some verbs take two objects: first the person, then the thing: 给我一本书, 问老师一个问题.

    When to use it

    At HSK 1 the main verbs are 给, 问, 叫 and 教. The thing usually has a number: 给我一本书.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没给我书. Question: 他给你什么了？

    • 妈妈给我一个苹果。Māma gěi wǒ yí ge píngguǒ.Mum gave me an apple.
    • 我想问你一个问题。Wǒ xiǎng wèn nǐ yí ge wèntí.I'd like to ask you a question.
    • 老师教我们汉语。Lǎoshī jiāo wǒmen Hànyǔ.The teacher teaches us Chinese.

    Exam note: Person first, thing second: 给我书, not 给书我.

  • Complex sentences 复句Complex sentences without connectives 不用关联词语的复句—Compound sentences without linking words

    clause, clause。

    Chinese often joins two clauses with just a comma; the link (and, so, but) is understood from context.

    When to use it

    The relation is clear from meaning: cause, contrast or sequence. Later levels add words like 因为…所以… and 但是.

    • 今天下雨，我不去了。Jīntiān xià yǔ, wǒ bú qù le.It's raining today, so I won't go.
    • 他是老师，她是医生。Tā shì lǎoshī, tā shì yīshēng.He's a teacher and she's a doctor.
    • 我很忙，没时间看电视。Wǒ hěn máng, méi shíjiān kàn diànshì.I'm busy and have no time for TV.
  • Complex sentences 复句Coordinate clauses 并列复句……，也………，也… (also)

    clause 1，subject + 也 + verb

    The second clause does the same as the first, linked by 也 before the verb: 他会说汉语，我也会.

    When to use it

    也 joins two clauses with the same predicate or two predicates of the same subject.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: …，也不… / …，也没…

    • 我喜欢看书，也喜欢看电影。Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū, yě xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.I like reading and I like films too.
    • 他去，我也去。Tā qù, wǒ yě qù.If he goes, I'll go too.
    • 他不会说汉语，也不会写汉字。Tā bú huì shuō Hànyǔ, yě bú huì xiě Hànzì.He can't speak Chinese or write characters.
  • Complex sentences 复句Coordinate clauses 并列复句……，还………，还… (and also)

    clause 1，还 + verb + more

    还 adds one more thing to what was just said: 我买了水果，还买了米.

    When to use it

    还 adds more of the same kind. In questions 还 asks “what else?”: 你还想吃什么？

    • 我会说汉语，还会写汉字。Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ, hái huì xiě Hànzì.I can speak Chinese, and I can write characters too.
    • 他喝了茶，还吃了米饭。Tā hē le chá, hái chī le mǐfàn.He had tea and also some rice.
    • 我去了北京，还去了上海。Wǒ qù le Běijīng, hái qù le Shànghǎi.I went to Beijing, and Shanghai too.

    Compare

    也 = another person does the same; 还 = the same person does more.

Aspect动作的态5
  • Completed aspect 完成态用动态助词“了1”表示Completed action with 了

    verb + 了 + number + object

    了 after the verb marks an action as completed. It usually needs a number or detail after it: 我吃了一个苹果. Negative: 没 + verb, no 了.

    When to use it

    A bare verb + 了 + object sounds unfinished: 我买了书… expects more. Add a number or end with 了: 我买书了.

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没（有）+ verb. Question: … 了吗？ / … 了没有？

    • 我昨天买了一件衣服。Wǒ zuótiān mǎi le yí jiàn yīfu.I bought a piece of clothing yesterday.
    • 你吃饭了吗？我没吃。Nǐ chī fàn le ma? Wǒ méi chī.Have you eaten? No, I haven't.
    • 我看了三个小时电视。Wǒ kàn le sān ge xiǎoshí diànshì.I watched TV for three hours.
  • Change of state 变化态用动态助词“了2”表示Change with 了 at the end

    sentence + 了

    了 at the end of a sentence shows a new situation: something has changed. 下雨了 it's started raining, 我饿了 I'm hungry now.

    When to use it

    End-了 also marks “no longer”: 不…了 — 我不去了 I'm not going any more (changed my mind).

    Negative and questions

    Question: … 了吗？: 你好了吗？ Are you better now?

    • 天冷了，多穿点儿衣服。Tiān lěng le, duō chuān diǎnr yīfu.It's turned cold, wear more clothes.
    • 我女儿八岁了。Wǒ nǚ'ér bā suì le.My daughter is eight now.
    • 我不想去了。Wǒ bù xiǎng qù le.I don't want to go any more.

    Compare

    Verb-了 = action completed; end-了 = situation changed. Both can appear together: 我吃了饭了.

  • Progressive aspect 进行态（1）……在/正在+动词Ongoing action: 在/正在 + verb

    subject + 在/正在 + verb

    在 or 正在 before the verb says the action is in progress: 他在看书.

    When to use it

    Used for actions, not states: 他在看书 (yes), 他在喜欢 (no).

    Negative and questions

    Negative: 没在 + verb. Question: 他在做什么？

    • 他们在上课。Tāmen zài shàngkè.They're in class.
    • 我正在写汉字。Wǒ zhèngzài xiě Hànzì.I'm writing characters right now.
    • 你在想什么？Nǐ zài xiǎng shénme?What are you thinking about?
  • Progressive aspect 进行态（2）……在/正在+动词……+呢Ongoing action: 在/正在 + verb … 呢

    subject + 在/正在 + verb (+ object) + 呢

    Adding 呢 at the end makes the “in progress” meaning stronger and more spoken: 他在睡觉呢.

    When to use it

    在 … 呢 is the most natural way in speech to say what someone is doing right now.

    • 妈妈在做饭呢。Māma zài zuò fàn ne.Mum is cooking.
    • 不要打电话，他正在睡觉呢。Búyào dǎ diànhuà, tā zhèngzài shuìjiào ne.Don't call, he's sleeping.
    • 他们在上课呢，下课再打电话吧。Tāmen zài shàngkè ne, xiàkè zài dǎ diànhuà ba.They're in class, call after class.
  • Progressive aspect 进行态（3）……呢Ongoing action: … 呢 alone

    subject + verb (+ object) + 呢

    In speech, 呢 alone at the end can show an ongoing action: 他看电视呢 he's watching TV.

    When to use it

    Mostly in short spoken answers; in writing prefer 在 + verb.

    • 妈妈，我吃饭呢。Māma, wǒ chī fàn ne.Mum, I'm eating.
    • 爸爸看电视呢。Bàba kàn diànshì ne.Dad is watching TV.
    • 你妈妈呢？她睡觉呢。Nǐ māma ne? Tā shuìjiào ne.Where's your mum? She's sleeping.
Special expressions特殊表达法4
  • Expressing numbers 数的表达法钱数表示法Money: 块, 毛, 元

    number + 块 (+ number + 毛)

    Spoken: 块 for yuan, 毛 for a tenth of a yuan: 三块五 3.50. Written and on price tags: 元. Ask the price with 多少钱？

    When to use it

    The last unit can be dropped in speech: 三块五 (毛). Prices with 元 are read the same way: 15元 = 十五块.

    Negative and questions

    Ask: 多少钱？ / 一共多少钱？

    • 这个杯子多少钱？十五块。Zhège bēizi duōshao qián? Shíwǔ kuài.How much is this cup? Fifteen yuan.
    • 一杯茶八块五。Yì bēi chá bā kuài wǔ.A cup of tea is 8.50.
    • 一斤苹果多少钱？Yì jīn píngguǒ duōshao qián?How much is a jin of apples?
  • Expressing numbers 数的表达法序数表达法1：第+数词Ordinals: 第 + number

    第 + number + measure word + noun

    第 before a number gives the order: 第一 first, 第三天 the third day. A measure word follows before a noun.

    When to use it

    Ask the order with 第几: 你是第几？

    • 今天是我第一天上课。Jīntiān shì wǒ dì yī tiān shàngkè.Today is my first day of classes.
    • 我住在第三个房间。Wǒ zhù zài dì sān ge fángjiān.I live in the third room.
    • 我住在三楼。Wǒ zhù zài sān lóu.I live on the third floor.

    Compare

    Floors, classes and years often use the number without 第: 三楼 the third floor, 一年级 first year.

  • Expressing time 时间表示法（1）年、月、日、星期表示法Dates: year, month, day, weekday

    year 年 + month 月 + day 号/日 + 星期…

    Dates go from big to small: 2025年3月8号 星期六. Years are read digit by digit: 二〇二五年. Weekdays are 星期一 to 星期六, and 星期日/星期天.

    When to use it

    Weekdays are 星期 + number; Sunday is 星期日 or 星期天. Ask with 几: 几月几号？星期几？

    • 今天是二〇二五年九月一号。Jīntiān shì èr líng èr wǔ nián jiǔ yuè yī hào.Today is 1 September 2025.
    • 明天星期几？明天星期天。Míngtiān xīngqī jǐ? Míngtiān xīngqītiān.What day is tomorrow? Sunday.
    • 你的生日是几月几号？Nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?When is your birthday?

    Exam note: Not 2025 年 read as 两千零二十五年; read 二〇二五年.

  • Expressing time 时间表示法（2）钟点表示法Telling the time

    number + 点 (+ number + 分) · 半

    点 is o'clock, 分 minutes, 半 half past: 八点 8:00, 八点十分 8:10, 八点半 8:30. Morning/afternoon words go first: 下午三点.

    When to use it

    Order: part of day + hour + minutes: 晚上七点半. 刻 (quarter, HSK 2) is also used: 八点一刻.

    Negative and questions

    Ask: 现在几点？ 你几点上班？

    • 现在几点？现在十点半。Xiànzài jǐ diǎn? Xiànzài shí diǎn bàn.What time is it? Half past ten.
    • 我们下午两点见。Wǒmen xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn jiàn.See you at two in the afternoon.
    • 你几点上班？早上八点。Nǐ jǐ diǎn shàngbān? Zǎoshang bā diǎn.What time do you start work? Eight in the morning.

    Exam note: 2:00 is 两点, not 二点.
